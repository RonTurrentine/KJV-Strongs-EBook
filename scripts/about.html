Description

A complete, offline-capable KJV Bible study tool with Strong's Hebrew/Greek lexicon, BDB/Thayer definitions, personal notes, verse highlighting, and full concordance — designed for both PC (localhost) and Kindle Fire tablet.

Bible Text Edition

This app uses the King James Version (1769) Blayney Standard Edition — the scholarly standard text that nearly all modern KJV Bibles are based on.

The KJV was first published in 1611. The text used here reflects Benjamin Blayney's 1769 Oxford revision, which standardized spelling and punctuation. Words of Christ (red letter) are aligned with Louis Klopsch's 1901 edition.
