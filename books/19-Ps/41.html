1 Blessed [H0835] is he that considereth [H7919] the poor [H1800]: the Lord [H3068] will deliver [H4422] him in time [H3117] of trouble [H7451].

2 The Lord [H3068] will preserve [H8104] him, and keep him alive [H2421]; and he shall be blessed [H0833] upon the earth [H0776]: and thou wilt not deliver [H5414] him unto the will [H5315] of his enemies [H0341].

10 But thou, O Lord [H3068], be merciful [H2603] unto me, and raise me up [H6965], that I may requite [H7999] them.

11 By this I know [H3045] that thou favourest [H2654] me, because mine enemy [H0341] doth not triumph [H7321] over me.

12 And as for me, thou upholdest [H8551] me in mine integrity [H8537], and settest [H5324] me before thy face [H6440] for ever [H5769].

13 Blessed [H1288] be the Lord [H3068] God [H0430] of Israel [H3478] from everlasting [H5769], and to everlasting [H5769]. Amen [H0543], and Amen [H0543].
