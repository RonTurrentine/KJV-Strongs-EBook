1 Unto thee, O God [H0430], do we give thanks [H3034], unto thee do we give thanks [H3034]: for that thy name [H8034] is near [H7138] thy wondrous works [H6381] declare [H5608].

2 When I shall receive [H3947] the congregation [H4150] I will judge [H8199] uprightly [H4339].

3 The earth [H0776] and all the inhabitants [H3427] thereof are dissolved [H4127]: I bear up [H8505] the pillars [H5982] of it. Selah [H5542].

4 I said [H0559] unto the fools [H1984], Deal not foolishly [H1984]: and to the wicked [H7563], Lift not up [H7311] the horn [H7161]:

5 Lift not up [H7311] your horn [H7161] on high [H4791]: speak [H1696] not with a stiff [H6277] neck [H6677].

6 For promotion [H2022][H7311] cometh neither from the east [H4161], nor from the west [H4628], nor from the south [H4057].

7 But God [H0430] is the judge [H8199]: he putteth down [H8213] one, and setteth up [H7311] another.

8 For in the hand [H3027] of the Lord [H3068] there is a cup [H3563], and the wine [H3196] is red [H2560]; it is full [H4392] of mixture [H4538]; and he poureth out [H5064] of the same [H2088]: but the dregs [H8105] thereof, all the wicked [H7563] of the earth [H0776] shall wring them out [H4680], and drink [H8354] them.

9 But I will declare [H5046] for ever [H5769]; I will sing praises [H2167] to the God [H0430] of Jacob [H3290].

10 All the horns [H7161] of the wicked [H7563] also will I cut off [H1438]; but the horns [H7161] of the righteous [H6662] shall be exalted [H7311].
