1 Blessed [H0835] is he whose transgression [H6588] is forgiven [H5375], whose sin [H2401] is covered [H3680].

2 Blessed [H0835] is the man [H0120] unto whom the Lord [H3068] imputeth [H2803] not iniquity [H5771], and in whose spirit [H7307] there is no guile [H7423].

3 When I kept silence [H2790], my bones [H6106] waxed old [H1086] through my roaring [H7581] all the day [H3117] long.

4 For day [H3119] and night [H3915] thy hand [H3027] was heavy [H3513] upon me: my moisture [H3955] is turned [H2015] into the drought [H2725] of summer [H7019]. Selah [H5542].

5 I acknowledged [H3045] my sin [H2403] unto thee, and mine iniquity [H5771] have I not hid [H3680]. I said [H0559], I will confess [H3034] my transgressions [H6588] unto the Lord [H3068]; and thou forgavest [H5375] the iniquity [H5771] of my sin [H2403]. Selah [H5542].

6 For this shall every one that is godly [H2623] pray [H6419] unto thee in a time [H6256] when thou mayest be found [H4672]: surely in the floods [H7858] of great [H7227] waters [H4325] they shall not come nigh [H5060] unto him.

7 Thou art my hiding place [H5643]; thou shalt preserve [H5341] me from trouble [H6862]; thou shalt compass [H5437] me about with songs [H7438] of deliverance [H6405]. Selah [H5542].

8 I will instruct [H7919] thee and teach [H3384] thee in the way [H1870] which [H2098] thou shalt go [H3212]: I will guide [H3289] thee with mine eye [H5869].

9 Be ye not as the horse [H5483], or as the mule [H6505], which have no understanding [H0995]: whose mouth [H5716] must be held [H1102] in with bit [H4964] and bridle [H7448], lest [H1077] they come near [H7126] unto thee.

10 Many [H7227] sorrows [H4341] shall be to the wicked [H7563]: but he that trusteth [H0982] in the Lord [H3068], mercy [H2617] shall compass [H5437] him about.

11 Be glad [H8055] in the Lord [H3068], and rejoice [H1523], ye righteous [H6662]: and shout [H7442] for joy, all ye that are upright [H3477] in heart [H3820].
