1 Why do the heathen [H1471] rage [H7283], and the people [H3816] imagine [H1897] a vain thing [H7385]?

2 The kings [H4428] of the earth [H0776] set [H3320] themselves, and the rulers [H7336] take counsel [H3245] together [H3162], against the Lord [H3068], and against his anointed [H4899], saying,

3 Let us break [H5423] their bands [H4147] asunder [H5423], and cast away [H7993] their cords [H5688] from us.

4 He that sitteth [H3427] in the heavens [H8064] shall laugh [H7832]: the Lord [H0136] shall have them in derision [H3932].

5 Then shall he speak [H1696] unto them in his wrath [H0639], and vex [H0926] them in his sore displeasure [H2740].

10 Be wise [H7919] now therefore, O ye kings [H4428]: be instructed [H3256], ye judges [H8199] of the earth [H0776].

11 Serve [H5647] the Lord [H3068] with fear [H3374], and rejoice [H1523] with trembling [H7461].

12 Kiss [H5401] the Son [H1248], lest he be angry [H0599], and ye perish [H0006] from the way [H1870], when his wrath [H0639] is kindled [H1197] but a little [H4592]. Blessed [H0835] are all they that put their trust [H2620] in him.
