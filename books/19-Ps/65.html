1 Praise [H8416] waiteth [H1747] for thee, O God [H0430], in Sion [H6726]: and unto thee shall the vow [H5088] be performed [H7999].

2 O thou that hearest [H8085] prayer [H8605], unto thee shall all flesh [H1320] come [H0935].

3 Iniquities [H5771][H1697] prevail [H1396] against me: as for our transgressions [H6588], thou shalt purge them away [H3722].

4 Blessed [H0835] is the man whom thou choosest [H0977], and causest to approach [H7126] unto thee, that he may dwell [H7931] in thy courts [H2691]: we shall be satisfied [H7646] with the goodness [H2898] of thy house [H1004], even of thy holy [H6918] temple [H1964].

7 Which stilleth [H7623] the noise [H7588] of the seas [H3220], the noise [H7588] of their waves [H1530], and the tumult [H1995] of the people [H3816].

11 Thou crownest [H5849] the year [H8141] with thy goodness [H2896]; and thy paths [H4570] drop [H7491] fatness [H1880].

12 They drop [H7491] upon the pastures [H4999] of the wilderness [H4057]: and the little hills [H1389] rejoice [H1524] on every side [H2296].

13 The pastures [H3733] are clothed [H3847] with flocks [H6629]; the valleys [H6010] also are covered over [H5848] with corn [H1250]; they shout for joy [H7321], they also sing [H7891].
