1 O God [H0430], my heart [H3820] is fixed [H3559]; I will sing [H7891] and give praise [H2167], even with my glory [H3519].

2 Awake [H5782], psaltery [H5035] and harp [H3658]: I myself will awake [H5782] early [H7837].

3 I will praise [H3034] thee, O Lord [H3068], among the people [H5971]: and I will sing praises [H2167] unto thee among the nations [H3816].

4 For thy mercy [H2617] is great [H1419] above the heavens [H8064]: and thy truth [H0571] reacheth unto the clouds [H7834].

5 Be thou exalted [H7311], O God [H0430], above the heavens [H8064]: and thy glory [H3519] above all the earth [H0776];

6 That thy beloved [H3039] may be delivered [H2502]: save [H3467] with thy right hand [H3225], and answer [H6030] me.

7 God [H0430] hath spoken [H1696] in his holiness [H6944]; I will rejoice [H5937], I will divide [H2505] Shechem [H7927], and mete out [H4058] the valley [H6010] of Succoth [H5523].

8 Gilead [H1568] is mine; Manasseh [H4519] is mine; Ephraim [H0669] also is the strength [H4581] of mine head [H7218]; Judah [H3063] is my lawgiver [H2710];

9 Moab [H4124] is my washpot [H5518][H7366]; over Edom [H0123] will I cast out [H7993] my shoe [H5275]; over Philistia [H6429] will I triumph [H7321].

10 Who will bring [H2986] me into the strong [H4013] city [H5892]? who will lead [H5148] me into Edom [H0123]?

11 Wilt not thou, O God [H0430], who hast cast us off [H2186]? and wilt not thou, O God [H0430], go forth [H3318] with our hosts [H6635]?

12 Give [H3051] us help [H5833] from trouble [H6862]: for vain [H7723] is the help [H8668] of man [H0120].

13 Through God [H0430] we shall do [H6213] valiantly [H2428]: for he it is that shall tread down [H0947] our enemies [H6862].
