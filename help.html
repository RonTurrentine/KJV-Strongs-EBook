KJV Strong's Bible

Help & Documentation — v1.3.0

🏠 Navigation

The main book index shows all 66 books of the Bible organized by Old and New Testament. Click any book to open its chapter list, then click a chapter to begin reading.

  • 🏠 Home button — returns to the main book index from any page
  • ◀B / B▶ — jump to the previous or next Bible book
  • ◀C / C▶ — jump to the previous or next chapter
  • Go To — jump directly to any book, chapter, and verse
  • Hebrew / Greek — browse the full Strong's lexicon index
  • Search — search the full KJV text (requires a live connection to your PC — see "English Word Search" below)

📱 Using Your Phone

Your phone connects to the same KJV Strong's Bible app running on your PC, over your home WiFi network. Once set up, you can read, take notes, and study fully offline — away from home, on a plane, anywhere — using content you've downloaded ahead of time.

One-time setup, on your PC:

  • The first time you connect a phone, Windows Firewall usually blocks the connection by default. Run this once, as Administrator: scripts\setup-phone-access.ps1 — see Troubleshooting below for details.

Connecting your phone (first time):

  • With the study server running on your PC, open the ☰ menu and tap Connect New Phone
  • A QR code appears on your PC screen
  • On your phone, scan it with your camera app — it'll open the app in your phone's browser automatically
  • A setup wizard appears the first time, offering to download the Bible text, download the Hebrew/Greek Lexicon, and sync your existing PC notes to the phone — go ahead and step through it

The Lexicon download is the big one (100+ MB) — worth doing on WiFi rather than cellular data, and it only needs to happen once per phone.

Installing it as a real app (recommended):

  • In Chrome, tap ⋮ (the three-dot menu) → "Install app" (wording varies by phone/Chrome version — "Add to Home screen" and "Install and create shortcut" are the same thing)
  • Important: do this while your phone shows it's connected to your PC (not the red "Offline mode" banner). Installing while disconnected can silently create a plain bookmark shortcut instead of a real app — it'll still work, but it'll keep showing the browser's address bar instead of opening full-screen like a proper app. If that happens, just uninstall it (long-press the icon → Uninstall), reconnect to your PC, and install again.
  • Once properly installed, you'll get a real app icon with no browser address bar — the same clean look as the PC version.

Downloading content for offline use:

  • ☰ menu → Download Bible Text for Offline / Download Lexicon for Offline — these are also what the first-time setup wizard runs for you automatically
  • Refresh Offline Content re-downloads everything fresh — useful if you've made a lot of changes on the PC and want your phone's copy fully up to date
  • If a download reports that some pages failed partway through (this can happen on a shaky WiFi connection), just run it again — it automatically skips everything that already succeeded and only retries what's missing

What "Offline mode" means: if your phone shows a banner saying you're offline, it just means it can't currently reach your PC — either you're away from home, or the PC app isn't running. Everything you've already downloaded (chapters, Lexicon entries, your notes) keeps working normally either way; only Search and syncing new notes back to your PC need that live connection. See English Word Search below.

App updates: when a new version is ready, your phone shows an "App Update Available" prompt with three choices — Update Now, Remind Me Later, or Remind Tomorrow. Updating only refreshes the app itself; your downloaded Bible text and Lexicon are stored separately and are never affected. If you snooze it, look for a manual Update Now item at the top of the ☰ menu whenever you're back on home WiFi — that's your reminder that an update is still waiting whenever you're ready for it.

📖 Strong's Numbers

Every word in the Bible text is linked to its Strong's reference number. These appear as small badges like H0430 (Hebrew) or G3056 (Greek) next to each word.

Click any badge to open the full lexicon entry for that word, which includes:

  • Strong's definition and how the KJV translates it
  • BDB (Hebrew) or Thayer (Greek) hierarchical definition with nested sub-meanings
  • Clickable cross-references to related words
  • A complete concordance — every verse in the Bible where that word appears

You can hide all Strong's badges using the 👁 Strong's links toggle in the ☰ settings menu, or by pressing H on your keyboard.

☰ Settings Menu

The hamburger menu (☰) in the top-right corner of every page contains the following options (some vary slightly by page):

  • Font size — increase or decrease the text size with the a↓ / A↑ buttons
  • ⚡ Sync to Kindle — push your notes and highlights to a connected Kindle Fire device (grayed out when no device is connected)
  • 📷 Connect New Phone — shows a QR code to connect a new phone over your home WiFi (see Using Your Phone above)
  • 📋 My Notes — opens the Notes Manager, where you can browse, filter, and tag every note and highlight across the whole Bible in one place (see below)
  • 🔄 Rebake Notes — re-embed your personal notes into the HTML after regenerating the Bible files
  • 💾 Export Notes / 📥 Import Notes — back up your notes to a file, or bring notes in from a backup or another device (with a conflict-resolution screen if anything overlaps)
  • Offline Access (phone only) — Download Bible Text / Download Lexicon / Refresh Offline Content
  • ❓ Help / Documentation — opens this page
  • ℹ️ About — shows version and project information
  • ✕ Exit — closes the application

📑 Notes Manager & Tags

Open My Notes from the ☰ menu to see every note and highlight you've ever made, sorted Old Testament → New Testament, in one scrollable list — no more hunting through chapters to find where you wrote something.

  • Tags — add one or more tags to any note (comma-separated) directly in the note editor, e.g. Justification, Second Coming
  • Filter by Book / Testament — narrow the list to a specific book or category; this panel can be collapsed to save space
  • Filter by Tags — tap any tag to narrow the list to notes with that tag; selecting more than one narrows further (a note must have all selected tags to match)
  • Has Any Tags / No Tags — two extra buttons to quickly find everything you've tagged, or everything you haven't gotten around to tagging yet
  • Tap Edit on any note in the list to update its text or tags without leaving the page

⌨ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • H — toggle Strong's number badges on/off
  • Ctrl + ] — increase font size
  • Ctrl + [ — decrease font size

✏ Personal Notes

You can add personal study notes to any verse. Click the gold ✏ pencil button that appears next to any verse to open the note editor.

  • Verse links — type [[Book.Ch.Vs]] in a note to create a clickable link to another verse (e.g. [[John.3.16]])
  • Book picker — use the book picker dropdown in the note editor to insert verse references easily
  • Baked notes — notes are embedded directly into the HTML pages so they appear even on Kindle without a server running
  • After regeneration — if you regenerate the Bible pages, run Rebake Notes from the ☰ menu to restore your notes

🌟 Verse Highlighting

You can highlight any verse in one of four pastel colors — yellow, green, red, or blue. The color picker is built into the note editor modal.

  • Highlights are baked into the HTML alongside notes
  • Visible in both study mode and reading mode
  • Sync to Kindle automatically includes highlights

👁 Reading Mode

The 👁 eye toggle in the top navigation bar switches between Study Mode (Strong's badges visible) and Reading Mode (clean text only, no badges). Your preference is remembered as you navigate between chapters.

🔍 English Word Search

The Search page lets you search the full KJV text for any English word or phrase. It is powered by the free Bible SuperSearch API.

  • Scope — search the whole Bible, a specific book, or any combination of categories (Torah, Historical, Poetic/Wisdom, Prophetic, Gospels, Acts, Paul's Epistles, General Epistles, Revelation)
  • Results per page — choose 50, 100, 200, or All
  • Click any result to jump directly to that verse
  • Results are remembered when you navigate to a verse and come back

On your PC: Search works whenever the KJV Strong's Bible app is running and your PC has an internet connection (it calls the Bible SuperSearch API, which is a separate live web service, not something built into the app itself). If your PC is offline, you'll see a friendly error explaining the search service isn't reachable right now.

On your phone: every search request is sent to your PC, which forwards it to the live search service — so Search only works while your phone is connected to your home WiFi with your PC's KJV Strong's Bible app running. Having cellular data or any other internet connection on your phone isn't enough by itself, since the connection that matters is specifically to your PC, not just to the internet in general. If you're away from home, the "Search" button and this notice are hidden automatically — everything else (chapters, dictionary entries, your notes) keeps working normally from what you've downloaded for offline use.

Not available on Kindle Fire.

📱 Kindle Fire Sync

If you have a Kindle Fire device connected via USB, you can sync the entire Bible (including your notes and highlights) to it using ⚡ Sync to Kindle in the ☰ settings menu.

On the Kindle, open the Silk browser and navigate to:
file:///data/local/tmp/index.html

The Sync to Kindle option will be grayed out when no Kindle device is detected. ADB (Android Debug Bridge) must be installed and the device must be connected via USB with USB debugging enabled.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Phone won't connect over WiFi / QR code doesn't work? This is almost always a Windows Firewall issue — by default, Windows blocks the port your PC uses to talk to your phone (port 8081).

To fix it, run this once on your PC, as Administrator:

scripts\setup-phone-access.ps1

This adds a one-time Windows Firewall rule allowing your phone to reach your PC over your home WiFi. You only need to run this once per PC — it isn't something the app can do on its own, since creating firewall rules requires administrator privileges that the app intentionally doesn't run with for everyday use. You may need to run it again if you set up on a new PC, or if your firewall settings ever get reset.

Installed app still shows the browser address bar? Two possible causes:

  • It was installed while your phone couldn't fully reach the PC — Chrome silently creates a plain shortcut instead of a real app in that situation, with no error to warn you. Fix: uninstall it (long-press the icon → Uninstall), reconnect to your PC first, then reinstall via ⋮ → "Install app" while connected.
  • The app is served over a plain local network address rather than HTTPS, which Android limits to showing the address bar no matter what — this is a Chrome security rule, not a bug, and affects every self-hosted app on a home network this way unless it's set up with a proper certificate. This is a known limitation and may be addressed in a future update.

Seeing a red "Offline mode" banner on your phone? This just means your phone can't currently reach your PC — either you're away from home, or the PC app isn't running. It's not an error to fix; everything you've downloaded keeps working normally. It'll clear on its own once you're back on home WiFi with the PC app running.
