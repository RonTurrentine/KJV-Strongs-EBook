אָשַׁר

Transliterationʼâshar
Phoneticaw-shar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be straight (used in the widest sense, especially to be level, right, happy); figuratively, to go forward, be honest, proper

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(call, be) bless(-ed, happy), go, guide, lead, relieve.
