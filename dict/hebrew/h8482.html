תַּחְתִּי

Transliterationtachtîy
Phonetictakh-tee'
Part of SpeechAdjective

Definition

lowermost; as noun (feminine plural) the depths (figuratively, a pit, the womb)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

low (parts, -er, -er parts, -est), nether (part).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: תּחתּי

Transliteration: tachtı̂y

Phonetic: takh-tee'

BDB Definition:

  1. low, lower, lowest
    1. lowest (as adjective)
    2. the lower parts (substantive)

Origin: from H8478

TWOT entry: 2504b
