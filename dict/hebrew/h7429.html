רָמַס

Transliterationrâmaç
Phoneticraw-mas'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to tread upon (as a potter, in walking or abusively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

oppressor, stamp upon, trample (under feet), tread (down, upon).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רמס

Transliteration: râmas

Phonetic: raw-mas'

BDB Definition:

  1. to trample
    1. (Qal)
      1. to trample
      2. trampler (participle)
    2. (Niphal) to be trampled

Origin: a primitive root
