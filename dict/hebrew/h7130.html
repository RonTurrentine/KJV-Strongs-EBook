קֶרֶב

Transliterationqereb
Phonetickeh'-reb
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, the nearest part, i.e. the center, whether literal, figurative or adverbial (especially with preposition)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] among, [idiom] before, bowels, [idiom] unto charge, [phrase] eat (up), [idiom] heart, [idiom] him, [idiom] in, inward ([idiom] -ly, part, -s, thought), midst, [phrase] out of, purtenance, [idiom] therein, [idiom] through, [idiom] within self.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: קרב

Transliteration: qereb

Phonetic: keh'-reb

BDB Definition:

  1. midst, among, inner part, middle
    1. inward part
      1. physical sense
      2. as seat of thought and emotion
      3. as faculty of thought and emotion
    2. in the midst, among, from among (of a number of persons)
    3. entrails (of sacrificial animals)
