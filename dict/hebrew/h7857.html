שָׁטַף

Transliterationshâṭaph
Phoneticshaw-taf'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to gush; by implication, to inundate, cleanse; by analogy, to gallop, conquer

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

drown, (over-) flow(-whelm, rinse, run, rush, (throughly) wash (away).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁטף

Transliteration: shâṭaph

Phonetic: shaw-taf'

BDB Definition:

  1. to wash, rinse, overflow, engulf, rinse or wash off
    1. (Qal)
      1. to overflow
      2. to flow, run
      3. to rinse or wash off
    2. (Niphal) to be swept away, be rinsed out
    3. (Pual) to be rinsed, be scoured and rinsed

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2373

Part(s) of speech: Verb
