מֲלְאָךְ

Transliterationmălʼâk
Phonetic mal-awk'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a messenger; specifically, of God, i.e. an angel (also a prophet, priest or teacher)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

ambassador, angel, king, messenger.
