עָרֵל

Transliterationʻârêl
Phoneticaw-rale'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to expose or remove the prepuce, whether literal (to go naked) or figurative (to refrain from using)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

count uncircumcised, foreskin to be uncovered.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ערל

Transliteration: ‛ârêl

Phonetic: aw-rale'

BDB Definition:

  1. to remain uncircumcised, count uncircumcised, count as foreskin
    1. (Qal)
      1. to regard as uncircumcised
      2. to remain unharvested (figuratively)
    2. (Niphal) to be counted as uncircumcised

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1695

Part(s) of speech: Verb
