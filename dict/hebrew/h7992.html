שְׁלִישִׁי

Transliterationshᵉlîyshîy
Phoneticshel-ee-shee'
Part of SpeechAdjective

Definition

third; feminine athird (part); by extension, a third (day, year or time); specifically, a third-story cell)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

third (part, rank, time), three (years old).
