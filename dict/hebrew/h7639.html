שְׂבָכָה

Transliterationsᵉbâkâh
Phoneticseb-aw-kaw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

a net-work, i.e. (in hunting) a snare, (in architecture) a ballustrade; also a reticulated ornament to apillar

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

checker, lattice, network, snare, wreath(-enwork).
