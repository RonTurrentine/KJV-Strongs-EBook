שָׁאוּל

TransliterationShâʼûwl
Phoneticshaw-ool'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Shaul, the name of an Edomite and two Israelites

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Saul, Shaul.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁאוּל

Transliteration: shâ'ûl

Phonetic: shaw-ool'

BDB Definition: Saul or Shaul = " desired"

  1. a Benjamite, son of Kish, and the 1st king of Israel
  2. an early king of Edom and a successor of Samlah
  3. a son of Simeon
  4. a Levite, son of Uzziah

Origin: pass part of H7592

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
