סָתַם

Transliterationçâtham
Phoneticsaw-tham'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to stop up; by implication, to repair; figuratively, to keep secret

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

closed up, hidden, secret, shut out (up), stop.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׂתם סתם

Transliteration: sâtham śâtham

BDB Definition:

  1. to stop up, shut up, keep close
    1. (Qal)
      1. to stop up
      2. to shut up, keep close
      3. secret (participle)
    2. (Niphal) to be stopped up
    3. (Piel) to stop up

Origin: a primitive root
