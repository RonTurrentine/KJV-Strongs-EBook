שָׁלַךְ

Transliterationshâlak
Phoneticshaw-lak
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to throw out, down or away (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

adventure, cast (away, down, forth, off, out), hurl, pluck, throw.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁלך

Transliteration: shâlak

Phonetic: shaw-lak

BDB Definition:

  1. to throw, cast, hurl, fling
    1. (Hiphil)
      1. to throw, cast, throw away, cast off, shed, cast down
      2. to cast (lots) (figuratively)
    2. (Hophal)
      1. to be thrown, be cast
      2. to be cast forth or out
      3. to be cast down
      4. to be cast (metaphorically)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2398

Part(s) of speech: Verb
