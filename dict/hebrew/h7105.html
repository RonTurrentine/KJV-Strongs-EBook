קָצִיר

Transliterationqâtsîyr
Phonetickaw-tseer'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

severed, i.e. harvest (as reaped), the crop, the time, the reaper, or figuratively; also a limb (of a tree, or simply foliage)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bough, branch, harvest (man).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: קציר

BDB Definition:

  1. harvest, harvesting

    2. crop, what is harvested or reaped
    3. time of harvest
  2. boughs, branches
