חָשַׂף

Transliterationchâsaph
Phonetickhaw-saf'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to strip off, i.e. generally to make naked (for exertion or in disgrace), to drain away or bail up (a liquid)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

make bare, clean, discover, draw out, take, uncover.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חשׂף

Transliteration: châśaph

Phonetic: khaw-saf'

BDB Definition:

  1. to strip, strip off, lay bare, make bare, draw out
    1. (Qal)
      1. to strip off
      2. to strip, lay bare
      3. to draw (water), skim, take from the surface

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 766

Part(s) of speech: Verb
