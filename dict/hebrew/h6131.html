עָקַר

Transliterationʻâqar
Phoneticaw-kar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to pluck up (especially by the roots); specifically, to hamstring; figuratively, to exterminate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dig down, hough, pluck up, root up.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: עקר

Transliteration: ‛âqar

Phonetic: aw-kar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to pluck up, root up
    1. (Qal) to pluck up, root up
    2. (Niphal) to be plucked up
  2. to cut, hamstring
    1. (Piel) to cut, hamstring

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1681,1682

Part(s) of speech: Verb
