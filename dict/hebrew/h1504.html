גָּזַר

Transliterationgâzar
Phoneticgaw-zar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to cut down or off; (figuratively) to destroy, divide, exclude, or decide

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cut down (off), decree, divide, snatch.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: גּזר

Transliteration: gâzar

Phonetic: gaw-zar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to cut, divide, cut down, cut off, cut in two, snatch, decree
    1. (Qal)
      1. to cut in two, divide
      2. to cut down
      3. to cut off, destroy, exterminate
      4. to decree
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be cut off, separated, excluded
      2. to be destroyed, cut off
      3. to be decreed

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 340

Part(s) of speech: Verb
