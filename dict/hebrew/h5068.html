נָדַב

Transliterationnâdab
Phoneticnaw-dab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to impel; hence, to volunteer (as a soldier), to present spontaneously

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

offer freely, be (give, make, offer self) willing(-ly).

  1. to incite, impel, make willing
    1. (Qal) to incite, impel
    2. (Hithpael)
      1. to volunteer
      2. to offer free-will offerings
