חָפַשׂ

Transliterationchâphas
Phonetickhaw-fas'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to seek; causatively, to conceal oneself (i.e. let be sought), or mask

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

change, (make) diligent (search), disquise self, hide, search (for, out).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חפשׂ

Transliteration: châphaś

Phonetic: khaw-fas'

BDB Definition:

  1. to search, search for, to search out, disguise oneself
    1. (Qal)
      1. to search for
      2. to think out, devise
      3. to search, test
    2. (Niphal) to be searched out, be exposed
    3. (Piel) to search, search through, search for
    4. (Pual) to be searched for, be sought out
    5. (Hithpael)
      1. to disguise oneself
      2. to let oneself be searched for

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 716

Part(s) of speech: Verb
