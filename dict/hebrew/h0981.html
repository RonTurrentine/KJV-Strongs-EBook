בָּטָא

Transliterationbâṭâʼ
Phoneticbaw-taw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to babble; hence, to vociferate angrily

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

pronounce, speak (unadvisedly).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּטה בּטא

Transliteration: bâṭâ' bâṭâh

Phonetic: baw-taw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to speak rashly or angrily, speak thoughtlessly
    1. (Qal) one that babbles, speaks rashly (participle)
    2. (Piel) to speak rashly, unadvisedly

Origin: a primitive root

Part(s) of speech: Verb
