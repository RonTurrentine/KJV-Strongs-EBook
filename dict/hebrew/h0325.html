אֲחַשְׁוֵרוֹשׁ

TransliterationʼĂchashvêrôwsh
Phoneticakh-ash-vay-rosh'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Achashverosh (i.e. Ahasuerus or Artaxerxes, but in this case Xerxes), the title (rather than name) of a Persian king

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Ahasuerus.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אחשׁרשׁ אחשׁורושׁ

Transliteration: 'ăchashvêrôsh 'achashrôsh

Phonetic: akh-ash-vay-rosh'

BDB Definition: Ahasuerus = " I will be silent and poor"

  1. title of the king of Persia, probably Xerxes

Origin: of Persian origin

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
