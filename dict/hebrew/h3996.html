מָבוֹא

Transliterationmâbôwʼ
Phoneticmaw-bo'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

an entrance (the place or the act); specifically sunset or the west; also (adverb with preposition) towards

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage
