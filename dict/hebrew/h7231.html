רָבַב

Transliterationrâbab
Phoneticraw-bab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to cast together , i.e. increase, especially in number; to multiply by the myriad

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

increase, be many(-ifold), be more, multiply, ten thousands.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רבב

Transliteration: râbab

Phonetic: raw-bab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be or become many, be or become much, be or become great
    1. (Qal)
      1. to be or become many
      2. to be or become great
      3. to be long (of journey)
  2. (Pual) ten thousands

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2099

Part(s) of speech: Verb
