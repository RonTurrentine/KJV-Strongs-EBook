רָשָׁע

Transliterationrâshâʻ
Phoneticraw-shaw'
Part of SpeechAdjective

Definition

morally wrong; concretely, an (actively) bad person

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[phrase] condemned, guilty, ungodly, wicked (man), that did wrong.
