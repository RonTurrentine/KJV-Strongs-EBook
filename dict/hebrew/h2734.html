חָרָה

Transliterationchârâh
Phonetickhaw-raw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to glow or grow warm; figuratively (usually) to blaze up, of anger, zeal, jealousy

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be angry, burn, be displeased, [idiom] earnestly, fret self, grieve, be (wax) hot, be incensed, kindle, [idiom] very, be wroth. See .

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חרה

Transliteration: chârâh

Phonetic: khaw-raw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be hot, furious, burn, become angry, be kindled
    1. (Qal) to burn, kindle (anger)
    2. (Niphal) to be angry with, be incensed
    3. (Hiphil) to burn, kindle
    4. (Hithpael) to heat oneself in vexation

Origin: a primitive root [compare H2787]

TWOT entry: 736

Part(s) of speech: Verb
