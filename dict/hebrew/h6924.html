קֶדֶם

Transliterationqedem
Phonetickeh'-dem
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

the front, of place (absolutely, the fore part, relatively the East) or time (antiquity); often used adverbially (before, anciently, eastward)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

aforetime, ancient (time), before, east (end, part, side, -ward), eternal, [idiom] ever(-lasting), forward, old, past. Compare .
