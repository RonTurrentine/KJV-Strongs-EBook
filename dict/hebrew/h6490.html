פִּקּוּד

Transliterationpiqqûwd
Phonetic pik-kood'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, appointed, i.e. a mandate (of God; plural only, collectively, for the Law)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

commandment, precept, statute.
