מָקוֹר

Transliterationmâqôwr
Phoneticmaw-kore'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, something dug, i.e. a (general) source (of water, even when naturally flowing; also of tears, blood (by euphemism, of the female pudenda); figuratively, of happiness, wisdom, progeny)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fountain, issue, spring, well(-spring).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מקר מקור

Transliteration: mâqôr mâqôr

BDB Definition:

    1. spring
      1. of source of life, joy, purification (figuratively)
    2. of the eye (figuratively)
    3. source (of menstruous blood)
    4. flow (of blood after child birth)
