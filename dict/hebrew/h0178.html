אוֹב

Transliterationʼôwb
Phoneticobe
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, a mumble, i.e. a water skin (from its hollow sound); hence a necromancer (ventriloquist, as from a jar)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bottle, familiar spirit.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אוב

Transliteration: 'ôb

Phonetic: obe

BDB Definition:

  1. water skin bottle
  2. necromancer
    1. necromancer, one who evokes the dead
    2. ghost, spirit of a dead one
    3. practice of necromancy
  3. one that has a familiar spirit

Origin: from the same as H0001 (apparently through the idea of prattling a father's name)

TWOT entry: 37a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
