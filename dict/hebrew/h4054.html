מִגְרָשׁ

Transliterationmigrâsh
Phoneticmig-rawsh'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a suburb (i.e. open country whither flocks are driven from pasture); hence, the area around abuilding, or the margin of the sea

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cast out, suburb.
