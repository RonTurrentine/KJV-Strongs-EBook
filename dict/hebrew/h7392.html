רָכַב

Transliterationrâkab
Phoneticraw-kab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to ride (on an animal or in a vehicle); causatively, to place upon (for riding or generally), to despatch

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bring (on (horse-) back), carry, get (oneself) up, on (horse-) back, put, (cause to, make to) ride (in a chariot, on, -r), set.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רכב

Transliteration: râkab

Phonetic: raw-kab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to mount and ride, ride
    1. (Qal)
      1. to mount, mount and sit or ride
      2. to ride, be riding
      3. rider (substantive)
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to cause to ride, cause to (mount and) ride
      2. to cause to draw (plough, etc)
      3. to cause to ride upon (figuratively)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2163

Part(s) of speech: Verb
