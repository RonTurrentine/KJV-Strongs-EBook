Definition

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fif(-teen), fifth, five ([idiom] apiece).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חמשּׁה חמשׁ

Transliteration: châmêsh chămishshâh

Phonetic: khaw-maysh'

BDB Definition:

  1. five
    1. five (cardinal number)
    2. a multiple of five (with another number)
    3. fifth (ordinal number)

Origin: a primitive numeral

TWOT entry: TWOT- 686a
