Definition

that, used as relative conjunction, and especially (with a preposition) in adverbial phrases; also as preposition of

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] as, but, for(-asmuch [phrase]), [phrase] now, of, seeing, than, that, therefore, until, [phrase] what (-soever), when, which, whom, whose.

BDB / Thayer Definition

BDB Definition:

  1. who, which, that (particle of relation)
  2. that of, which belongs to, that (mark of genitive)
  3. that, because (conjunction)
