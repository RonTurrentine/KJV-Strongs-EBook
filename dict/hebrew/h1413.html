גָּדַד

Transliterationgâdad
Phoneticgaw-dad'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to crowd; also to gash (as if by pressing into)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

assemble (selves by troops), gather (selves together, self in troops), cut selves.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: גּדד

Transliteration: gâdad

Phonetic: gaw-dad'

BDB Definition:

  1. to penetrate, cut, attack, invade
    1. (Qal) to penetrate, cut into
    2. (Hithpoel)
      1. to cut oneself
      2. to gather in troops or crowds

Origin: a primitive root [compare H1464]
