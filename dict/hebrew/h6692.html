צוּץ

Transliterationtsûwts
Phonetictsoots
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to twinkle, i.e. glance; by analogy, to blossom (figuratively, flourish)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bloom, blossom, flourish, shew self.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: צוּץ

Transliteration: tsûts

Phonetic: tsoots

BDB Definition:

  1. to blossom, shine, sparkle
    1. (Qal)
      1. to flourish
      2. to shine, gleam
    2. (Hiphil) to blossom, put forth blossoms, produce blossoms
  2. (Hiphil) to gaze, peep, glance, make the eyes sparkle

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1893,1894

Part(s) of speech: Verb
