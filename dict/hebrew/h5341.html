נָצַר

Transliterationnâtsar
Phoneticnaw-tsar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to guard, in a good sense (to protect, maintain, obey, etc.) or a bad one (to conceal, etc.)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

besieged, hidden thing, keep(-er, -ing), monument, observe, preserve(-r), subtil, watcher(-man).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נצר

Transliteration: nâtsar

Phonetic: naw-tsar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to guard, watch, watch over, keep
    1. (Qal)
      1. to watch, guard, keep
      2. to preserve, guard from dangers
      3. to keep, observe, guard with fidelity
      4. to guard, keep secret
      5. to be kept close, be blockaded
      6. watchman (participle)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1407

Part(s) of speech: Verb
