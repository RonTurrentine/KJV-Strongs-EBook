אָפִיק

Transliterationʼâphîyq
Phoneticaw-feek'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, containing, i.e. a tube; also a bed or valley of astream; also a strong thing or a hero

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

brook, channel, mighty, river, [phrase] scale, stream, strong piece.
