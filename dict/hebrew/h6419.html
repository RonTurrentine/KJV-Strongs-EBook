פָּלַל

Transliterationpâlal
Phoneticpaw-lal'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to judge (officially or mentally); by extension, to intercede, pray

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

intreat, judge(-ment), (make) pray(-er, -ing), make supplication.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: פּלל

Transliteration: pâlal

Phonetic: paw-lal'

BDB Definition:

  1. to intervene, interpose, pray
    1. (Piel) to mediate, judge
    2. (Hithpael)
      1. to intercede
      2. to pray

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1776

Part(s) of speech: Verb
