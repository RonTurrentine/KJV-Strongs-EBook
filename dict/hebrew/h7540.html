רָקַד

Transliterationrâqad
Phoneticraw-kad'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to stamp, i.e. to spring about (wildly or for joy)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dance, jump, leap, skip.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רקד

Transliteration: râqad

Phonetic: raw-kad'

BDB Definition:

  1. to skip about
    1. (Qal) to skip about
    2. (Piel) to dance, leap
    3. (Hiphil) to make to skip

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2214

Part(s) of speech: Verb
