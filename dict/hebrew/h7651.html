שֶׁבַע

Transliterationshebaʻ
Phoneticsheh'-bah
Part of SpeechNoun

Definition

seven (as the sacred full one); also (adverbially) seven times; by implication, a week; by extension, an indefinite number

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

([phrase] by) seven(-fold),-s, (-teen, -teenth), -th, times). Compare .

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁבעה שׁבע

Transliteration: sheba‛ shib‛âh

Phonetic: sheh'-bah

BDB Definition:

  1. seven (cardinal number)
    1. as ordinal number
    2. in combination - 17, 700 etc
