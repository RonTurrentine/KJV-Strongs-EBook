Definition

to flee for protection; figuratively, to confide in

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

have hope, make refuge, (put) trust.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חסה

Transliteration: châsâh

Phonetic: khaw-saw'

BDB Definition:

  1. (Qal) to seek refuge, flee for protection
    1. to put trust in (God), confide or hope in (God) (figuratively)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 700

Part(s) of speech: Verb
