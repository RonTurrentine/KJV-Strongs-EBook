בְּתוּלָה

Transliterationbᵉthûwlâh
Phoneticbeth-oo-law'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

a virgin (from her privacy); sometimes (by continuation) a bride; also (figuratively) a city or state

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

maid, virgin.

BDB / Thayer Definition
