Sepharvaim

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ספרים ספרוים o

Transliteration: sepharvayim sephârı̂ym

Phonetic: sef-ar-vah'-yim

BDB Definition: Sepharvaim = " the two Sipparas"

  1. a city in Syria conquered by the king of Assyria
    1. perhaps near the modern 'Mosaib' and on the Euphrates above Babylon

Origin: of foreign derivation

TWOT entry: None
