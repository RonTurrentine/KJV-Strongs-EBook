כָּתַב

Transliterationkâthab
Phonetickaw-thab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to grave, by implication, to write (describe, inscribe, prescribe, subscribe)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

describe, record, prescribe, subscribe, write(-ing, -ten).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּתב

Transliteration: kâthab

Phonetic: kaw-thab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to write, record, enroll
    1. (Qal)
      1. to write, inscribe, engrave, write in, write on
      2. to write down, describe in writing
      3. to register, enroll, record
      4. to decree
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be written
      2. to be written down, be recorded, be enrolled
    3. (Piel) to continue writing

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1053

Part(s) of speech: Verb
