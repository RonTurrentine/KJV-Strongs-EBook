מִנְחָה

Transliterationminchâh
Phoneticmin-khaw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

a donation; euphemistically, tribute; specifically a sacrificial offering (usually bloodless and voluntary)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

gift, oblation, (meat) offering, present, sacrifice.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מנחה

Transliteration: minchâh

Phonetic: min-khaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. gift, tribute, offering, present, oblation, sacrifice, meat offering
    1. gift, present
    2. tribute
    3. offering (to God)
    4. grain offering

Origin: from an unused root meaning to apportion, i.e. bestow

TWOT entry: 1214a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
