יָגַע

Transliterationyâgaʻ
Phoneticyaw-gah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to gasp; hence, to be exhausted, to tire, to toil

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

faint, (make to) labour, (be) weary.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יגע

Transliteration: yâga‛

Phonetic: yaw-gah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to toil, labour, grow weary, be weary
    1. (Qal)
      1. to toil, labour
      2. to grow weary, be weary
    2. (Piel) to weary, make weary, cause to go toilsomely
    3. (Hiphil) to make to toil, make weary, cause to be weary

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 842

Part(s) of speech: Verb
