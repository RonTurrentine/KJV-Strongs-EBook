TransliterationTêymân
Phonetictay-mawn'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine / Noun Proper Location

Definition

Teman, the name of two Edomites, and of the region and descendant of one of them

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

south, Teman.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: תּמן תּימן

Transliteration: têymân têmân

Phonetic: tay-mawn'

BDB Definition: Teman = " south"

  1. son of Eliphaz, grandson of Esau, and one of the dukes of Edom (noun proper masculine)
  2. the tribe descended from 1 noted for the wisdom of its people (noun proper masculine)
  3. the region occupied by the descendants of 1, located east of Idumea (noun proper locative)
