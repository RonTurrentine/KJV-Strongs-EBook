רָמַשׂ

Transliterationrâmas
Phoneticraw-mas'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to glide swiftly, i.e. to crawl or move with short steps; by analogy to swarm

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

creep, move.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רמשׂ

Transliteration: râmaś

Phonetic: raw-mas'

BDB Definition:

  1. to creep, move lightly, move about, walk on all fours
    1. (Qal)
      1. to creep, teem (of all creeping things)
      2. to creep (of animals)
      3. to move lightly, glide about (of water animals)
      4. to move about (of all land animals generally)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2177

Part(s) of speech: Verb
