BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אביעזר

Transliteration: 'ăbı̂y‛ezer

Phonetic: ab-ee-ay'-zer

BDB Definition: Abiezer = " my father is help"

  1. a Manassite, called " son" of Gilead, also son of Gilead's sister
  2. a Benjamite, warrior of David

Origin: from H0001 and H5829
