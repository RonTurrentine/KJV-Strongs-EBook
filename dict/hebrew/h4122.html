Definition

Maher-Shalal-Chash-Baz; the symbolical name of the son of Isaiah

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Maher-sha-lal-bash-baz.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מהר שׁלל חשׁ בּז

Transliteration: mahêr shâlâl châsh baz

Phonetic: mah-hare' shaw-lawl' khawsh baz

BDB Definition: = Maher-shalal-hash-baz " swift is booty, speedy is prey"

  1. symbolic name given by Isaiah by the Lord's direction to Isaiah's son; prophetic indication that Damascus and Samaria were soon to be plundered by the king of Assyria

Origin: from H4118 and H7998 and H2363 and H0957

TWOT entry: None
