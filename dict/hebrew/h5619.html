סָקַל

Transliterationçâqal
Phoneticsaw-kal'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to be weighty; but used only in the sense of lapidation or its contrary (as if a delapidation)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(cast, gather out, throw) stone(-s), [idiom] surely.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: סקל

Transliteration: sâqal

Phonetic: saw-kal'

BDB Definition:

  1. to stone (to death), put to death by stoning
    1. (Qal) to pelt with stones, stone to death
    2. (Niphal) to be stoned to death
    3. (Piel)
      1. to stone, pelt with stones
      2. to free from stones (of vineyard, highway)
    4. (Pual) to be stoned to death

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1541

Part(s) of speech: Verb
