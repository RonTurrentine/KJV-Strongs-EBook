אַבְרָהָם

TransliterationʼAbrâhâm
Phoneticab-raw-hawm'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Abraham, the later name of Abram

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Abraham.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אברהם

Transliteration: 'abrâhâm

Phonetic: ab-raw-hawm'

BDB Definition: Abraham = " father of a multitude" or " chief of multitude"

  1. friend of God and founder of Hebrew nation via God's elective covenant

Origin: contracted from H0001 and an unused root (probably meaning to be populous)

TWOT entry: 4b

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
