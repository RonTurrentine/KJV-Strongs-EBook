אֵם

Transliterationʼêm
Phoneticame
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

a mother (as the bond of the family); in a wide sense (both literally and figuratively (like father))

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dam, mother, [idiom] parting.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אם

Transliteration: 'êm

Phonetic: ame

BDB Definition:

  1. mother
    1. of humans
    2. of Deborah's relationship to the people (figuratively)
    3. of animals
  2. point of departure or division

Origin: a primitive word

TWOT entry: 115a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
