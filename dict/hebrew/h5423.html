נָתַק

Transliterationnâthaq
Phoneticnaw-thak'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to tear off

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

break (off), burst, draw (away), lift up, pluck (away, off), pull (out), root out.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נתק

Transliteration: nâthaq

Phonetic: naw-thak'

BDB Definition:

  1. to pull or tear or draw off or away or apart, draw out, pluck up, break, lift, root out
    1. (Qal)
      1. to draw away
      2. to draw or pull off
      3. to pull or tear away
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be drawn away, be drawn out
      2. to be torn apart or in two, be snapped
      3. to be separated
    3. (Piel)
      1. to tear apart, snap
      2. to tear out, tear up, tear away
    4. (Hiphil)
      1. to draw away
      2. to drag away
    5. (Hophal) to be drawn away

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1447

Part(s) of speech: Verb
