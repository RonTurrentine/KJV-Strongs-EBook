מָחָר

Transliterationmâchâr
Phoneticmaw-khar'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, deferred, i.e. the morrow; usually (adverbially) tomorrow; indefinitely, hereafter

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

time to come, tomorrow.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מחר

Transliteration: mâchâr

Phonetic: maw-khar'

BDB Definition:

  1. tomorrow, in time to come, in the future
    1. tomorrow (as the day following the present day)
    2. in future time

Origin: probably from H0309

TWOT entry: 1185a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
