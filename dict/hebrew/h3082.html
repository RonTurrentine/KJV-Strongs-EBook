BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יהונדב

Transliteration: yehônâdâb

Phonetic: yeh-ho-naw-dawb'

BDB Definition: Jehonadab = " Jehovah is willing"

  1. a son of Rechab, chief of the Rechabites, in the time of Jehu and Ahab
  2. a nephew of David

Origin: from H3068 and H5068

TWOT entry: None
