זָמַר

Transliterationzâmar
Phoneticzaw-mar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

play upon it; to make music, accompanied by the voice; hence to celebrate in song and music

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

give praise, sing forth praises, psalms.
