מִלֻּא

Transliterationmilluʼ
Phonetic mil-loo'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a fulfilling (only in plural), i.e. (literally) a setting (of gems), or (technically) consecration (also concretely, a dedicatory sacrifice)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

consecration, be set.
