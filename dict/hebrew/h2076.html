זָבַח

Transliterationzâbach
Phoneticzaw-bakh'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to slaughter an animal (usually in sacrifice)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

kill, offer, (do) sacrifice, slay.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: זבח

Transliteration: zâbach

Phonetic: zaw-bakh'

BDB Definition:

  1. to slaughter, kill, sacrifice, slaughter for sacrifice
    1. (Qal)
      1. to slaughter for sacrifice
      2. to slaughter for eating
      3. to slaughter in divine judgment
    2. (Piel) to sacrifice, offer sacrifice

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 525

Part(s) of speech: Verb
