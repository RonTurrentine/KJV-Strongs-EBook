יוֹנַת אֵלֶם רְחֹקִים

Transliterationyôwnath ʼêlem rᵉchôqîym
Phoneticyo-nath' ay'-lem rekh-o-keem'
Part of SpeechNoun

Definition

dove of (the) silence (i.e. dumb Israel) of (i.e. among) distances (i.e. strangers); the title of a ditty (used for a name of its melody)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Jonath-elem-rechokim.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יונת אלם רחקים

Transliteration: yônath 'êlem rechôqı̂ym

Phonetic: yo-nath' ay'-lem rekh-o-keem'

BDB Definition: Jonath-elem-recho-kim = " the silent dove of far-off places"

  1. meaning uncertain
