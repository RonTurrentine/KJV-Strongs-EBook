Definition

Jechizkijah, the name of five Israelites

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Hezekiah, Jehizkiah. Compare .

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יחזקיּהוּ יחזקיּה

Transliteration: yechizqı̂yâh yechizqı̂yâhû

Phonetic: yekh-iz-kee-yaw'

BDB Definition: Hezekiah or Jehizkiah = " Jehovah has made strong"

  1. son of king Ahaz by Abi or Abijah and the 12th king of Judah; his reign was characterised by his godly conduct; reigned for 29 years
  2. head of a family of returning exiles in the time of Nehemiah
  3. an Ephraimite in the time of Ahaz

Origin: from H2388 and H3050

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
