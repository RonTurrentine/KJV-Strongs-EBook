Definition

a voice or sound

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[phrase] aloud, bleating, crackling, cry ([phrase] out), fame, lightness, lowing, noise, [phrase] hold peace, (pro-) claim, proclamation, [phrase] sing, sound, [phrase] spark, thunder(-ing), voice, [phrase] yell.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: קל קול

Transliteration: qôl qôl

Phonetic: kole

BDB Definition:

  1. voice, sound, noise
    1. voice
    2. sound (of instrument)
  2. lightness, frivolity

Origin: from an unused root meaning to call aloud

TWOT entry: 1998a,2028b
