רוּשׁ

Transliterationrûwsh
Phoneticroosh
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be destitute

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

lack, needy, (make self) poor (man).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רוּשׁ

Transliteration: rûsh

Phonetic: roosh

BDB Definition:

  1. to be poor, be in want, lack
    1. (Qal)
      1. to be poor
      2. to be in want or hunger
      3. poor man (substantive)
    2. (Hithpolel) one impoverishing himself (participle)

Origin: a primitive root

Part(s) of speech: Verb
