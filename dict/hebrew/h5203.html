נָטַשׁ

Transliterationnâṭash
Phoneticnaw-tash'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to pound, i.e. smite; by implication (as if beating out, and thus expanding) to disperse; also, to thrust off, down, out or upon (inclusively, reject, let alone, permit, remit, etc.)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cast off, drawn, let fall, forsake, join (battle), leave (off), lie still, loose, spread (self) abroad, stretch out, suffer.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נטשׁ

Transliteration: nâṭash

Phonetic: naw-tash'

BDB Definition:

  1. to leave, permit, forsake, cast off or away, reject, suffer, join, spread out or abroad, be loosed, cease, abandon, quit, hang loose, cast down, make a raid, lie fallow, let fall, forgo, draw
    1. (Qal)
      1. to leave, let alone, lie fallow, entrust to
      2. to forsake, abandon
      3. to permit
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be forsaken
      2. to be loosened, be loose
      3. to be let go, spread abroad
    3. (Pual) to be abandoned, be deserted

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1357

Part(s) of speech: Verb
