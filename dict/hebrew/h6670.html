צָהַל

Transliterationtsâhal
Phonetictsaw-hal'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to gleam, i.e. (figuratively) be cheerful; by transference to sound clear (of various animal or human expressions)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bellow, cry aloud (out), lift up, neigh, rejoice, make to shine, shout.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: צהל

Transliteration: tsâhal

Phonetic: tsaw-hal'

BDB Definition:

  1. to neigh, cry shrilly
    1. (Qal)
      1. to neigh (of men as stallions - figuratively)
      2. (Piel) to cry shrilly (in distress)
  2. (Hiphil) to make shining

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1881,1882

Part(s) of speech: Verb
