at, (hard) by, (from) (beside), near (unto), toward, with. See also .

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אצל

Transliteration: 'êtsel

BDB Definition:

  1. beside, by, near, nearness, joining, proximity
  2. (BDB) (substantive)
    1. conjunction, proximity
    2. beside, in proximity, contiguous to, from beside
