דָּקַר

Transliterationdâqar
Phoneticdaw-kar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to stab; by analogy, to starve; figuratively, to revile

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

pierce, strike (thrust) through, wound.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: דּקר

Transliteration: dâqar

Phonetic: daw-kar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to pierce, thrust through, pierce through
    1. (Qal) to pierce, run through
    2. (Niphal) to be pierced through
    3. (Pual) pierced, riddled (participle)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 449

Part(s) of speech: Verb
