חֵסֵד

Transliterationchêçêd
Phonetickheh'-sed
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

kindness; by implication (towards God) piety; rarely (by opposition) reproof, or (subject.) beauty

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

favour, good deed(-liness, -ness), kindly, (loving-) kindness, merciful (kindness), mercy, pity, reproach, wicked thing.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חסד

Transliteration: chêsêd

Phonetic: kheh'-sed

BDB Definition:

  1. goodness, kindness, faithfulness
  2. a reproach, shame

Origin: from H2616

TWOT entry: 698a,699a
