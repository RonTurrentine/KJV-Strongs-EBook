כּוֹס

Transliterationkôwç
Phonetickoce
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

a cup (as a container), often figuratively, a lot (as if a potion); also some unclean bird, probably an owl (perhaps from the cup-like cavity of its eye)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cup, (small) owl. Compare .

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּוס

Transliteration: kôs

Phonetic: koce

BDB Definition:

  1. cup (noun feminine)
  2. a kind of owl (an unclean bird) (noun masculine)

Origin: from an unused root meaning to hold together
