Transliterationkâbar
Phonetickaw-bar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to plait together, i.e. (figuratively) to augment (especially in number or quantity, to accumulate)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

in abundance, multiply.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Transliteration: kâbar

Phonetic: kaw-bar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be much, be many, be in abundance, intertwine, multiply
    1. (Hiphil) to make many, make great

Origin: a primitive root
