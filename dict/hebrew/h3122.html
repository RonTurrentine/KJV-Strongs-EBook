BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יונדב

Transliteration: yônâdâb

Phonetic: yo-naw-dawb'

BDB Definition: Jonadab or Jehonadab = " Jehovah is willing"

  1. a son of Rechab, chief of the Rechabites, in the time of Jehu and Ahab
  2. a nephew of David

Origin: a form of H3082
