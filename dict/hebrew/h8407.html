Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Tiglath-Pileser or Tilgath-pilneser, an Assyryrian king

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Tiglath-pileser, Tilgath-pilneser.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: תּלגּת פּלנאסר תּגלת פּלאסר

Transliteration: tiglath pil'eser tilgath pilne'eser

Phonetic: tig-lath' pil-eh'-ser

BDB Definition: Tiglath-pileser or Tilgath-pilneser = " thou will uncover the wonderful bond"

  1. an Assyrian king who attacked Samaria or northern kingdom of Israel in the reign of Pekah

Origin: of foreign derivation

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
