כֹּהֵן

Transliterationkôhên
Phoneticko-hane'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

literally one officiating, a priest; also (by courtesy) an acting priest (although a layman)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

chief ruler, [idiom] own, priest, prince, principal officer.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּהן

Transliteration: kôhên

Phonetic: ko-hane'

BDB Definition:

  1. priest, principal officer or chief ruler
    1. priest-king (Melchizedek, Messiah)
    2. pagan priests
    3. priests of Jehovah
    4. Levitical priests
    5. Zadokite priests
    6. Aaronic priests
    7. the high priest

Origin: active participle of H3547
