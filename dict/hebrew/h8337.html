שֵׁשׁ

Transliterationshêsh
Phoneticshaysh
Part of SpeechNoun

Definition

six (as an overplus beyond five or the fingers of the hand); as ord. sixth

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

six(-teen, -teenth), sixth.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁשּׁה שׁשׁ

Transliteration: shêsh shishshâh

Phonetic: shaysh

BDB Definition:

  1. six
    1. six (cardinal number)
    2. sixth (ordinal number)
    3. in combination with other numbers

Origin: a primitive number

TWOT entry: 2336a

Part(s) of speech: Noun
