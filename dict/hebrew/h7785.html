BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁוק

Transliteration: shôq

Phonetic: shoke

BDB Definition:

  1. leg, thigh
    1. of man; specifically the lower leg (calf) as opposed to the thigh
    2. of sacrificial animal; specifically the thigh, upper leg, hind leg, as the portion eaten

Origin: from H7783
