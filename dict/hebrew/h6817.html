צָעַק

Transliterationtsâʻaq
Phonetictsaw-ak'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to shriek; (by implication) to proclaim (an assembly)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] at all, call together, cry (out), gather (selves) (together).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: צעק

Transliteration: tsâ‛aq

Phonetic: tsaw-ak'

BDB Definition:

  1. to cry, cry out, call, cry for help
    1. (Qal)
      1. to cry, cry out (for help)
      2. to cry, cry out (in distress or need)
      3. to make outcry, clamour
    2. (Niphal) to be summoned
    3. (Piel) to cry aloud (in grief)
    4. (Hiphil) to call together

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1947

Part(s) of speech: Verb
