סָכַךְ

Transliterationçâkak
Phoneticsaw-kak'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to entwine as ascreen; by implication, to fence in, cover over, (figuratively) protect

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cover, defence, defend, hedge in, join together, set, shut up.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׂכך סכך

Transliteration: sâkak śâkak

Phonetic: saw-kak'

BDB Definition:

  1. (Qal) to hedge, fence about, shut in
  2. to block, overshadow, screen, stop the approach, shut off, cover
    1. (Qal)
      1. to screen, cover
      2. to cover oneself
      3. protector (participle)
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to screen, cover
      2. to cover, defecate (euphemism)
  3. (Qal) to cover, lay over
  4. to weave together
    1. (Qal) to weave together
    2. (Pilpel) to weave, weave together

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1475,1492,2259,2260

Part(s) of speech: Verb
