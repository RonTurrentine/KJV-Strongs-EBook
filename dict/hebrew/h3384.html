יָרָה

Transliterationyârâh
Phoneticyaw-raw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to flow as water (i.e. to rain); transitively, to lay or throw (especially an arrow, i.e. to shoot); figuratively, to point out (as if by aiming the finger), to teach

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

([phrase]) archer, cast, direct, inform, instruct, lay, shew, shoot, teach(-er,-ing), through.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ירא ירה

Transliteration: yârâh yârâ'

Phonetic: yaw-raw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to throw, shoot, cast, pour
    1. (Qal)
      1. to throw, cast
      2. to cast, lay, set
      3. to shoot arrows
      4. to throw water, rain
    2. (Niphal) to be shot
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to throw, cast
      2. to shoot
      3. to point out, show
      4. to direct, teach, instruct
      5. to throw water, rain

Origin: a primitive root
