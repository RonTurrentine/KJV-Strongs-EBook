נָכָה

Transliterationnâkâh
Phoneticnaw-kaw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to strike (lightly or severely, literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beat, cast forth, clap, give (wounds), [idiom] go forward, [idiom] indeed, kill, make (slaughter), murderer, punish, slaughter, slay(-er, -ing), smite(-r, -ing), strike, be stricken, (give) stripes, [idiom] surely, wound.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נכה

Transliteration: nâkâh

Phonetic: naw-kaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to strike, smite, hit, beat, slay, kill
    1. (Niphal) to be stricken or smitten
    2. (Pual) to be stricken or smitten
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to smite, strike, beat, scourge, clap, applaud, give a thrust
      2. to smite, kill, slay (man or beast)
      3. to smite, attack, attack and destroy, conquer, subjugate, ravage
      4. to smite, chastise, send judgment upon, punish, destroy
    4. (Hophal) to be smitten
      1. to receive a blow
      2. to be wounded
      3. to be beaten
      4. to be (fatally) smitten, be killed, be slain
      5. to be attacked and captured
      6. to be smitten (with disease)
      7. to be blighted (of plants)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1364

Part(s) of speech: Verb
