לָבִיא

Transliterationlâbîyʼ
Phoneticlaw-bee'
Part of SpeechNoun

Definition

to roar; a lion (properly, a lioness as the fiercer (although not a roarer;))

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(great, old, stout) lion, lioness, young (lion).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: לבאות לבאים לביּא לביא

Phonetic: law-bee'

BDB Definition:

  1. lion (noun masculine)
  2. lioness (noun feminine)

Origin: from an unused root men. to roar
