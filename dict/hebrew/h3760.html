Definition

Karmel, the name of a hill and of a town in Palestine

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Carmel, fruitful (plentiful) field, (place).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּרמל

Transliteration: karmel

Phonetic: kar-mel'

BDB Definition: Carmel = " garden-land"

  1. a mountain on the Mediterranean coast of northern Israel, just below Haifa
  2. a town in the mountains on the west side of the Dead Sea and south of Hebron

Origin: the same as H3759
