צֹאן

Transliterationtsôʼn
Phonetictsone
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

a collective name for a flock (of sheep or goats); also figuratively (of men)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(small) cattle, flock ([phrase] -s), lamb ([phrase] -s), sheep(-cote, -fold, -shearer, -herds).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: צאון צאן o

Transliteration: tsô'n tse'ôn

Phonetic: tsone

BDB Definition:

  1. small cattle, sheep, sheep and goats, flock, flocks
    1. small cattle (usually of sheep and goats)
    2. of multitude (simile)
    3. of multitude (metaphor)

Origin: from an unused root meaning to migrate

TWOT entry: 1864a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
