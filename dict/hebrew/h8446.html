תּוּר

Transliterationtûwr
Phonetictoor
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to meander (causatively, guide) about, especially fortrade or reconnoitring

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

chap(-man), sent to descry, be excellent, merchant(-man), search (out), seek, (e-) spy (out).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: תּוּר

Transliteration: tûr

Phonetic: toor

BDB Definition:

  1. to seek, search out, spy out, explore
    1. (Qal)
      1. to seek out, select, find out how to do something
      2. to spy out, explore
        1. explorers, spies (participle)
      3. to go about
        1. merchant, trader (participle)
    2. (Hiphil) to make a search, make a reconnaissance

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2500

Part(s) of speech: Verb
