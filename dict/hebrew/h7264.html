רָגַז

Transliterationrâgaz
Phoneticraw-gaz'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to quiver (with any violent emotion, especially anger or fear)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be afraid, stand in awe, disquiet, fall out, fret, move, provoke, quake, rage, shake, tremble, trouble, be wroth.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רגז

Transliteration: râgaz

Phonetic: raw-gaz'

BDB Definition:

  1. tremble, quake, rage, quiver, be agitated, be excited, be perturbed
    1. (Qal) to quake, be disquieted, be excited, be perturbed
    2. (Hiphil) to cause to quake, disquiet, enrage, disturb
    3. (Hithpael) to excite oneself

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2112

Part(s) of speech: Verb
