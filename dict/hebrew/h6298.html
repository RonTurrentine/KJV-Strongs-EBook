פָּגַשׁ

Transliterationpâgash
Phoneticpaw-gash'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to come in contact with, whether by accident or violence; figuratively, to concur

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

meet (with, together).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: פּגשׁ

Transliteration: pâgash

Phonetic: paw-gash'

BDB Definition:

  1. to meet, join, encounter
    1. (Qal) to meet, encounter
    2. (Niphal) to meet together, meet each other
    3. (Piel) to meet, encounter

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1733

Part(s) of speech: Verb
