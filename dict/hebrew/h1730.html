דּוֹד

Transliterationdôwd
Phoneticdode
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

(figuratively) to love; by implication, a love-token, lover, friend; specifically an uncle

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(well-) beloved, father's brother, love, uncle.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: דּד דּוד

Transliteration: dôd dôd

Phonetic: dode

BDB Definition:

  1. beloved, love, uncle
    1. loved one, beloved
    2. uncle
    3. love (plural abstract)

Origin: from an unused root meaning properly, to boil

TWOT entry: 410a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
