חֹק

Transliterationchôq
Phonetickhoke
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

an enactment; hence, an appointment (of time, space, quantity, labor or usage)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

appointed, bound, commandment, convenient, custom, decree(-d), due, law, measure, [idiom] necessary, ordinance(-nary), portion, set time, statute, task.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חק

Transliteration: chôq

Phonetic: khoke

BDB Definition:

  1. statute, ordinance, limit, something prescribed, due
    1. prescribed task
    2. prescribed portion
    3. action prescribed (for oneself), resolve
    4. prescribed due
    5. prescribed limit, boundary
    6. enactment, decree, ordinance
      1. specific decree
      2. law in general
    7. enactments, statutes
      1. conditions
      2. enactments
      3. decrees
      4. civil enactments prescribed by God
