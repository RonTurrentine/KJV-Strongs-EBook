Transliterationqesheth
Phonetickeh'-sheth
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

a bow, forshooting (hence, figuratively, strength) or the iris

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] arch(-er), [phrase] arrow, bow(-man, -shot).

  1. bow

    2. bowmen, archers
    3. bow (figuratively of might)
    4. rainbow
