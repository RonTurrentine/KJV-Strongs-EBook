עָנָה

Transliterationʻânâh
Phonetic aw-naw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to eye or (generally) to heed, i.e. pay attention; by implication, to respond; by extension to begin to speak; specifically to sing, shout, testify, announce

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

give account, afflict (by mistake for ), (cause to, give) answer, bring low (by mistake for ), cry, hear, Leannoth, lift up, say, [idiom] scholar, (give a) shout, sing (together by course), speak, testify, utter, (bear) witness. See also , .
