סוּת

Transliterationçûwth
Phoneticsooth
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to prick, i.e. (figuratively) stimulate; by implication, to seduce

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

entice, move, persuade, provoke, remove, set on, stir up, take away.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: סוּת

Transliteration: sûth

Phonetic: sooth

BDB Definition:

  1. to incite, allure, instigate, entice
    1. (Hiphil)
      1. to incite (to a request)
      2. to allure, lure
      3. to instigate (bad sense)

Origin: perhaps denominative from H7898
