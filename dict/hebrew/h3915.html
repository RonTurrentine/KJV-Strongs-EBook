לַיִל

Transliterationlayil
Phoneticlah'-yil
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, a twist (away of the light), i.e. night; figuratively, adversity

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(mid-)night (season).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: לילה ליל ליל

Transliteration: layil lêyl layelâh

Phonetic: lah'-yil

BDB Definition:

  1. night
    1. night (as opposed to day)
    2. of gloom, protective shadow (figuratively)

Origin: from the same as H3883

TWOT entry: 1111

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
