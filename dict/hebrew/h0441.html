אַלּוּף

Transliterationʼallûwph
Phonetical-loof'
Part of SpeechAdjective Masculine

Definition

familiar; a friend, also gentle; hence, a bullock (as being tame; applied, although masculine, to a cow); and so, a chieftain (as notable, like neat cattle)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

captain, duke, (chief) friend, governor, guide, ox.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אלּף אלּוּף

Transliteration: 'alûph 'allûph

Phonetic: al-loof'

BDB Definition:

  1. tame, docile
  2. friend, intimate
  3. chief
