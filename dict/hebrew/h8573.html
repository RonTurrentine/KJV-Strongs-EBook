תְּנוּפָה

Transliterationtᵉnûwphâh
Phoneticten-oo-faw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

a brandishing (in threat); by implication, tumult; specifically, the official undulation of sacrificial offerings

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

offering, shaking, wave (offering).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: תּנוּפה

Transliteration: tenûphâh

Phonetic: ten-oo-faw'

BDB Definition:

  1. swinging, waving, wave offering, offering
    1. a swinging, brandishing
      1. of God's hand, weapons
    2. waving, wave-offering (technical term of sacrifice)
    3. offering (of gold or brass)
