שֵׁם

Transliterationshêm
Phoneticshame
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

an appellation, as amark or memorial of individuality; by implication honor, authority, character

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[phrase] base, (in-) fame(-ous), named(-d), renown, report.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁם

Transliteration: shêm

Phonetic: shame

BDB Definition:

  1. name
    1. name
    2. reputation, fame, glory
    3. the Name (as designation of God)
    4. memorial, monument

Origin: a primitive word [perhaps rather from H7760 through the idea of definite and conspicuous position
