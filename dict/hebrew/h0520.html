אַמָּה

Transliterationʼammâh
Phoneticam-maw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

properly, a mother (i.e. unit of measure, or the fore-arm (below the elbow), i.e. a cubit; also a door-base (as a bond of the entrance)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cubit, [phrase] hundred (by exchange for ), measure, post.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אמּה

Transliteration: 'ammâh

Phonetic: am-maw'

BDB Definition:

  1. cubit - a measure of distance (the forearm), roughly 18 inches (.5 meters). There are several cubits used in the OT, the cubit of a man or common cubit (Deu 3:11), the legal cubit or cubit of the sanctuary (Eze 40:5) plus others. See a Bible Dictionary for a complete treatment.

Origin: prolonged from H0517

TWOT entry: 115c

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
