קָדַם

Transliterationqâdam
Phonetic kaw-dam'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to project (one self), i.e. precede; hence, to anticipate, hasten, meet (usually for help)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

come (go, (flee)) before, [phrase] disappoint, meet, prevent.
