קָדַם

Transliterationqâdam
Phonetickaw-dam'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to project (one self), i.e. precede; hence, to anticipate, hasten, meet (usually for help)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

come (go, (flee)) before, [phrase] disappoint, meet, prevent.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: קדם

Transliteration: qâdam

Phonetic: kaw-dam'

BDB Definition:

  1. to meet, come or be in front, confront, go before
    1. (Piel)
      1. to meet, confront, come to meet, receive
      2. to go before, go in front, be in front
      3. to lead, be beforehand, anticipate, forestall
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to come in front
      2. to confront, anticipate

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1988

Part(s) of speech: Verb
