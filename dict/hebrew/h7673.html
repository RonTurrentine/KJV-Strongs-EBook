שָׁבַת

Transliterationshâbath
Phoneticshaw-bath'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to repose, i.e. desist from exertion; used in many implied relations (causative, figurative or specific)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(cause to, let, make to) cease, celebrate, cause (make) to fail, keep (sabbath), suffer to be lacking, leave, put away (down), (make to) rest, rid, still, take away.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁבת

Transliteration: shâbath

Phonetic: shaw-bath'

BDB Definition:

  1. to cease, desist, rest
    1. (Qal)
      1. to cease
      2. to rest, desist (from labour)
    2. (Niphal) to cease
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to cause to cease, put an end to
      2. to exterminate, destroy
      3. to cause to desist from
      4. to remove
      5. to cause to fail
  2. (Qal) to keep or observe the sabbath

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2323, 2323c

Part(s) of speech: Verb
