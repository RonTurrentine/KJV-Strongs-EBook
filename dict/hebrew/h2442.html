חָכָה

Transliterationchâkâh
Phonetickhaw-kaw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to adhere to; hence, to await

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

long, tarry, wait.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חכה

Transliteration: châkâh

Phonetic: khaw-kaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to wait, wait for, await
    1. (Qal) to wait for
    2. (Piel)
      1. to wait, tarry
      2. to wait (in ambush)
      3. to wait for, long for

Origin: a primitive root [apparently akin to H2707 through the idea of piercing]

TWOT entry: 645

Part(s) of speech: Verb
