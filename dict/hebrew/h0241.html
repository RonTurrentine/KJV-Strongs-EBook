אֹזֶן

Transliterationʼôzen
Phonetico'-zen
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

broadness. i.e. (concrete) the ear (from its form in man)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[phrase] advertise, audience, [phrase] displease, ear, hearing, [phrase] show.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אזן

Transliteration: 'ôzen

Phonetic: o'-zen

BDB Definition:

  1. ear, as part of the body
  2. ear, as organ of hearing
  3. (subjective) to uncover the ear to reveal; the receiver of divine revelation

Origin: from H0238

TWOT entry: 57a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
