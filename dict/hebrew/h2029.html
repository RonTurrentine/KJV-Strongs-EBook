Definition

to be (or become) pregnant, conceive (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

been, be with child, conceive, progenitor.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: הרה

Transliteration: hârâh

Phonetic: haw-raw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to conceive, become pregnant, bear, be with child, be conceived, progenitor
    1. (Qal) to conceive, become pregnant
    2. (Pual) to be conceived
    3. (Poel) to conceive, contrive, devise

Origin: a primitive root
