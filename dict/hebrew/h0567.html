BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אמרי

Transliteration: 'ĕmôrı̂y

Phonetic: em-o-ree'

BDB Definition: Amorite = " a sayer"

  1. one of the peoples of east Canaan and beyond the Jordan, dispossessed by the Israelite incursion from Egypt

Origin: probably a patronymic from an unused name derived from H0559 in the sense of publicity, i.e. prominence

TWOT entry: 119

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
