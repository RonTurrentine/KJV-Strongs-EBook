שֶׁלַח

Transliterationshelach
Phoneticsheh'-lakh
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a missile of attack, i.e. spear; also (figuratively) a shoot of growth; i.e. branch

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dart, plant, [idiom] put off, sword, weapon.
