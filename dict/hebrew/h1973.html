הָלְאָה

Transliterationhâlᵉʼâh
Phonetichaw-leh-aw'
Part of SpeechAdverb

Definition

to the distance, i.e. far away; also (of time) thus far

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

back, beyond, (hence,-) forward, hitherto, thence, forth, yonder.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: הלאה

Transliteration: hâle'âh

Phonetic: haw-leh-aw'

BDB Definition:

  1. out there, onwards, further
    1. yonder, beyond (of place)
    2. onwards (of time)

Origin: from the primitive form of the article [hal]

TWOT entry: 496a

Part(s) of speech: Adverb
