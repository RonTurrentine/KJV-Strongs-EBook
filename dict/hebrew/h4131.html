מוֹט

Transliterationmôwṭ
Phoneticmote
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to waver; by implication, to slip, shake, fall

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be carried, cast, be out of course, be fallen in decay, [idiom] exceedingly, fall(-ing down), be (re-) moved, be ready, shake, slide, slip.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מוט

Transliteration: môṭ

Phonetic: mote

BDB Definition:

  1. to totter, shake, slip
    1. (Qal) to totter, shake, slip
    2. (Niphal) to be shaken, be moved, be overthrown
    3. (Hiphil) to dislodge, let fall, drop
    4. (Hithpael) to be greatly shaken

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1158

Part(s) of speech: Verb
