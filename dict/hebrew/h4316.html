Micha.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מיכא

Transliteration: mı̂ykâ'

Phonetic: mee-kaw'

BDB Definition: Micha = " who is like God"

  1. son of Mephibosheth
  2. a Levite who signed the covenant with Nehemiah
  3. father of Mattaniah, a Gershonite Levite and descendant of Asaph

Origin: a variation for H4318

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
