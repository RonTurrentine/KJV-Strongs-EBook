גְּלָה

Transliterationgᵉlâh
Phoneticghel-aw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

{to denude (especially in a disgraceful sense); by implication, to exile (captives being usually stripped); figuratively, to reveal}

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bring over, carry away, reveal.
