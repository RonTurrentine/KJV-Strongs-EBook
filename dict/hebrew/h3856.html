לָהַהּ

Transliterationlâhahh
Phoneticlaw-hah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be rabid (figuratively, insane); also (from the exhaustion of frenzy) to languish

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

faint, mad.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: לההּ

Transliteration: lâhahh

Phonetic: law-hah'

BDB Definition:

  1. (Qal) to languish, faint
  2. to amaze, startle
    1. (Hithpalpel) madman (substantive)

Origin: a primitive root
