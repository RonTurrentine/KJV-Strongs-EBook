רִיב

Transliterationrîyb
Phoneticreeb
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to toss, i.e. grapple; mostly figuratively, to wrangle, i.e. hold a controversy; (by implication) to defend

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

adversary, chide, complain, contend, debate, [idiom] ever, [idiom] lay wait, plead, rebuke, strive, [idiom] thoroughly.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רוּב ריב o

Transliteration: rı̂yb rûb

Phonetic: reeb

BDB Definition:

  1. to strive, contend
    1. (Qal)
      1. to strive
        1. physically
        2. with words
      2. to conduct a case or suit (legal), sue
      3. to make complaint
      4. to quarrel
    2. (Hiphil) to contend against

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2159

Part(s) of speech: Verb
