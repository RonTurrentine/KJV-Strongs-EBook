בְּלִיַּעַל

Transliterationbᵉlîyaʻal
Phoneticbel-e-yah'-al
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

without profit, worthlessness; by extension, destruction, wickedness

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Belial, evil, naughty, ungodly (men), wicked.
