שׁוּט

Transliterationshûwṭ
Phoneticshoot
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to push forth; (but used only figuratively) to lash, i.e. (the sea with oars) to row; by implication, to travel

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

go (about, through, to and fro), mariner, rower, run to and fro.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁוּט

Transliteration: shûṭ

Phonetic: shoot

BDB Definition:

  1. to go, go or rove about, go to and fro
    1. (Qal) to go or rove about
    2. (Polel) to go to and fro, go eagerly or quickly to and fro
    3. (Hithpolel) to run to and fro
  2. to row

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2344,2344d

Part(s) of speech: Verb
