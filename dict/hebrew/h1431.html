גָּדַל

Transliterationgâdal
Phoneticgaw-dal'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be (causatively make) large (in various senses, as in body, mind, estate or honor, also in pride)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

advance, boast, bring up, exceed, excellent, be(-come, do, give, make, wax), great(-er, come to... estate, [phrase] things), grow(up), increase, lift up, magnify(-ifical), be much set by, nourish (up), pass, promote, proudly (spoken), tower.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: גּדל

Transliteration: gâdal

Phonetic: gaw-dal'

BDB Definition:

  1. to grow, become great or important, promote, make powerful, praise, magnify, do great things
    1. (Qal)
      1. to grow up
      2. to become great
      3. to be magnified
    2. (Piel)
      1. to cause to grow
      2. to make great, powerful
      3. to magnify
    3. (Pual) to be brought up
    4. (Hiphil)
      1. to make great
      2. to magnify
      3. to do great things
    5. (Hithpael) to magnify oneself

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 315

Part(s) of speech: Verb
