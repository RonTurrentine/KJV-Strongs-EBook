דֶּרֶךְ

Transliterationderek
Phoneticdeh'-rek
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a road (as trodden); figuratively, a course of life or mode of action, often adverb

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

along, away, because of, [phrase] by, conversation, custom, (east-) ward, journey, manner, passenger, through, toward, (high-) (path-) way(-side), whither(-soever).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: דּרך

Transliteration: derek

BDB Definition:

  1. way, road, distance, journey, manner
    1. road, way, path
    2. journey
    3. direction
    4. manner, habit, way
    5. of course of life (figuratively)
    6. of moral character (figuratively)
