שָׁאָה

Transliterationshâʼâh
Phoneticshaw-aw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to rush; by implication, to desolate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be desolate, (make a) rush(-ing), (lay) waste.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁאה

Transliteration: shâ'âh

Phonetic: shaw-aw'

BDB Definition:

  1. crash, make a din or crash, crash into ruins, be in ruins, be desolated
    1. (Qal) to crash into ruins
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be ruined
      2. to crash
      3. to be left (a desolation)
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to cause to crash
      2. to cause to be desolated

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2301

Part(s) of speech: Verb
