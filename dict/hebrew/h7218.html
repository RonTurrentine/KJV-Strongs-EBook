רֹאשׁ

Transliterationrôʼsh
Phoneticroshe
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

the head (as most easily shaken), whether literal or figurative (in many applications, of place, time, rank, itc.)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

band, beginning, captain, chapiter, chief(-est place, man, things), company, end, [idiom] every (man), excellent, first, forefront, (be-)head, height, (on) high(-est part, (priest)), [idiom] lead, [idiom] poor, principal, ruler, sum, top.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ראשׁ

Transliteration: rô'sh

Phonetic: roshe

BDB Definition:

  1. head, top, summit, upper part, chief, total, sum, height, front, beginning
    1. head (of man, animals)
    2. top, tip (of mountain)
    3. height (of stars)
    4. chief, head (of man, city, nation, place, family, priest)
    5. head, front, beginning
    6. chief, choicest, best
    7. head, division, company, band
    8. sum

Origin: from an unused root apparently meaning to shake
