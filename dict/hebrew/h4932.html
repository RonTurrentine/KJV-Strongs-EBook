מִשְׁנֶה

Transliterationmishneh
Phoneticmish-neh'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, a repetition, i.e. a duplicate (copy of a document), or a double (in amount); by implication, a second (in order, rank, age, quality or location)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

college, copy, double, fatlings, next, second (order), twice as much.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: משׁנה

Transliteration: mishneh

Phonetic: mish-neh'

BDB Definition:

  1. double, copy, second, repetition
    1. double
    2. copy (of law)
    3. second (in order)
      1. second rank
      2. second in age
    4. second quarter or district
