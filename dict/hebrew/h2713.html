חָקַר

Transliterationchâqar
Phonetickhaw-kar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to penetrate; hence, to examine intimately

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

find out, (make) search (out), seek (out), sound, try.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חקר

Transliteration: châqar

Phonetic: khaw-kar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to search, search for, search out, examine, investigate
    1. (Qal)
      1. to search (for)
      2. to search through, explore
      3. to examine thoroughly
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be searched out, be found out, be ascertained, be examined
    3. (Piel) to search out, seek out

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 729

Part(s) of speech: Verb
