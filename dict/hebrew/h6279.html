עָתַר

Transliterationʻâthar
Phoneticaw-thar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to burn incense in worship, i.e. intercede (reciprocally, listen to prayer)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

intreat, (make) pray(-er).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: עתר

Transliteration: ‛âthar

Phonetic: aw-thar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to pray, entreat, supplicate
    1. (Qal) to pray, entreat
    2. (Niphal) to be supplicated, be entreated
    3. (Hiphil) to make supplication, plead

Origin: a primitive root [rather denominative from H6281]

TWOT entry: 1722

Part(s) of speech: Verb
