חוּשׁ

Transliterationchûwsh
Phonetickoosh
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to hurry; figuratively, to be eager with excitement or enjoyment

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(make) haste(-n), ready.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חוּשׁ

Transliteration: chûsh

Phonetic: koosh

BDB Definition:

  1. to haste, make haste, hurry
    1. (Qal) to make haste
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to show haste, act quickly, hasten, come quickly
      2. to enjoy, be excited

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 631

Part(s) of speech: Verb
