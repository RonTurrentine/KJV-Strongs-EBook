אֶלֶף

Transliterationʼeleph
Phoneticeh'-lef
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

hence (the ox's head being the first letter of the alphabet, and this eventually used as a numeral) a thousand

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

thousand.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אלף

Transliteration: 'eleph

Phonetic: eh'-lef

BDB Definition:

  1. a thousand
    1. as numeral
  2. a thousand, company
    1. as a company of men under one leader, troops

Origin: prop, the same as H0504

TWOT entry: 109a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
