חָכָם

Transliterationchâkâm
Phonetickhaw-kawm'
Part of SpeechAdjective

Definition

wise, (i.e. intelligent, skilful or artful)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cunning (man), subtil, (un-), wise((hearted), man).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חכם

Transliteration: châkâm

Phonetic: khaw-kawm'

BDB Definition:

  1. wise, wise (man)
    1. skilful (in technical work)
    2. wise (in administration)
    3. shrewd, crafty, cunning, wily, subtle
    4. learned, shrewd (class of men)
    5. prudent
    6. wise (ethically and religiously)

Origin: from H2449

TWOT entry: 647b

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
