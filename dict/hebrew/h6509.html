פָּרָה

Transliterationpârâh
Phoneticpaw-raw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to bear fruit (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bear, bring forth (fruit), (be, cause to be, make) fruitful, grow, increase.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: פּרה

Transliteration: pârâh

Phonetic: paw-raw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to bear fruit, be fruitful, branch off
    1. (Qal) to bear fruit, be fruitful
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to cause to bear fruit
      2. to make fruitful
      3. to show fruitfulness, bear fruit

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1809

Part(s) of speech: Verb
