זֶבַח

Transliterationzebach
Phoneticzeh'-bakh
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, a slaughter, i.e. the flesh of an animal; by implication, a sacrifice (the victim or the act)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

offer(-ing), sacrifice.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: זבח

Transliteration: zebach

Phonetic: zeh'-bakh

BDB Definition:

  1. sacrifice
    1. sacrifices of righteousness
    2. sacrifices of strife
    3. sacrifices to dead things
    4. the covenant sacrifice
    5. the passover
    6. annual sacrifice
    7. thank offering
