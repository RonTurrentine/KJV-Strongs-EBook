בָּעָה

Transliterationbâʻâh
Phoneticbaw-aw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to gush over, i.e. to swell; (figuratively) to desire earnestly; by implication to ask

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cause, inquire, seek up, swell out.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּעה

Transliteration: bâ‛âh

Phonetic: baw-aw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to seek out, swell, cause to swell, boil up, enquire
    1. (Qal)
      1. to seek, enquire
      2. to cause to boil up
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be searched out
      2. to be swelling, bulging, swelling out

Origin: a primitive root
