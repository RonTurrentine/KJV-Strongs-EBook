יַנְשׁוּף

Transliterationyanshûwph
Phonetic yan-shoof'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

an unclean (acquatic) bird; probably the heron (perhaps from its blowing cry, or because the nightheron is meant ))

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(great) owl.s
