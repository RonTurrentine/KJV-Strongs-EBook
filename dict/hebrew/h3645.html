Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Chemosh.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּמישׁ כּמושׁ o

Transliteration: kemôsh kemı̂ysh

Phonetic: kem-oshe'

BDB Definition: Chemosh = " subduer"

  1. the national deity of the Moabites and a god of the Ammonites
    1. also identified with 'Baal-peor', 'Baal-zebub', 'Mars' and 'Saturn'
    2. worship of this god was introduced into Jerusalem by Solomon and abolished by king Josiah of Judah

Origin: from an unused root meaning to subdue

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name
