מַחֲנֶה

Transliterationmachăneh
Phoneticmakh-an-eh'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

an encampment (of travellers or troops); hence, an army, whether literal (of soldiers) or figurative (of dancers, angels, cattle, locusts, stars; or even the sacred courts)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

army, band, battle, camp, company, drove, host, tents.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מחנה

Transliteration: machăneh

Phonetic: makh-an-eh'

BDB Definition:

  1. encampment, camp
    1. camp, place of encampment
    2. camp of armed host, army camp
    3. those who encamp, company, body of people
