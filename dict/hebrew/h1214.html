בָּצַע

Transliterationbâtsaʻ
Phoneticbaw-tsah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

figuratively, to finish, or (intransitively) stop

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(be) covet(-ous), cut (off), finish, fulfill, gain (greedily), get, be given to (covetousness), greedy, perform, be wounded.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּצע

Transliteration: bâtsa‛

Phonetic: baw-tsah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to cut off, break off, gain by unrighteous violence, get, finish, be covetous, be greedy
    1. (Qal)
      1. to cut off
      2. to stop
      3. to gain wrongfully or by violence
    2. (Piel)
      1. to cut off, sever
      2. to finish, complete, accomplish
      3. to violently make gain of

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 267

Part(s) of speech: Verb
