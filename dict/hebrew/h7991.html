שָׁלִישׁ

Transliterationshâlîysh
Phoneticshaw-leesh'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a triple, i.e. (as a musical instrument) a triangle (or perhaps rather threestringed lute); also (as an indefinite, great quantity) a three-fold measure (perhaps a treble ephah); also (as an officer) a general of the third rank (upward, i.e. the highest)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

captain, instrument of musick, (great) lord, (great) measure, prince, three (from the margin).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁלשׁ שׁלושׁ שׁלישׁ o

BDB Definition:

  1. third part

  2. a musical instrument

    2. perhaps a sistrum or triangle
  3. shield carrier, adjutant, officer, captain
