מָצוֹד

Transliterationmâtsôwd
Phoneticmaw-tsode'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a net (for capturing animals or fishes); afastness or (beseiging) tower

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bulwark, hold, munition, net, snare.
