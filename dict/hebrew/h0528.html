multitude, populous.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אמון

Transliteration: 'âmôn

Phonetic: aw-mone'

BDB Definition: Amon or Amun = " to nourish: to be faithful"

  1. an Egyptian god, originally the local god of Thebes, later head of the Egyptian pantheon

Origin: of Egyptian derivation

TWOT entry: None
