נָעַל

Transliterationnâʻal
Phoneticnaw-al'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to fasten up, i.e. with abar or cord; hence, to sandal, i.e. furnish with slippers

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bolt, inclose, lock, shoe, shut up.
