בָּרַר

Transliterationbârar
Phoneticbaw-rar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to clarify (i.e. brighten), examine, select

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

make bright, choice, chosen, cleanse (be clean), clearly, polished, (shew self) pure(-ify), purge (out).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּרר

Transliteration: bârar

Phonetic: baw-rar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to purify, select, polish, choose, purge, cleanse or make bright, test or prove
    1. (Qal)
      1. to purge, purge out, purify
      2. to choose, select
      3. to cleanse, make shining, polish
      4. to test, prove
    2. (Niphal) to purify oneself
    3. (Piel) to purify
    4. (Hiphil)
      1. to purify
      2. to polish arrows
    5. (Hithpael)
      1. to purify oneself
      2. to show oneself pure, just, kind

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 288

Part(s) of speech: Verb
