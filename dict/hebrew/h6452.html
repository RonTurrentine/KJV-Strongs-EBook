פָּסַח

Transliterationpâçach
Phoneticpaw-sakh'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to hop, i.e. (figuratively) skip over (or spare); by implication, to hesitate; also (literally) to limp, to dance

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

halt, become lame, leap, pass over.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: פּסח

Transliteration: pâsach

Phonetic: paw-sakh'

BDB Definition:

  1. to pass over, spring over
    1. (Qal) to pass over
    2. (Piel) to skip, pass over
  2. to limp
    1. (Qal) to limp
    2. (Niphal) to be lame
    3. (Piel) to limp

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1786,1787

Part(s) of speech: Verb
