יָצַב

Transliterationyâtsab
Phoneticyaw-tsab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to place (any thing so as to stay); reflexively, to station, offer, continue

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

present selves, remaining, resort, set (selves), (be able to, can, with-) stand (fast, forth, -ing, still, up).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יצב

Transliteration: yâtsab

Phonetic: yaw-tsab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to place, set, stand, set or station oneself, present oneself
    1. (Hithpael) to station oneself, take one's stand, stand, present oneself, stand with someone

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 894

Part(s) of speech: Verb
