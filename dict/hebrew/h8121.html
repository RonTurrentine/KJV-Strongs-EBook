שֶׁמֶשׁ

Transliterationshemesh
Phoneticsheh'-mesh
Part of SpeechNoun

Definition

the sun; by implication, the east; figuratively, a ray, i.e. (architectural) a notched battlement

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[phrase] east side(-ward), sun (rising), [phrase] west(-ward), window. See also .

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁמשׁ

Transliteration: shemesh

Phonetic: sheh'-mesh

BDB Definition:

  1. sun
    1. sun
    2. sunrise, sun-rising, east, sun-setting, west (of direction)
    3. sun (as object of illicit worship)
    4. openly, publicly (in other phrases)
    5. pinnacles, battlements, shields (as glittering or shining)

Origin: from an unused root meaning to be brilliant

TWOT entry: 2417a

Part(s) of speech: Noun
