נֶגֶב

Transliterationnegeb
Phoneticneh'-gheb
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

the south (from its drought); specifically, the Negeb or southern district of Judah, occasionally, Egypt (as south to Palestine)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

south (country, side, -ward).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נגב

Transliteration: negeb

Phonetic: neh'-gheb

BDB Definition:

  1. south-country, Nekeb, south
    1. south-country
      1. region of southern Judah, boundaries not specific
    2. south

Origin: from an unused root meaning to be parched

TWOT entry: 1288a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
