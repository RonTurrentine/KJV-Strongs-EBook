שְׂמֹאול

Transliterationsᵉmôʼwl
Phoneticsem-ole'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, dark (as enveloped), i.e. the north; hence (by orientation), the left hand

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

left (hand, side).
