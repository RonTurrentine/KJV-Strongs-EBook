Definition

Jahats or Jahtsah, a place East of the Jordan

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Jahaz, Jahazah, Jahzah.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יהצה יהצה יהץ

Transliteration: yahats yahtsâh yahtsâh

Phonetic: yah'-hats

BDB Definition: Jahaz or Jahazah or Jahzah = " trodden down"

  1. a place in Reuben east of the Dead Sea where Israel defeated Sihon king of the Amorites; site uncertain

Origin: from an unused root meaning to stamp

TWOT entry: None
