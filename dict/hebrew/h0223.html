אוּרִיָּה

TransliterationʼÛwrîyâh
Phoneticoo-ree-yaw'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Urijah, the name of one Hittite and five Israelites

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Uriah, Urijah.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אוּריּהוּ אוּריּה

Transliteration: 'ûrı̂yâh 'ûrı̂yâhû

Phonetic: oo-ree-yaw'

BDB Definition: Uriah or Urijah = " Jehovah (Yahweh) is my light (flame)"

  1. Hittite husband of Bathsheba
  2. a priest who built king Ahaz' heathen altar
  3. a priest who rebuilt Jerusalem's wall
  4. a prophet slain by Jehoiakim

Origin: from H0217 and H3050

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
