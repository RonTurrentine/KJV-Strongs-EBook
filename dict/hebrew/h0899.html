בֶּגֶד

Transliterationbeged
Phoneticbehg'-ed
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a covering, i.e. clothing; also treachery or pillage

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

apparel, cloth(-es, ing), garment, lap, rag, raiment, robe, [idiom] very (treacherously), vesture, wardrobe.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּגד

Transliteration: beged

Phonetic: behg'-ed

BDB Definition:

  1. treachery, deceit
  2. (CLBL) garment, clothing (used indiscriminately)
