BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יסף

Transliteration: yâsaph

Phonetic: yaw-saf'

BDB Definition:

  1. to add, increase, do again
    1. (Qal) to add, increase, do again
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to join, join oneself to
      2. to be joined, be added to
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to cause to add, increase
      2. to do more, do again
