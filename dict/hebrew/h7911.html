שָׁכַח

Transliterationshâkach
Phoneticshaw-kakh'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to mislay, i.e. to be oblivious of, from want of memory or attention

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] at all, (cause to) forget.
