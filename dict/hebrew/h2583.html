חָנָה

Transliterationchânâh
Phonetickhaw-naw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to incline; by implication, to decline (of the slanting rays of evening); specifically, to pitch atent; gen. to encamp (for abode or siege)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

abide (in tents), camp, dwell, encamp, grow to an end, lie, pitch (tent), rest in tent.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חנה

Transliteration: chânâh

Phonetic: khaw-naw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to decline, incline, encamp, bend down, lay siege against
    1. (Qal)
      1. to decline
      2. to encamp

Origin: a primitive root [compare H2603]

TWOT entry: TWOT- 690

Part(s) of speech: Verb
