אֹהֶל

Transliterationʼôhel
Phonetico'-hel
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a tent (as clearly conspicuous from a distance)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

covering, (dwelling) (place), home, tabernacle, tent.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אהל

Transliteration: 'ôhel

Phonetic: o'-hel

BDB Definition:

  1. tent
    1. nomad's tent, and thus symbolic of wilderness life, transience
    2. dwelling, home, habitation
    3. the sacred tent of Jehovah (the tabernacle)

Origin: from H0166

TWOT entry: 32a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
