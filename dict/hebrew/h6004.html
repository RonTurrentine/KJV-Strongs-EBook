עָמַם

Transliterationʻâmam
Phoneticaw-mam'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to associate; by implication, to overshadow (by huddling together)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

become dim, hide.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: עמם

Transliteration: ‛âmam

Phonetic: aw-mam'

BDB Definition:

  1. to dim, darken, grow dark
    1. (Qal) to dim, eclipse, be held dark
    2. (Hophal) to be dimmed, grow dark

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1641
