דָּקַק

Transliterationdâqaq
Phoneticdaw-kak'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to crush (or intransitively) crumble

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beat in pieces (small), bruise, make dust, (into) [idiom] powder, (be, very) small, stamp (small).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: דּקק

Transliteration: dâqaq

Phonetic: daw-kak'

BDB Definition:

  1. to crush, pulverise, thresh
    1. (Qal)
      1. to crush
      2. to be fine
    2. (Hiphil) to pulverise, make dust of
    3. (Hophal) to be crushed

Origin: a primitive root [compare H1915]
