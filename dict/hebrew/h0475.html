Definition

Eljashib, the name of six Israelites

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Eliashib.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אלישׁיב

Transliteration: 'elyâshı̂yb

Phonetic: el-yaw-sheeb'

BDB Definition: Eliashib = " God restores"

  1. a priest in David's reign
  2. a descendant of David
  3. a high priest in Nehemiah's time
  4. a temple singer with foreign wife
  5. one of the line of Zattu
  6. one of the line of Bani

Origin: from H0410 and H7725

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
