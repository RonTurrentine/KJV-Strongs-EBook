כָּזַב

Transliterationkâzab
Phonetickaw-zab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to lie (i.e. deceive), literally or figuratively

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fail, (be found a, make a) liar, lie, lying, be in vain.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּזב

Transliteration: kâzab

Phonetic: kaw-zab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to lie, tell a lie, be a liar, be found a liar, be in vain, fail
    1. (Qal) liar (participle)
    2. (Niphal) to be proven to be lying
    3. (Piel)
      1. to lie, tell a lie, tell a lie with, deceive
      2. to disappoint, fail
    4. (Hiphil) to make a liar, prove to be a liar

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 970

Part(s) of speech: Verb
