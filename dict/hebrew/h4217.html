מִזְרָח

Transliterationmizrâch
Phoneticmiz-rawkh'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

sunrise, i.e. the east

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

east (side, -ward), (sun-) rising (of the sun).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מזרח

Transliteration: mizrâch

Phonetic: miz-rawkh'

BDB Definition:

  1. place of sunrise, east
    1. sunrise, east (with 'sun')
    2. the east (without 'sun')
      1. to or toward the place of sunrise
      2. to the east, eastward

Origin: from H2224

TWOT entry: 580c

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
