Definition

bitter (literally or figuratively); also (as noun) bitterness, or (adverbially) bitterly

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[phrase] angry, bitter(-ly, -ness), chafed, discontented, [idiom] great, heavy.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מרה מר

Transliteration: mar mârâh

Phonetic: mar

BDB Definition:

  1. bitter, bitterness (adjective)
    1. of water or food
    2. of harlot's end, end of wickedness, cry (figuratively)
    3. of pain (substantive)
  2. bitterly (adverb)
