Definition

Mephibosheth, the name of two Israelites

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Mephibosheth.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מפבשׁת מפיבשׁת

Transliteration: mephı̂ybôsheth mephibôsheth

Phonetic: mef-ee-bo'-sheth

BDB Definition: Mephibosheth = " exterminating the idol"

  1. grandson of Saul and son of Rizpah the daughter of Aiah, Saul's concubine; he and his brother Armoni were among the 7 victims surrendered by David to the Gibeonites to avert a famine
  2. son of Jonathan and grandson of Saul
    1. also 'Merib-baal'

Origin: probably from H6284 and H1322

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
