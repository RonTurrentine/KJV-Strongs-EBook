תָּמַךְ

Transliterationtâmak
Phonetictaw-mak'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to sustain; by implication, to obtain, keep fast; figuratively, to help, follow close

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(take, up-) hold (up), maintain, retain, stay (up).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: תּמך

Transliteration: tâmak

Phonetic: taw-mak'

BDB Definition:

  1. to grasp, hold, support, attain, lay hold of, hold fast
    1. (Qal)
      1. to grasp, lay hold of, attain
      2. to hold up, support
      3. to hold, keep
      4. to take hold of each other
    2. (Niphal) to be seized, be held

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2520

Part(s) of speech: Verb
