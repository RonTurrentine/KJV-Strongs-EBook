Transliterationshᵉmûwʻâh
Phoneticsehm-oo-aw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

something heard, i.e. an announcement

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bruit, doctrine, fame, mentioned, news, report, rumor, tidings.
