שְׁרֵא

Transliterationshᵉrêʼ
Phoneticsher-ay'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to free, separate; figuratively, to unravel, commence; by implication (of unloading beasts) to reside

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

begin, dissolve, dwell, loose.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁרא

Transliteration: sherê'

Phonetic: sher-ay'

BDB Definition:

  1. to loosen, abide, begin
    1. (Peal)
      1. to loosen
      2. to abide (from loosing girths for camp)
    2. (Pael) to begin, open
    3. (Ithpael) to be loosened

Origin: a root corresponding to that of H8293

TWOT entry: 3048

Part(s) of speech: Verb
