כָּבַס

Transliterationkâbaç
Phonetickaw-bas'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to trample; hence, to wash (properly, by stamping with the feet), whether literal (including the fulling process) or figurative

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fuller, wash(-ing).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּבס

Transliteration: kâbas

Phonetic: kaw-bas'

BDB Definition:

  1. to wash (by treading), be washed, perform the work of a fuller
    1. (Qal) washer, fuller, treader (participle)
    2. (Piel) to wash (garments, person)
    3. (Pual) to be washed
    4. (Hothpael) to be washed out

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 946

Part(s) of speech: Verb
