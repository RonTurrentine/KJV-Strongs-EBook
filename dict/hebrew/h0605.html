אָנַשׁ

Transliterationʼânash
Phoneticaw-nash'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be frail, feeble, or (figuratively) melancholy

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

desperate(-ly wicked), incurable, sick, woeful.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אנשׁ

Transliteration: 'ânash

Phonetic: aw-nash'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be weak, sick, frail
    1. (Qal)
      1. to be incurable
      2. to be sick
      3. desperate, incurable, desperately wicked, woeful, very sick (passive participle) (metaphorically)
    2. (Niphal) to be sick

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 135

Part(s) of speech: Verb
