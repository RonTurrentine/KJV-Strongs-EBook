בָּקָר

Transliterationbâqâr
Phoneticbaw-kawr'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

beef cattle or an animal of the ox family of either gender (as used for plowing); collectively, a herd

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beeve, bull ([phrase] -ock), [phrase] calf, [phrase] cow, great (cattle), [phrase] heifer, herd, kine, ox.
