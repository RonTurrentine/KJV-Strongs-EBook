עָבַט

Transliterationʻâbaṭ
Phoneticaw-bat'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to pawn; causatively, to lend (on security); figuratively, to entangle

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

borrow, break (ranks), fetch (a pledge), lend, [idiom] surely.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: עבט

Transliteration: ‛âbaṭ

Phonetic: aw-bat'

BDB Definition:

  1. to take a pledge, give a pledge (for a debt)
    1. (Qal) to take a pledge, give a pledge (for a debt)
    2. (Piel) to interchange
    3. (Hiphil) to cause to give a pledge

Origin: a primitive root
