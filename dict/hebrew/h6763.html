צֵלָע

Transliterationtsêlâʻ
Phonetictsay-law'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

a rib (as curved), literally (of the body) or figuratively (of a door, i.e. leaf); hence, a side, literally (of a person) or figuratively (of an object or the sky, i.e. quarter); architecturally, a (especially floor or ceiling) timber or plank (single or collective, i.e. a flooring)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beam, board, chamber, corner, leaf, plank, rib, side (chamber).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: צלעה צלע

Transliteration: tsêlâ‛ tsal‛âh

Phonetic: tsay-law'

BDB Definition:

  1. side, rib, beam
    1. rib (of man)
    2. rib (of hill, ridge, etc)
    3. side-chambers or cells (of temple structure)
    4. rib, plank, board (of cedar or fir)
    5. leaves (of door)
    6. side (of ark)
