חָלַף

Transliterationchâlaph
Phonetickhaw-laf'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to slide by, i.e. (by implication) to hasten away, pass on, spring up, pierce or change

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

abolish, alter, change, cut off, go on forward, grow up, be over, pass (away, on, through), renew, sprout, strike through.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חלף

Transliteration: châlaph

Phonetic: khaw-laf'

BDB Definition:

  1. to pass on or away, pass through, pass by, go through, grow up, change, to go on from
    1. (Qal)
      1. to pass on quickly
      2. to pass away (vanish)
      3. to come on anew, sprout again (of grass)
      4. to pass through
      5. to overstep, transgress
    2. (Piel) to cause to pass, change
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to change, substitute, alter, change for better, renew
      2. to show newness (of tree)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 666

Part(s) of speech: Verb
