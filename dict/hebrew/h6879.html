צָרַע

Transliterationtsâraʻ
Phonetictsaw-rah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to scourge, i.e. (intransitive and figurative) to be stricken with leprosy

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

leper, leprous.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: צרע

Transliteration: tsâra‛

Phonetic: tsaw-rah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be diseased of skin, be leprous
    1. (Qal) to be a leper
    2. (Pual) to have leprosy

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1971

Part(s) of speech: Verb
