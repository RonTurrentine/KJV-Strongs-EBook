יָאַל

Transliterationyâʼal
Phoneticyaw-al'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to be slack, i.e. (figuratively) to be foolish

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dote, be (become, do) foolish(-ly).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יאל

Transliteration: yâ'al

Phonetic: yaw-al'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be foolish, become fools, act foolishly, show wicked folly
    1. (Niphal)
      1. to show wicked folly
      2. to become fools

Origin: a primitive root
