מָשַׁשׁ

Transliterationmâshash
Phoneticmaw-shash'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to feel of; by implication, to grope

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

feel, grope, search.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: משׁשׁ

Transliteration: mâshash

Phonetic: maw-shash'

BDB Definition:

  1. to feel, grope
    1. (Qal) to feel, grope
    2. (Piel) to feel over or through, grope
      1. to feel through
      2. to grope
    3. (Hiphil) to feel

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1262

Part(s) of speech: Verb
