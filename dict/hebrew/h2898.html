טוּב

Transliterationṭûwb
Phonetictoob
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

good (as a noun), in the widest sense, especially goodness (superlative concretely, the best), beauty, gladness, welfare

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fair, gladness, good(-ness, thing, -s), joy, go well with.

BDB / Thayer Definition

BDB Definition:

    2. goods, property
    3. fairness, beauty, joy, prosperity, goodness (abstract)
    4. goodness (of taste, discernment)
    5. goodness (of God) (abstract)
