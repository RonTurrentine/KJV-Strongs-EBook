מִסְגֶּרֶת

Transliterationmiçgereth
Phoneticmis-gheh'-reth
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

something enclosing, i.e. a margin (of a region, of a panel); concretely, a stronghold

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

border, close place, hole.
