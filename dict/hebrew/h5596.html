סָפַח

Transliterationçâphach
Phoneticsaw-fakh'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to scrape out, but in certain peculiar senses (of removal or association)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

abiding, gather together, cleave, smite with the scab.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׂפח ספח

Transliteration: sâphach śâphach

Phonetic: saw-fakh'

BDB Definition:

  1. to join, attach to, join together
    1. (Qal) to join, attach to
    2. (Niphal) to attach oneself
    3. (Piel) joining (participle)
    4. (Pual) to be joined together, hold oneself together
    5. (Hithpael) to join oneself
  2. (Piel) to cause a scab upon, smite with scab

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1532,1534

Part(s) of speech: Verb
