מַשָּׂא

Transliterationmassâʼ
Phoneticmas-saw'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine / Noun Proper Masculine

Definition

a burden; specifically, tribute, or (abstractly) porterage; figuratively, an utterance, chiefly adoom, especially singing; mental, desire

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

burden, carry away, prophecy, [idiom] they set, song, tribute.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: משּׂא

Transliteration: maśśâ'

Phonetic: mas-saw'

BDB Definition: Massa = " burden"

  1. load, bearing, tribute, burden, lifting (noun masculine)
    1. load, burden
    2. lifting, uplifting, that to which the soul lifts itself up
    3. bearing, carrying
    4. tribute, that which is carried or brought or borne
  2. utterance, oracle, burden (noun masculine)
  3. a son of Ishmael (noun proper masculine)
