שֶׁקֶר

Transliterationsheqer
Phoneticsheh'-ker
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

an untruth; by implication, a sham (often adverbial)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

without a cause, deceit(-ful), false(-hood, -ly), feignedly, liar, [phrase] lie, lying, vain (thing), wrongfully.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁקר

Transliteration: sheqer

Phonetic: sheh'-ker

BDB Definition:

  1. lie, deception, disappointment, falsehood
    1. deception (what deceives or disappoints or betrays one)
    2. deceit, fraud, wrong
      1. fraudulently, wrongfully (as adverb)
    3. falsehood (injurious in testimony)
      1. testify falsehood, false oath, swear falsely
    4. falsity (of false or self-deceived prophets)
    5. lie, falsehood (in general)
      1. false tongue
    6. in vain
