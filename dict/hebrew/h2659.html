חָפֵר

Transliterationchâphêr
Phonetickhaw-fare'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to blush; figuratively, to be ashamed, disappointed; causatively, to shame, reproach

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be ashamed, be confounded, be brought to confusion (unto shame), come (be put to) shame, bring reproach.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חפר

Transliteration: châphêr

Phonetic: khaw-fare'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be ashamed, be confounded, be abashed, feel abashed
    1. (Qal) to be ashamed, be abashed
    2. (Hiphil) to display shame, demonstrate shame, cause embarrassment

Origin: a primitive root [perhaps rath, the same as H2658 through the idea of detection]

TWOT entry: 715

Part(s) of speech: Verb
