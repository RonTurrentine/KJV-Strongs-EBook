גְּבוּל

Transliterationgᵉbûwl
Phoneticgheb-ool'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, a cord (as twisted), i.e. (by implication) a boundary; by extension the territory inclosed

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

border, bound, coast, [idiom] great, landmark, limit, quarter, space.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: גּבל גּבוּל

Transliteration: gebûl gebûl

Phonetic: gheb-ool'

BDB Definition:

  1. border, territory
    1. border
    2. territory (enclosed within boundary)
    3. region, territory (of darkness) (figuratively)

Origin: from H1379
