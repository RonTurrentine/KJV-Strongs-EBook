יָחַם

Transliterationyâcham
Phoneticyaw-kham'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

probably to be hot; figuratively, to conceive

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

get heat, be hot, conceive, be warm.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יחם

Transliteration: yâcham

Phonetic: yaw-kham'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be hot, conceive
    1. (Qal)
      1. to be hot, become hot
      2. to mate (of animals)
      3. to be or become hot (figuratively of anger)
    2. (Piel)
      1. to conceive (sexually)
      2. to be in heat (of animals)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 860

Part(s) of speech: Verb
