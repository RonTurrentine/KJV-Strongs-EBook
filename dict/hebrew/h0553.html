אָמַץ

Transliterationʼâmats
Phoneticaw-mats'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be alert, physically (on foot) or mentally (in courage)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

confirm, be courageous (of good courage, stedfastly minded, strong, stronger), establish, fortify, harden, increase, prevail, strengthen (self), make strong (obstinate, speed).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אמץ

Transliteration: 'âmats

Phonetic: aw-mats'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be strong, alert, courageous, brave, stout, bold, solid, hard
    1. (Qal) to be strong, brave, bold
    2. (Piel) to strengthen, secure (for oneself), harden (heart), make firm, make obstinate, assure
    3. (Hithpael) to be determined, to make oneself alert, strengthen oneself, confirm oneself, persist in, prove superior to
    4. (Hiphil) to exhibit strength, be strong, feel strong

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 117

Part(s) of speech: Verb
