לָקַט

Transliterationlâqaṭ
Phoneticlaw-kat'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to pick up, i.e. (generally) to gather; specifically, to glean

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

gather (up), glean.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: לקט

Transliteration: lâqaṭ

Phonetic: law-kat'

BDB Definition:

  1. to pick up, gather, glean, gather up
    1. (Qal)
      1. to pick up, gather
      2. to glean
    2. (Piel)
      1. to gather, gather up
      2. to collect (money)
      3. to glean
    3. (Pual) to be picked up
    4. (Hithpael) to collect oneself

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1125

Part(s) of speech: Verb
