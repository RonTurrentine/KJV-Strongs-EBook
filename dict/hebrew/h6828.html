צָפוֹן

Transliterationtsâphôwn
Phonetictsaw-fone'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

properly, hidden, i.e. dark; used only of the north as aquarter (gloomy and unknown)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

north(-ern, side, -ward, wind).
