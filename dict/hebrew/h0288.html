Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Ahimelech.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אחימלך

Transliteration: 'ăchı̂ymelek

Phonetic: akh-ee-meh'-lek

BDB Definition: Ahimelech = " my brother is king" or " brother of Melek"

  1. a priest murdered by Doeg at Saul's command, for supposedly assisting David
  2. a Hittite warrior under David

Origin: from H0251 and H4428

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
