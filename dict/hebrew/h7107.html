קָצַף

Transliterationqâtsaph
Phonetickaw-tsaf'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to crack off, i.e. (figuratively) burst out in rage

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(be) anger(-ry), displease, fret self, (provoke to) wrath (come), be wroth.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: קצף

Transliteration: qâtsaph

Phonetic: kaw-tsaf'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be displeased, be angry, fret oneself, be wroth
    1. (Qal) to be wroth, to be full of wrath, to be furious
    2. (Hiphil) to provoke to wrath or anger
    3. (Hithpael) to put oneself in a rage, anger oneself

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2058

Part(s) of speech: Verb
