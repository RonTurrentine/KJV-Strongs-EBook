שָׁגַג

Transliterationshâgag
Phoneticshaw-gag'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to stray, i.e. (figuratively) sin (with more or less apology)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] also for that, deceived, err, go astray, sin ignorantly.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁגג

Transliteration: shâgag

Phonetic: shaw-gag'

BDB Definition:

  1. to go astray, err, commit sin or error
    1. (Qal)
      1. to err (mentally)
      2. to sin (ignorantly or inadvertently)

Origin: a primitive root
