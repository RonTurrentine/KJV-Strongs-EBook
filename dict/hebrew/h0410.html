אֵל

Transliterationʼêl
Phoneticale
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

strength; as adjective, mighty; especially the Almighty (but used also of any deity)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

God (god), [idiom] goodly, [idiom] great, idol, might(-y one), power, strong. Compare names in '-el.'

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אל

Transliteration: 'êl

Phonetic: ale

BDB Definition:

  1. god, god-like one, mighty one
    1. mighty men, men of rank, mighty heroes
    2. angels
    3. god, false god, (demons, imaginations)
    4. God, the one true God, Jehovah
  2. mighty things in nature
  3. strength, power

Origin: shortened from H0352

TWOT entry: 93a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
