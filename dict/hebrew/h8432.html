תָּוֶךְ

Transliterationtâvek
Phonetictaw'-vek
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a bisection, i.e. (by implication) the centre

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

among(-st), [idiom] between, half, [idiom] (there-, where-), in(-to), middle, mid(-night), midst (among), [idiom] out (of), [idiom] through, [idiom] with(-in).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: תּוך

Transliteration: tâvek

Phonetic: taw'-vek

BDB Definition:

  1. midst, middle
    1. midst, middle
    2. into, through (after verbs of motion)
    3. among (of a number of persons)
    4. between (of things arranged by twos)
    5. from among (as to take or separate etc)

Origin: from an unused root meaning to sever

TWOT entry: 2498

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
