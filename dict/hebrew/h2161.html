זָמַם

Transliterationzâmam
Phoneticzaw-mam'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to plan, usually in a bad sense

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

consider, devise, imagine, plot, purpose, think (evil).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: זמם

Transliteration: zâmam

Phonetic: zaw-mam'

BDB Definition:

  1. to have a thought, devise, plan, consider, purpose
    1. (Qal)
      1. to consider, fix thought upon
      2. to purpose, devise
      3. to plot (of evil intent)

Origin: a primitive root
