שֵׁנִי

Transliterationshênîy
Phoneticshay-nee'
Part of SpeechNoun

Definition

properly, double, i.e. second; also adverbially, again

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

again, either (of them), (an-) other, second (time).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁני

Transliteration: shênı̂y

Phonetic: shay-nee'

BDB Definition:

  1. second
    1. second (the ordinal number)
    2. again (a second time)
    3. another, other (something as distinct from something else)

Origin: from H8138

TWOT entry: 2421b
