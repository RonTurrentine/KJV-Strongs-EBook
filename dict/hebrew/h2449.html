חָכַם

Transliterationchâkam
Phonetickhaw-kam'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be wise (in mind, word or act)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] exceeding, teach wisdom, be (make self, shew self) wise, deal (never so) wisely, make wiser.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חכם

Transliteration: châkam

Phonetic: khaw-kam'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be wise
    1. (Qal) to be or become wise, act wisely
    2. (Piel) to make wise, teach wisdom, instruct
    3. (Pual) to be made wise
    4. (Hiphil) to make wise
    5. (Hithpael) to show oneself wise, deceive, show one's wisdom

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 647

Part(s) of speech: Verb
