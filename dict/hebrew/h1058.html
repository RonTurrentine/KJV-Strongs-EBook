בָּכָה

Transliterationbâkâh
Phoneticbaw-kaw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to weep; generally to bemoan

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] at all, bewail, complain, make lamentation, [idiom] more, mourn, [idiom] sore, [idiom] with tears, weep.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּכה

Transliteration: bâkâh

Phonetic: baw-kaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to weep, bewail, cry, shed tears
    1. (Qal)
      1. to weep (in grief, humiliation, or joy)
      2. to weep bitterly (with cognate acc.)
      3. to weep upon (embrace and weep)
      4. to bewail
    2. (Piel) participle
      1. lamenting
      2. bewailing

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 243

Part(s) of speech: Verb
