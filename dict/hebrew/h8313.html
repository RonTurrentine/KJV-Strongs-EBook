שָׂרַף

Transliterationsâraph
Phoneticsaw-raf'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be (causatively, set) on fire

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(cause to, make a) burn((-ing), up) kindle, [idiom] utterly.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׂרף

Transliteration: śâraph

Phonetic: saw-raf'

BDB Definition:

  1. to burn
    1. (Qal) to burn
    2. (Niphal) to be burned
    3. (Piel) burner, burning (participle)
    4. (Pual) to be burnt up, be burned

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2292

Part(s) of speech: Verb
