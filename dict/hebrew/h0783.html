אַרְתַּחְשַׁשְׁתָּא

TransliterationʼArtachshashtâʼ
Phoneticar-takh-shash-taw'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Artachshasta (or Artaxerxes), a title (rather than name) of several Persian kings

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Artaxerxes.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ארתּחשׁשׁתּא ארתּחשׁשׁתּא

Transliteration: 'artachshashtâ' 'artachshasht'

Phonetic: ar-takh-shash-taw'

BDB Definition: Artaxerxes = " I will make the spoiled to boil: I will stir myself (in) winter"

  1. son and successor of Xerxes as emperor of Persia, 465-424 BC

Origin: of foreign origin

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
