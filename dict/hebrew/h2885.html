טַבַּעַת

Transliterationṭabbaʻath
Phonetictab-bah'-ath
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, a seal (as sunk into the wax), i.e. signet (for sealing); hence (generally) a ring of any kind

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

ring.
