יָהַב

Transliterationyâhab
Phoneticyaw-hab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to give (whether literal or figurative); generally, to put; imperatively (reflexive) come

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

ascribe, bring, come on, give, go, set, take.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יהב

Transliteration: yâhab

Phonetic: yaw-hab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to give, provide, ascribe, come
    1. (Qal)
      1. to give
      2. to set, place
      3. to provide (with reflexive)
      4. to ascribe (glory)
      5. to grant, permit, come now

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 849

Part(s) of speech: Verb
