מַסֵּכָה

Transliterationmaççêkâh
Phoneticmas-say-kaw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

properly, a pouring over, i.e. fusion of metal (especially a cast image); by implication, a libation, i.e. league; concretely a coverlet (as if poured out)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

covering, molten (image), vail.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מסּכה

Transliteration: massêkâh

Phonetic: mas-say-kaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. a pouring, libation, molten metal, cast image, drink offering
    1. libation (with covenant sacrifice)
    2. molten metal, molten image, molten gods
  2. web, covering, veil, woven stuff

Origin: from H5258

TWOT entry: 1375c,1376a
