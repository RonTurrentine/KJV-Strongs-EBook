BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּתוּאל

Transliteration: bethû'êl

Phonetic: beth-oo-ale'

BDB Definition: Bethuel = " God destroys" or " man of God" or " dweller in God"

  1. nephew of Abraham, son of Nahor by Milcah, father of Rebekah (noun proper masculine)
  2. a town of Simeon in the south (noun proper locative)

Origin: apparently from the same as H1326 and H0410
