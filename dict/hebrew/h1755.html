דּוֹר

Transliterationdôwr
Phoneticdore
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, a revolution of time, i.e. an age or generation; also a dwelling

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

age, [idiom] evermore, generation, (n-) ever, posterity.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: דּר דּור

Transliteration: dôr dôr

Phonetic: dore

BDB Definition:

  1. period, generation, habitation, dwelling
    1. period, age, generation (period of time)
    2. generation (those living during a period)
    3. generation (characterised by quality, condition, class of men)
    4. dwelling-place, habitation
