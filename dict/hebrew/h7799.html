שׁוּשַׁן

Transliterationshûwshan
Phoneticshoo-shan'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a lily (from its whiteness), as aflower of architectural ornament; also a (straight) trumpet (from the tubular shape)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

lily, Shoshannim.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁושׁנּה שׁשׁן שׁושׁן שׁוּשׁן

Transliteration: shûshan shôshân shôshân shôshannâh

Phonetic: shoo-shan'

BDB Definition:

  1. lily
    1. probably any lily-like flower
    2. Shoshannim, in title of Ps. 45
