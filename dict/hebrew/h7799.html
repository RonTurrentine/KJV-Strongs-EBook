שׁוּשַׁן

Transliterationshûwshan
Phonetic shoo-shan'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a lily (from its whiteness), as aflower of architectural ornament; also a (straight) trumpet (from the tubular shape)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

lily, Shoshannim.
