נָעוּר

Transliterationnâʻûwr
Phoneticnaw-oor'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

(only in plural collectively or emphatic form) youth, the state (juvenility) or the persons (young people)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

childhood, youth.
