בָּשָׂר

Transliterationbâsâr
Phoneticbaw-sawr'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

flesh (from its freshness); by extension, body, person; also (by euphemistically) the pudenda of aman

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

body, (fat, lean) flesh(-ed), kin, (man-) kind, [phrase] nakedness, self, skin.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּשׂר

Transliteration: bâśâr

Phonetic: baw-sawr'

BDB Definition:

  1. flesh
    1. of the body
      1. of humans
      2. of animals
    2. the body itself
    3. male organ of generation (euphemism)
    4. kindred, blood-relations
    5. flesh as frail or erring (man against God)
    6. all living things
    7. animals
    8. mankind

Origin: from H1319
