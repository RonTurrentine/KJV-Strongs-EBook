Transliterationsâʻîyr
Phoneticsaw-eer'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

shaggy; as noun, a he-goat; by analogy, a faun

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

devil, goat, hairy, kid, rough, satyr.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׂער שׂעיר

Transliteration: śâ‛ı̂yr śâ‛ir

Phonetic: saw-eer'

BDB Definition:

  1. hairy (adjective)
  2. he-goat, buck (noun masculine)
    1. as sacrificial animal
    2. satyr, may refer to a demon possessed goat like the swine of Gadara (Mat 8:30-32)

Origin: from H8175
