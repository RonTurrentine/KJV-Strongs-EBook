יָקַע

Transliterationyâqaʻ
Phoneticyaw-kah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to sever oneself, i.e. (by implication) to be dislocated; figuratively, to abandon; causatively, to impale (and thus allow to drop to pieces by rotting)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be alienated, depart, hang (up), be out of joint.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יקע

Transliteration: yâqa‛

Phonetic: yaw-kah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be dislocated, be alienated
    1. (Qal) to be dislocated, be torn away, be alienated
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to execute slowly (by exposure or impalation)
      2. to hang
    3. (Hophal) to be executed

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 903

Part(s) of speech: Verb
