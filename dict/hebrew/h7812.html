שָׁחָה

Transliterationshâchâh
Phoneticshaw-khaw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to depress, i.e. prostrate (especially reflexive, in homage to royalty or God)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bow (self) down, crouch, fall down (flat), humbly beseech, do (make) obeisance, do reverence, make to stoop, worship.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁחה

Transliteration: shâchâh

Phonetic: shaw-khaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to bow down
    1. (Qal) to bow down
    2. (Hiphil) to depress (figuratively)
    3. (Hithpael)
      1. to bow down, prostrate oneself
        1. before superior in homage
        2. before God in worship
        3. before false gods
        4. before angel

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2360

Part(s) of speech: Verb
