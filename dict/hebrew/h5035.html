נֶבֶל

Transliterationnebel
Phoneticneh'-bel
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a skin-bag for liquids (from collapsing when empty); hence, a vase (as similar in shape when full); also a lyre (as having a body of like form)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bottle, pitcher, psaltery, vessel, viol.
