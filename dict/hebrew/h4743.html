מָקַק

Transliterationmâqaq
Phoneticmaw-kak'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to melt; figuratively, to flow, dwindle, vanish

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

consume away, be corrupt, dissolve, pine away.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מקק

Transliteration: mâqaq

Phonetic: maw-kak'

BDB Definition:

  1. to decay, pine away, rot, fester
    1. (Niphal)
      1. to fester (of wounds)
      2. to rot, rot away
      3. to moulder away
      4. to pine away
    2. (Hiphil) to cause to rot

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1237

Part(s) of speech: Verb
