רָהַב

Transliterationrâhab
Phoneticraw-hab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to urge severely, i.e. (figuratively) importune, embolden, capture, act insolently

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

overcome, behave self proudly, make sure, strengthen.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רהב

Transliteration: râhab

Phonetic: raw-hab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to behave proudly, act stormily or boisterously or arrogantly
    1. (Qal) to act insolently, storm against, beset, importune
    2. (Hiphil) to disturb, alarm, awe, confuse, make bold or proud

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2125

Part(s) of speech: Verb
