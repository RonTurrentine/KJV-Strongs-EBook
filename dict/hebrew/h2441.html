חֵךְ

Transliterationchêk
Phonetickhake
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, the palate or inside of the mouth; hence, the mouth itself (as the organ of speech, taste and kissing)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(roof of the) mouth, taste.
