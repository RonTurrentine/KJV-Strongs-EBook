שָׁקַף

Transliterationshâqaph
Phoneticshaw-kaf'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to lean out (of a window), i.e. (by implication) peep or gaze (passively, be a spectacle)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

appear, look (down, forth, out).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁקף

Transliteration: shâqaph

Phonetic: shaw-kaf'

BDB Definition:

  1. to overlook, look down or out, overhang, look out and down
    1. (Niphal) to lean over (and look), look down
    2. (Hiphil) to look down, look down upon

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2457

Part(s) of speech: Verb
