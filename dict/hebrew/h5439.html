סָבִיב

Transliterationçâbîyb
Phoneticsaw-beeb'
Part of SpeechAdverb

Definition

(as noun) a circle, neighbour, or environs; but chiefly (as adverb, with or without preposition) around

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(place, round) about, circuit, compass, on every side.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: סביבה סביב

Transliteration: sâbı̂yb sebı̂ybâh

Phonetic: saw-beeb'

BDB Definition:

  1. places round about, circuit, round about (substantive)
  2. in a circuit, a circuit, round about (adverb)
  3. in the circuit, from every side (preposition)

Origin: from H5437
