רִנָּה

Transliterationrinnâh
Phoneticrin-naw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

properly, a creaking (or shrill sound), i.e. shout (of joy or grief)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cry, gladness, joy, proclamation, rejoicing, shouting, sing(-ing), triumph.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רנּה

Phonetic: rin-naw'

BDB Definition:

  1. ringing cry
    1. of entreaty, supplication
    2. in proclamation, joy, praise
