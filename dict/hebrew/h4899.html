מָשִׁיחַ

Transliterationmâshîyach
Phoneticmaw-shee'-akh
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

anointed; usually a consecrated person (as a king, priest, or saint); specifically, the Messiah

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

anointed, Messiah.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: משׁיח

Transliteration: mâshı̂yach

Phonetic: maw-shee'-akh

BDB Definition:

  1. anointed, anointed one
    1. of the Messiah, Messianic prince
    2. of the king of Israel
    3. of the high priest of Israel
    4. of Cyrus
    5. of the patriarchs as anointed kings

Origin: from H4886
