יָרֵךְ

Transliterationyârêk
Phoneticyaw-rake'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

the thigh (from its fleshy softness); by euphemistically the generative parts; figuratively, a shank, flank, side

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] body, loins, shaft, side, thigh.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ירך

Transliteration: yârêk

Phonetic: yaw-rake'

BDB Definition:

  1. thigh, side, loin, base
    1. thigh
      1. outside of thigh (where sword was worn)
      2. loins (as the seat of procreative power)
    2. side (flank) (of object)
    3. base

Origin: from an unused root meaning to be soft

TWOT entry: 916a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
