עָשַׁק

Transliterationʻâshaq
Phoneticaw-shak'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to press upon, i.e. oppress, defraud, violate, overflow

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

get deceitfully, deceive, defraud, drink up, (use) oppress(-ion), -or), do violence (wrong).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: עשׁק

BDB Definition:

  1. to press upon, oppress, violate, defraud, do violence, get deceitfully, wrong, extort
    1. (Qal)
      1. to oppress, wrong, extort
      2. to oppress
    2. (Pual) to be exploited, be crushed
