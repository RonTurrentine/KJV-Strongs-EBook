Transliterationshîyth
Phoneticsheeth
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to place (in a very wide application)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

apply, appoint, array, bring, consider, lay (up), let alone, [idiom] look, make, mark, put (on), [phrase] regard, set, shew, be stayed, [idiom] take.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁית

Transliteration: shı̂yth

Phonetic: sheeth

BDB Definition:

  1. to put, set
    1. (Qal)
      1. to put, lay (hand upon)
      2. to set, station, appoint, fix, set mind to
      3. to constitute, make (one something), make like, perform
      4. to take one's stand
      5. to lay waste
    2. (Hophal) to be imposed, be set upon

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2380

Part(s) of speech: Verb
