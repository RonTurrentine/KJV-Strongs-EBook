חָזָה

Transliterationchâzâh
Phonetickhaw-zaw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to gaze at; mentally to perceive, contemplate (with pleasure); specifically, to have avision of

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

behold, look, prophesy, provide, see.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חזה

Transliteration: châzâh

Phonetic: khaw-zaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to see, perceive, look, behold, prophesy, provide
    1. (Qal)
      1. to see, behold
      2. to see as a seer in the ecstatic state
      3. to see, perceive
        1. with the intelligence
        2. to see (by experience)
        3. to provide

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 633

Part(s) of speech: Verb
