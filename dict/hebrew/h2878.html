טִבְחָה

Transliterationṭibchâh
Phonetic tib-khaw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

{properly, something slaughtered; hence, a beast (or meat, as butchered); abstractly butchery (or concretely, a place of slaughter)}

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

flesh, slaughter.
