מַקֵּל

Transliterationmaqqêl
Phoneticmak-kale
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a shoot, i.e. stick (with leaves on, or for walking, striking, guiding, divining)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

rod, (hand-)staff.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מקּלה מקּל

Transliteration: maqqêl maqqelâh

Phonetic: mak-kale

BDB Definition:

  1. rod, staff
    1. rod, stick
    2. staff (in travel)
    3. wand (of diviner)

Origin: from an unused root meaning apparently to germinate
