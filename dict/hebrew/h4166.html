מוּצָקָה

Transliterationmûwtsâqâh
Phonetic moo-tsaw-kaw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

properly, something poured out, i.e. a casting (of metal); by implication, a tube (as cast)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

when it was cast, pipe.
