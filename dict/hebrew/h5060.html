נָגַע

Transliterationnâgaʻ
Phoneticnaw-gah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to touch, i.e. lay the hand upon (for any purpose; euphemistically, to lie with a woman); by implication, to reach (figuratively, to arrive, acquire); violently, to strike (punish, defeat, destroy, etc.)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beat, ([idiom] be able to) bring (down), cast, come (nigh), draw near (nigh), get up, happen, join, near, plague, reach (up), smite, strike, touch.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נגע

Transliteration: nâga‛

Phonetic: naw-gah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to touch, reach, strike
    1. (Qal)
      1. to touch
      2. to strike
      3. to reach, extend to
      4. to be stricken
        1. stricken (participle)
    2. (Niphal) to be stricken, be defeated
    3. (Piel) to strike
    4. (Pual) to be stricken (by disease)
    5. (Hiphil) to cause to touch, reach, approach, arrive
      1. to cause to touch, apply
      2. to reach, extend, attain, arrive, come
      3. to approach (of time)
      4. to befall (of fate)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1293

Part(s) of speech: Verb
