שֶׁמֶן

Transliterationshemen
Phoneticsheh'-men
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

grease, especially liquid (as from the olive, often perfumed); figuratively, richness

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

anointing, [idiom] fat (things), [idiom] fruitful, oil(-ed), ointment, olive, [phrase] pine.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁמן

Transliteration: shemen

Phonetic: sheh'-men

BDB Definition:

  1. fat, oil
    1. fat, fatness
    2. oil, olive oil
      1. as staple, medicament or unguent
      2. for anointing
    3. fat (of fruitful land, valleys) (metaphorically)

Origin: from H8080
