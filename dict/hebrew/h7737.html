שָׁוָה

Transliterationshâvâh
Phoneticshaw-vaw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to level, i.e. equalize; figuratively, to resemble; by implication, to adjust (i.e. counterbalance, be suitable, compose, place, yield, etc.)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

avail, behave, bring forth, compare, countervail, (be, make) equal, lay, be (make, a-) like, make plain, profit, reckon.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁוה

Transliteration: shâvâh

Phonetic: shaw-vaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to agree with, be or become like, level, resemble
    1. (Qal)
      1. to be like
      2. equivalent (participle)
      3. to set, place
      4. setting (participle)
    2. (Piel) to level, smooth, still
    3. (Hiphil) to make like
    4. (Nithpael) to be alike
  2. (Piel) to set, place

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2342,2343

Part(s) of speech: Verb
