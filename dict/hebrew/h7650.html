שָׁבַע

Transliterationshâbaʻ
Phoneticshaw-bah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to seven oneself, i.e. swear (as if by repeating a declaration seven times)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

adjure, charge (by an oath, with an oath), feed to the full (by mistake for ), take an oath, [idiom] straitly, (cause to, make to) swear.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁבע

Transliteration: shâba‛

Phonetic:

BDB Definition:

  1. to swear, adjure
    1. (Qal) sworn (participle)
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to swear, take an oath
      2. to swear (of Jehovah by Himself)
      3. to curse
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to cause to take an oath
      2. to adjure

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2318
