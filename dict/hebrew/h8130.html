שָׂנֵא

Transliterationsânêʼ
Phoneticsaw-nay'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to hate (personally)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

enemy, foe, (be) hate(-ful, -r), odious, [idiom] utterly.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׂנא

Transliteration: śânê'

Phonetic: saw-nay'

BDB Definition:

  1. to hate, be hateful
    1. (Qal) to hate
      1. of man
      2. of God
      3. hater, one hating, enemy (participle) (substantive)
    2. (Niphal) to be hated
    3. (Piel) hater (participle)
      1. of persons, nations, God, wisdom

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2272

Part(s) of speech: Verb
