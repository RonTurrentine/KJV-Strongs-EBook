שָׁאַר

Transliterationshâʼar
Phoneticshaw-ar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to swell up, i.e. be (causatively, make) redundant

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

leave, (be) left, let, remain, remnant, reserve, the rest.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁאר

Transliteration: shâ'ar

Phonetic: shaw-ar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to remain, be left over, be left behind
    1. (Qal) to remain
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be left over, be left alive, survive
        1. remainder, remnant (participle)
      2. to be left behind
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to leave over, spare
      2. to leave or keep over
      3. to have left
      4. to leave (as a gift)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2307,2308

Part(s) of speech: Verb
