נָהַר

Transliterationnâhar
Phoneticnaw-har'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to sparkle, i.e. (figuratively) be cheerful; hence (from the sheen of a running stream) to flow, i.e. (figuratively) assemble

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

flow (together), be lightened.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נהר

Transliteration: nâhar

Phonetic: naw-har'

BDB Definition:

  1. to shine, beam, light, burn
    1. (Qal) to beam, be radiant
  2. to flow, stream
    1. (Qal) to flow, stream

Origin: a primitive root
