יָבֵשׁ

Transliterationyâbêsh
Phoneticyaw-bashe'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be ashamed, confused or disappointed; also (as failing) to dry up (as water) or wither (as herbage)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be ashamed, clean, be confounded, (make) dry (up), (do) shame(-fully), [idiom] utterly, wither (away).
