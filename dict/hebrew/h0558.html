TransliterationʼĂmatsyâh
Phoneticam-ats-yaw'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Amatsjah, the name of four Israelites

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Amaziah.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אמציהוּ אמציה

Transliteration: 'ămatsyâh 'ămatsyâhû

Phonetic: am-ats-yaw'

BDB Definition: Amaziah = " Jehovah is mighty"

  1. a king of Judah, son Joash, father of Azariah
  2. a priest of Bethel under Jeroboam II
  3. father of Joshah, of the tribe of Simeon
  4. a Levite tabernacle singer in David's day

Origin: from H0553 and H3050

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
