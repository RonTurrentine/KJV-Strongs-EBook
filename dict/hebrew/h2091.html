זָהָב

Transliterationzâhâb
Phoneticzaw-hawb'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

gold, figuratively, something gold-colored (i.e. yellow), as oil, a clear sky

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

gold(-en), fair weather.
