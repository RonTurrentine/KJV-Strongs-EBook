כָּחַד

Transliterationkâchad
Phonetickaw-khad'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to secrete, by act or word; hence (intensively) to destroy

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

conceal, cut down (off), desolate, hide.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּחד

Transliteration: kâchad

Phonetic: kaw-khad'

BDB Definition:

  1. to hide, conceal, cut off, cut down, make desolate, kick
    1. (Niphal)
      1. to be hidden
      2. to be effaced, be destroyed, be cut off
    2. (Piel) to cover, hide
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to hide
      2. to efface, annihilate

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 972

Part(s) of speech: Verb
