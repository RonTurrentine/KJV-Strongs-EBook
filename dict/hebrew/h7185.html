קָשָׁה

Transliterationqâshâh
Phonetickaw-shaw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to be dense, i.e. tough or severe (in various applications)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be cruel, be fiercer, make grievous, be ((ask a), be in, have, seem, would) hard(-en, (labour), -ly, thing), be sore, (be, make) stiff(-en, (-necked)).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: קשׁה

Transliteration: qâshâh

Phonetic: kaw-shaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be hard, be severe, be fierce, be harsh
    1. (Qal)
      1. to be hard, be difficult
      2. to be hard, be severe
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be ill-treated
      2. to be hard pressed
    3. (Piel) to have severe labour (of women)
    4. (Hiphil)
      1. to make difficult, make difficulty
      2. to make severe, make burdensome
      3. to make hard, make stiff, make stubborn
        1. of obstinacy (figuratively)
      4. to show stubbornness

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2085

Part(s) of speech: Verb
