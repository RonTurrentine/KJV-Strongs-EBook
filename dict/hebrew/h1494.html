גַּזָז

Transliterationgazâz
Phoneticgaw-zaz'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to cut off; specifically to shear aflock or shave the hair; figuratively to destroy an enemy

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cut off (down), poll, shave, (sheep-) shear(-er).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: גּזז

BDB Definition:

  1. to shear, mow
    1. (Qal)
      1. to shear
      2. shearer (participle)
    2. (Niphal) to be cut off, be destroyed
