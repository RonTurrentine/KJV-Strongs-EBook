קָדוֹשׁ

Transliterationqâdôwsh
Phonetickaw-doshe'
Part of SpeechAdjective

Definition

sacred (ceremonially or morally); (as noun) God (by eminence), an angel, a saint, a sanctuary

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

holy (One), saint.
