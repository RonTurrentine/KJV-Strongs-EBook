מַטֶּה

Transliterationmaṭṭeh
Phoneticmat-teh'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a branch (as extending); figuratively, a tribe; also a rod, whether for chastising (figuratively, correction), ruling (a sceptre), throwing (a lance), or walking (a staff; figuratively, a support of life, e.g. bread)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

rod, staff, tribe.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מטּה מטּה

Transliteration: maṭṭeh maṭṭâh

Phonetic: mat-teh'

BDB Definition:

  1. staff, branch, tribe
    1. staff, rod, shaft
    2. branch (of vine)
    3. tribe
      1. company led by chief with staff (originally)
