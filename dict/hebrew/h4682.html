מַצָּה

Transliterationmatstsâh
Phoneticmats-tsaw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

properly, sweetness; concretely, sweet (i.e. not soured or bittered with yeast); specifically, an unfermented cake or loaf, or (elliptically) the festival of Passover (because no leaven was then used)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

unleaved (bread, cake), without leaven.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מצּה

Transliteration: matstsâh

Phonetic: mats-tsaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. unleavened (bread, cake), without leaven.

Origin: from H4711 in the sense of greedily devouring for sweetness
