שָׂבַר

Transliterationsâbar
Phoneticsaw-bar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to scrutinize; by implication (of watching) to expect (with hope and patience)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

hope, tarry, view, wait.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁבר שׂבר

Transliteration: śâbar shâbar

Phonetic: saw-bar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to inspect, examine, wait, hope, wait upon
    1. (Qal) examined (participle)
    2. (Piel)
      1. to wait (for), wait upon
      2. to hope (for)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2232

Part(s) of speech: Verb
