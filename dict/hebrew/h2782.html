חָרַץ

Transliterationchârats
Phonetickhaw-rats'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to point sharply, i.e. (literally) to wound; figuratively, to be alert, to decide

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bestir self, decide, decree, determine, maim, move.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חרץ

Transliteration: chârats

Phonetic: khaw-rats'

BDB Definition:

  1. to cut, sharpen, decide, decree, determine, maim, move, be decisive, be mutilated
    1. (Qal)
      1. to cut, mutilate
      2. to sharpen
      3. to decide
    2. (Niphal) to be decisive

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 752

Part(s) of speech: Verb
