עֲרֻבָּה

Transliterationʻărubbâh
Phonetic ar-oob-baw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

something given as security, i.e. (literally) a token (of safety) or (metaphorically) a bondsman

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

pledge, surety.
