אָחַר

Transliterationʼâchar
Phoneticaw-khar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to loiter (i.e. be behind); by implication to procrastinate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

continue, defer, delay, hinder, be late (slack), stay (there), tarry (longer).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אחר

Transliteration: 'âchar

Phonetic: aw-khar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to delay, hesitate, tarry, defer, remain behind
    1. (Qal)
      1. to delay, tarry (intensive)
      2. to cause one to delay, hinder, keep back
    2. (Piel) to delay, wait, stay behind (but not in hope)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 68

Part(s) of speech: Verb
