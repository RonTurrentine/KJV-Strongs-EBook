שָׁלַט

Transliterationshâlaṭ
Phoneticshaw-lat'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to dominate, i.e. govern; by implication, to permit

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(bear, have) rule, have dominion, give (have) power.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁלט

Transliteration: shâlaṭ

Phonetic: shaw-lat'

BDB Definition:

  1. to domineer, exercise power over, dominate, have mastery, be master, lord it over
    1. (Qal) to domineer, lord it over, become master
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to give power of
      2. to get mastery of

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2396

Part(s) of speech: Verb
