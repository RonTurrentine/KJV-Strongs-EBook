Transliterationchârab
Phonetickhaw-rab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to parch (through drought) i.e. (by analogy,) to desolate, destroy, kill

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

decay, (be) desolate, destroy(-er), (be) dry (up), slay, [idiom] surely, (lay, lie, make) waste.
