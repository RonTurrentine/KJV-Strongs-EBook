BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: קוּם

Transliteration: qûm

Phonetic: koom

BDB Definition:

  1. to arise, stand
    1. (Peal)
      1. to arise from
      2. to come on the scene (figuratively)
      3. to arise (out of inaction)
      4. to stand
      5. to endure
    2. (Pael) to set up, establish
    3. (Aphel)
      1. to set up
      2. to lift up
      3. to establish
      4. to appoint
    4. (Hophal) to be made to stand
