קָרָה

Transliterationqârâh
Phonetickaw-raw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to light upon (chiefly by accident); causatively, to bring about; specifically, to impose timbers (for roof or floor)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

appoint, lay (make) beams, befall, bring, come (to pass unto), floor, (hap) was, happen (unto), meet, send good speed.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: קרה

Transliteration: qârâh

Phonetic: kaw-raw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to encounter, meet, befall, happen, come to meet
    1. (Qal)
      1. to encounter, meet
      2. to befall
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to encounter, meet (without pre-arrangement)
      2. to chance to be present
      3. to come to meet
    3. (Hiphil) to cause to meet, appoint
  2. to build with beams
    1. (Piel) to lay the beams of, furnish with beams

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2068,2068e

Part(s) of speech: Verb
