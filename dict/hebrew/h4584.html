Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine / Noun Proper Location

Definition

Maon, the name of an Israelite and of a place in Palestine

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Maon, Maonites. Compare , lemma מְעוּנָי third vowel, corrected to מְעוּנִי.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מעון

Transliteration: mâ‛ôn

Phonetic: maw-ohn'

BDB Definition: Maon or Maonites = " habitation"

  1. a man of Judah (noun proper masculine)
  2. the inhabitants of the city of Maon (noun proper masculine)
  3. a city of Judah located 8 miles south of Hebron (noun proper locative)
