יָשֻׁבִי לֶחֶם

TransliterationYâshubîy Lechem
Phoneticyaw-shoo-bee' leh'-khem
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Jashubi-Lechem, an Israelite

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Jashubi-lehem. (Prob. the text should be pointed and rendered '(they were) inhabitants of Lechem,' i.e. of Bethlehem (by contraction). Compare ).
