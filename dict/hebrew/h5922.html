Definition

{above, over, upon, or against (yet always in this last relation with a downward aspect) in a great variety of applications}

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

about, against, concerning, for, (there-) fore, from, in, [idiom] more, of, (there-, up-) on, (in-) to, [phrase] why with.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: על

Transliteration: ‛al

Phonetic: al

BDB Definition:

  1. upon, over, on account of, above, to, against
    1. upon, over, on account of, regarding, concerning, on behalf of
    2. over (with verbs of ruling)
    3. above, beyond (in comparison)
    4. to, against (of direction)

Origin: corresponding to H5921

TWOT entry: 2908

Part(s) of speech: Preposition
