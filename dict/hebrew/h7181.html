קָשַׁב

Transliterationqâshab
Phonetickaw-shab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to prick up the ears, i.e. hearken

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

attend, (cause to) hear(-ken), give heed, incline, mark (well), regard.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: קשׁב

Transliteration: qâshab

Phonetic: kaw-shab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to hear, be attentive, heed, incline (of ears), attend (of ears), hearken, pay attention, listen
    1. (Qal) incline, attend (of ears), hearken, pay attention, listen
    2. (Hiphil) to pay attention, give attention

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2084

Part(s) of speech: Verb
