שָׁכַר

Transliterationshâkar
Phoneticshaw-kar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to become tipsy; in a qualified sense, to satiate with astimulating drink or (figuratively) influence

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(be filled with) drink (abundantly), (be, make) drunk(-en), be merry. (Superlative of .)

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁכר

Transliteration: shâkar

Phonetic: shaw-kar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be or become drunk or drunken, be intoxicated
    1. (Qal) to become drunken
    2. (Piel) to make drunken, cause to be drunk
    3. (Hiphil) to cause to be drunk
    4. (Hithpael) to make oneself drunk

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2388

Part(s) of speech: Verb
