עָצַב

Transliterationʻâtsab
Phoneticaw-tsab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to carve, i.e. fabricate or fashion; hence (in a bad sense) to worry, pain or anger

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

displease, grieve, hurt, make, be sorry, vex, worship, wrest.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: עצב

Transliteration: ‛âtsab

Phonetic: aw-tsab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to hurt, pain, grieve, displease, vex, wrest
    1. (Qal) to hurt, pain
    2. (Niphal) to be in pain, be pained, be grieved
    3. (Piel) to vex, torture
    4. (Hiphil) to cause pain
    5. (Hithpael) to feel grieved, be vexed
  2. to shape, fashion, make, form, stretch into shape, (TWOT) worship
    1. (Piel) to shape, form
    2. (Hiphil) to form, copy, fashion

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1666,1667

Part(s) of speech: Verb
