בָּלַג

Transliterationbâlag
Phoneticbaw-lag'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to break off or loose (in a favorable or unfavorable sense), i.e. desist (from grief) or invade (with destruction)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

comfort, (recover) strength(-en).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּלג

Transliteration: bâlag

Phonetic: baw-lag'

BDB Definition:

  1. to gleam, smile
    1. (Hiphil) only
      1. to show a smile, look pleasant
      2. to cause to burst

Origin: a primitive root
