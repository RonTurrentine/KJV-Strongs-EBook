חָפַז

Transliterationchâphaz
Phonetickhaw-faz'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to start up suddenly, i.e. (by implication) to hasten away, to fear

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(make) haste (away), tremble.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חפז

Transliteration: châphaz

Phonetic: khaw-faz'

BDB Definition:

  1. to hurry, flee, hasten, fear, be terrified
    1. (Qal) to be in a hurry, be alarmed
    2. (Niphal) to be in a hurry

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 708

Part(s) of speech: Verb
