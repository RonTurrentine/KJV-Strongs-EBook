מִקְנֶה

Transliterationmiqneh
Phoneticmik-neh'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

something bought, i.e. property, but only livestock; abstractly, acquisition

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cattle, flock, herd, possession, purchase, substance.

BDB / Thayer Definition

BDB Definition:

  1. cattle, livestock
    1. cattle, livestock
      1. in general of a purchasable domestic animal
    2. cows, sheep, goats (in herds and flocks)
