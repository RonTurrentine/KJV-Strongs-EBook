חָצֵר

Transliterationchâtsêr
Phonetickhaw-tsare'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a yard (as inclosed by a fence); also a hamlet (as similarly surrounded with walls)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

court, tower, village.
