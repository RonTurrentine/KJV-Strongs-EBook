זְרוֹעַ

Transliterationzᵉrôwaʻ
Phoneticzer-o'-ah
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

the arm (as stretched out), or (of animals) the foreleg; figuratively, force

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

arm, [phrase] help, mighty, power, shoulder, strength.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: זרעה זרועה זרע זרוע

BDB Definition:

  1. arm, forearm, shoulder, strength
    1. arm
    2. arm (as symbol of strength)
    3. forces (political and military)
    4. shoulder (of animal sacrificed)
