BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אבירם

Transliteration: 'ăbı̂yrâm

Phonetic: ab-ee-rawm'

BDB Definition: Abiram = " my father is exalted" or " (the) Exalted One is (my) father"

  1. a Reubenite, son of Eliab in exodus
  2. son of Hiel the Bethelite who worked to rebuild Jericho

Origin: from H0001 and H7311
