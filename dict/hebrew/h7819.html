שָׁחַט

Transliterationshâchaṭ
Phoneticshaw-khat'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to slaughter (in sacrifice or massacre)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

kill, offer, shoot out, slay, slaughter.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁחט

Transliteration: shâchaṭ

Phonetic: shaw-khat'

BDB Definition:

  1. to kill, slaughter, beat (verb)
    1. (Qal)
      1. to slaughter
        1. beast for food
        2. sacrifice
        3. person in human sacrifice
        4. beaten, hammered (of shekels)
    2. (Niphal) to be slaughtered, be slain (of food or sacrifice)
  2. (BDB) slaughtering (noun feminine)
    1. word doubtful

Origin: a primitive root
