תּוֹכֵחָה

Transliterationtôwkêchâh
Phoneticto-kay-khaw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

chastisement; figuratively (by words) correction, refutation, proof (even in defence)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

argument, [idiom] chastened, correction, reasoning, rebuke, reproof, [idiom] be (often) reproved.
