צוּר

Transliterationtsûwr
Phonetictsoor
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, a cliff (or sharp rock, as compressed); generally, a rock or boulder; figuratively, a refuge; also an edge (as precipitous)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

edge, [idiom] (mighty) God (one), rock, [idiom] sharp, stone, [idiom] strength, [idiom] strong. See also .
