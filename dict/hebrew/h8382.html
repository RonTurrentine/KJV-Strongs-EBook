תָּאַם

Transliterationtâʼam
Phonetictaw-am'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be (causatively, make) twinned, i.e. (figuratively) duplicate or (architectural) jointed

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

coupled (together), bear twins.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Transliteration: tâ'am

Phonetic: taw-am'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be double, be joined
    1. (Qal) to be double
    2. (Hiphil) to bear twins
