נָגַף

Transliterationnâgaph
Phoneticnaw-gaf'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to push, gore, defeat, stub (the toe), inflict (a disease)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beat, dash, hurt, plague, slay, smite (down), strike, stumble, [idiom] surely, put to the worse.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נגף

Transliteration: nâgaph

Phonetic: naw-gaf'

BDB Definition:

  1. to strike, smite
    1. (Qal) to strike, smite
    2. (Niphal) to be stricken, be smitten
    3. (Hithpael) to stumble

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1294

Part(s) of speech: Verb
