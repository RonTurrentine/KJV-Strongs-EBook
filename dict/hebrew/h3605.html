כֹּל

Transliterationkôl
Phonetickole
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, the whole; hence, all, any or every (in the singular only, but often in a plural sense)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(in) all (manner, (ye)), altogether, any (manner), enough, every (one, place, thing), howsoever, as many as, (no-) thing, ought, whatsoever, (the) whole, whoso(-ever).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּול כּל o

Transliteration: kôl kôl

Phonetic: kole

BDB Definition:

  1. all, the whole
    1. all, the whole of
    2. any, each, every, anything
    3. totality, everything
