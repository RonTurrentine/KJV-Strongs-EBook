Transliterationʼôwr
Phoneticore
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

illumination or (concrete) luminary (in every sense, including lightning, happiness, etc.)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bright, clear, [phrase] day, light (-ning), morning, sun.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אור

Transliteration: 'ôr

Phonetic: ore

BDB Definition:

  1. light
    1. light of day
    2. light of heavenly luminaries (moon, sun, stars)
    3. day-break, dawn, morning light
    4. daylight
    5. lightning
    6. light of lamp
    7. light of life
    8. light of prosperity
    9. light of instruction
    10. light of face (figuratively)
    11. Jehovah as Israel's light

Origin: from H0215

TWOT entry: 52a
