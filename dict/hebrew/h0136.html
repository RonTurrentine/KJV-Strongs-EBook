אֲדֹנָי

TransliterationʼĂdônây
Phoneticad-o-noy'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

the Lord (used as a proper name of God only)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(my) Lord.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אדני

Transliteration: 'ădônây

Phonetic: ad-o-noy'

BDB Definition:

  1. my lord, lord
    1. of men
    2. of God
  2. Lord - title, spoken in place of Yahweh in Jewish display of reverence

Origin: an emphatic form of H0113

TWOT entry: 27b

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
