גָּרַע

Transliterationgâraʻ
Phoneticgaw-rah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to scrape off; by implication, to shave, remove, lessen, withhold

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

abate, clip, (di-) minish, do (take) away, keep back, restrain, make small, withdraw.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: גּרע

Transliteration: gâra‛

Phonetic: gaw-rah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to diminish, restrain, withdraw, abate, keep back, do away, take from, clip
    1. (Qal)
      1. to diminish
      2. to restrain
      3. to withdraw
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be withdrawn
      2. to be restrained
    3. (Piel) to withdraw, draw up

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 384

Part(s) of speech: Verb
