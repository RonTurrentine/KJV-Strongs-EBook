עָרָה

Transliterationʻârâh
Phoneticaw-raw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be (causatively, make) bare; hence, to empty, pour out, demolish

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

leave destitute, discover, empty, make naked, pour (out), rase, spread self, uncover.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ערה

Transliteration: ‛ârâh

Phonetic: aw-raw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be bare, be nude, uncover, leave destitute, discover, empty, raze, pour out
    1. (Piel)
      1. to bare, lay bare
      2. to lay bare by emptying, empty
      3. to pour out
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to make naked, strip bare (of sexual offences)
      2. to pour out
    3. (Niphal) to be poured out, be exposed
    4. (Hithpael)
      1. to expose oneself, make oneself naked
      2. pouring oneself, spreading oneself (participle)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1692

Part(s) of speech: Verb
