יָתַר

Transliterationyâthar
Phoneticyaw-thar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to jut over or exceed; by implication, to excel; (intransitively) to remain or be left; causatively, to leave, cause to abound, preserve

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

excel, leave (a remnant), left behind, too much, make plenteous, preserve, (be, let) remain(-der, -ing, -nant), reserve, residue, rest.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יתר

Transliteration: yâthar

Phonetic: yaw-thar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be left over, remain, remain over, leave
    1. (Qal) remainder (participle)
    2. (Niphal) to be left over, remain over, be left behind
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to leave over, leave
      2. to save over, preserve alive
      3. to excel, show pre-eminence
      4. to show excess, have more than enough, have an excess

Origin: a primitive root
