צָבָא

Transliterationtsâbâʼ
Phonetictsaw-baw'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a mass of persons (or figuratively, things), especially reg. organized forwar (an army); by implication, a campaign, literally or figuratively (specifically, hardship, worship)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

appointed time, ([phrase]) army, ([phrase]) battle, company, host, service, soldiers, waiting upon, war(-fare).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: צבאה צבא

Transliteration: tsâbâ' tsebâ'âh

Phonetic: tsaw-baw'

BDB Definition:

  1. that which goes forth, army, war, warfare, host
    1. army, host
      1. host (of organised army)
      2. host (of angels)
      3. of sun, moon, and stars
      4. of whole creation
    2. war, warfare, service, go out to war
    3. service
