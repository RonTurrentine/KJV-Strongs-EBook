הֵנָּה

Transliterationhênnâh
Phonetichane'-naw
Part of SpeechAdverb

Definition

hither or thither (but used both of place and time)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

here, hither(-to), now, on this (that) side, [phrase] since, this (that) way, thitherward, [phrase] thus far, to...fro, [phrase] yet.
