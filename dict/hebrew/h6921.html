קָדִים

Transliterationqâdîym
Phonetickaw-deem'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

the fore or front part; hence (by orientation) the East (often adverbially, eastward, for brevity the east wind)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

east(-ward, wind).
