גָּלַח

Transliterationgâlach
Phoneticgaw-lakh'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to be bald, i.e. (causatively) to shave; figuratively to lay waste

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

poll, shave (off).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: גּלח

Transliteration: gâlach

Phonetic: gaw-lakh'

BDB Definition:

  1. to poll, shave, shave off, be bald
    1. (Piel)
      1. to shave
      2. to shave off
      3. (figuratively of devastation)
    2. (Pual) to be shaven
    3. (Hithpael) to shave oneself

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 351

Part(s) of speech: Verb
