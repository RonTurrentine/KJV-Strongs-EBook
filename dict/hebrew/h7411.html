רָמָה

Transliterationrâmâh
Phoneticraw-maw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to hurl; specifically, to shoot; figuratively, to delude or betray (as if causing to fall)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beguile, betray, (bow-) man, carry, deceive, throw.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רמה

Transliteration: râmâh

Phonetic: raw-maw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to cast, shoot, hurl
    1. (Qal)
      1. to throw
      2. bow-shooters, bowmen (participle)
    2. (Piel) to throw down
  2. to beguile, deceive, mislead, deal treacherously
    1. (Piel)
      1. to beguile, deceive, mislead, trick
      2. to deal treacherously with, betray

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2168,2169

Part(s) of speech: Verb
