כָּרַע

Transliterationkâraʻ
Phonetickaw-rah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to bend the knee; by implication, to sink, to prostrate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bow (down, self), bring down (low), cast down, couch, fall, feeble, kneeling, sink, smite (stoop) down, subdue, [idiom] very.
