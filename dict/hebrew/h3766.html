כָּרַע

Transliterationkâraʻ
Phonetickaw-rah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to bend the knee; by implication, to sink, to prostrate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bow (down, self), bring down (low), cast down, couch, fall, feeble, kneeling, sink, smite (stoop) down, subdue, [idiom] very.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּרע

Transliteration: kâra‛

Phonetic: kaw-rah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to bend, kneel, bow, bow down, sink down to one's knees, kneel down to rest (of animals), kneel in reverence
    1. (Qal)
      1. to bow
      2. to bow down, crouch
      3. to bow down over
      4. to tilt, lean
    2. (Hiphil) to cause to bow

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1044

Part(s) of speech: Verb
