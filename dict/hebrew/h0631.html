אָסַר

Transliterationʼâçar
Phoneticaw-sar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to yoke or hitch; by analogy, to fasten in any sense, to join battle

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bind, fast, gird, harness, hold, keep, make ready, order, prepare, prison(-er), put in bonds, set in array, tie.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אסר

Transliteration: 'âsar

Phonetic: aw-sar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to tie, bind, imprison
    1. (Qal)
      1. to tie, bind
      2. to tie, harness
      3. to bind (with cords)
      4. to gird (rare and late)
      5. to begin the battle, make the attack
      6. of obligation of oath (figurative)
    2. (Niphal) to be imprisoned, bound
    3. (Pual) to be taken prisoner

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 141

Part(s) of speech: Verb
