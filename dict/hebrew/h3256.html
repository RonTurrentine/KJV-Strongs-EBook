יָסַר

Transliterationyâçar
Phoneticyaw-sar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to chastise, literally (with blows) or figuratively (with words); hence, to instruct

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bind, chasten, chastise, correct, instruct, punish, reform, reprove, sore, teach.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יסר

Transliteration: yâsar

Phonetic: yaw-sar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to chasten, discipline, instruct, admonish
    1. (Qal)
      1. to chasten, admonish
      2. to instruct
      3. to discipline
    2. (Niphal) to let oneself be chastened or corrected or admonished
    3. (Piel)
      1. to discipline, correct
      2. to chasten, chastise
    4. (Hiphil) to chasten
    5. (Nithpael) to teach

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 877

Part(s) of speech: Verb
