יָשַׁב

Transliterationyâshab
Phoneticyaw-shab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to sit down (specifically as judge. in ambush, in quiet); by implication, to dwell, to remain; causatively, to settle, to marry

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(make to) abide(-ing), continue, (cause to, make to) dwell(-ing), ease self, endure, establish, [idiom] fail, habitation, haunt, (make to) inhabit(-ant), make to keep (house), lurking, [idiom] marry(-ing), (bring again to) place, remain, return, seat, set(-tle), (down-) sit(-down, still, -ting down, -ting (place) -uate), take, tarry.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ישׁב

Transliteration: yâshab

Phonetic: yaw-shab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to dwell, remain, sit, abide
    1. (Qal)
      1. to sit, sit down
      2. to be set
      3. to remain, stay
      4. to dwell, have one's abode
    2. (Niphal) to be inhabited
    3. (Piel) to set, place
    4. (Hiphil)
      1. to cause to sit
      2. to cause to abide, set
      3. to cause to dwell
      4. to cause (cities) to be inhabited
      5. to marry (give an dwelling to)
    5. (Hophal)
      1. to be inhabited
      2. to make to dwell

Origin: a primitive root
