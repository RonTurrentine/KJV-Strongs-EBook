צָעַד

Transliterationtsâʻad
Phonetictsaw-ad'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to pace, i.e. step regularly; (upward) to mount; (along) to march; (down and causatively) to hurl

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bring, go, march (through), run over.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: צעד

Transliteration: tsâ‛ad

Phonetic: tsaw-ad'

BDB Definition:

  1. to step, march, stride
    1. (Qal) to step, march
    2. (Hiphil) to cause to march

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1943

Part(s) of speech: Verb
