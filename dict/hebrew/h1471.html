גּוֹי

Transliterationgôwy
Phoneticgo'-ee
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine / Noun Proper Masculine

Definition

a foreign nation; hence, a Gentile; also (figuratively) a troop of animals, or a flight of locusts

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Gentile, heathen, nation, people.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: גּי גּוי

Transliteration: gôy gôy

Phonetic: go'-ee

BDB Definition:

  1. nation, people (noun masculine)
    1. nation, people
      1. usually of non-Hebrew people
      2. of descendants of Abraham
      3. of Israel
    2. of swarm of locusts, other animals (figuratively)
    3. Goyim? = " nations" (noun proper masculine)

Origin: apparently from the same root as H1465
