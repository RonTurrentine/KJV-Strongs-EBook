שָׁקַע

Transliterationshâqaʻ
Phoneticshaw-kah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to subside; by implication, to be overflowed, cease; causatively, to abate, subdue

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

make deep, let down, drown, quench, sink.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁקע

Transliteration: shâqa‛

Phonetic: shaw-kah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to sink, sink down, subside
    1. (Qal) to sink
    2. (Niphal) to sink, collapse
    3. (Hiphil) to sink down, cause to settle

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2456

Part(s) of speech: Verb
