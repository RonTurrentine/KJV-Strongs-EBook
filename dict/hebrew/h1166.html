בָּעַל

Transliterationbâʻal
Phoneticbaw-al'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be master; hence, to marry

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

have dominion (over), be husband, marry(-ried, [idiom] wife).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּעל

Transliteration: bâ‛al

Phonetic: baw-al'

BDB Definition:

  1. to marry, rule over, possess, own
    1. (Qal)
      1. to marry, be lord (husband) over
      2. to rule over
    2. (Niphal) to be married

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 262

Part(s) of speech: Verb
