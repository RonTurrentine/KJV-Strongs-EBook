לָחַץ

Transliterationlâchats
Phoneticlaw-khats'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to press, i.e. (figuratively) to distress

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

afflict, crush, force, hold fast, oppress(-or), thrust self.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: לחץ

Transliteration: lâchats

Phonetic: law-khats'

BDB Definition:

  1. to squeeze, press, oppress
    1. (Qal)
      1. to squeeze, press
      2. to oppress
    2. (Niphal) to squeeze oneself

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1106

Part(s) of speech: Verb
