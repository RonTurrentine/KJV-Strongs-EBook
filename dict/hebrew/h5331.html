נֶצַח

Transliterationnetsach
Phoneticneh'-tsakh
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, a goal, i.e. the bright object at adistance travelled towards; hence (figuratively), splendor, or (subjectively) truthfulness, or (objectively) confidence; but usually (adverbially), continually (i.e. to the most distant point of view)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

alway(-s), constantly, end, ([phrase] n-) ever(more), perpetual, strength, victory.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נצח נצח

BDB Definition:

  1. eminence, perpetuity, strength, victory, enduring, everlastingness
    1. eminence
    2. enduring of life
    3. endurance in time, perpetual, continual, unto the end
    4. everlastingness, ever
