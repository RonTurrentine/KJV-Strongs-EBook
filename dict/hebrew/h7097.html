Transliterationqâtseh
Phonetickaw-tseh'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

an extremity

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] after, border, brim, brink, edge, end, (in-) finite, frontier, outmost coast, quarter, shore, (out-) side, [idiom] some, ut(-ter-) most (part).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: קצה קצה

Transliteration: qâtseh qêtseh

Phonetic: kaw-tseh'

BDB Definition:

  1. end, extremity
    1. end, mouth, extremity
    2. border, outskirts
    3. the whole (condensed term for what is included within extremities)
    4. at the end of (a certain time)

Origin: from H7096

TWOT entry: 2053a,2053c
