מִקְדָּשׁ

Transliterationmiqdâsh
Phoneticmik-dawsh'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a consecrated thing or place, especially, a palace, sanctuary (whether of Jehovah or of idols) or asylum

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

chapel, hallowed part, holy place, sanctuary.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מקּדשׁ מקדּשׁ

Transliteration: miqdâsh miqqedâsh

Phonetic: mik-dawsh'

BDB Definition:

  1. sacred place, sanctuary, holy place
    1. sanctuary
      1. of the temple
      2. of the tabernacle
      3. of Ezekiel's temple
      4. of Jehovah
