שֵׁבֶט

Transliterationshêbeṭ
Phoneticshay'-bet
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a scion, i.e. (literally) a stick (for punishing, writing, fighting, ruling, walking, etc.) or (figuratively) a clan

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] correction, dart, rod, sceptre, staff, tribe.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁבט

Transliteration: shêbeṭ

Phonetic: shay'-bet

BDB Definition:

  1. rod, staff, branch, offshoot, club, sceptre, tribe
    1. rod, staff
    2. shaft (of spear, dart)
    3. club (of shepherd's implement)
    4. truncheon, sceptre (mark of authority)
    5. clan, tribe

Origin: from an unused root probably meaning to branch off

TWOT entry: 2314a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
