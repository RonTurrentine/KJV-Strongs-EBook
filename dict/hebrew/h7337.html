רָחַב

Transliterationrâchab
Phoneticraw-khab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to broaden (intransitive or transitive, literal or figurative)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be an en-(make) large(-ing), make room, make (open) wide.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רחב

Transliteration: râchab

Phonetic: raw-khab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be or grow wide, be or grow large
    1. (Qal) to be widened, be enlarged
    2. (Niphal) broad or roomy pasture (participle)
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to make large
      2. to enlarge

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2143

Part(s) of speech: Verb
