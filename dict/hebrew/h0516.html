אַל תַּשְׁחֵת

TransliterationʼAl tashchêth
Phonetical tash-kayth'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

'Thou must not destroy'; probably the opening words to a popular song

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Al-taschith.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אל תּשׁחת

Transliteration: 'al tashchêth

Phonetic: al tash-kayth'

BDB Definition: Al-taschith = " do not destroy"

  1. (Hiphil) a command to the chief musician, or perhaps the title of a melody used for several Psalms

Origin: from H0408 and H7843

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
