עָלַם

Transliterationʻâlam
Phoneticaw-lam'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to veil from sight, i.e. conceal (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] any ways, blind, dissembler, hide (self), secret (thing).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: עלם

Transliteration: ‛âlam

Phonetic: aw-lam'

BDB Definition:

  1. to conceal, hide, be hidden, be concealed, be secret
    1. (Qal) secret (participle)
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be concealed
      2. concealed, dissembler (participle)
    3. (Hiphil) to conceal, hide
    4. (Hithpael) to hide oneself

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1629

Part(s) of speech: Verb
