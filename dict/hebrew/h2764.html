חֵרֶם

Transliterationchêrem
Phonetickhay'-rem
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

physical (as shutting in) a net (either literally or figuratively); usually a doomed object; abstractly extermination

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(ac-) curse(-d, -d thing), dedicated thing, things which should have been utterly destroyed, (appointed to) utter destruction, devoted (thing), net.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חרם חרם

Transliteration: chêrem cherem

Phonetic: khay'-rem

BDB Definition:

  1. a thing devoted, thing dedicated, ban, devotion
  2. a net, thing perforated
  3. have been utterly destroyed, (appointed to) utter destruction

Origin: from H2763

TWOT entry: 744a,745a
