אַחֲרִית

Transliterationʼachărîyth
Phoneticakh-ar-eeth'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

the last or end, hence, the future; also posterity

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(last, latter) end (time), hinder (utter) -most, length, posterity, remnant, residue, reward.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אחרית

Transliteration: 'achărı̂yth

Phonetic: akh-ar-eeth'

BDB Definition:

  1. after part, end
    1. end, issue, event
    2. latter time (prophetic for future time)
    3. posterity
    4. last, hindermost

Origin: from H0310

TWOT entry: 68f
