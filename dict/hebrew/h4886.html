מָשַׁח

Transliterationmâshach
Phoneticmaw-shakh'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to rub with oil, i.e. to anoint; by implication, to consecrate; also to paint

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

anoint, paint.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: משׁח

Transliteration: mâshach

Phonetic: maw-shakh'

BDB Definition:

  1. to smear, anoint, spread a liquid
    1. (Qal)
      1. to smear
      2. to anoint (as consecration)
      3. to anoint, consecrate
    2. (Niphal) to be anointed

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1255

Part(s) of speech: Verb
