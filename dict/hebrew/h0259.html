Transliterationʼechâd
Phoneticekh-awd'
Part of SpeechAdjective

Definition

properly, united, i.e. one; or (as an ordinal) first

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

a, alike, alone, altogether, and, any(-thing), apiece, a certain, (dai-) ly, each (one), [phrase] eleven, every, few, first, [phrase] highway, a man, once, one, only, other, some, together,

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אחד

Transliteration: 'echâd

Phonetic: ekh-awd'

BDB Definition:

  1. one (number)
    1. one (number)
    2. each, every
    3. a certain
    4. an (indefinite article)
    5. only, once, once for all
    6. one...another, the one...the other, one after another, one by one
    7. first
    8. eleven (in combination), eleventh (ordinal)
