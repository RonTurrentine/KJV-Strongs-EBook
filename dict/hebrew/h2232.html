זָרַע

Transliterationzâraʻ
Phoneticzaw-rah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to sow; figuratively, to disseminate, plant, fructify

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bear, conceive seed, set with sow(-er), yield.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: זרע

Transliteration: zâra‛

Phonetic: zaw-rah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to sow, scatter seed
    1. (Qal)
      1. to sow
      2. producing, yielding seed
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be sown
      2. to become pregnant, be made pregnant
    3. (Pual) to be sown
    4. (Hiphil) to produce seed, yield seed

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 582

Part(s) of speech: Verb
