מָעַט

Transliterationmâʻaṭ
Phoneticmaw-at'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to pare off, i.e. lessen; intransitively, to be (or causatively, to make) small or few (or figuratively, ineffective)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

suffer to decrease, diminish, (be, [idiom] borrow a, give, make) few (in number, -ness), gather least (little), be (seem) little, ([idiom] give the) less, be minished, bring to nothing.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מעט

Transliteration: mâ‛aṭ

Phonetic: maw-at'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be or become small, be few, be diminished
    1. (Qal)
      1. to diminish
      2. to be too small
    2. (Piel) to become few
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to make small, make few, diminish
      2. to give less

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1228

Part(s) of speech: Verb
