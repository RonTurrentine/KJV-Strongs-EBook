beauty, greatly beloved, covet, delectable thing, ([idiom] great) delight, desire, goodly, lust, (be) pleasant (thing), precious (thing).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חמד

Transliteration: châmad

Phonetic: khaw-mad'

BDB Definition:

  1. to desire, covet, take pleasure in, delight in (verb)
    1. (Qal) to desire
    2. (Niphal) to be desirable
    3. (Piel) to delight greatly, desire greatly
  2. desirableness, preciousness (noun feminine)

Origin: a primitive root
