שָׁרַשׁ

Transliterationshârash
Phoneticshaw-rash'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to root, i.e. strike into the soil, or (by implication) to pluck from it

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(take, cause to take) root (out).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁרשׁ

Transliteration: shârash

Phonetic: shaw-rash'

BDB Definition:

  1. to uproot, take root, deal with the roots
    1. (Piel) to root up, root out
    2. (Pual) to be rooted up or out (of produce)
    3. (Poel) to take root
    4. (Poal) to take root
    5. (Hiphil) to take root, cause to take root

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2471

Part(s) of speech: Verb
