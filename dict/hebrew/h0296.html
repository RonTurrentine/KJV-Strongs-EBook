BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אחיקם

Transliteration: 'ăchı̂yqâm

Phonetic: akh-ee-kawm'

BDB Definition: Ahikam = " my brother has risen (arisen)"

  1. son of Shaphan, an officer in Josiah's reign
  2. protector of Jeremiah
  3. father of Gedaliah

Origin: from H0251 and H6965
