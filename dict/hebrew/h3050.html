Definition

Jah, the sacred name

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Jah, the Lord, most vehement. Compare names in '-iah,' '-jah.'

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יהּ

Transliteration: yâhh

Phonetic: yaw

BDB Definition:

  1. Jah (Jehovah in the shortened form)
    1. the proper name of the one true God
    2. used in many compounds
      1. names beginning with the letters 'Je'
      2. names ending with 'iah' or 'jah'

Origin: contraction for H3068, and meaning the same

TWOT entry: 484b

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name
