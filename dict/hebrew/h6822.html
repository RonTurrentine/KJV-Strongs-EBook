צָפָה

Transliterationtsâphâh
Phonetictsaw-faw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to lean forward, i.e. to peer into the distance; by implication, to observe, await

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

behold, espy, look up (well), wait for, (keep the) watch(-man).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: צפה

Transliteration: tsâphâh

Phonetic: tsaw-faw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to look out or about, spy, keep watch, observe, watch
    1. (Qal) to keep watch, spy
    2. (Piel) to watch, watch closely

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1950

Part(s) of speech: Verb
