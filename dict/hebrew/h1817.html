דֶּלֶת

Transliterationdeleth
Phoneticdeh'-leth
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

something swinging, i.e. the valve of adoor

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

door (two-leaved), gate, leaf, lid. (Psalm 141:3).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: דּלת

Transliteration: deleth

Phonetic: deh'-leth

BDB Definition:

  1. door, gate
    1. a door
    2. a gate
    3. (figuratively)
      1. of chest lid
      2. of crocodile jaws
      3. of doors of the heavens
      4. of an easily-accessible woman

Origin: from H1802

TWOT entry: 431a,e
