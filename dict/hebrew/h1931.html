הוּא

Transliterationhûwʼ
Phonetichoo
Part of SpeechPronoun

Definition

he (she or it); only expressed when emphatic or without a verb; also (intensively) self, or (especially with the article) the same; sometimes (as demonstrative) this or that; occasionally (instead of copula) as or are

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

he, as for her, him(-self), it, the same, she (herself), such, that (...it), these, they, this, those, which (is), who.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: היא הוּא

Transliteration: hû' hı̂y'

Phonetic: hoo

BDB Definition:

  1. he, she, it (pronoun third person singular)
    1. himself (with emphasis)
    2. resuming subject with emphasis
    3. (with minimum emphasis following predicate)
    4. (anticipating subject)
    5. (emphasizing predicate)
    6. that, it (neuter)
  2. that (with article) (demonstrative pronoun)

Origin: a primitive word
