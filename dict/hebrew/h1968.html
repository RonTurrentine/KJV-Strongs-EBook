BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: הימן

Transliteration: hêymân

Phonetic: hay-mawn'

BDB Definition: Heman = " faithful"

  1. a wise man to whom Solomon was compared
  2. son of Joel, grandson of Samuel, and a Levitical singer and author of PS 88
  3. a seer

Origin: probably from H0539

TWOT entry: None
