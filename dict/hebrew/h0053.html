אֲבִישָׁלוֹם

TransliterationʼĂbîyshâlôwm
Phoneticab-ee-shaw-lome'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Abshalom, a son of David; also (the fuller form) a later Israelite

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Abishalom, Absalom.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אבשׁלום אבישׁלום

Transliteration: 'ăbı̂yshâlôm 'abshâlôm

Phonetic: ab-ee-shaw-lome'

BDB Definition: Absalom or Abishalom = " my father is peace"

  1. father-in-law of Rehoboam
  2. third son of David, killer of first-born son Amnon, also leader of revolt against his father - David

Origin: from H0001 and H7965

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
