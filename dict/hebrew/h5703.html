עַד

Transliterationʻad
Phoneticad
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, a (peremptory) terminus, i.e. (by implication) duration, in the sense of advance or perpetuity (substantially as a noun, either with or without a preposition)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

eternity, ever(-lasting, -more), old, perpetually, [phrase] world without end.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: עד

Transliteration: ‛ad

Phonetic: ad

BDB Definition:

  1. perpetuity, for ever, continuing future
    1. ancient (of past time)
    2. for ever (of future time)
      1. of continuous existence
    3. for ever (of God's existence)
