נוּס

Transliterationnûwç
Phoneticnoos
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to flit, i.e. vanish away (subside, escape; causatively, chase, impel, deliver)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] abate, away, be displayed, (make to) flee (away, -ing), put to flight, [idiom] hide, lift up a standard.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נוּס

Transliteration: nûs

Phonetic: noos

BDB Definition:

  1. to flee, escape
    1. (Qal)
      1. to flee
      2. to escape
      3. to take flight, depart, disappear
      4. to fly (to the attack) on horseback
    2. (Polel) to drive at
    3. (Hithpolel) to take flight
    4. (Hiphil)
      1. to put to flight
      2. to drive hastily
      3. to cause to disappear, hide

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1327

Part(s) of speech: Verb
