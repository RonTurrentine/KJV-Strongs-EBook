רָבַע

Transliterationrâbaʻ
Phoneticraw-bah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to squat or lie out flat, i.e. (specifically) in copulation

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

let gender, lie down.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רבע

Transliteration: râba‛

Phonetic: raw-bah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to lie stretched out, lie down
    1. (Qal)
      1. to lie down
        1. for repose
        2. for copulation (of woman with beast)
    2. (Hiphil) to cause to lay down (of cattle breeding)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2108

Part(s) of speech: Verb
