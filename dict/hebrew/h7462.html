רָעָה

Transliterationrâʻâh
Phoneticraw-aw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to tend a flock; i.e. pasture it; intransitively, to graze (literally or figuratively); generally to rule; by extension, to associate with (as a friend)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] break, companion, keep company with, devour, eat up, evil entreat, feed, use as a friend, make friendship with, herdman, keep (sheep) (-er), pastor, [phrase] shearing house, shepherd, wander, waste.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רעה

Transliteration: râ‛âh

Phonetic: raw-aw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to pasture, tend, graze, feed
    1. (Qal)
      1. to tend, pasture
        1. to shepherd
        2. of ruler, teacher (figuratively)
        3. of people as flock (figuratively)
        4. shepherd, herdsman (substantive)
      2. to feed, graze
        1. of cows, sheep etc (literal)
        2. of idolater, Israel as flock (figuratively)
    2. (Hiphil) shepherd, shepherdess
  2. to associate with, be a friend of (meaning probable)
    1. (Qal) to associate with
    2. (Hithpael) to be companions
  3. (Piel) to be a special friend

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2185,2186

Part(s) of speech: Verb
