סוּג

Transliterationçûwg
Phoneticsoog
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to flinch, i.e. (by implication) to go back, literally (to retreat) or figuratively (to apostatize)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

backslider, drive, go back, turn (away, back).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: סוּג

Transliteration: sûg

Phonetic: soog

BDB Definition:

  1. to move, go, turn back, move away, backslide
    1. (Qal) to backslide, prove recreant to
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to turn oneself away, turn back
      2. to be turned or driven back, be repulsed

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1469

Part(s) of speech: Verb
