שָׁעַע

Transliterationshâʻaʻ
Phoneticshaw-ah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

(in a good acceptation) to look upon (with complacency), i.e. fondle, please or amuse (self); (in a bad one) to look about (in dismay), i.e. stare

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cry (out) (by confusion with ), dandle, delight (self), play, shut.
