חָתַן

Transliterationchâthan
Phonetickhaw-than'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to give (a daughter) away in marriage; hence (generally) to contract affinity by marriage

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

join in affinity, father in law, make marriages, mother in law, son in law.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חתן

Transliteration: châthan

Phonetic: khaw-than'

BDB Definition:

  1. to become a son-in-law, make oneself a daughter's husband
    1. (Qal) wife's father, wife's mother, father-in-law, mother-in-law (participle)
    2. (Hithpael) to make oneself a daughter's husband

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 781b

Part(s) of speech: Verb
