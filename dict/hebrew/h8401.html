תֶּבֶן

Transliterationteben
Phoneticteh'-ben
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, material, i.e. (specifically) refuse haum or stalks of grain (as chopped in threshing and used for fodder)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

chaff, straw, stubble.
