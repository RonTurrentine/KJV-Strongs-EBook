נָבָא

Transliterationnâbâʼ
Phoneticnaw-baw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to prophesy, i.e. speak (or sing) by inspiration (in prediction or simple discourse)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

prophesy(-ing), make self a prophet.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נבא

Transliteration: nâbâ'

Phonetic: naw-baw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to prophesy
    1. (Niphal)
      1. to prophesy
        1. under influence of divine spirit
        2. of false prophets
    2. (Hithpael)
      1. to prophesy
        1. under influence of divine spirit
        2. of false prophets
