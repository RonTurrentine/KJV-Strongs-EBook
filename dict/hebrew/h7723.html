שָׁוְא

Transliterationshâvᵉʼ
Phoneticshawv
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

evil (as destructive), literally (ruin) or morally (especially guile); figuratively idolatry (as false, subjective), uselessness (as deceptive, objective; also adverbially, in vain)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

false(-ly), lie, lying, vain, vanity.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁו שׁוא o

Transliteration: shâv' shav

Phonetic: shawv

BDB Definition:

  1. emptiness, vanity, falsehood
    1. emptiness, nothingness, vanity
    2. emptiness of speech, lying
    3. worthlessness (of conduct)

Origin: from the same as H7722 in the sense of desolating

TWOT entry: 2338a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
