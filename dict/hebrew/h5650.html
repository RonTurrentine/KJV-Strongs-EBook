עֶבֶד

Transliterationʻebed
Phoneticeh'-bed
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a servant

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] bondage, bondman, (bond-) servant, (man-) servant.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: עבד

Transliteration: ‛ebed

Phonetic: eh'-bed

BDB Definition:

  1. slave, servant
    1. slave, servant, man-servant
    2. subjects
    3. servants, worshippers (of God)
    4. servant (in special sense as prophets, Levites etc)
    5. servant (of Israel)
    6. servant (as form of address between equals)

Origin: from H5647

TWOT entry: 1553a
