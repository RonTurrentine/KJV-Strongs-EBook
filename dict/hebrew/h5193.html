נָטַע

Transliterationnâṭaʻ
Phoneticnaw-tah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to strike in, i.e. fix; specifically, to plant (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fastened, plant(-er).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נטע

Transliteration: nâṭa‛

Phonetic: naw-tah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to plant, fasten, fix, establish
    1. (Qal)
      1. to plant
      2. to plant, establish (figuratively)
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be planted
      2. to be established (figuratively)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1354
