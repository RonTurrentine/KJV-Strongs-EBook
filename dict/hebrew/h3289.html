יָעַץ

Transliterationyâʻats
Phoneticyaw-ats'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to advise; reflexively, to deliberate or resolve

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

advertise, take advise, advise (well), consult, (give, take) counsel(-lor), determine, devise, guide, purpose.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יעץ

Transliteration: yâ‛ats

Phonetic: yaw-ats'

BDB Definition:

  1. to advise, consult, give counsel, counsel, purpose, devise, plan
    1. (Qal)
      1. to advise, counsel, give counsel, consult
      2. counsellor (participle)
    2. (Niphal) to consult together, exchange counsel, deliberate, counsel together
    3. (Hithpael) to conspire

Origin: a primitive root
