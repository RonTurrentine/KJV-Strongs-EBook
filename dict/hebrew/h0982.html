בָּטַח

Transliterationbâṭach
Phoneticbaw-takh'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

figuratively, to trust, be confident or sure

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be bold (confident, secure, sure), careless (one, woman), put confidence, (make to) hope, (put, make to) trust.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּטח

Transliteration: bâṭach

Phonetic: baw-takh'

BDB Definition:

  1. to trust
    1. (Qal)
      1. to trust, trust in
      2. to have confidence, be confident
      3. to be bold
      4. to be secure
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to cause to trust, make secure
  2. (TWOT) to feel safe, be careless

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 233

Part(s) of speech: Verb
