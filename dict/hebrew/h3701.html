כֶּסֶף

Transliterationkeçeph
Phonetickeh'-sef
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

silver (from its pale color); by implication, money

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

money, price, silver(-ling).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּסף

Transliteration: keseph

Phonetic: keh'-sef

BDB Definition:

  1. silver, money
    1. silver
      1. as metal
      2. as ornament
      3. as colour
    2. money, shekels, talents

Origin: from H3700

TWOT entry: 1015a
