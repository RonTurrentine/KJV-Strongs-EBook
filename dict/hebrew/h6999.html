קָטַר

Transliterationqâṭar
Phonetickaw-tar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to smoke, i.e. turn into fragrance by fire (especially as an act of worship)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

burn (incense, sacrifice) (upon), (altar for) incense, kindle, offer (incense, a sacrifice).
