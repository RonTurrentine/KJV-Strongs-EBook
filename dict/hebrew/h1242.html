בֹּקֶר

Transliterationbôqer
Phoneticbo'-ker
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, dawn (as the break of day); generally, morning

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

([phrase]) day, early, morning, morrow.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּקר

Transliteration: bôqer

Phonetic: bo'-ker

BDB Definition:

  1. morning, break of day
    1. morning
      1. of end of night
      2. of coming of daylight
      3. of coming of sunrise
      4. of beginning of day
      5. of bright joy after night of distress (figuratively)
    2. morrow, next day, next morning

Origin: from H1239

TWOT entry: 274c

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
