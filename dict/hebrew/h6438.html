פִּנָּה

Transliterationpinnâh
Phoneticpin-naw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

an angle; by implication, a pinnacle; figuratively, a chieftain

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bulwark, chief, corner, stay, tower.
