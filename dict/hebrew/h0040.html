Definition

Abimelek, the name of two Philistine kings and of two Israelites

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Abimelech.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אבימלך

Transliteration: 'ăbı̂ymelek

Phonetic: ab-ee-mel'-ek

BDB Definition: Abimelech = " Melek is father" or " my father is king"

  1. king of Gerar in Abraham's time
  2. king of Gath in David's time; maybe title of Philistine kings
  3. son of Gideon by a concubine
  4. priest, son of Abiathar

Origin: from H0001 and H4428

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
