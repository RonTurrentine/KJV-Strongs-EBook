כָּסָה

Transliterationkâçâh
Phonetickaw-saw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to plump, i.e. fill up hollows; by implication, to cover (for clothing or secrecy)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

clad self, close, clothe, conceal, cover (self), (flee to) hide, overwhelm. Compare .

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּסה

Transliteration: kâsâh

Phonetic: kaw-saw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to cover, conceal, hide
    1. (Qal) conceal, covered (participle)
    2. (Niphal) to be covered
    3. (Piel)
      1. to cover, clothe
      2. to cover, conceal
      3. to cover (for protection)
      4. to cover over, spread over
      5. to cover, overwhelm
    4. (Pual)
      1. to be covered
      2. to be clothed
    5. (Hithpael) to cover oneself, clothe oneself

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1008

Part(s) of speech: Verb
