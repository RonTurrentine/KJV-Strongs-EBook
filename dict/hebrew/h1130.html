TransliterationBen-Hădad

Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Ben-Hadad, the name of several Syrian kings

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Benhadad.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּן־הדד

Transliteration: ben-hădad

Phonetic: ben-had-ad'

BDB Definition: Ben-hadad = " son of [the false god] Hadad"

  1. the king of Syria, contemporary with Asa of Judah
  2. the son of Hazael, also king of Syria

Origin: from H1121 and H1908

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
