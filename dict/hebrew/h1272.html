בָּרַח

Transliterationbârach
Phoneticbaw-rakh'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to bolt, i.e. figuratively, to flee suddenly

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

chase (away); drive away, fain, flee (away), put to flight, make haste, reach, run away, shoot.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּרח

Transliteration: bârach

Phonetic: baw-rakh'

BDB Definition:

  1. to go through, flee, run away, chase, drive away, put to flight, reach, shoot (extend), hurry away
    1. (Qal)
      1. to go, pass through
      2. to flee
      3. to hasten, come quickly
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to pass through
      2. to cause to flee, put to flight
      3. to drive away

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 284

Part(s) of speech: Verb
