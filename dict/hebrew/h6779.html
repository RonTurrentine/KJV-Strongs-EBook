צָמַח

Transliterationtsâmach
Phonetictsaw-makh'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to sprout (transitive or intransitive, literal or figurative)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bear, bring forth, (cause to, make to) bud (forth), (cause to, make to) grow (again, up), (cause to) spring (forth, up).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: צמח

Transliteration: tsâmach

Phonetic: tsaw-makh'

BDB Definition:

  1. to sprout, spring up, grow up
    1. (Qal)
      1. to sprout, spring up
        1. of plants
        2. of hair
        3. of speech (figuratively)
    2. (Piel) to grow abundantly or thickly
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to cause to grow
      2. to cause to sprout

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1928

Part(s) of speech: Verb
