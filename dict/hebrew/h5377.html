נָשָׁא

Transliterationnâshâʼ
Phoneticnaw-shaw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to lead astray, i.e. (mentally) to delude, or (morally) to seduce

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beguile, deceive, [idiom] greatly, [idiom] utterly.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נשׁא

Transliteration: nâshâ'

Phonetic: naw-shaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to beguile, deceive
    1. (Niphal) to be beguiled
    2. (Hiphil) to beguile, deceive
    3. (Qal) utterly (infinitif)

Origin: a primitive root
