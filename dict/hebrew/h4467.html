מַמְלָכָה

Transliterationmamlâkâh
Phoneticmam-law-kaw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

dominion, i.e. (abstractly) the estate (rule) or (concretely) the country (realm)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

kingdom, king's, reign, royal.
