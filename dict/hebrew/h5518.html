סִיר

Transliterationçîyr
Phoneticseer
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a pot; also a thorn (as springing up rapidly); by implication, a hook

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

caldron, fishhook, pan, (wash-)pot, thorn.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: סרה סירה סיר

Transliteration: sı̂yr sı̂yrâh sirâh

Phonetic: seer

BDB Definition:

  1. pot
    1. pot (household utensil)
    2. pot (of temple)
  2. thorn, hook, brier
    1. thorns
    2. hooks

Origin: from a primitive root meaning to boil up

TWOT entry: 1489,1490

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
