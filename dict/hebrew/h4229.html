מָחָה

Transliterationmâchâh
Phoneticmaw-khaw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to stroke or rub; by implication, to erase; also to smooth (as if with oil), i.e. grease or make fat; also to touch, i.e. reach to

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

abolish, blot out, destroy, full of marrow, put out, reach unto, [idiom] utterly, wipe (away, out).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מחה

Transliteration: mâchâh

Phonetic: maw-khaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to wipe, wipe out
    1. (Qal)
      1. to wipe
      2. to blot out, obliterate
      3. to blot out, exterminate
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be wiped out
      2. to be blotted out
      3. to be exterminated
    3. (Hiphil) to blot out (from memory)
  2. (Qal) to strike
  3. (Pual) full of marrow (participle)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1178,1179,1181c

Part(s) of speech: Verb
