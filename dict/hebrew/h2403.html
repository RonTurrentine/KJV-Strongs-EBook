חַטָּאָה

Transliterationchaṭṭâʼâh
Phonetickhat-taw-aw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

an offence (sometimes habitual sinfulness), and its penalty, occasion, sacrifice, or expiation; also (concretely) an offender

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

punishment (of sin), purifying(-fication for sin), sin(-ner, offering).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חטּאת חטּאה

Transliteration: chaṭṭâ'âh chaṭṭâ'th

Phonetic: khat-taw-aw'

BDB Definition:

  1. sin, sinful
  2. sin, sin offering
    1. sin
    2. condition of sin, guilt of sin
    3. punishment for sin
    4. sin-offering
    5. purification from sins of ceremonial uncleanness

Origin: from H2398

TWOT entry: 638e
