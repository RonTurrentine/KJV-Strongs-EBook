גָּדַע

Transliterationgâdaʻ
Phoneticgaw-dah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to fell a tree; generally, to destroy anything

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cut (asunder, in sunder, down, off), hew down.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: גּדע

Transliteration: gâda‛

Phonetic: gaw-dah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to cut, hew, chop, cut down, hew down, hew off, cut off, cut in two, shave off
    1. (Qal) to hew, chop in two
    2. (Niphal) to be chopped off, be hewn off
    3. (Piel) to cut off or down in two, hew off or down in two
    4. (Pual) to chop down, hew down

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 316

Part(s) of speech: Verb
