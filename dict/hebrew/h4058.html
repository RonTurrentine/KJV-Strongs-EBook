מָדַד

Transliterationmâdad
Phoneticmaw-dad'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to stretch; by implication, to measure (as if by stretching a line); figuratively, to be extended

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

measure, mete, stretch self.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מדד

Transliteration: mâdad

Phonetic: maw-dad'

BDB Definition:

  1. to measure, stretch
    1. (Qal) to measure
    2. (Niphal) to be measured
    3. (Piel)
      1. to extend, continue
      2. to measure, measure off
    4. (Po) measured
    5. (Hithpolel) to extend oneself, stretch oneself

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1146

Part(s) of speech: Verb
