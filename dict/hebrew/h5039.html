נְבָלָה

Transliterationnᵉbâlâh
Phoneticneb-aw-law'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

foolishness, i.e. (morally) wickedness; concretely, a crime; by extension, punishment

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

folly, vile, villany.
