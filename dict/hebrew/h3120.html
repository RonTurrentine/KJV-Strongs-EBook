יָוָן

TransliterationYâvân
Phoneticyaw-vawn'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine / Noun Proper Location

Definition

Javan, the name of a son of Joktan, and of the race (Ionians, i.e. Greeks) descended from him, with their territory; also of a place in Arabia

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Javan.
