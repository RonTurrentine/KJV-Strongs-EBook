אַחַר

Transliterationʼachar
Phoneticakh-ar'
Part of SpeechAdverb

Definition

properly, the hind part; generally used as an adverb or conjunction, after (in various senses)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

after (that, -ward), again, at, away from, back (from, -side), behind, beside, by, follow (after, -ing), forasmuch, from, hereafter, hinder end, [phrase] out (over) live, [phrase] persecute, posterity, pursuing, remnant, seeing, since, thence(-forth), when, with.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אחר

Transliteration: 'achar

Phonetic: akh-ar'

BDB Definition:

  1. after the following part, behind (of place), hinder, afterwards (of time)
    1. as an adverb
      1. behind (of place)
      2. afterwards (of time)
    2. as a preposition
      1. behind, after (of place)
      2. after (of time)
      3. besides
    3. as a conjunction
    4. after that
    5. as a substantive
      1. hinder part
    6. with other prepositions
      1. from behind
      2. from following after

Origin: from H0309
