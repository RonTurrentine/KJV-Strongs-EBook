בָּחַר

Transliterationbâchar
Phoneticbaw-khar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to try, i.e. (by implication) select

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

acceptable, appoint, choose (choice), excellent, join, be rather, require.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּחר

Transliteration: bâchar

Phonetic: baw-khar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to choose, elect, decide for
    1. (Qal) to choose
    2. (Niphal) to be chosen
    3. (Pual) to be chosen, selected

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 231

Part(s) of speech: Verb
