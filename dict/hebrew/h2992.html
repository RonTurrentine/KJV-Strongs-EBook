יָבַם

Transliterationyâbam
Phoneticyaw-bam'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to marry a (deceased) brother's widow

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

perform the duty of a husband's brother, marry.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: יבם

Transliteration: yâbam

Phonetic: yaw-bam'

BDB Definition:

  1. (Piel) to perform levirate marriage, perform the duty of a brother-in-law
    1. the duty due to a brother who died childless - to marry his widow and have a son for his name

Origin: a primitive root of doubtful meaning

TWOT entry: 836

Part(s) of speech: Verb
