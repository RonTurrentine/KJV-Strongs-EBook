קָדַר

Transliterationqâdar
Phonetickaw-dar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be ashy, i.e. darkcolored; by implication, to mourn (in sackcloth or sordid garments)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be black(-ish), be (make) dark(-en), [idiom] heavily, (cause to) mourn.
