מַעַל

Transliterationmaʻal
Phoneticmah'al
Part of SpeechAdverb

Definition

properly, the upper part, used only adverbially with prefix upward, above, overhead, from the top, etc.

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

above, exceeding(-ly), forward, on ([idiom] very) high, over, up(-on, -ward), very.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מעל

Transliteration: ma‛al

Phonetic: mah'al

BDB Definition:

  1. higher part, upper part (substantive)
    1. above (adverb)
    2. on the top of, above, on higher ground than (preposition)
    3. upwards, higher, above (with locative)

Origin: from H5927
