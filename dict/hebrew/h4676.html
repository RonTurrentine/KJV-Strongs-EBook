מַצֵּבָה

Transliterationmatstsêbâh
Phoneticmats-tsay-baw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

something stationed, i.e. a column or (memorial stone); by analogy, an idol

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

garrison, (standing) image, pillar.
