תִּירוֹשׁ

Transliterationtîyrôwsh
Phonetictee-roshe'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

must or fresh grape-juice (as just squeezed out); by implication (rarely) fermented wine

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(new, sweet) wine.
