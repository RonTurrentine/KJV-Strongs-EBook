נָצַח

Transliterationnâtsach
Phoneticnaw-tsakh'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to glitter from afar, i.e. to be eminent (as a superintendent, especially of the Temple services and its music); to be permanent

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

excel, chief musician (singer), oversee(-r), set forward.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נצח

Transliteration: nâtsach

Phonetic: naw-tsakh'

BDB Definition:

  1. to excel, be bright, be preeminent, be perpetual, be overseer, be enduring
    1. (Niphal) enduring (participle)
    2. (Piel) to act as overseer or superintendent or director or chief

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1402

Part(s) of speech: Verb
