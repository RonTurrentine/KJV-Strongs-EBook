רָחַץ

Transliterationrâchats
Phoneticraw-khats'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to lave (the whole or a part of a thing)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bathe (self), wash (self).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רחץ

Transliteration: râchats

Phonetic: raw-khats'

BDB Definition:

  1. to wash, wash off, wash away, bathe
    1. (Qal)
      1. to wash, wash off, wash away
      2. to wash, bathe (oneself)
    2. (Pual) to be washed
    3. (Hithpael) to wash oneself

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2150

Part(s) of speech: Verb
