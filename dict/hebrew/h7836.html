שָׁחַר

Transliterationshâchar
Phoneticshaw-khar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to dawn, i.e. (figuratively) be (up) early at any task (with the implication of earnestness); by extension, to search for (with painstaking)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(do something) betimes, enquire early, rise (seek) betimes, seek diligently) early, in the morning).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁחר

Transliteration: shâchar

Phonetic: shaw-khar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to seek, seek early or earnestly, look early or diligently for
    1. (Qal) to look for diligently, seek
    2. (Piel) to seek, seek early

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2369

Part(s) of speech: Verb
