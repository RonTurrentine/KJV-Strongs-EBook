Transliterationʼishshâh
Phoneticish-shaw'

Definition

a woman

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(adulter) ess, each, every, female, [idiom] many, [phrase] none, one, [phrase] together, wife, woman. Often unexpressed in English.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נשׁים אשּׁה

Transliteration: 'ishshâh nâshı̂ym

Phonetic: ish-shaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. woman, wife, female
    1. woman (opposite of man)
    2. wife (woman married to a man)
    3. female (of animals)
    4. each, every (pronoun)

Origin: from H0376 or H0582

TWOT entry: 137a
