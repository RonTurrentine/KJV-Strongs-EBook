מוּג

Transliterationmûwg
Phoneticmoog
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to melt, i.e. literally (to soften, flow down, disappear), or figuratively (to fear, faint)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

consume, dissolve, (be) faint(-hearted), melt (away), make soft.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מוּג

Transliteration: mûg

Phonetic: moog

BDB Definition:

  1. to melt, cause to melt
    1. (Qal)
      1. to melt, faint
      2. to cause to melt
    2. (Niphal) to melt away
    3. (Polel) to soften, dissolve, dissipate
    4. (Hithpolel) to melt, flow

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1156

Part(s) of speech: Verb
