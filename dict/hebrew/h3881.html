לֵוִיִּי

TransliterationLêvîyîy
Phoneticlay-vee-ee'
Part of SpeechAdjective

Definition

a Levite or descendant of Levi

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Leviite.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: לוי לויּי

Transliteration: lêvı̂yı̂y lêvı̂y

Phonetic: lay-vee-ee'

BDB Definition: Levite = see Levi " joined to"

  1. the descendants of Levi, the 3rd son of Jacob by Leah
    1. the tribe descended from Levi specially set aside by God for His service

Origin: patronymically from H3878

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
