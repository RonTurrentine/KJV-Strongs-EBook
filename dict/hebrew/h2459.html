חֶלֶב

Transliterationcheleb
Phonetickheh'-leb
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

fat, whether literally or figuratively; hence, the richest or choice part

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] best, fat(-ness), [idiom] finest, grease, marrow.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חלב חלב

Transliteration: cheleb chêleb

Phonetic: kheh'-leb

BDB Definition:

  1. fat
    1. fat (of humans)
    2. fat (of beasts)
    3. choicest, best part, abundance (of products of the land)

Origin: from an unused root meaning to be fat

TWOT entry: 651a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
