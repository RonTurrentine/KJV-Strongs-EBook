גַב

Transliterationgab
Phoneticgab
Part of SpeechNoun

Definition

the back (as rounded); by analogy, the top or rim, a boss, a vault, arch of eye, bulwarks, etc.

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

back, body, boss, eminent (higher) place, (eye) brows, nave, ring.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: גב

Transliteration: gab

Phonetic: gab

BDB Definition:

  1. convex surface, back
    1. back (of man)
    2. mound (for illicit worship)
    3. boss (convex projection of shield)
    4. bulwarks, breastworks (of arguments - figuratively)
    5. brow, eyebrow
    6. rim (of wheel)

Origin: from an unused root

TWOT entry: 303a

Part(s) of speech: Noun
