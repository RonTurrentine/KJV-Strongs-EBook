פָּרַע

Transliterationpâraʻ
Phoneticpaw-rah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to loosen; by implication, to expose, dismiss; figuratively, absolve, begin

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

avenge, avoid, bare, go back, let, (make) naked, set at nought, perish, refuse, uncover.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: פּרע

Transliteration: pâra‛

Phonetic: paw-rah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to lead, act as leader
  2. to let go, let loose, ignore, let alone
    1. (Qal)
      1. to let go, let loose
      2. to let alone, avoid, neglect
      3. to loosen
    2. (Niphal) to be let loose, be loosened of restraint
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to cause to refrain
      2. to show lack of restraint
      3. to let loose restraints

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1822,1823,1824

Part(s) of speech: Verb
