דָּכָא

Transliterationdâkâʼ
Phoneticdaw-kaw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to crumble; transitively, to bruise (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beat to pieces, break (in pieces), bruise, contrite, crush, destroy, humble, oppress, smite.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: דּכא

Transliteration: dâkâ'

Phonetic: daw-kaw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to crush, be crushed, be contrite, be broken
    1. (Niphal)
      1. to be crushed
      2. to be contrite (figuratively)
    2. (Piel) to crush
    3. (Pual)
      1. to be crushed, be shattered
      2. to be made contrite
    4. (Hithpael) to allow oneself to be crushed

Origin: a primitive root (compare H1794)

TWOT entry: 427

Part(s) of speech: Verb
