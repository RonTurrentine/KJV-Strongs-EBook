מָרוֹם

Transliterationmârôwm
Phoneticmaw-rome'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

altitude, i.e. concretely (an elevated place), abstractly (elevation, figuratively (elation), or adverbially (aloft)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(far) above, dignity, haughty, height, (most, on) high (one, place), loftily, upward.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מרום

Transliteration: mârôm

Phonetic: maw-rome'

BDB Definition:

  1. height
    1. height, elevation, elevated place
      1. in a high place (adverb)
    2. height
    3. proudly (adverb)
    4. of nobles (figuratively)
