תְּמוֹל

Transliterationtᵉmôwl
Phonetictem-ole'
Part of SpeechAdverb

Definition

properly, ago, i.e. a (short or long) time since; especially yesterday, or day before yesterday

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[phrase] before (-time), [phrase] these (three) days, [phrase] heretofore, [phrase] time past, yesterday.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: תּמל תּמול

Transliteration: temôl temôl

Phonetic: tem-ole'

BDB Definition:

  1. before, before that time, beforetime, heretofore, of late, of old, these days, time(s) past, yesterday, day before yesterday, recently, formerly
    1. yesterday
      1. recently, formerly (usual usage) (figuratively)
    2. as formerly, so more recently
    3. from yesterday, already

Origin: probably for H0865
