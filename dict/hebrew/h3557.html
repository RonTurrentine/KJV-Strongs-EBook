כּוּל

Transliterationkûwl
Phonetickool
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to keep in; hence, to measure; figuratively, to maintain (in various senses)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(be able to, can) abide, bear, comprehend, contain, feed, forbearing, guide, hold(-ing in), nourish(-er), be present, make provision, receive, sustain, provide sustenance (victuals).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּוּל

Transliteration: kûl

Phonetic: kool

BDB Definition:

  1. to seize, contain, measure
    1. (Qal) to measure, calculate
    2. (Pilpel) to sustain, maintain, contain
      1. to sustain, support, nourish
      2. to contain, hold in, restrain
      3. to support, endure
    3. (Polpal) to be supplied
    4. (Hiphil) to contain, hold, hold in, endure
      1. to contain
      2. to sustain, endure

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 962

Part(s) of speech: Verb
