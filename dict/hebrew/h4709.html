מִצְפָּה

TransliterationMitspâh
Phoneticmits-paw'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Feminine

Definition

Mitspah, the name of two places in Palestine

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Mitspah. (This seems rather to be only an orthographic variation of when 'in pause'.)

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מצפּה

Transliteration: mitspâh

Phonetic: mits-paw'

BDB Definition: Mizpah = " watchtower"

  1. a place in Gilead north of Jabbok and location of Laban's cairn
  2. a place in Gilead south of Jabbok; site unknown
  3. a place near Mount Hermon
  4. an old sacred place in Benjamin

Origin: from H4708

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Feminine
