בָּלַל

Transliterationbâlal
Phonetic baw-lal'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to overflow (specifically with oil.); by implication, to mix; to fodder

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

anoint, confound, [idiom] fade, mingle, mix (self), give provender, temper.
