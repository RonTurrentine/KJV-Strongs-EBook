כָּנַע

Transliterationkânaʻ
Phonetickaw-nah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to bend the knee; hence, to humiliate, vanquish

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bring down (low), into subjection, under, humble (self), subdue.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּנע

Transliteration: kâna‛

Phonetic: kaw-nah'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be humble, be humbled, be subdued, be brought down, be low, be under, be brought into subjection
    1. (Niphal)
      1. to humble oneself
      2. to be humbled, be subdued
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to humble
      2. to subdue

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1001

Part(s) of speech: Verb
