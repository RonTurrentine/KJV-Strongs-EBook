כְּפוֹר

Transliterationkᵉphôwr
Phonetic kef-ore'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, a cover, i.e. (by implication) a tankard (or covered goblet); also white frost (as covering the ground)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bason, hoar(-y) frost.
