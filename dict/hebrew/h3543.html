כָּהָה

Transliterationkâhâh
Phonetickaw-haw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be weak, i.e. (figuratively) to despond (causatively, rebuke), or (of light, the eye) to grow dull

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

darken, be dim, fail, faint, restrain, [idiom] utterly.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּהה

Transliteration: kâhâh

Phonetic: kaw-haw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to grow weak, grow dim, grow faint, falter, be weak, be dim, be darkened, be restrained, be faint, fail
    1. (Qal) to grow dim, grow faint
    2. (Piel) to faint, grow weak, grow faint

Origin: a primitive root
