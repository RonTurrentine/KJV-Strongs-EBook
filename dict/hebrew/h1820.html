דָּמָה

Transliterationdâmâh
Phoneticdaw-mam'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be dumb or silent; hence, to fail or perish; trans. to destroy

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cease, be cut down (off), destroy, be brought to silence, be undone, [idiom] utterly.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: דּמה

Transliteration: dâmâh

Phonetic: daw-mam'

BDB Definition:

  1. to cease, cause to cease, cut off, destroy, perish
    1. (Qal)
      1. to cease
      2. to cause to cease, destroy
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be cut off
      2. to be undone, be cut off at sight of the theophany

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 438

Part(s) of speech: Verb
