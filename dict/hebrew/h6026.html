עָנַג

Transliterationʻânag
Phoneticaw-nag'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be soft or pliable, i.e. (figuratively) effeminate or luxurious

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

delicate(-ness), (have) delight (self), sport self.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ענג

Transliteration: ‛ânag

Phonetic: aw-nag'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be soft, be delicate, be dainty
    1. (Pual) to be delicate
    2. (Hithpael)
      1. to be of dainty habit, be pampered
      2. to be happy about, take exquisite delight
      3. to make merry over, make sport of

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1648

Part(s) of speech: Verb
