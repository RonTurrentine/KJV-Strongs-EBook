נָבַט

Transliterationnâbaṭ
Phoneticnaw-bat'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to scan, i.e. look intently at; by implication, to regard with pleasure, favor or care

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(cause to) behold, consider, look (down), regard, have respect, see.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נבט

Transliteration: nâbaṭ

Phonetic: naw-bat'

BDB Definition:

  1. to look, regard
    1. (Piel) to look
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to look
      2. to regard, show regard to, pay attention to, consider
      3. to look upon, regard, show regard to
