אֵת

Transliterationʼêth
Phoneticayth
Part of SpeechPreposition

Definition

properly, nearness (used only as a preposition or an adverb), near; hence, generally, with, by, at, among, etc.

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

against, among, before, by, for, from, in(-to), (out) of, with. Often with another prepositional prefix.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: את

Transliteration: 'êth

Phonetic: ayth

BDB Definition:

  1. with, near, together with
    1. with, together with
    2. with (of relationship)
    3. near (of place)
    4. with (poss.)
    5. from...with, from (with other preposition)

Origin: probably from H0579
