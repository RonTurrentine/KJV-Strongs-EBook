תַּעָב

Transliterationtaʻâb
Phonetictaw-ab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to loathe, i.e. (morally) detest

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(make to be) abhor(-red), (be, commit more, do) abominable(-y), [idiom] utterly.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: תּעב

Transliteration: tâ‛ab

Phonetic: taw-ab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to abhor, be abominable, do abominably
    1. (Niphal) to be abhorred, be detested
      1. in the ritual sense
      2. in the ethical sense
    2. (Piel)
      1. to loathe, abhor, regard as an abomination
        1. in the ritual sense
        2. in the ethical sense
      2. to cause to be an abomination
    3. (Hiphil) to make abominable, do abominably
      1. in the ritual sense
      2. in the ethical sense

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2530

Part(s) of speech: Verb
