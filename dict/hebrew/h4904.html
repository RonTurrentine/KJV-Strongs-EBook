מִשְׁכָּב

Transliterationmishkâb
Phoneticmish-kawb'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a bed (figuratively, a bier); abstractly, sleep; by euphemism, carnal intercourse

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bed(-chamber), couch, lieth (lying) with.

BDB / Thayer Definition

BDB Definition:

  1. a lying down, couch, bier, act of lying
    1. couch, bed
    2. act of lying, lying down or sleeping room, bedroom
    3. lying down (for sexual contact)
