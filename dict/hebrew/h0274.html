אֲחַזְיָה

TransliterationʼĂchazyâh
Phoneticakh-az-yaw'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Achazjah, the name of a Jewish and an Israelite king

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Ahaziah.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אחזיהוּ אחזיה

Transliteration: 'ăchazyâh 'ăchazyâhû

Phonetic: akh-az-yaw'

BDB Definition: Ahaziah = " Jehovah (Yahu) holds (possesses)"

  1. ruler of Israel, son of Ahab
  2. ruler of Judah, son of Jehoram (Joram)

Origin: from H0270 and H3050

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
