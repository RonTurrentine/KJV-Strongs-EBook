אָשַׁם

Transliterationʼâsham
Phoneticaw-sham'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be guilty; by implication to be punished or perish

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] certainly, be(-come, made) desolate, destroy, [idiom] greatly, be(-come, found, hold) guilty, offend (acknowledge offence), trespass.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אשׁם אשׁם

Transliteration: 'âsham 'âshêm

Phonetic: aw-sham'

BDB Definition:

  1. to offend, be guilty, trespass
    1. (Qal)
      1. to do wrong, offend, trespass, commit an offense, do injury
      2. to be or become guilty
      3. to be held guilty
      4. to be incriminated
    2. (Niphal) to suffer punishment
    3. (Hiphil) to declare guilty
  2. (TWOT) to be desolate, acknowledge offense

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 180

Part(s) of speech: Verb
