פּוּחַ

Transliterationpûwach
Phoneticpoo'akh
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to puff, i.e. blow with the breath or air; hence, to fan (as a breeze), to utter, to kindle (a fire), to scoff

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

blow (upon), break, puff, bring into a snare, speak, utter.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: פּוּח

Transliteration: pûach

Phonetic: poo'akh

BDB Definition:

  1. to breathe, blow
    1. (Qal) to breathe
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to cause to exhale or breathe
      2. to puff, snort
      3. to excite, inflame
      4. to puff, pant for it
      5. to breathe out, utter
      6. to blow, blast

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1741

Part(s) of speech: Verb
