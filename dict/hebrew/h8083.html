שְׁמֹנֶה

Transliterationshᵉmôneh
Phoneticshem-o-neh'
Part of SpeechNoun

Definition

a cardinal number, eight (as if a surplus above the 'perfect' seven); also (as ordinal) eighth

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

eight(-een, -eenth), eighth.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁמונה שׁמנה שׁמונה שׁמנה

Transliteration: shemôneh shemôneh shemônâh shemônâh

Phonetic: shem-o-neh'

BDB Definition:

  1. eight, eighth
    1. eight (as cardinal number)
    2. eighth (as ordinal number)
    3. in combination with other numbers

Origin: apparently from H8082 through the idea of plumpness

TWOT entry: 2411a

Part(s) of speech: Noun
