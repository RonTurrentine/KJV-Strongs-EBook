צָפַן

Transliterationtsâphan
Phonetictsaw-fan'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to hide (by covering over); by implication, to hoard or reserve; figuratively to deny; specifically (favorably) to protect, (unfavorably) to lurk

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

esteem, hide(-den one, self), lay up, lurk (be set) privily, (keep) secret(-ly, place).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: צפן

Transliteration: tsâphan

Phonetic: tsaw-fan'

BDB Definition:

  1. to hide, treasure, treasure or store up
    1. (Qal)
      1. to hide, treasure, treasure up
      2. to lie hidden, lurk
    2. (Niphal) to be hidden, be stored up
    3. (Hiphil) to hide, hide from discovery

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1953

Part(s) of speech: Verb
