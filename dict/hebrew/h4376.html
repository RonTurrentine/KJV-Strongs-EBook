מָכַר

Transliterationmâkar
Phoneticmaw-kar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to sell, literally (as merchandise, a daughter in marriage, into slavery), or figuratively (to surrender)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] at all, sell (away, -er, self).
