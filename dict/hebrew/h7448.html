Transliterationreçen
Phoneticreh'-sen
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bridle.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רסן

Transliteration: resen

Phonetic: reh'-sen

BDB Definition:

  1. something that restrains, halter, jaw
    1. halter
      1. of restraint thrown off (figuratively)
    2. jaw (of crocodile)

Origin: from an unused root meaning to curb
