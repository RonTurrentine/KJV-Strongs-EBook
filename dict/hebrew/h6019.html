BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: עמרם

Transliteration: ‛amrâm

Phonetic: am-rawm'

BDB Definition: Amram = " exalted people"

  1. a descendant of Kohath and Levi and father of Moses
  2. one of the sons of Bani who had a foreign wife in the time of Ezra

Origin: probably from H5971 and H7311

TWOT entry: None
