מַרְאֶה

Transliterationmarʼeh
Phoneticmar-eh'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a view (the act of seeing); also an appearance (the thing seen), whether (real) a shape (especially if handsome, comeliness; often plural the looks), or (mental) a vision

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] apparently, appearance(-reth), [idiom] as soon as beautiful(-ly), countenance, fair, favoured, form, goodly, to look (up) on (to), look(-eth), pattern, to see, seem, sight, visage, vision.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מראה

Transliteration: mar'eh

Phonetic: mar-eh'

BDB Definition:

  1. sight, appearance, vision
    1. sight, phenomenon, spectacle, appearance, vision
    2. what is seen
    3. a vision (supernatural)
    4. sight, vision (power of seeing)
