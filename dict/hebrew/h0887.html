בָּאַשׁ

Transliterationbâʼash
Phoneticbaw-ash'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to smell bad; figuratively, to be offensive morally

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(make to) be abhorred (had in abomination, loathsome, odious), (cause a, make to) stink(-ing savour), [idiom] utterly.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּאשׁ

Transliteration: bâ'ash

Phonetic: baw-ash'

BDB Definition:

  1. to have a bad smell, stink, smell bad
    1. (Qal) to stink, smell bad
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to become odious
      2. to make oneself odious
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to stink, emit a stinking odour
      2. to cause to stink
      3. of wickedness (figuratively)
    4. (Hithpael) to make oneself odious
  2. (TWOT) to abhor

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 195

Part(s) of speech: Verb
