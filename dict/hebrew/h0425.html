TransliterationʼÊlâh
Phoneticay-law'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper

Definition

Elah, the name of an Edomite, of four Israelites, and also of a place in Palestine

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Elah.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אלה

Transliteration: 'êlâh

Phonetic: ay-law'

BDB Definition: Elah = " An oak"

  1. an Edomite chief
  2. a king of Israel for two years, son of Baasha
  3. the father of king Hoshea of Israel
  4. a son of Caleb
  5. son of Uzzi

Origin: the same as H0424
