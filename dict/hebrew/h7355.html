רָחַם

Transliterationrâcham
Phoneticraw-kham'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to fondle; by implication, to love, especially to compassionate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

have compassion (on, upon), love, (find, have, obtain, shew) mercy(-iful, on, upon), (have) pity, Ruhamah, [idiom] surely.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רחם

Transliteration: râcham

Phonetic: raw-kham'

BDB Definition:

  1. to love, love deeply, have mercy, be compassionate, have tender affection, have compassion
    1. (Qal) to love
    2. (Piel)
      1. to have compassion, be compassionate
        1. of God, man
    3. (Pual) to be shown compassion, be compassionate

Origin: a primitive root
