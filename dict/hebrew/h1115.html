בִּלְתִּי

Transliterationbiltîy
Phoneticbil-tee'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper

Definition

properly, a failure of, i.e. (used only as a negative particle, usually with a prepositional prefix) not, except, without, unless, besides, because not, until, etc.

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

because un(satiable), beside, but, [phrase] continual, except, from, lest, neither, no more, none, not, nothing, save, that no, without.
