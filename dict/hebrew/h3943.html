לָפַת

Transliterationlâphath
Phoneticlaw-fath'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to bend, i.e. (by implication) to clasp; also (reflexively) to turn around or aside

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

take hold, turn aside (self).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: לפת

Transliteration: lâphath

Phonetic: law-fath'

BDB Definition:

  1. to twist, grasp, turn, grasp with a twisting motion
    1. (Qal) to grasp
    2. (Niphal) to twist, weave, wind

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1123

Part(s) of speech: Verb
