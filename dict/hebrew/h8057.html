Transliterationsimchâh
Phoneticsim-khaw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

blithesomeness or glee, (religious or festival)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[idiom] exceeding(-ly), gladness, joy(-fulness), mirth, pleasure, rejoice(-ing).
