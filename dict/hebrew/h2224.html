זָרַח

Transliterationzârach
Phoneticzaw-rakh'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to irradiate (or shoot forth beams), i.e. to rise (as the sun); specifically, to appear (as a symptom of leprosy)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

arise, rise (up), as soon as it is up.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: זרח

Transliteration: zârach

Phonetic: zaw-rakh'

BDB Definition:

  1. to rise, come forth, break out, arise, rise up, shine
    1. (Qal)
      1. to rise
      2. to come out, appear

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 580
