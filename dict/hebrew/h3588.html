כִּי

Transliterationkîy
Phonetickee
Part of SpeechConjunction

Definition

(by implication) very widely used as a relative conjunction or adverb (as below); often largely modified by other particles annexed

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

and, + (forasmuch, inasmuch, where-) as, assured(-ly), + but, certainly, doubtless, + else, even, + except, for, how, (because, in, so, than) that, + nevertheless, now, rightly, seeing, since, surely, then, therefore, + (al-) though, + till, truly, + until, when, whether, while, whom, yea, yet.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּי

Transliteration: kı̂y

Phonetic: kee

BDB Definition:

  1. that, for, because, when, as though, as, because that, but, then, certainly, except, surely, since
    1. that
      1. yea, indeed
    2. when (of time)
      1. when, if, though (with a concessive force)
    3. because, since (causal connection)
    4. but (after negative)
    5. that if, for if, indeed if, for though, but if
    6. but rather, but
    7. except that
    8. only, nevertheless
    9. surely
    10. that is
    11. but if
    12. for though
    13. forasmuch as, for therefore

Origin: a primitive particle
