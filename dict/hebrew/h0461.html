BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אליעזר

Transliteration: 'ĕlı̂y‛ezer

Phonetic: el-ee-eh'-zer

BDB Definition: Eliezer = " God is help"

  1. Abraham's Damascene servant
  2. a son of Moses
  3. a Benjamite
  4. a priest who helped move the ark
  5. a Reubenite
  6. a prophet who spoke to Jehoshaphat
  7. a Levite chief
  8. son of Harim
  9. priest with foreign wife

Origin: from H0410 and H5828

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
