שַׂר

Transliterationsar
Phoneticsar
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a head person (of any rank or class)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

captain (that had rule), chief (captain), general, governor, keeper, lord,(-task-)master, prince(-ipal), ruler, steward.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׂר

Transliteration: śar

Phonetic: sar

BDB Definition:

  1. prince, ruler, leader, chief, chieftain, official, captain
    1. chieftain, leader
    2. vassal, noble, official (under king)
    3. captain, general, commander (military)
    4. chief, head, overseer (of other official classes)
    5. heads, princes (of religious office)
    6. elders (of representative leaders of people)
    7. merchant-princes (of rank and dignity)
    8. patron-angel
    9. Ruler of rulers (of God)
    10. warden
