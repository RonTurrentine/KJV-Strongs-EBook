יָדָה

Transliterationyâdâh
Phoneticyaw-daw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

physically, to throw (a stone, an arrow) at or away; especially to revere or worship (with extended hands); intensively, to bemoan (by wringing the hands)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cast (out), (make) confess(-ion), praise, shoot, (give) thank(-ful, -s, -sgiving).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ידה

Transliteration: yâdâh

Phonetic: yaw-daw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to throw, shoot, cast
    1. (Qal) to shoot (arrows)
    2. (Piel) to cast, cast down, throw down
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to give thanks,, laud, praise
      2. to confess, confess (the name of God)
    4. (Hithpael)
      1. to confess (sin)
      2. to give thanks

Origin: a primitive root; used only as denominative from H3027
