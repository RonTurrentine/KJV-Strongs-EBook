שָׁוַע

Transliterationshâvaʻ
Phoneticshaw-vah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to be free; but used only causatively and reflexively, to halloo (for help, i.e. freedom from some trouble)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cry (aloud, out), shout.
