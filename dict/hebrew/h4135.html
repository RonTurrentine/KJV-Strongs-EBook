מוּל

Transliterationmûwl
Phoneticmool
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to cut short, i.e. curtail (specifically the prepuce, i.e. to circumcise); by implication, to blunt; figuratively, to destroy

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

circumcise(-ing), selves), cut down (in pieces), destroy, [idiom] must needs.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מוּל

Transliteration: mûl

Phonetic: mool

BDB Definition:

  1. to circumcise, let oneself be circumcised, cut, be cut off
    1. (Qal) to circumcise
    2. (Niphal) to be circumcised, circumcise oneself
    3. (Hiphil) to cause to be circumcised
      1. of destruction (figuratively)
    4. (Hithpolel) to be cut off
    5. (Polel) cut down

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1161

Part(s) of speech: Verb
