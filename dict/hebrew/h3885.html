לוּן

Transliterationlûwn
Phoneticloon
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to stop (usually over night); by implication, to stay permanently; hence (in a bad sense) to be obstinate (especially in words, to complain)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

abide (all night), continue, dwell, endure, grudge, be left, lie all night, (cause to) lodge (all night, in, -ing, this night), (make to) murmur, remain, tarry (all night, that night).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: לין לוּן

Transliteration: lûn lı̂yn

Phonetic: loon

BDB Definition:

  1. to lodge, stop over, pass the night, abide
    1. (Qal)
      1. to lodge, pass the night
      2. to abide, remain (figuratively)
    2. (Hiphil) to cause to rest or lodge
    3. (Hithpalpel) to dwell, abide
  2. to grumble, complain, murmur
    1. (Niphal) to grumble
    2. (Hiphil) to complain, cause to grumble

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1096,1097

Part(s) of speech: Verb
