שָׁכַם

Transliterationshâkam
Phoneticshaw-kam'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

literally, to load up (on the back of man or beast), i.e. to start early in the morning

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(arise, be up, get (oneself) up, rise up) early (betimes), morning.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁכם

Transliteration: shâkam

Phonetic: shaw-kam'

BDB Definition:

  1. to rise or start early
    1. (Hiphil)
      1. to rise early, make an early start
      2. early (as adverb)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2386

Part(s) of speech: Verb
