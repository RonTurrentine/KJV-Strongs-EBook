Transliterationtôwlâʻ
Phoneticto-law'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

the crimson-grub, but used only (in this connection) of the colorfrom it, and cloths dyed therewith

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

crimson, scarlet, worm.
