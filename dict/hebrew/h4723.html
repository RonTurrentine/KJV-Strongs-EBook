מִקְוֶה

Transliterationmiqveh
Phoneticmik-veh'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine / Noun Proper Masculine

Definition

something waited for, i.e. confidence (objective or subjective); also a collection, i.e. (of water) a pond, or (of men and horses) a caravan or drove

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

abiding, gathering together, hope, linen yarn, plenty (of water), pool.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מּקוא מקוה מקוה o

Transliteration: miqveh miqvêh miqvê'

Phonetic: mik-veh'

BDB Definition:

  1. hope
    1. hope
    2. ground of hope
    3. things hoped for, outcome
  2. collection, collected mass
