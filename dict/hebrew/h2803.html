חָשַׁב

Transliterationchâshab
Phonetickhaw-shab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to plait or interpenetrate, i.e. (literally) to weave or (generally) to fabricate; figuratively, to plot or contrive (usually in a malicious sense); hence (from the mental effort) to think, regard, value, compute

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(make) account (of), conceive, consider, count, cunning (man, work, workman), devise, esteem, find out, forecast, hold, imagine, impute, invent, be like, mean, purpose, reckon(-ing be made), regard, think.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: חשׁב

Transliteration: châshab

Phonetic: khaw-shab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to think, plan, esteem, calculate, invent, make a judgment, imagine, count
    1. (Qal)
      1. to think, account
      2. to plan, devise, mean
      3. to charge, impute, reckon
      4. to esteem, value, regard
      5. to invent
    2. (Niphal)
      1. to be accounted, be thought, be esteemed
      2. to be computed, be reckoned
      3. to be imputed
    3. (Piel)
      1. to think upon, consider, be mindful of
      2. to think to do, devise, plan
      3. to count, reckon
    4. (Hithpael) to be considered

Origin: a primitive root
