לוּץ

Transliterationlûwts
Phoneticloots
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to make mouths at, i.e. to scoff; hence (from the effort to pronounce a foreign language) to interpret, or (generally) intercede

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

ambassador, have in derision, interpreter, make a mock, mocker, scorn(-er, -ful), teacher.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: לוּץ

Transliteration: lûts

Phonetic: loots

BDB Definition:

  1. to scorn, make mouths at, talk arrogantly
    1. (Qal)
      1. to boast
      2. to scorn
    2. (Hiphil)
      1. to mock, deride
      2. to interpret (language)
        1. interpreter (participle)
        2. ambassador (figuratively)
    3. (Hithpalpel) to be inflated, scoff, act as a scorner, show oneself a mocker

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1113

Part(s) of speech: Verb
