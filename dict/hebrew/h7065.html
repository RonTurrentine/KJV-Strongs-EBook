קָנָא

Transliterationqânâʼ
Phonetickaw-naw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to be (causatively, make) zealous, i.e. (in a bad sense) jealous or envious

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(be) envy(-ious), be (move to, provoke to) jealous(-y), [idiom] very, (be) zeal(-ous).
