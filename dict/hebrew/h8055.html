שָׂמַח

Transliterationsâmach
Phoneticsaw-makh'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

probably to brighten up, i.e. (figuratively) be (causatively, make) blithe or gleesome

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cheer up, be (make) glad, (have, make) joy(-ful), be (make) merry, (cause to, make to) rejoice, [idiom] very.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׂמח

Transliteration: śâmach

Phonetic: saw-makh'

BDB Definition:

  1. to rejoice, be glad
    1. (Qal)
      1. to rejoice
      2. to rejoice (arrogantly), exult (at)
      3. to rejoice (religiously)
    2. (Piel) to cause to rejoice, gladden, make glad
    3. (Hiphil) to cause to rejoice, gladden, make glad

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2268

Part(s) of speech: Verb
