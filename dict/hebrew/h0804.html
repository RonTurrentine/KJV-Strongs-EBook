אַשּׁוּר

TransliterationʼAshshûwr
Phoneticash-shoor'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine / Noun Proper Location

Definition

Ashshur, the second son of Shem; also his descendants and the country occupied by them (i.e. Assyria), its region and its empire

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Asshur, Assur, Assyria, Assyrians. See .
