BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּית שׁמשׁ

Transliteration: bêyth shemesh

Phonetic: bayth sheh'-mesh

BDB Definition: Beth-shemesh = " house of the sun" or " sun-temple"

  1. a town in southwest Judah
  2. a town in Naphtali
  3. a town in Issachar
  4. a town in Egypt

Origin: from H1004 and H8121
