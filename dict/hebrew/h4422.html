מָלַט

Transliterationmâlaṭ
Phoneticmaw-lat'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to be smooth, i.e. (by implication) to escape (as if by slipperiness); causatively, to release or rescue; specifically, to bring forth young, emit sparks

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

deliver (self), escape, lay, leap out, let alone, let go, preserve, save, [idiom] speedily, [idiom] surely.
