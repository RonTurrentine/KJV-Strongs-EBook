בָּקַק

Transliterationbâqaq
Phoneticbaw-kah'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to pour out, i.e. to empty, figuratively, to depopulate; by analogy, to spread out (as a fruitful vine)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(make) empty (out), fail, [idiom] utterly, make void.
