יַעַר

Transliterationyaʻar
Phoneticyah'-ar
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a copse of bushes; hence, a forest; hence, honey in the comb (as hived in trees)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(honey-) comb, forest, wood.
