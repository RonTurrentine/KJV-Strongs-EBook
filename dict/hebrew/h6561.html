פָּרַק

Transliterationpâraq
Phoneticpaw-rak'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to break off or crunch; figuratively, to deliver

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

break (off), deliver, redeem, rend (in pieces), tear in pieces.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: פּרק

Transliteration: pâraq

Phonetic: paw-rak'

BDB Definition:

  1. to tear apart or away, tear off, break away
    1. (Qal) to tear away, snatch, rescue
    2. (Piel) to tear off
    3. (Hithpael) to tear off from oneself, be broken off, be broken in pieces

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 1828

Part(s) of speech: Verb
