Transliterationmêʼâh
Phoneticmay-aw'
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

a hundred; also as a multiplicative and a fraction

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

hundred((-fold), -th), [phrase] sixscore.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: מאיה מאה

Transliteration: mê'âh mê'yâh

Phonetic: may-aw'

BDB Definition:

  1. hundred
    1. as simple number
    2. as part of larger number
    3. as a fraction - one one-hundredth (1/100)

Origin: properly, a primitive numeral; a hundred
