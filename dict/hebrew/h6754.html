צֶלֶם

Transliterationtselem
Phonetictseh'-lem
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a phantom, i.e. (figuratively) illusion, resemblance; hence, a representative figure, especially an idol

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

image, vain shew.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: צלם

Transliteration: tselem

Phonetic: tseh'-lem

BDB Definition:

  1. image
    1. images (of tumours, mice, heathen gods)
    2. image, likeness (of resemblance)
    3. mere, empty, image, semblance (figuratively)

Origin: from an unused root meaning to shade

TWOT entry: 1923a

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
