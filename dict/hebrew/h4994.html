נָא

Transliterationnâʼ
Phoneticnaw
Part of SpeechInterjection

Definition

'I pray', 'now', or 'then'; added mostly to verbs (in the Imperative or Future), or to interjections, occasionally to an adverb or conjunction

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

I beseech (pray) thee (you), go to, now, oh.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נא

Transliteration: nâ'

Phonetic: naw

BDB Definition:

  1. I (we) pray, now, please
    1. used in entreaty or exhortation

Origin: a primitive particle of incitement and entreaty, which may usually be rendered: "I pray," "now," or "then"
