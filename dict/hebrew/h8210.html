שָׁפַךְ

Transliterationshâphak
Phoneticshaw-fak'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to spill forth (blood, a libation, liquid metal; or even a solid, i.e. to mound up); also (figuratively) to expend (life, soul, complaint, money, etc.); intensively, to sprawl out

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cast (up), gush out, pour (out), shed(-der, out), slip.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: שׁפך

Transliteration: shâphak

Phonetic: shaw-fak'

BDB Definition:

  1. to pour, pour out, spill
    1. (Qal)
      1. to pour, pour out
      2. to shed (blood)
      3. to pour out (anger or heart) (figuratively)
    2. (Niphal) to be poured out, be shed
    3. (Pual) to be poured out, be shed
    4. (Hithpael)
      1. to be poured out
      2. to pour out oneself

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2444

Part(s) of speech: Verb
