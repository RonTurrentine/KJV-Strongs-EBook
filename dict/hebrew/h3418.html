יֶרֶק

Transliterationyereq
Phoneticyeh'-rek
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

properly, pallor, i.e. hence, the yellowish green of young and sickly vegetation; concretely, verdure, i.e. grass or vegetation

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

grass, green (thing).
