מָסַס

Transliterationmâçaç
Phoneticmaw-sas'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to liquefy; figuratively, to waste (with disease), to faint (with fatigue, fear or grief)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

discourage, faint, be loosed, melt (away), refuse, [idiom] utterly.
