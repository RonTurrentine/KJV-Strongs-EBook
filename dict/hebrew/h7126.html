קָרַב

Transliterationqârab
Phonetickaw-rab'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to approach (causatively, bring near) for whatever purpose

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(cause to) approach, (cause to) bring (forth, near), (cause to) come (near, nigh), (cause to) draw near (nigh), go (near), be at hand, join, be near, offer, present, produce, make ready, stand, take.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: קרב

Transliteration: qârab

Phonetic: kaw-rab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to come near, approach, enter into, draw near
    1. (Qal) to approach, draw near
    2. (Niphal) to be brought near
    3. (Piel) to cause to approach, bring near, cause to draw near
    4. (Hiphil) to bring near, bring, present

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2065

Part(s) of speech: Verb
