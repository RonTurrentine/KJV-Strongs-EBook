נָזַל

Transliterationnâzal
Phoneticnaw-zal'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to drip, or shed by trickling

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

distil, drop, flood, (cause to) flow(-ing), gush out, melt, pour (down), running water, stream.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נזל

Transliteration: nâzal

Phonetic: naw-zal'

BDB Definition:

  1. to flow, distil, flow forth or down, trickle, drop
    1. (Qal)
      1. to flow
        1. streams, floods (participle)
      2. to distil
    2. (Hiphil) to cause to flow

Origin: a primitive root
