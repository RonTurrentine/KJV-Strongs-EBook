כַּף

Transliterationkaph
Phonetickaf
Part of SpeechNoun Feminine

Definition

the hollow hand or palm (so of the paw of an animal, of the sole, and even of the bowl of a dish or sling, the handle of a bolt, the leaves of a palm-tree); figuratively, power

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

branch, [phrase] foot, hand((-ful), -dle, (-led)), hollow, middle, palm, paw, power, sole, spoon.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: כּף

Transliteration: kaph

Phonetic: kaf

BDB Definition:

  1. palm, hand, sole, palm of the hand, hollow or flat of the hand
    1. palm, hollow or flat of the hand
    2. power
    3. sole (of the foot)
    4. hollow, objects, bending objects, bent objects
      1. of thigh-joint
      2. pan, vessel (as hollow)
      3. hollow (of sling)
      4. hand-shaped branches or fronds (of palm trees)
      5. handles (as bent)
