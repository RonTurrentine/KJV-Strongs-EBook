רָקַם

Transliterationrâqam
Phoneticraw-kam'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to variegate color, i.e. embroider; by implication, to fabricate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

embroiderer, needlework, curiously work.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רקם

Transliteration: râqam

Phonetic: raw-kam'

BDB Definition:

  1. to variegate, mix colours
    1. (Qal) variegator (worker in colours) (participle)
    2. (Pual) to be skilfully wrought or woven

Origin: a primitive root
