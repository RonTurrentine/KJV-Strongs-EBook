עֲדָא

Transliterationʻădâʼ
Phoneticad-aw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

{to advance, i.e. pass on or continue; causatively, to remove; specifically, to bedeck (i.e. bring an ornament upon)}

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

alter, depart, pass (away), remove, take (away).

    1. (Peal)

      2. to pass away
