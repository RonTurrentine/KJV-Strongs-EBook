נְחֹשֶׁת

Transliterationnᵉchôsheth
Phoneticnekh-o'-sheth
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

copper, hence, something made of that metal, i.e. coin, a fetter; figuratively, base (as compared with gold or silver)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

brasen, brass, chain, copper, fetter (of brass), filthiness, steel.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: נחשׁת

Transliteration: nechôsheth

Phonetic: nekh-o'-sheth

BDB Definition:

  1. copper, bronze
    1. copper (ore), bronze (as copper alloy)
    2. fetters (of copper or bronze)
    3. copper (as value)
  2. lust, harlotry
    1. meaning dubious

Origin: for H5154

TWOT entry: 1349a,1350a
