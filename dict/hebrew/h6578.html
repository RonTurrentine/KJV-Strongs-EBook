פְּרָת

TransliterationPᵉrâth
Phoneticper-awth'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Perath (i.e. Euphrates), a river of the East

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Euphrates.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: פּרת

Transliteration: perâth

Phonetic: per-awth'

BDB Definition: Euphrates = " fruitfulness"

  1. the largest and longest river of western Asia; rises from two chief sources in the Armenian mountains and flows into the Persian Gulf

Origin: from an unused root meaning to break forth

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
