אַחְאָב

TransliterationʼAchʼâb
Phoneticakh-awb'
Part of SpeechNoun Proper Masculine

Definition

Achab, the name of a king of Israel and of a prophet at Babylon

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Ahab.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אחב אחאב

Transliteration: 'ach'âb 'echâb

Phonetic: akh-awb'

BDB Definition: Ahab = " father's brother" once (by contraction) 'Echab (Jer 29:22)

  1. king of Israel, son of Omri, husband of Jezebel
  2. false prophet executed by Nebuchadrezzar, time of Jeremiah

Origin: from H0251 and H0001

TWOT entry: pr n m
