Definition

Aphek (or Aphik), the name of three places in Palestine

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Aphek, Aphik.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אפיק אפק

Transliteration: 'ăphêq 'ăphı̂yq

Phonetic: af-ake'

BDB Definition: Aphek or Aphik = " enclosure"

  1. a Canaanite city near Jezreel
  2. a city in territory of Asher
  3. a city northeast of Beirut in Transjordan

Origin: from H0662 (in the sense of strength)

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Location
