גָּלָה

Transliterationgâlâh
Phoneticgaw-law'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to denude (especially in a disgraceful sense); by implication, to exile (captives being usually stripped); figuratively, to reveal

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

[phrase] advertise, appear, bewray, bring, (carry, lead, go) captive (into captivity), depart, disclose, discover, exile, be gone, open, [idiom] plainly, publish, remove, reveal, [idiom] shamelessly, shew, [idiom] surely, tell, uncover.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: גּלה

Transliteration: gâlâh

Phonetic: gaw-law'

BDB Definition:

  1. to uncover, remove
    1. (Qal)
      1. to uncover
      2. to remove, depart
      3. to go into exile
    2. (Niphal)
      1. (reflexive)
        1. to uncover oneself
        2. to discover or show oneself
        3. to reveal himself (of God)
      2. (passive)
        1. to be uncovered
        2. to be disclosed, be discovered
        3. to be revealed
      3. to be removed
    3. (Piel)
      1. to uncover (nakedness)
        1. nakedness
        2. general
      2. to disclose, discover, lay bare
      3. to make known, show, reveal
    4. (Pual) to be uncovered
    5. (Hiphil) to carry away into exile, take into exile
    6. (Hophal) to be taken into exile
    7. (Hithpael)
      1. to be uncovered
      2. to reveal oneself

Origin: a primitive root
