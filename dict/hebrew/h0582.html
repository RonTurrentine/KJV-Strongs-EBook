אֱנוֹשׁ

Transliterationʼĕnôwsh
Phoneticen-oshe'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a man in general (singly or collectively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

another, [idiom] (blood-) thirsty, certain, chap(-man); divers, fellow, [idiom] in the flower of their age, husband, (certain, mortal) man, people, person, servant, some ([idiom] of them), [phrase] stranger, those, [phrase] their trade. It is often unexpressed in the English versions, especially when used in apposition with another word. Compare .

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אנושׁ

Transliteration: 'ĕnôsh

Phonetic: en-oshe'

BDB Definition:

  1. man, mortal man, person, mankind
    1. of an individual
    2. men (collective)
    3. man, mankind

Origin: from H0605
