אֱנוֹשׁ

Transliterationʼĕnôwsh
Phoneticen-oshe'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a man in general (singly or collectively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

another, [idiom] (blood-) thirsty, certain, chap(-man); divers, fellow, [idiom] in the flower of their age, husband, (certain, mortal) man, people, person, servant, some ([idiom] of them), [phrase] stranger, those, [phrase] their trade.
