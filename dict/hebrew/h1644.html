גָּרַשׁ

Transliterationgârash
Phoneticgaw-rash'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to drive out from a possession; especially to expatriate or divorce

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cast up (out), divorced (woman), drive away (forth, out), expel, [idiom] surely put away, trouble, thrust out.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: גּרשׁ

Transliteration: gârash

Phonetic: gaw-rash'

BDB Definition:

  1. to drive out, expel, cast out, drive away, divorce, put away, thrust away, trouble, cast up
    1. (Qal) to thrust out, cast out
    2. (Niphal) to be driven away, be tossed
    3. (Piel) to drive out, drive away
    4. (Pual) to be thrust out

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 388

Part(s) of speech: Verb
