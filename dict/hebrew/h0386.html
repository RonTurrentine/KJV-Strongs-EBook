אֵיתָן

Transliterationʼêythân
Phoneticay-thawn'
Part of SpeechAdjective

Definition

permanence; hence (concrete) permanent; specifically a chieftain

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

hard, mighty, rough, strength, strong.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: איתן

Transliteration: 'êythân

Phonetic: ay-thawn'

BDB Definition:

  1. perpetual, constant, perennial, ever-flowing
    1. ever-flowing (of a stream)
    2. permanence, permanent, enduring (figuratively)

Origin: from an unused root (meaning to continue)

TWOT entry: 935a

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
