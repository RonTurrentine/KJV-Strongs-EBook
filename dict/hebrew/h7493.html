רָעַשׁ

Transliterationrâʻash
Phoneticraw-ash
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to undulate (as the earth, the sky, etc.; also a field of grain), particularly through fear; specifically, to spring (as a locust)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

make afraid, (re-) move, quake, (make to) shake, (make to) tremble.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רעשׁ

Transliteration: râ‛ash

Phonetic: raw-ash

BDB Definition:

  1. to quake, shake
    1. (Qal) to quake, shake
    2. (Niphal) to be made to quake
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to cause to quake
      2. to cause to spring or leap (of horse)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2195

Part(s) of speech: Verb
