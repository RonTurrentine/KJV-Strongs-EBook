Definition

to make an uproar, or agitate greatly

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

destroy, move, make a noise, put, ring again.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: הוּם

Transliteration: hûm

Phonetic: hoom

BDB Definition:

  1. to distract, ring again, make a (great) noise, murmur, roar, discomfit, be moved
    1. (Qal) to discomfit
    2. (Niphal) to be in a stir
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to murmur
      2. to show disquietude

Origin: a primitive root [compare H2000]

TWOT entry: 486
