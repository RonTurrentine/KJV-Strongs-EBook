Definition

Elitsaphan or Eltsaphan, an Israelite

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Elizaphan, Elzaphan.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אלצפן אליצפן

Transliteration: 'ĕlı̂ytsâphân 'eltsâphân

Phonetic: el-ee-tsaw-fawn'

BDB Definition: Elizaphan or Elzaphan = " my God has protected"

  1. a Kohathite chief in the wilderness
  2. a Zebulunite chief
  3. Uzziel's son, Mishael's brother in time of Moses

Origin: from H0410 and H6845

TWOT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Proper Name Masculine
