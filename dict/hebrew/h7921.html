שָׁכֹל

Transliterationshâkôl
Phoneticshaw-kole'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to miscarry, i.e. suffer abortion; by analogy, to bereave (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bereave (of children), barren, cast calf (fruit, young), be (make) childless, deprive, destroy, [idiom] expect, lose children, miscarry, rob of children, spoil.
