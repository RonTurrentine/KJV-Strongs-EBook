אָבָה

Transliterationʼâbâh
Phoneticaw-baw'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to breathe after, i.e. (figuratively) to be acquiescent

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

consent, rest content will, be willing.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: אבה

Transliteration: 'âbâh

Phonetic: aw-baw'

BDB Definition:

  1. to be willing, consent
    1. (Qal)
      1. to be willing
      2. to consent, yield to, accept
      3. to desire

Origin: a primitive root
