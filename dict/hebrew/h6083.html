עָפָר

Transliterationʻâphâr
Phoneticaw-fawr'
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

dust (as powdered or gray); hence, clay, earth, mud

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

ashes, dust, earth, ground, morter, powder, rubbish.
