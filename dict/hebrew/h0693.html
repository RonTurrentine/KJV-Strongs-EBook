Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(lie in) ambush(-ment), lay (lie in) wait.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ארב

Transliteration: 'ârab

Phonetic: aw-rab'

BDB Definition:

  1. to lie in wait, ambush, lurk
    1. (Qal)
      1. to lie in wait
      2. ambush (participle as subst)
    2. (Piel) ambushers, liers-in-wait (plural participle)
    3. (Hiphil) to lay an ambush

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 156

Part(s) of speech: Verb
