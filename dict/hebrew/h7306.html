רוּחַ

Transliterationrûwach
Phoneticroo'-akh
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to blow, i.e. breathe; only (literally) to smell or (by implication, perceive (figuratively, to anticipate, enjoy)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

accept, smell, [idiom] touch, make of quick understanding.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: רוּח

Transliteration: rûach

Phonetic: roo'-akh

BDB Definition:

  1. (Hiphil) to smell, scent, perceive odour, accept
    1. of horse
    2. of delight (metaphorically)

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 2131

Part(s) of speech: Verb
