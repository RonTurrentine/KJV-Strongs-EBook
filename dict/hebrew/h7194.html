קָשַׁר

Transliterationqâshar
Phonetickaw-shar'
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

to tie, physically (gird, confine, compact) or mentally (in love, league)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bind (up), (make a) conspire(-acy, -ator), join together, knit, stronger, work (treason).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: קשׁר

Transliteration: qâshar

Phonetic: kaw-shar'

BDB Definition:

  1. to bind, tie, bind together, league together, conspire
    1. (Qal)
      1. to bind, confine
      2. to league together, conspire
    2. (Niphal) to be bound, be bound up
    3. (Piel)
      1. to bind on
      2. to bind fast
      3. to bind, tie
      4. to bind to oneself
    4. (Pual) robust, vigorous (participle)
    5. (Hithpael) to conspire

Origin: a primitive root: to tie, physically (gird, confine, compact) or mentally (in love, league)
