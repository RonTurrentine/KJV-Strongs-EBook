Transliterationshachaq
Phoneticshakh'-ak
Part of SpeechNoun Masculine

Definition

a powder (as beaten small); by analogy, a thin vapor; by extension, the firmament

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cloud, small dust, heaven, sky.
