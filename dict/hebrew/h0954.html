בּוּשׁ

Transliterationbûwsh
Phoneticboosh
Part of SpeechVerb

Definition

properly, to pale, i.e. by implication to be ashamed; also (by implication) to be disappointed or delayed

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(be, make, bring to, cause, put to, with, a-) shamed(-d), be (put to) confounded(-fusion), become dry, delay, be long.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: בּוּשׁ

Transliteration: bûsh

Phonetic: boosh

BDB Definition:

  1. to put to shame, be ashamed, be disconcerted, be disappointed
    1. (Qal)
      1. to feel shame
      2. to be ashamed, disconcerted, disappointed (by reason of)
    2. (Piel) to delay (in shame)
    3. (Hiphil)
      1. to put to shame
      2. to act shamefully
      3. to be ashamed
    4. (Hithpolel) to be ashamed before one another

Origin: a primitive root

TWOT entry: 222

Part(s) of speech: Verb
