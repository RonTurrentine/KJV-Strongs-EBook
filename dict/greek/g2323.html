θεραπεύω

Transliterationtherapeúō
Phoneticther-ap-yoo'-o
Originfrom the same as G2324;

Definition

to wait upon menially, i.e. (figuratively) to adore (God), or (specially) to relieve (of disease)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cure, heal, worship.
