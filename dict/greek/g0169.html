ἀκάθαρτος

Transliterationakáthartos
Phoneticak-ath'-ar-tos
Originfrom G0001 (as a negative particle) and a presumed derivative of G2508 (meaning cleansed);

Definition

impure (ceremonially, morally (lewd) or specially, (demonic))

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

foul, unclean.
