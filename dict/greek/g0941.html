βαστάζω

Transliterationbastázō
Phoneticbas-tad'-zo
Originperhaps remotely derived from the base of G0939 (through the idea of removal);

Definition

to lift, literally or figuratively (endure, declare, sustain, receive, etc.)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bear, carry, take up.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: βαστάζω

Transliteration: bastazō

Phonetic: bas-tad'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to take up with the hands
  2. to take up in order to carry or bear, to put upon one's self
    1. to bear what is burdensome
  3. to bear, to carry
    1. to carry on one's person
    2. to sustain, i.e. uphold, support
  4. to bear away, carry off

Origin: perhaps remotely derived from the base of G0939 (through the idea of removal)

TDNT entry: 10:56,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
