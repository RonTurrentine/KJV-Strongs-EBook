ὀμνύω

Transliterationomnýō
Phoneticom-noo'-o
Origina prolonged form of a primary, but obsolete ὄμω, for which another prolonged form ὀμόωom-o'-o is used in certain tenses;

Definition

to swear, i.e. take (or declare on) oath

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

swear.
