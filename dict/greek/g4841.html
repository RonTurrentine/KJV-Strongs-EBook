συμπάσχω

Transliterationsympáschō
Phoneticsoom-pas'-kho
Originfrom G4862 and G3958 (including its alternate);

Definition

to experience pain jointly or of the same kind (specially, persecution; to "sympathize")

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

suffer with.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: συμπάσχω

Transliteration: sumpaschō

Phonetic: soom-pas'-kho

Thayer Definition:

  1. to suffer or feel pain together
  2. to suffer evils (troubles, persecutions) in the like manner with another

Origin: from G4862 and G3958 (including its alternate)

TDNT entry: 5:925 & 7:787,798 & 1102
