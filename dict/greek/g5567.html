ψάλλω

Transliterationpsállō
Phoneticpsal'-lo
Originprobably strengthened from ψάω (to rub or touch the surface; compare G5597);

Definition

to twitch or twang, i.e. to play on a stringed instrument (celebrate the divine worship with music and accompanying odes)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

make melody, sing (psalms).
