χάραξ

Transliterationchárax
Phonetickhar'-ax
Originfrom χαράσσω (to sharpen to a point; akin to G1125 through the idea of scratching);

Definition

a stake, i.e. (by implication) a palisade or rampart (military mound for circumvallation in a siege)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

trench.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: χάραξ

Transliteration: charax

Phonetic: khar'-ax

Thayer Definition:

  1. a pale or stake, a palisade
  2. a palisade or rampart
    1. pales between which earth, stones, trees, and timbers are heaped and packed together

Origin: from charasso (to sharpen to a point, akin to G1125 through the idea of scratching)

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
