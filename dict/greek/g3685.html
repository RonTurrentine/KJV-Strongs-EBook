ὀνίνημι

Transliterationonínēmi
Phoneticon-in'-ay-mee
Origina prolonged form of an apparently primary verb (ὄνομαι, to slur);

Definition

for which another prolonged form (ὀνάω) is used as an alternate in some tenses (unless indeed it be identical with the base of G3686 through the idea of notoriety); to gratify, i.e. (middle voice) to derive pleasure or advantage from

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

have joy.
