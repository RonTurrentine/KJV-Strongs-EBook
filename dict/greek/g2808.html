κλείω

Transliterationkleíō
Phonetickli'-o
Origina primary verb;

Definition

to close (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

shut (up).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κλείω

Transliteration: kleiō

Phonetic: kli'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to shut, shut up
  2. metaphorically
    1. to cause the heavens to withhold rain
    2. to shut up compassion so that it is like a thing inaccessible to one, to be devoid of pity towards one
    3. to obstruct the entrance into the kingdom of heaven

Origin: a primary verb

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
