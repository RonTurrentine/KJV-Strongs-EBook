πωλέω

Transliterationpōléō
Phoneticpo-leh'-o
Originprobably ultimately from πέλομαι (to be busy, to trade);

Definition

to barter (as a pedlar), i.e. to sell

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

sell, whatever is sold.
