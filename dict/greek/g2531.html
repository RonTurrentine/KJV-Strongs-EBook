BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: καθώς

Transliteration: kathōs

Phonetic: kath-oce'

Thayer Definition:

  1. according as
    1. just as, even as
    2. in proportion as, in the degree that
  2. since, seeing that, agreeably to the fact that
  3. when, after that

Origin: from G2596 and G5613

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Adverb
