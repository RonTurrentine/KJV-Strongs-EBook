ἀποκτείνω

Transliterationapokteínō
Phoneticap-ok-ti'-no
Originfrom G0575 and κτείνω (to slay);

Definition

to kill outright; figuratively, to destroy

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

put to death, kill, slay.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀποκτείνω

Transliteration: apokteinō

Phonetic: ap-ok-ti'-no

Thayer Definition:

  1. to kill in any way whatever
    1. to destroy, to allow to perish
  2. metaphorically to extinguish, abolish
    1. to inflict mortal death
    2. to deprive of spiritual life and procure eternal misery in hell

Origin: from G0575 and kteino (to slay)

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
