BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἰάομαι

Transliteration: iaomai

Phonetic: ee-ah'-om-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to cure, heal
  2. to make whole
    1. to free from errors and sins, to bring about (one's) salvation
