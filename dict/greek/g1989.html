ἐπιστέλλω

Transliterationepistéllō
Phonetic ep-ee-stel'-lo
Originfrom G1909 and G4724;

Definition

to enjoin (by writing), i.e. (genitive case) to communicate by letter (for any purpose)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

write (a letter, unto).
