διεγείρω

Transliterationdiegeírō
Phoneticdee-eg-i'-ro
Originfrom G1223 and G1453;

Definition

to wake fully; i.e. arouse (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

arise, awake, raise, stir up.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: διεγείρω

Transliteration: diegeirō

Phonetic: dee-eg-i'-ro

Thayer Definition:

  1. to wake up, awaken, arouse (from sleep)
    1. of the sea, which begins to be agitated, to rise
    2. metaphorically
      1. to arouse the mind
      2. stir up, render active

Origin: from G1223 and G1453

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
