διαμερίζω

Transliterationdiamerízō
Phoneticdee-am-er-id'-zo
Originfrom G1223 and G3307;

Definition

to partition thoroughly (literally in distribution, figuratively in dissension)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cloven, divide, part.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: διαμερίζω

Transliteration: diamerizō

Phonetic: dee-am-er-id'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to cleave asunder, cut in pieces
  2. to be divided into opposing parts, to be at variance, in dissension
  3. to distribute

Origin: from G1223 and G3307

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
