θυγάτηρ

Transliterationthygátēr
Phoneticthoo-gat'-air
Originapparently a primary word (compare "daughter");

Definition

a female child, or (by Hebraism) descendant (or inhabitant)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

daughter.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: θυγάτηρ

Transliteration: thugatēr

Phonetic: thoo-gat'-air

Thayer Definition:

  1. a daughter
    1. a daughter of God
      1. acceptable to God, rejoicing in God's peculiar care and protection
    2. with the name of a place, city, or region
      1. denotes collectively all its inhabitants and citizens
    3. a female descendant

Origin: "apparently a root word [cf "daughter"]"

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
