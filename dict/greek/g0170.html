ἀκαιρέομαι

Transliterationakairéomai
Phoneticak-ahee-reh'-om-ahee
Originfrom a compound of G1 (as a negative particle) and G2540 (meaning unseasonable);

Definition

to be inopportune (for oneself), i.e. to fail of a proper occasion

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

lack opportunity.
