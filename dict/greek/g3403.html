μιμνήσκω

Transliterationmimnḗskō
Phoneticmim-nace'-ko
Origina prolonged form of G3415 (from which some of the tenses are borrowed);

Definition

to remind, i.e. (middle voice) to recall to mind

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be mindful, remember.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: μιμνήσκω

Transliteration: mimnēskō

Phonetic: mim-nace'-ko

Thayer Definition:

  1. to remind
    1. to be recalled or to return to one's mind, to remind one's self of, to remember
    2. to be recalled to mind, to be remembered, had in remembrance
    3. to remember a thing
    4. be mindful of

Origin: a prolonged form of G3415 (from which some of the tenses are borrowed)

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
