μετρέω

Transliterationmetréō
Phoneticmet-reh'-o
Originfrom G3358;

Definition

to measure (i.e. ascertain in size by a fixed standard); by implication, to admeasure (i.e. allot by rule)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

figuratively, to estimate:--measure, mete.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: μετρέω

Transliteration: metreō

Phonetic: met-reh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to measure, to measure out or off
    1. any space or distance with a measurer's reed or rule
    2. metaphorically to judge according to any rule or standard, to estimate
  2. to measure out, mete out to, i.e. to give by measure

Origin: from G3358

TDNT entry: 14:32,6

Part(s) of speech: Verb
