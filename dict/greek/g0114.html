ἀθετέω

Transliterationathetéō
Phonetic ath-et-eh'-o
Originfrom a compound of G1 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of G5087;

Definition

to set aside, i.e. (by implication) to disesteem, neutralize or violate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cast off, despise, disannul, frustrate, bring to nought, reject.
