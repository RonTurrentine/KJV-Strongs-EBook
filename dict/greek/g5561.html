χώρα

Transliterationchṓra
Phonetickho'-rah
Originfeminine of a derivative of the base of G5490 through the idea of empty expanse;

Definition

room, i.e. a space of territory (more or less extensive; often including its inhabitants)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

coast, county, fields, ground, land, region.
