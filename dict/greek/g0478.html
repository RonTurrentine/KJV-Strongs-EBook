ἀντικαθίστημι

Transliterationantikathístēmi
Phonetican-tee-kath-is'-tay-mee
Originfrom G0473 and G2525;

Definition

to set down (troops) against, i.e. withstand

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

resist.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀντικαθίστημι

Transliteration: antikathistēmi

Phonetic: an-tee-kath-is'-tay-mee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to put in place of another
  2. to place in opposition
    1. to dispose troops, set an army in line of battle
    2. to stand against, resist

Origin: from G0473 and G2525

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
