ἐριθεία

Transliterationeritheía
Phoneticer-ith-i'-ah
Originperhaps as the same as G2042;

Definition

properly, intrigue, i.e. (by implication) faction

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

contention(-ious), strife.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐριθεία

Transliteration: eritheia

Phonetic: er-ith-i'-ah

Thayer Definition:

  1. electioneering or intriguing for office
    1. apparently, in the NT a courting distinction, a desire to put one's self forward, a partisan and fractious spirit which does not disdain low arts
    2. partisanship, fractiousness

Origin: perhaps as the same as G2042

TDNT entry: 13:00,3

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
