ὑμνέω

Transliterationhymnéō
Phonetichoom-neh'-o
Originfrom G5215;

Definition

to hymn, i.e. sing a religious ode; by implication, to celebrate (God) in song

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

sing a hymn (praise unto).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὑμνέω

Transliteration: humneō

Phonetic: hoom-neh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to sing the praise of, sing hymns to
  2. to sing a hymn, to sing
    1. singing of paschal hymns these were Psalms 113 - 118 and 136, which the Jews called the "great Hallel"

Origin: from G5215

TDNT entry: 16:09,1
