σεβαστός

Transliterationsebastós
Phoneticseb-as-tos'
Originfrom G4573;

Definition

venerable (august), i.e. (as noun) a title of the Roman Emperor, or (as adjective) imperial

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Augustus(-').

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: σεβαστός

Transliteration: sebastos

Phonetic: seb-as-tos'

Thayer Definition:

  1. reverend, venerable
  2. the title of the Roman emperors
    1. Augustan, i,e, taking its name from the emperor
    2. a title of honour which used to be given certain legions, or cohorts, or battalions, "for valour"

Origin: from G4573

TDNT entry: 09:54,1

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
