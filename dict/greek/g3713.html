ὀρέγομαι

Transliterationorégomai
Phoneticor-eg'-om-ahee
Originmiddle voice of apparently a prolonged form of an obsolete primary (compare G3735);

Definition

to stretch oneself, i.e. reach out after (long for)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

covet after, desire.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὀρέγομαι

Transliteration: oregomai

Phonetic: or-eg'-om-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to stretch one's self out in order to touch or to grasp something, to reach after or desire something
  2. to give one's self up to the love of money

Origin: middle voice of apparently a prolonged form of an obsolete primary [cf G3735]

TDNT entry: 12:27,7

Part(s) of speech: Verb
