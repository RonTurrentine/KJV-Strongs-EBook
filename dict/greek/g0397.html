Transliterationanatréphō
Phonetican-at-ref'-o
Originfrom G0303 and G5142;

Definition

to rear (physically or mentally)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bring up, nourish (up).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀνατρέφω

Transliteration: anatrephō

Phonetic: an-at-ref'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to nurse up, nourish up
    1. of young children and animals nourished to promote growth
  2. to bring up
    1. with the predominant idea of forming the mind

Origin: from G0303 and G5142

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
