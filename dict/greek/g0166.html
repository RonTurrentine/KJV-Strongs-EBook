BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: αἰώνιος

Transliteration: aiōnios

Phonetic: ahee-o'-nee-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. without beginning and end, that which always has been and always will be
  2. without beginning
  3. without end, never to cease, everlasting
