Originfrom a compound of G1909 and G0757 (meaning a governor of a district, "eparch");

Definition

a special region of government, i.e. a Roman præfecture

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

province.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐπαρχία

Transliteration: eparchia

Phonetic: ep-ar-khee'-ah

Thayer Definition:

  1. the office of a governor or prefect
  2. the region subject to a prefect
    1. a province of the Roman empire, either a larger province, or an appendage to a larger province, as Palestine was to that of Syria

Origin: "from a compound of G1909 and G0757 (meaning a governor of a district, "eparch")"

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
