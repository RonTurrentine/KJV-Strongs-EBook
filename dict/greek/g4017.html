BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: περιβλέπω

Transliteration: periblepō

Phonetic: per-ee-blep'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to look around
  2. to look around about one's self
  3. to look round on one (i.e. to look for one's self at one near by)

Origin: from G4012 and G0991

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
