ἐπαγγελία

Transliterationepangelía
Phoneticep-ang-el-ee'-ah
Originfrom G1861;

Definition

an announcement (for information, assent or pledge; especially a divine assurance of good)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

message, promise.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐπαγγελία

Transliteration: epaggelia

Phonetic: ep-ang-el-ee'-ah

Thayer Definition:

  1. announcement
  2. promise
    1. the act of promising, a promise given or to be given
    2. a promised good or blessing

Origin: from G1861

TDNT entry: 11:36,2

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
