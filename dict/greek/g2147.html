εὑρίσκω

Transliterationheurískō
Phonetic hyoo-ris'-ko
Origina prolonged form of a primary εὕρωhyoo'-ro, which (together with another cognate form) εὑρέωhyoo-reh'-o is used for it in all the tenses except the present and imperfect;

Definition

to find (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

find, get, obtain, perceive, see.
