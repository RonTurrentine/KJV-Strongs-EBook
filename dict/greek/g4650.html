σκορπίζω

Transliterationskorpízō
Phoneticskor-pid'-zo
Originapparently from the same as G4651 (through the idea of penetrating);

Definition

to dissipate, i.e. (figuratively) put to flight, waste, be liberal

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

disperse abroad, scatter (abroad).
