κρύπτω

Transliterationkrýptō
Phonetickroop'-to
Origina primary verb;

Definition

to conceal (properly, by covering)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

hide (self), keep secret, secret(-ly).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κρύπτω

Transliteration: kruptō

Phonetic: kroop'-to

Thayer Definition:

  1. to hide, conceal, to be hid
  2. escape notice
  3. metaphorically to conceal (that it may not become known)

Origin: a primary verb

TDNT entry: 18:57,5

Part(s) of speech: Verb
