εὐλογία

Transliterationeulogía
Phoneticyoo-log-ee'-ah
Originfrom the same as G2127;

Definition

fine speaking, i.e. elegance of language; commendation ("eulogy"), i.e. (reverentially) adoration; religiously, benediction; by implication, consecration; by extension, benefit or largess

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

blessing (a matter of) bounty (X -tifully), fair speech.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: εὐλογία

Transliteration: eulogia

Phonetic: yoo-log-ee'-ah

Thayer Definition:

  1. praise, laudation, panegyric: of Christ or God
  2. fine discourse, polished language
    1. in a bad sense, language artfully adapted to captivate the hearer: fair speaking, fine speeches
  3. an invocation of blessing, benediction
  4. consecration
  5. a (concrete) blessing, benefit

Origin: from the same as G2127

TDNT entry: 14:34,3

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
