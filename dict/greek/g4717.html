σταυρόω

Transliterationstauróō
Phoneticstow-ro'-o
Originfrom G4716;

Definition

to impale on the cross; figuratively, to extinguish (subdue) passion or selfishness

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

crucify.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: σταυρόω

Transliteration: stauroō

Phonetic: stow-ro'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to stake, drive down stakes
  2. to fortify with driven stakes, to palisade
  3. to crucify
    1. to crucify one
    2. metaphorically to crucify the flesh, destroy its power utterly (the nature of the figure implying that the destruction is attended with intense pain)

Origin: from G4716

TDNT entry: 16:41,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
