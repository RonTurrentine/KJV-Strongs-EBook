κακία

Transliterationkakía
Phonetickak-ee'-ah
Originfrom G2556;

Definition

badness, i.e. (subjectively) depravity, or (actively) malignity, or (passively) trouble

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

evil, malice(-iousness), naughtiness, wickedness.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κακία

Transliteration: kakia

Phonetic: kak-ee'-ah

Thayer Definition:

  1. malignity, malice, ill-will, desire to injure
  2. wickedness, depravity
    1. wickedness that is not ashamed to break laws
  3. evil, trouble

Origin: from G2556

TDNT entry: 11:02,4

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
