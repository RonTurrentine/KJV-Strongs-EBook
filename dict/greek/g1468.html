ἐγκρατής

Transliterationenkratḗs
Phoneticeng-krat-ace'
Originfrom G1722 and G2904;

Definition

strong in a thing (masterful), i.e. (figuratively and reflexively) self-controlled (in appetite, etc.)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

temperate.
