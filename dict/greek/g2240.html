ἥκω

Transliterationhḗkō
Phonetichay'-ko
Origina primary verb;

Definition

to arrive, i.e. be present (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

come.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἥκω

Transliteration: hēkō

Phonetic: hay'-ko

Thayer Definition:

  1. to have come, have arrived, be present
  2. metaphorically
    1. to come to one, i.e. to seek an intimacy with one, become his follower: to come upon one (unexpectedly)
    2. to come upon one, of things endured

Origin: a primary verb

TDNT entry: 17:26,3

Part(s) of speech: Verb
