πρᾶγμα

Transliterationprâgma
Phoneticprag'-mah
Originfrom G4238;

Definition

a deed; by implication, an affair; by extension, an object (material)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

business, matter, thing, work.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πρᾶγμα

Transliteration: pragma

Phonetic: prag'-mah

Thayer Definition:

  1. that which has been done, a deed, an accomplished fact
  2. what is done or being accomplished
    1. spec. business, a commercial transaction
  3. a matter, question, affair
    1. spec. in a forensic sense, a matter at law, case, suit
  4. that which is or exists, a thing

Origin: from G4238

TDNT entry: 16:38,9

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
