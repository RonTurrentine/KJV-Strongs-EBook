πικραίνω

Transliterationpikraínō
Phoneticpik-rah'-ee-no
Originfrom G4089;

Definition

to embitter (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be (make) bitter.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πικραίνω

Transliteration: pikrainō

Phonetic: pik-rah'-ee-no

Thayer Definition:

  1. to make bitter
    1. to produce a bitter taste in the stomach
  2. to embitter. exasperate
    1. render angry, indignant
    2. to be embittered, irritated
    3. to visit with bitterness, to grieve (deal bitterly with)

Origin: from G4089

TDNT entry: 08:02,8

Part(s) of speech: Verb
