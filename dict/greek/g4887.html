συνδέω

Transliterationsyndéō
Phoneticsoon-deh'-o
Originfrom G4862 and G1210;

Definition

to bind with, i.e. (passively) be a fellow-prisoner (figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be bound with.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: συνδέω

Transliteration: sundeō

Phonetic: soon-deh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to tie together, to bind together
  2. to bind or fasten on all sides
  3. to bind just as (i.e. jointly with) another

Origin: from G4862 and G1210

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
