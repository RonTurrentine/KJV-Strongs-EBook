κατοικέω

Transliterationkatoikéō
Phonetickat-oy-keh'-o
Originfrom G2596 and G3611;

Definition

to house permanently, i.e. reside (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dwell(-er), inhabitant(-ter).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κατοικέω

Transliteration: katoikeō

Phonetic: kat-oy-keh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to dwell, settle
    1. metaphorically divine powers, influences, etc., are said to dwell in his soul, to pervade, prompt, govern it
  2. to dwell in, inhabit
    1. God is said to dwell in the temple, i.e. to be always present for worshippers

Origin: from G2596 and G3611

TDNT entry: 07:33,7

Part(s) of speech: Verb
