μάλιστα

Transliterationmálista
Phoneticmal'-is-tah
Originneuter plural of the superlative of an apparently primary adverb μάλα (very);

Definition

(adverbially) most (in the greatest degree) or particularly

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

chiefly, most of all, (e-)specially.
