ἀπάγχομαι

Transliterationapánchomai
Phoneticap-ang'-khom-ahee
Originfrom G0575 and ἄγχω (to choke; akin to the base of G0043);

Definition

to strangle oneself off (i.e. to death)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

hang himself.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀπάγχομαι

Transliteration: apagchomai

Phonetic: ap-ang'-khom-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to throttle, strangle, in order to put out of the way or kill
  2. to hang one's self, to end one's life by hanging

Origin: from G0575 and agcho (to choke akin to the base of G0043)

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
