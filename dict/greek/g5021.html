τάσσω

Transliterationtássō
Phonetic tas'-so
Origina prolonged form of a primary verb (which latter appears only in certain tenses);

Definition

to arrange in an orderly manner, i.e. assign or dispose (to a certain position or lot)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

addict, appoint, determine, ordain, set.
