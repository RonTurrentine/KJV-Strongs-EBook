πυνθάνομαι

Transliterationpynthánomai
Phoneticpoon-than'-om-ahee
Originmiddle voice prolonged from a primary πύθω (which occurs only as an alternate in certain tenses);

Definition

to question, i.e. ascertain by inquiry (as a matter of information merely; and thus differing from G2065, which properly means a request as a favor; and from G0154, which is strictly a demand for something due; as well as from G2212, which implies a search for something hidden; and from G1189, which involves the idea of urgent need); by implication, to learn (by casual intelligence)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

ask, demand, enquire, understand.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πυνθάνομαι

Transliteration: punthanomai

Phonetic: poon-than'-om-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to enquire, ask
  2. to ascertain, by enquiry

Origin: middle voice prolonged from a primary putho (which occurs only as an alternate in certain tenses)

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
