ἐμβαίνω

Transliterationembaínō
Phonetic em-ba'-hee-no
Originfrom G1722 and the base of G939;

Definition

to walk on, i.e. embark (aboard a vessel), reach (a pool)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

come (get) into, enter (into), go (up) into, step in, take ship.
