Transliterationádikos
Phoneticad'-ee-kos
Originfrom G0001 (as a negative particle) and G1349;

Definition

unjust; by extension wicked; by implication, treacherous; specially, heathen

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

unjust, unrighteous.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἄδικος

Transliteration: adikos

Phonetic: ad'-ee-kos

Thayer Definition:

  1. descriptive of one who violates or has violated justice
    1. unjust
    2. unrighteous, sinful
    3. of one who deals fraudulently with others, deceitful

Origin: from G0001 (as a negative particle) and G1349

TDNT entry: 03:29,2

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
