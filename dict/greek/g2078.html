ἔσχατος

Transliterationéschatos
Phonetic es'-khat-os
Origina superlative probably from G2192 (in the sense of contiguity);

Definition

farthest, final (of place or time)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

ends of, last, latter end, lowest, uttermost.
