ἀπέχω

Transliterationapéchō
Phoneticap-ekh'-o
Originfrom G575 and G2192;

Definition

(actively) to have out, i.e. receive in full; (intransitively) to keep (oneself) away, i.e. be distant (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be, have, receive.
