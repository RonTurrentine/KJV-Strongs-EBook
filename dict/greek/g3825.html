πάλιν

Transliterationpálin
Phoneticpal'-in
Originprobably from the same as G3823 (through the idea of oscillatory repetition);

Definition

(adverbially) anew, i.e. (of place) back, (of time) once more, or (conjunctionally) furthermore or on the other hand

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

again.
