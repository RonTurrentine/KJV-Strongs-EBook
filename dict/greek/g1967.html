ἐπιούσιος

Transliterationepioúsios
Phonetic ep-ee-oo'-see-os
Originperhaps from the same as G1966;

Definition

tomorrow's; but more probably from G1909 and a derivative of the present participle feminine of G1510; for subsistence, i.e. needful

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

daily.
