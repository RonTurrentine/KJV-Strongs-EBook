πρόθεσις

Transliterationpróthesis
Phoneticproth'-es-is
Originfrom G4388;

Definition

a setting forth, i.e. (figuratively) proposal (intention); specially, the show-bread (in the Temple) as exposed before God

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

purpose, shew(-bread).
