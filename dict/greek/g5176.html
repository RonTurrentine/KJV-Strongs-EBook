τρώγω

Transliterationtrṓgō
Phonetic tro'-go
Originprobably strengthened from a collateral form of the base of G5134 and G5147 through the idea of corrosion or wear;

Definition

or perhaps rather of a base of G5167 and G5149 through the idea of a craunching sound; to gnaw or chew, i.e. (generally) to eat

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

eat.
