στοιχέω

Transliterationstoichéō
Phoneticstoy-kheh'-o
Originfrom a derivative of στείχω (to range in regular line);

Definition

to march in (military) rank (keep step), i.e. (figuratively) to conform to virtue and piety

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

walk (orderly).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: στοιχέω

Transliteration: stoicheō

Phonetic: stoy-kheh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to proceed in a row as the march of a soldier, go in order
    1. metaphorically to go on prosperously, to turn out well
  2. to walk
    1. to direct one's life, to live

Origin: from a derivative of steicho (to range in regular line)

TDNT entry: 18:06,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
