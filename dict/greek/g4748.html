στοιχέω

Transliterationstoichéō
Phoneticstoy-kheh'-o
Originfrom a derivative of στείχω (to range in regular line);

Definition

to march in (military) rank (keep step), i.e. (figuratively) to conform to virtue and piety

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

walk (orderly).
