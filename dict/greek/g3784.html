ὀφείλω

Transliterationopheílō
Phoneticof-i'-lo
Originprobably from the base of G3786 (through the idea of accruing);

Definition

to owe (pecuniarily); figuratively, to be under obligation (ought, must, should); morally, to fail in duty

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

behove, be bound, (be) debt(-or), (be) due(-ty), be guilty (indebted), (must) need(-s), ought, owe, should.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὀφείλω ὀφειλέω

Transliteration: opheilō opheileō

Phonetic: of-i'-lo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to owe
    1. to owe money, be in debt for
      1. that which is due, the debt
  2. metaphorically the goodwill due

Origin: "or (in certain tenses) its prolonged form opheileo of-i-leh'-o; probably from the base of G3786 (through the idea of accruing)"

TDNT entry: 14:19,7

Part(s) of speech: Verb
