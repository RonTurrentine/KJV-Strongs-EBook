ὀφείλω

Transliterationopheílō
Phoneticof-i'-lo
Originprobably from the base of G3786 (through the idea of accruing);

Definition

to owe (pecuniarily); figuratively, to be under obligation (ought, must, should); morally, to fail in duty

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

behove, be bound, (be) debt(-or), (be) due(-ty), be guilty (indebted), (must) need(-s), ought, owe, should.
