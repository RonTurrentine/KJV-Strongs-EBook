σωφρονίζω

Transliterationsōphronízō
Phoneticso-fron-id'-zo
Originfrom G4998;

Definition

to make of sound mind, i.e. (figuratively) to discipline or correct

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

teach to be sober.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: σωφρονίζω

Transliteration: sōphronizō

Phonetic: so-fron-id'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. restore one to his senses
  2. to moderate, control, curb, disciple
  3. to hold one to his duty
  4. to admonish, to exhort earnestly

Origin: from G4998

TDNT entry: 25:24,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
