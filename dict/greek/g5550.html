χρόνος

Transliterationchrónos
Phonetickhron'-os
Originof uncertain derivation;

Definition

a space of time (in general, and thus properly distinguished from G2540, which designates a fixed or special occasion; and from G0165, which denotes a particular period) or interval; by extension, an individual opportunity; by implication, delay

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

+ years old, season, space, (X often-)time(-s), (a) while.
