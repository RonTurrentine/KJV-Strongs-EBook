πίνω

Transliterationpínō
Phonetic pee'-no
Origina prolonged form of πίωpee'-o; which (together with another form) πόωpo'-o; occurs only as an alternate in certain tenses;

Definition

to imbibe (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

drink.
