Originfrom G5228;

Definition

insolence (as over-bearing), i.e. insult, injury

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

harm, hurt, reproach.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὕβρις

Transliteration: hubris

Phonetic: hoo'-bris

Thayer Definition:

  1. insolence
    1. impudence, pride, haughtiness
  2. a wrong springing from insolence, an injury, affront, insult
  3. mental injury and wantonness of its infliction being prominent
  4. injury inflicted by the violence of a tempest

Origin: from G5228

TDNT entry: 12:55,1

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
