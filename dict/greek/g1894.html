Transliterationepeidḗ
Phoneticep-i-day'
Originfrom G1893 and G1211;

Definition

since now, i.e. (of time) when, or (of cause) whereas

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

after that, because, for (that, -asmuch as), seeing, since.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐπειδή

Transliteration: epeidē

Phonetic: ep-i-day'

Thayer Definition:

  1. when now, since now
    1. of time: when now, after that
    2. of cause: since, seeing that, forasmuch as

Origin: from G1893 and G1211

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Conjunction
