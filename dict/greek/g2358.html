θριαμβεύω

Transliterationthriambeúō
Phonetic three-am-byoo'-o
Originfrom a prolonged compound of the base of G2360;

Definition

and a derivative of G680 (meaning a noisy iambus, sung in honor of Bacchus); to make an acclamatory procession, i.e. (figuratively) to conquer or (by Hebraism) to give victory

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(cause) to triumph (over).
