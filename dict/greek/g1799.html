Transliterationenṓpion
Phoneticen-o'-pee-on
Originneuter of a compound of G1722 and a derivative of G3700;

Definition

in the face of (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

before, in the presence (sight) of, to.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐνώπιον

Transliteration: enōpion

Phonetic: en-o'-pee-on

Thayer Definition:

  1. in the presence of, before
    1. of occupied place: in that place which is before, or over against, opposite, any one and towards which another turns his eyes

Origin: neuter of a compound of G1722 and a derivative of G3700

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Preposition
