ὑπέρακμος

Transliterationhypérakmos
Phonetic hoop-er'-ak-mos
Originfrom G5228 and the base of G188;

Definition

beyond the "acme", i.e. figuratively (of a daughter) past the bloom (prime) of youth

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

+ pass the flower of (her) age.
