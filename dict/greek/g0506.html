Transliterationanypótaktos

Originfrom G1 (as a negative particle) and a presumed derivative of G5293;

Definition

unsubdued, i.e. insubordinate (in fact or temper)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

disobedient, that is not put under, unruly.
