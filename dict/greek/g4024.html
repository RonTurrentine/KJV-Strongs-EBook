περιζώννυμι

Transliterationperizṓnnymi
Phoneticper-id-zone'-noo-mee
Originfrom G4012 and G2224;

Definition

to gird all around, i.e. (middle voice or passive) to fasten on one's belt (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage
