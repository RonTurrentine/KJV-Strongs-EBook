εὐτραπελία

Transliterationeutrapelía
Phoneticyoo-trap-el-ee'-ah
Originfrom a compound of G2095 and a derivative of the base of G5157 (meaning well-turned, i.e. ready at repartee, jocose);

Definition

witticism, i.e. (in a vulgar sense) ribaldry

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

jesting.
