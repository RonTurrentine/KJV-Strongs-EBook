μέγας

Transliterationmégas
Phonetic meg'-as
Origin(including the prolonged forms, feminine μεγάλη, plural μεγάλοι, etc.; compare also G3176, G3187);

Definition

big (literally or figuratively, in a very wide application)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(+ fear) exceedingly, great(-est), high, large, loud, mighty, + (be) sore (afraid), strong, X to years.
