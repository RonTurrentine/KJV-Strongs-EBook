φορτίζω

Transliterationphortízō
Phoneticfor-tid'-zo
Originfrom G5414;

Definition

to load up (properly, as a vessel or animal), i.e. (figuratively) to overburden with ceremony (or spiritual anxiety)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

lade, by heavy laden.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: φορτίζω

Transliteration: phortizō

Phonetic: for-tid'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to place a burden upon, to load
  2. metaphorically to load one with a burden (of rites and unwarranted precepts)

Origin: from G5414
