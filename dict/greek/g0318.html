ἀνάγκη

Transliterationanánkē
Phonetican-ang-kay'
Originfrom G0303 and the base of G0043;

Definition

constraint (literally or figuratively); by implication, distress

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

distress, must needs, (of) necessity(-sary), needeth, needful.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀναγκή

Transliteration: anagkē

Phonetic: an-ang-kay'

Thayer Definition:

  1. necessity, imposed either by the circumstances, or by law of duty regarding to one's advantage, custom, argument
  2. calamity, distress, straits

Origin: from G0303 and the base of G0043

TDNT entry: 06:44,6

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
