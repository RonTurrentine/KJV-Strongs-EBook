βίος

Transliterationbíos
Phoneticbee'-os
Origina primary word;

Definition

life, i.e. (literally) the present state of existence; by implication, the means of livelihood

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

good, life, living.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: βίος

Transliteration: bios

Phonetic: bee'-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. life
    1. life extensively
      1. the period or course of life
    2. that by which life is sustained, resources, wealth, goods

Origin: a primary word

TDNT entry: 15:52,3

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
