ποῦ

Transliterationpoû
Phoneticpoo
Origingenitive case of an interrogative pronoun πός (what) otherwise obsolete (perhaps the same as G4225 used with the rising slide of inquiry);

Definition

as adverb of place; at (by implication, to) what locality

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

where, whither.
