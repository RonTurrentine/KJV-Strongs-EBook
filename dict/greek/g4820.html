συμβάλλω

Transliterationsymbállō
Phoneticsoom-bal'-lo
Originfrom G4862 and G0906;

Definition

to combine, i.e. (in speaking) to converse, consult, dispute, (mentally) to consider, (by implication) to aid, (personally) to join, attack

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

confer, encounter, help, make, meet with, ponder.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: συμβάλλω

Transliteration: sumballō

Phonetic: soom-bal'-lo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to throw together, to bring together
    1. to converse
    2. to bring together in one's mind, confer with one's self
    3. to come together, meet
      1. to encounter in a hostile sense
      2. to fight with one
      3. to bring together of one's property, to contribute, aid, help

Origin: from G4862 and G0906

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
