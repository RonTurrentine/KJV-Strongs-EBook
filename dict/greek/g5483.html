χαρίζομαι

Transliterationcharízomai
Phonetickhar-id'-zom-ahee
Originmiddle voice from G5485;

Definition

to grant as a favor, i.e. gratuitously, in kindness, pardon or rescue

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

deliver, (frankly) forgive, (freely) give, grant.
