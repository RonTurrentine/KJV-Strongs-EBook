παρακαταθήκη

Transliterationparakatathḗkē
Phonetic par-ak-at-ath-ay'-kay
Originfrom a compound of G3844 and G2698;

Definition

something put down alongside, i.e. a deposit (sacred trust)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

that (thing) which is committed (un-)to (trust).
