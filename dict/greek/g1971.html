ἐπιποθέω

Transliterationepipothéō
Phoneticep-ee-poth-eh'-o
Originfrom G1909 and potheo (to yearn);

Definition

to dote upon, i.e. intensely crave possession (lawfully or wrongfully)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(earnestly) desire (greatly), (greatly) long (after), lust.
