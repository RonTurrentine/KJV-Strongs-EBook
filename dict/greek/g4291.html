προΐστημι

Transliterationproḯstēmi
Phoneticpro-is'-tay-mee
Originfrom G4253 and G2476;

Definition

to stand before, i.e. (in rank) to preside, or (by implication) to practise

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

maintain, be over, rule.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: προΐ́στημι

Transliteration: proistēmi

Phonetic: pro-is'-tay-mee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to set or place before
    1. to set over
    2. to be over, to superintend, preside over
    3. to be a protector or guardian
      1. to give aid
    4. to care for, give attention to
      1. profess honest occupations

Origin: from G4253 and G2476

TDNT entry: 6:700,*

Part(s) of speech: Verb
