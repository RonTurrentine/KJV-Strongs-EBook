γέεννα

Transliterationgéenna
Phoneticgheh'-en-nah
Originof Hebrew origin (H1516 and H2011);

Definition

valley of (the son of) Hinnom; ge-henna (or Ge-Hinnom), a valley of Jerusalem, used (figuratively) as a name for the place (or state) of everlasting punishment

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

hell.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: γέεννα

Transliteration: geenna

Phonetic: gheh'-en-nah

Thayer Definition:

  1. Hell is the place of the future punishment call "Gehenna" or "Gehenna of fire" . This was originally the valley of Hinnom, south of Jerusalem, where the filth and dead animals of the city were cast out and burned; a fit symbol of the wicked and their future destruction.

Origin: of Hebrew origin H1516 and H2011
