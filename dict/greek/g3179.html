μεθίστημι

Transliterationmethístēmi
Phonetic meth-is'-tay-mee
Originfrom G3326 and G2476;

Definition

to transfer, i.e. carry away, depose or (figuratively) exchange, seduce

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

put out, remove, translate, turn away.
