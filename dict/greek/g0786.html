ἄσπονδος

Transliterationáspondos
Phoneticas'-pon-dos
Originfrom G0001 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of G4689;

Definition

literally, without libation (which usually accompanied a treaty), i.e. (by implication) truceless

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

implacable, truce-breaker.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἄσπονδος

Transliteration: aspondos

Phonetic: as'-pon-dos

Thayer Definition:

  1. without a treaty or covenant
    1. of things not mutually agreed upon e.g. abstinences from hostilities
  2. that cannot be persuaded to enter into a covenant, implacable

Origin: from G0001 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of G4689

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
