γινώσκω

Transliterationginṓskō
Phoneticghin-oce'-ko
Origina prolonged form of a primary verb;

Definition

to "know" (absolutely) in a great variety of applications and with many implications (as follow, with others not thus clearly expressed)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

allow, be aware (of), feel, (have) know(-ledge), perceived, be resolved, can speak, be sure, understand.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: γινώσκω

Transliteration: ginōskō

Phonetic: ghin-oce'-ko

Thayer Definition:

  1. to learn to know, come to know, get a knowledge of perceive, feel
    1. to become known
  2. to know, understand, perceive, have knowledge of
    1. to understand
    2. to know
  3. Jewish idiom for sexual intercourse between a man and a woman
  4. to become acquainted with, to know

Origin: a prolonged form of a primary verb

TDNT entry: 12:29,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
