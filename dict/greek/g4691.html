σπερμολόγος

Transliterationspermológos
Phoneticsper-mol-og'-os
Originfrom G4690 and G3004;

Definition

a seed-picker (as the crow), i.e. (figuratively) a sponger, loafer (specially, a gossip or trifler in talk)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

babbler.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: σπερμολόγος

Transliteration: spermologos

Phonetic: sper-mol-og'-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. picking up seed
    1. of birds, especially of the crow or daw that picks up grain in fields
  2. metaphorically
    1. lounging about the market place and picking up a substance by whatever may chance to fall from the loads of merchandise
    2. hence, beggarly, abject, vile, (a parasite)
    3. getting a living by flattery and bufferoonery
    4. an empty talker, babbler

Origin: from G4690 and G3004

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
