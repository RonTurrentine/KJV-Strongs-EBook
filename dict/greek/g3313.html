μέρος

Transliterationméros
Phoneticmer'-os
Originfrom an obsolete but more primary form of μείρομαι (to get as a section or allotment);

Definition

a division or share (literally or figuratively, in a wide application)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

behalf, course, coast, craft, particular (+ -ly), part (+ -ly), piece, portion, respect, side, some sort(-what).
