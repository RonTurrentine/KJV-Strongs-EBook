ἄζυμος

Transliterationázymos
Phoneticad'-zoo-mos
Originfrom G1 (as a negative particle) and G2219;

Definition

unleavened, i.e. (figuratively) uncorrupted; (in the neutral plural) specially (by implication) the Passover week

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage
