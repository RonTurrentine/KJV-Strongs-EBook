σκάνδαλον

Transliterationskándalon
Phoneticskan'-dal-on
Origin("scandal"); probably from a derivative of G2578;

Definition

a trap-stick (bent sapling), i.e. snare (figuratively, cause of displeasure or sin)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

occasion to fall (of stumbling), offence, thing that offends, stumblingblock.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: σκάνδαλον

Transliteration: skandalon

Phonetic: skan'-dal-on

Thayer Definition:

  1. the movable stick or trigger of a trap, a trap stick
    1. a trap, snare
    2. any impediment placed in the way and causing one to stumble or fall, (a stumbling block, occasion of stumbling), i.e. a rock which is a cause of stumbling
    3. fig. applied to Jesus Christ, whose person and career were so contrary to the expectations of the Jews concerning the Messiah, that they rejected him and by their obstinacy made shipwreck of their salvation
  2. any person or thing by which one is (entrapped) drawn into error or sin

Origin: probably from a derivative of G2578

TDNT entry: 12:39,1

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
