ζητέω

Transliterationzētéō
Phoneticdzay-teh'-o
Originof uncertain affinity;

Definition

to seek (literally or figuratively); specially, (by Hebraism) to worship (God), or (in a bad sense) to plot (against life)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be (go) about, desire, endeavour, enquire (for), require, (X will) seek (after, for, means).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ζητέω

Transliteration: zēteō

Phonetic: dzay-teh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to seek in order to find
    1. to seek a thing
    2. to seek [in order to find out] by thinking, meditating, reasoning, to enquire into
    3. to seek after, seek for, aim at, strive after
  2. to seek, i.e. require, demand
    1. to crave, demand something from someone

Origin: of uncertain affinity

TDNT entry: 16:52,3

Part(s) of speech: Verb
