χειροτονέω

Transliterationcheirotonéō
Phonetickhi-rot-on-eh'-o
Originfrom a compound of G5495 and τείνω (to stretch);

Definition

to be a hand-reacher or voter (by raising the hand), i.e. (generally) to select or appoint

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

choose, ordain.
