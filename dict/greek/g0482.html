ἀντιλαμβάνομαι

Transliterationantilambánomai
Phonetic an-tee-lam-ban'-om-ahee
Originfrom G473 and the middle voice of G2983;

Definition

to take hold of in turn, i.e. succor; also to participate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

help, partaker, support.
