ἐκφέρω

Transliterationekphérō
Phoneticek-fer'-o
Originfrom G1537 and G5342;

Definition

to bear out (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bear, bring forth, carry forth (out).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐκφέρω

Transliteration: ekpherō

Phonetic: ek-fer'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to carry out, to bear forth
    1. the dead for burial
  2. to bring, i.e. lead out
  3. to bring forth, i.e. produce
    1. of the earth bearing plants

Origin: from G1537 and G5342

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
