ἐπισκιάζω

Transliterationepiskiázō
Phoneticep-ee-skee-ad'-zo
Originfrom G1909 and a derivative of G4639;

Definition

to cast a shade upon, i.e. (by analogy) to envelop in a haze of brilliancy; figuratively, to invest with preternatural influence

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

overshadow.
