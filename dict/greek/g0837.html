αὐξάνω

Transliterationauxánō
Phoneticowx-an'-o
Origina prolonged form of a primary verb;

Definition

to grow ("wax"), i.e. enlarge (literal or figurative, active or passive)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

grow (up), (give the) increase.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: αὐξάνω

Transliteration: auxanō

Phonetic: owx-an'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to cause to grow, augment
  2. to increase, become greater
  3. to grow, increase
    1. of plants
    2. of infants
    3. of a multitude of people
    4. of inward Christian growth

Origin: a prolonged form of a primary verb

TDNT entry: 8:517,*

Part(s) of speech: Verb
