νεκρός

Transliterationnekrós
Phoneticnek-ros'
Originfrom an apparently primary νέκυς (a corpse);

Definition

dead (literally or figuratively; also as noun)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dead.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: νεκρός

Transliteration: nekros

Phonetic: nek-ros'

Thayer Definition:

  1. properly
    1. one that has breathed his last, lifeless
    2. deceased, departed, one whose soul is in heaven or hell
    3. destitute of life, without life, inanimate
  2. metaphorically
    1. spiritually dead
      1. destitute of a life that recognises and is devoted to God, because given up to trespasses and sins
      2. inactive as respects doing right
    2. destitute of force or power, inactive, inoperative

Origin: from an apparently primary nekus (a corpse)

TDNT entry: 18:52,6

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
