τόπος

Transliterationtópos
Phonetictop'-os
Originapparently a primary word;

Definition

a spot (generally in space, but limited by occupancy; whereas G5561 is a large but particular locality), i.e. location (as a position, home, tract, etc.); figuratively, condition, opportunity; specially, a scabbard

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

coast, licence, place, X plain, quarter, + rock, room, where.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: τόπος

Transliteration: topos

Phonetic: top'-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. place, any portion or space marked off, as it were from surrounding space
    1. an inhabited place, as a city, village, district
    2. a place (passage) in a book
  2. metaphorically
    1. the condition or station held by one in any company or assembly
    2. opportunity, power, occasion for acting

Origin: apparently a primary word

TDNT entry: 11:07,1

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
