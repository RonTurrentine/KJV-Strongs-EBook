ἐλαύνω

Transliterationelaúnō
Phoneticel-ow'-no
Origina prolonged form of a primary verb (obsolete except in certain tenses as an alternative of this) of uncertain affinity;

Definition

to push (as wind, oars or dæmonical power)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

carry, drive, row.
