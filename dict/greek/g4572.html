σεαυτοῦ

Transliterationseautoû
Phonetic seh-ow-too'
Origingenitive case from G4571 and G846, also dative case of the same, σεαυτῷseh-ow-to', and accusative case σεαυτόνseh-ow-ton', likewise contracted σαυτοῦsow-too', σαυτῷsow-to', and σαυτόνsow-ton', respectively;

Definition

of (with, to) thyself

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

thee, thine own self, (thou) thy(-self).
