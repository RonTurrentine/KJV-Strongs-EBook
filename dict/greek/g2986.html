λαμπρός

Transliterationlamprós
Phoneticlam-pros'
Originfrom the same as G2985;

Definition

radiant; by analogy, limpid; figuratively, magnificent or sumptuous (in appearance)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bright, clear, gay, goodly, gorgeous, white.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: λαμπρός

Transliteration: lampros

Phonetic: lam-pros'

Thayer Definition:

  1. shining
    1. brilliant
    2. clear, transparent
  2. splendid, magnificent
    1. splendid things, i.e. luxuries or elegancies in dress or style

Origin: from the same as G2985
