παιδεία

Transliterationpaideía
Phoneticpahee-di'-ah
Originfrom G3811;

Definition

tutorage, i.e. education or training; by implication, disciplinary correction

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

chastening, chastisement, instruction, nurture.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: παιδεία

Transliteration: paideia

Phonetic: pahee-di'-ah

Thayer Definition:

  1. the whole training and education of children (which relates to the cultivation of mind and morals, and employs for this purpose now commands and admonitions, now reproof and punishment) It also includes the training and care of the body
  2. whatever in adults also cultivates the soul, especially by correcting mistakes and curbing passions.
    1. instruction which aims at increasing virtue
    2. chastisement, chastening, (of the evils with which God visits men for their amendment)

Origin: from G3811

TDNT entry: 14:56,8

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
