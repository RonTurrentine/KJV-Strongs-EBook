ἐκπίπτω

Transliterationekpíptō
Phoneticek-pip'-to
Originfrom G1537 and G4098;

Definition

to drop away; specially, be driven out of one's course; figuratively, to lose, become inefficient

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be cast, fail, fall (away, off), take none effect.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐκπίπτω

Transliteration: ekpiptō

Phonetic: ek-pip'-to

Thayer Definition:

  1. to fall out of, to fall down from, to fall off
  2. metaphorically
    1. to fall from a thing, to lose it
    2. to perish, to fall
      1. to fall from a place from which one cannot keep
      2. fall from a position
      3. to fall powerless, to fall to the ground, be without effect
        1. of the divine promise of salvation

Origin: from G1537 and G4098

TDNT entry: 08:47,8

Part(s) of speech: Verb
