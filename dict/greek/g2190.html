ἐχθρός

Transliterationechthrós
Phoneticech-thros'
Originfrom a primary ἔχθω (to hate);

Definition

hateful (passively, odious, or actively, hostile); usually as a noun, an adversary (especially Satan)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

enemy, foe.
