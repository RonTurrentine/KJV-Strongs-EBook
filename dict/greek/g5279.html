ὑπομιμνήσκω

Transliterationhypomimnḗskō
Phonetichoop-om-im-nace'-ko
Originfrom G5259 and G3403;

Definition

to remind quietly, i.e. suggest to the (middle voice, one's own) memory

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

put in mind, remember, bring to (put in) remembrance.
