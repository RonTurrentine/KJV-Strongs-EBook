Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

certify, declare, make known, give to understand, do to wit, wot.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: γνωρίζω

Transliteration: gnōrizō

Phonetic: gno-rid'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to make known
    1. to become known, be recognised
  2. to know, to gain knowledge of, have thorough knowledge of
    1. in earlier Greek it means "to gain a knowledge of" or "have thorough knowledge of"

Origin: from a derivative of G1097

TDNT entry: 12:58,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
