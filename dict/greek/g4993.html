σωφρονέω

Transliterationsōphronéō
Phoneticso-fron-eh'-o
Originfrom G4998;

Definition

to be of sound mind, i.e. sane, (figuratively) moderate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be in right mind, be sober (minded), soberly.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: σωφρονέω

Transliteration: sōphroneō

Phonetic: so-fron-eh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to be of sound mind
    1. to be in one's right mind
    2. to exercise self control
      1. to put a moderate estimate upon one's self, think of one's self soberly
      2. to curb one's passions

Origin: from G4998

TDNT entry: 25:17,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
