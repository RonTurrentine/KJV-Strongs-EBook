ἐπίσκοπος

Transliterationepískopos
Phoneticep-is'-kop-os
Originfrom G1909 and G4649 (in the sense of G1983);

Definition

a superintendent, i.e. Christian officer in genitive case charge of a (or the) church (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bishop, overseer.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐπίσκοπος

Transliteration: episkopos

Phonetic: ep-is'-kop-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. an overseer
    1. a man charged with the duty of seeing that things to be done by others are done rightly, any curator, guardian or superintendent
    2. the superintendent, elder, or overseer of a Christian church

Origin: from G1909 and G4649 (in the sense of G1983)

TDNT entry: 12:08,2

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
