φωτίζω

Transliterationphōtízō
Phoneticfo-tid'-zo
Originfrom G5457;

Definition

to shed rays, i.e. to shine or (transitively) to brighten up (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

enlighten, illuminate, (bring to, give) light, make to see.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: φωτίζω

Transliteration: phōtizō

Phonetic: fo-tid'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to give light, to shine
  2. to enlighten, light up, illumine
  3. to bring to light, render evident
    1. to cause something to exist and thus come to light and become clear to all
  4. to enlighten, spiritually, imbue with saving knowledge
    1. to instruct, to inform, teach
    2. to give understanding to

Origin: from G5457

TDNT entry: 14:10,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
