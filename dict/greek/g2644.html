καταλλάσσω

Transliterationkatallássō
Phonetickat-al-las'-so
Originfrom G2596 and G0236;

Definition

to change mutually, i.e. (figuratively) to compound a difference

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

reconcile.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: καταλλάσσω

Transliteration: katallassō

Phonetic: kat-al-las'-so

Thayer Definition:

  1. to change, exchange, as coins for others of equivalent value
    1. to reconcile (those who are at variance)
    2. return to favour with, be reconciled to one
    3. to receive one into favour

Origin: from G2596 and G0236

TDNT entry: 05:14,4

Part(s) of speech: Verb
