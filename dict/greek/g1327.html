διέξοδος

Transliterationdiéxodos
Phoneticdee-ex'-od-os
Originfrom G1223 and G1841;

Definition

an outlet through, i.e. probably an open square (from which roads diverge)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

highway.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: διέξοδος

Transliteration: diexodos

Phonetic: dee-ex'-od-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. a way out through, outlet, exit
    1. literally the ways through which ways go out
    2. the places before the city where the roads from the country terminate, therefore outlets of the country highways, also entrances into the city
    3. of boundaries of countries

Origin: from G1223 and G1841

TDNT entry: 06:43,7

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
