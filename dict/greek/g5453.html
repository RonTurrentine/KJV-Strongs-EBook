φύω

Transliterationphýō
Phoneticfoo'-o
Origina primary verb;

Definition

probably originally, to "puff" or blow, i.e. to swell up; but only used in the implied sense, to germinate or grow (sprout, produce), literally or figuratively

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

spring (up).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: φύω

Transliteration: phuō

Phonetic: foo'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to beget, bring forth, produce
  2. to be born, to spring up, to grow
  3. to shoot forth, spring up

Origin: "a primary verb, probably originally, to "puff" or blow, i.e. to swell up"

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
