ἡγέομαι

Transliterationhēgéomai
Phonetichayg-eh'-om-ahee
Originmiddle voice of a (presumed) strengthened form of G0071;

Definition

to lead, i.e. command (with official authority); figuratively, to deem, i.e. consider

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

account, (be) chief, count, esteem, governor, judge, have the rule over, suppose, think.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἡγέομαι

Transliteration: hēgeomai

Phonetic: hayg-eh'-om-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to lead
    1. to go before
    2. to be a leader
      1. to rule, command
      2. to have authority over
      3. a prince, of regal power, governor, viceroy, chief, leading as respects influence, controlling in counsel, overseers or leaders of the churches
      4. used of any kind of leader, chief, commander
      5. the leader in speech, chief, spokesman
  2. to consider, deem, account, think

Origin: middle voice of a (presumed) strengthened form of G0071

TDNT entry: 17:07,3

Part(s) of speech: Verb
