νύμφη

Transliterationnýmphē
Phoneticnoom-fay'
Originfrom a primary but obsolete verb νύπτω (to veil as a bride; compare Latin "nupto," to marry);

Definition

a young married woman (as veiled), including a betrothed girl; by implication, a son's wife

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bride, daughter in law.
