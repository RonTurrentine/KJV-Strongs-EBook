Definition

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

wind.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἄνεμος

Transliteration: anemos

Phonetic: an'-em-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. wind, a violent agitation and stream of air
  2. a very strong tempestuous wind
  3. the four principal or cardinal winds, hence the four corners of heaven

Origin: from the base of G0109

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
