κατόρθωμα

Transliterationkatórthōma
Phonetickat-or'-tho-mah
Originfrom a compound of G2596 and a derivative of G3717 (compare G1357);

Definition

something made fully upright, i.e. (figuratively) rectification (specially, good public administration)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage
