καταναρκάω

Transliterationkatanarkáō
Phonetickat-an-ar-kah'-o
Originfrom G2596 and ναρκάω (to be numb);

Definition

to grow utterly torpid, i.e. (by implication) slothful (figuratively, expensive)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be burdensome (chargeable).
