ὑποδείκνυμι

Transliterationhypodeíknymi
Phonetichoop-od-ike'-noo-mee
Originfrom G5259 and G1166;

Definition

to exhibit under the eyes, i.e. (figuratively) to exemplify (instruct, admonish)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

show, (fore-)warn.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὑποδείκνυμι

Transliteration: hupodeiknumi

Phonetic: hoop-od-ike'-noo-mee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to show by placing under (i.e. before) the eyes
  2. to show by words and arguments, i.e. to teach
  3. to show by make known future things

Origin: from G5259 and G1166

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
