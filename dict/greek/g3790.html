ὀφρῦς

Transliterationophrŷs
Phonetic of-roos'
Originperhaps from G3700 (through the idea of the shading or proximity to the organ of vision);

Definition

the eye-"brow" or forehead, i.e. (figuratively) the brink of a precipice

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

brow.
