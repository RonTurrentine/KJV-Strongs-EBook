τίθημι

Transliterationtíthēmi
Phonetictith'-ay-mee
Origina prolonged form of a primary θέωtheh'-o (which is used only as alternate in certain tenses);

Definition

to place (in the widest application, literally and figuratively; properly, in a passive or horizontal posture, and thus different from G2476, which properly denotes an upright and active position, while G2749 is properly reflexive and utterly prostrate)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

+ advise, appoint, bow, commit, conceive, give, X kneel down, lay (aside, down, up), make, ordain, purpose, put, set (forth), settle, sink down.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: τίθημι

Transliteration: tithēmi

Phonetic: tith'-ay-mee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to set, put, place
    1. to place or lay
    2. to put down, lay down
      1. to bend down
      2. to lay off or aside, to wear or carry no longer
      3. to lay by, lay aside money
    3. to set on (serve) something to eat or drink
    4. to set forth, something to be explained by discourse
  2. to make
    1. to make (or set) for one's self or for one's use
  3. to set, fix establish
    1. to set forth
    2. to establish, ordain

Origin: a prolonged form of a primary theo theh'-o (which is used only as alternate in certain tenses)

TDNT entry: 10:32,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
