ὁ ὢν καί ὁ ἦν καί ὁ ἐρχόμενος

Transliterationho ṑn kaí ho ēn kaí ho erchómenos
Phoneticho own kahee ho ane kahee ho er-khom'-en-os
Origina phrase combining G3588 with the present participle and imperfect of G1510 and the present participle of G2064 by means of G2532;

Definition

the one being and the one that was and the one coming, i.e. the Eternal, as a divine epithet of Christ

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

which art (is, was), and (which) wast (is, was), and art (is) to come (shalt be).
