εὐπερίστατος

Transliterationeuperístatos
Phoneticyoo-per-is'-tat-os
Originfrom G2095 and a derivative of a presumed compound of G4012 and G2476;

Definition

well standing around, i.e. (a competitor) thwarting (a racer) in every direction (figuratively, of sin in genitive case)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

which doth so easily beset.
