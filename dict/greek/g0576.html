ἀποβαίνω

Transliterationapobaínō
Phoneticap-ob-ah'-ee-no
Originfrom G0575 and the base of G0939;

Definition

literally, to disembark; figuratively, to eventuate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

become, go out, turn.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀποβαίνω

Transliteration: apobainō

Phonetic: ap-ob-ah'-ee-no

Thayer Definition:

  1. to come down from, i.e. a ship
  2. to turn out, result, to be the outcome

Origin: from G0575 and the base of G0939

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
