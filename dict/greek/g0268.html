ἁμαρτωλός

Transliterationhamartōlós
Phoneticham-ar-to-los'
Originfrom G0264;

Definition

sinful, i.e. a sinner

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

sinful, sinner.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἁμαρτωλός

Transliteration: hamartōlos

Phonetic: ham-ar-to-los'

Thayer Definition:

  1. devoted to sin, a sinner
    1. not free from sin
    2. pre-eminently sinful, especially wicked
      1. all wicked men
      2. specifically of men stained with certain definite vices or crimes
        1. tax collectors, heathen

Origin: from G0264

TDNT entry: 06:17,5

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
