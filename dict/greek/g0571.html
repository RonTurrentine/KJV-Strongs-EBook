ἄπιστος

Transliterationápistos
Phoneticap'-is-tos
Originfrom G0001 (as a negative particle) and G4103;

Definition

(actively) disbelieving, i.e. without Christian faith (specially, a heathen); (passively) untrustworthy (person), or incredible (thing)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

that believeth not, faithless, incredible thing, infidel, unbeliever(-ing).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἄπιστος

Transliteration: apistos

Phonetic: ap'-is-tos

Thayer Definition:

  1. unfaithful, faithless, (not to be trusted, perfidious)
  2. incredible
    1. of things
  3. unbelieving, incredulous
    1. without trust (in God)

Origin: from G0001 (as a negative particle) and G4103

TDNT entry: 08:54,8

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
