παιδίσκη

Transliterationpaidískē
Phoneticpahee-dis'-kay
Originfeminine diminutive of G3816;

Definition

a girl, i.e. (specially), a female slave or servant

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bondmaid(-woman), damsel, maid(-en).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: παιδίσκη

Transliteration: paidiskē

Phonetic: pahee-dis'-kay

Thayer Definition:

  1. a young girl, damsel
  2. a maid-servant, a young female slave
