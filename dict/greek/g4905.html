συνέρχομαι

Transliterationsynérchomai
Phoneticsoon-er'-khom-ahee
Originfrom G4862 and G2064;

Definition

to convene, depart in company with, associate with, or (specially), cohabit (conjugally)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

accompany, assemble (with), come (together), come (company, go) with, resort.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: συνέρχομαι

Transliteration: sunerchomai

Phonetic: soon-er'-khom-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to come together
    1. to assemble
    2. of conjugal cohabitation
  2. to go (depart) or come with one, to accompany one

Origin: from G4862 and G2064

TDNT entry: 13:24,3

Part(s) of speech: Verb
