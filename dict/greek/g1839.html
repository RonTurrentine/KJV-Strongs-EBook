ἐξίστημι

Transliterationexístēmi
Phoneticex-is'-tay-mee
Originfrom G1537 and G2476;

Definition

to put (stand) out of wits, i.e. astound, or (reflexively) become astounded, insane

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

amaze, be (make) astonished, be beside self (selves), bewitch, wonder.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐξίστημι

Transliteration: existēmi

Phonetic: ex-is'-tay-mee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to throw out of position, displace
    1. to amaze, to astonish, throw into wonderment
    2. to be amazed, astounded
    3. to be out of one's mind, besides one's self, insane

Origin: from G1537 and G2476

TDNT entry: 09:39,2

Part(s) of speech: Verb
