ἀφίημι

Transliterationaphíēmi
Phoneticaf-ee'-ay-mee
Originfrom G0575 and ἵημι (to send; an intensive form of εἶμι, to go);

Definition

to send forth, in various applications (as follow)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cry, forgive, forsake, lay aside, leave, let (alone, be, go, have), omit, put (send) away, remit, suffer, yield up.
