ἀποδίδωμι

Transliterationapodídōmi
Phoneticap-od-eed'-o-mee
Originfrom G575 and G1325;

Definition

to give away, i.e. up, over, back, etc. (in various applications)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

deliver (again), give (again), (re-)pay(-ment be made), perform, recompense, render, requite, restore, reward, sell, yield.
