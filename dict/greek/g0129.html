αἷμα

Transliterationhaîma
Phonetichah'-ee-mah
Originof uncertain derivation;

Definition

blood, literally (of men or animals), figuratively (the juice of grapes) or specially (the atoning blood of Christ); by implication, bloodshed, also kindred

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

blood.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: αἷμα

Transliteration: aima

Phonetic: hah'-ee-mah

Thayer Definition:

  1. blood
    1. of man or animals
    2. refers to the seat of life
    3. of those things that resemble blood, grape juice
  2. blood shed, to be shed by violence, slay, murder

Origin: of uncertain derivation

TDNT entry: 03:52,3

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
