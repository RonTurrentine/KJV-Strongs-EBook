φωνέω

Transliterationphōnéō
Phoneticfo-neh'-o
Originfrom G5456;

Definition

to emit a sound (animal, human or instrumental); by implication, to address in words or by name, also in imitation

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

call (for), crow, cry.
