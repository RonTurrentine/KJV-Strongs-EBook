ἁμαρτάνω

Transliterationhamartánō
Phoneticham-ar-tan'-o
Originperhaps from G0001 (as a negative particle) and the base of G3313;

Definition

properly, to miss the mark (and so not share in the prize), i.e. (figuratively) to err, especially (morally) to sin

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

for your faults, offend, sin, trespass.
