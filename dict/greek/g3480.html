Ναζωραῖος

TransliterationNazōraîos
Phoneticnad-zo-rah'-yos
Originfrom G3478;

Definition

a Nazoræan, i.e. inhabitant of Nazareth; by extension, a Christian

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Nazarene, of Nazareth.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: Ναζωραῖος

Transliteration: Nazōraios

Phonetic: nad-zo-rah'-yos

Thayer Definition: Nazarite = "one separated"

  1. an inhabitant of Nazareth
  2. a title given to Jesus in the NT
  3. a name given to Christians by the Jews, Act 24:5

Origin: from G3478

TDNT entry: 18:34,6

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
