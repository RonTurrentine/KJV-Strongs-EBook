στοιβάς

Transliterationstoibás
Phoneticstoy-bas'
Originfrom a primary στείβω (to "step" or "stamp");

Definition

a spread (as if tramped flat) of loose materials for a couch, i.e. (by implication) a bough of a tree so employed

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

branch.
