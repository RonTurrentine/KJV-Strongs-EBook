ἐξαιτέομαι

Transliterationexaitéomai
Phoneticex-ahee-teh'-om-ahee
Originmiddle voice from G1537 and G0154;

Definition

to demand (for trial)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

desire.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐξαιτέομαι

Transliteration: exaiteomai

Phonetic: ex-ahee-teh'-om-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to ask from, demand of
    1. to ask or beg for one's self, to ask that one be given up to one from the power of another
    2. in a good sense
      1. to beg one from another
      2. ask for the pardon
      3. the safety of some one
    3. in a bad sense
      1. for torture
      2. for punishment

Origin: middle voice from G1537 and G0154

TDNT entry: 1:194,*

Part(s) of speech: Verb
