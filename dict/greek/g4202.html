πορνεία

Transliterationporneía
Phoneticpor-ni'-ah
Originfrom G4203;

Definition

harlotry (including adultery and incest); figuratively, idolatry

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fornication.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πορνεία

Transliteration: porneia

Phonetic: por-ni'-ah

Thayer Definition:

  1. illicit sexual intercourse
    1. adultery, fornication, homosexuality, lesbianism, intercourse with animals etc.
    2. sexual intercourse with close relatives; Lev. 18
    3. sexual intercourse with a divorced man or woman; Mar 10:11,Mar 10:12
  2. metaphorically the worship of idols
    1. of the defilement of idolatry, as incurred by eating the sacrifices offered to idols

Origin: from G4203

TDNT entry: 15:39,9

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
