Phoneticpar-at-ay-reh'-o
Originfrom G3844 and G5083;

Definition

to inspect alongside, i.e. note insidiously or scrupulously

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

observe, watch.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: παρατηρέω

Transliteration: paratēreō

Phonetic: par-at-ay-reh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to stand beside and watch, to watch assiduously, observe carefully
    1. to watch, attend to with the eyes
    2. of auguries, to see what he is going to do
    3. in a bad sense, to watch insidiously
    4. to watch one's self
    5. to observe, keep scrupulously
      1. to neglect nothing requisite to the religious observance of

Origin: from G3844 and G5083

TDNT entry: 10:26,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
