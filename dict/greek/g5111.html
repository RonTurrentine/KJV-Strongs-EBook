τολμάω

Transliterationtolmáō
Phonetictol-mah'-o
Originfrom τόλμα (boldness; probably itself from the base of G5056 through the idea of extreme conduct);

Definition

to venture (objectively or in act; while G2292 is rather subjective or in feeling); by implication, to be courageous

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be bold, boldly, dare, durst.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: τολμάω

Transliteration: tolmaō

Phonetic: tol-mah'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. not to dread or shun through fear
  2. to bear, endure
  3. to bring one's self to
  4. to be bold
  5. bear one's self boldly, deal boldly

Origin: from tolma (boldness, probably itself from the base of G5056 through the idea of extreme conduct)

TDNT entry: 11:01,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
