σήμερον

Transliterationsḗmeron
Phoneticsay'-mer-on
Originneuter (as adverb) of a presumed compound of the article G3588 (t changed to s) and G2250;

Definition

on the (i.e. this) day (or night current or just passed); generally, now (i.e. at present, hitherto)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

this (to-)day.
