προσανατίθημι

Transliterationprosanatíthēmi
Phoneticpros-an-at-ith'-ay-mee
Originfrom G4314 and G0394;

Definition

to lay up in addition, i.e. (middle voice and figuratively) to impart or (by implication) to consult

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

in conference add, confer.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: προσανατίθημι

Transliteration: prosanatithēmi

Phonetic: pros-an-at-ith'-ay-mee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to lay upon in addition to
  2. to lay upon one's self in addition
    1. to undertake besides
    2. to put one's self upon another by going to him
    3. to commit or betake one's self to another for the purpose of consulting him
    4. to consult, to take one into counsel
    5. to add from one's store
  3. to communicate, impart

Origin: from G4314 and G0394

TDNT entry: 06:53,6

Part(s) of speech: Verb
