λειτουργός

Transliterationleitourgós
Phoneticli-toorg-os'
Originfrom a derivative of G2992 and G2041;

Definition

a public servant, i.e. a functionary in the Temple or Gospel, or (genitive case) a worshipper (of God) or benefactor (of man)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

minister(-ed).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: λειτουργός

Transliteration: leitourgos

Phonetic: li-toorg-os'

Thayer Definition:

  1. a public minister, a servant of the state
  2. a minister, servant
    1. so of military labourers
    2. of the temple
      1. of one busied with holy things
      2. of a priest
    3. of the servants of a king

Origin: from a derivative of G2992 and G2041

TDNT entry: 07:49,5

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
