λειτουργός

Transliterationleitourgós
Phoneticli-toorg-os'
Originfrom a derivative of G2992 and G2041;

Definition

a public servant, i.e. a functionary in the Temple or Gospel, or (genitive case) a worshipper (of God) or benefactor (of man)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

minister(-ed).
