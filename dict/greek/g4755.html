στρατηγός

Transliterationstratēgós
Phoneticstrat-ay-gos'
Originfrom the base of G4756 and G71 or G2233;

Definition

a general, i.e. (by implication or analogy) a (military) governor (prætor), the chief (præfect) of the (Levitical) temple-wardens

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

captain, magistrate.
