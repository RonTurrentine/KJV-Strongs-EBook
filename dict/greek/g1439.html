ἐάω

Transliterationeáō
Phoneticeh-ah'-o
Originof uncertain affinity;

Definition

to let be, i.e. permit or leave alone

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

commit, leave, let (alone), suffer.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐάω

Transliteration: eaō

Phonetic: eh-ah'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to allow, permit, let
  2. to allow one to do as he wishes, not to restrain, to let alone
  3. to give up, let go, leave

Origin: of uncertain affinity

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
