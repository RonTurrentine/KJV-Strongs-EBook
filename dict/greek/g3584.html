ξηρός

Transliterationxērós
Phoneticxay-ros'
Originfrom the base of G3582 (through the idea of scorching);

Definition

arid; by implication, shrunken, earth (as opposed to water)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dry land, withered.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ξηρός

Transliteration: xēros

Phonetic: xay-ros'

Thayer Definition:

  1. dry
    1. of members of the body deprived of their natural juices, shrunk, wasted, withered
    2. of the land in distinction from the water

Origin: from the base of G3582 (through the idea of scorching)

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
