ἀσπάζομαι

Transliterationaspázomai
Phoneticas-pad'-zom-ahee
Originfrom G1 (as a particle of union) and a presumed form of G4685;

Definition

to enfold in the arms, i.e. (by implication) to salute, (figuratively) to welcome

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

embrace, greet, salute, take leave.
