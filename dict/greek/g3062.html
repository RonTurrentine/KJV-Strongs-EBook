λοιποί

Transliterationloipoí
Phoneticloy-poy'
Originmasculine plural of a derivative of G3007;

Definition

remaining ones

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

other, which remain, remnant, residue, rest.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: λοιποί

Transliteration: loipoi

Phonetic: loy-poy'

Thayer Definition:

  1. remaining, the rest
    1. the rest of any number or class under consideration
    2. with a certain distinction and contrast, the rest, who are not of a specific class or number
    3. the rest of the things that remain

Origin: masculine plural of a derivative of G3007

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Adverb
