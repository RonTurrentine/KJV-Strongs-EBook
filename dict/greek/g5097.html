τιμωρέω

Transliterationtimōréō
Phonetictim-o-reh'-o
Originfrom a compound of G5092 and οὖρος ouros (a guard);

Definition

properly, to protect one's honor, i.e. to avenge (inflict a penalty)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

punish.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: τιμωρέω

Transliteration: timōreō

Phonetic: tim-o-reh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to be a guardian or avenger of honour
    1. to succour, come to the help of
  2. to avenge
  3. in the NT, to take vengeance on one, to punish

Origin: from a comparative of G5092 and ouros (a guard)

TDNT entry: None
