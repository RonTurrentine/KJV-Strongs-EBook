Definition

an image (i.e. for worship); by implication, a heathen god, or (plural) the worship of such

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

idol.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: εἴδωλον

Transliteration: eidōlon

Phonetic: i'-do-lon

Thayer Definition:

  1. an image, likeness
    1. i.e. whatever represents the form of an object, either real or imaginary
    2. used of the shades of the departed, apparitions, spectres, phantoms of the mind, etc.
  2. the image of an heathen god
  3. a false god

Origin: "from G1491; an image (i.e. for worship)"

TDNT entry: 08:15,2

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
