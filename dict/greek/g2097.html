εὐαγγελίζω

Transliterationeuangelízō
Phonetic yoo-ang-ghel-id'-zo
Originfrom G2095 and G32;

Definition

to announce good news ("evangelize") especially the gospel

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

declare, bring (declare, show) glad (good) tidings, preach (the gospel).
