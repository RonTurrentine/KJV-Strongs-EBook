ἀσθενέω

Transliterationasthenéō
Phoneticas-then-eh'-o
Originfrom G0772;

Definition

to be feeble (in any sense)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be diseased, impotent folk (man), (be) sick, (be, be made) weak.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀσθενέω

Transliteration: astheneō

Phonetic: as-then-eh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to be weak, feeble, to be without strength, powerless
  2. to be weak in means, needy, poor
  3. to be feeble, sick

Origin: from G0772

TDNT entry: 09:10,8

Part(s) of speech: Verb
