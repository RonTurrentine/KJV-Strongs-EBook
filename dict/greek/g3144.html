μάρτυς

Transliterationmártys
Phoneticmar'-toos
Originof uncertain affinity;

Definition

a witness (literally (judicially) or figuratively (genitive case)); by analogy, a "martyr"

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

martyr, record, witness.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: μάρτυς

Transliteration: martus

Phonetic: mar'-toos

Thayer Definition:

  1. a witness
    1. in a legal sense
    2. an historical sense
      1. one who is a spectator of anything, e.g. of a contest
    3. in an ethical sense
      1. those who after his example have proved the strength and genuineness of their faith in Christ by undergoing a violent death

Origin: of uncertain affinity

TDNT entry: 11:54,6

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
