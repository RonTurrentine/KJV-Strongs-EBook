σπουδάζω

Transliterationspoudázō
Phoneticspoo-dad'-zo
Originfrom G4710;

Definition

to use speed, i.e. to make effort, be prompt or earnest

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

do (give) diligence, be diligent (forward), endeavour, labour, study.
