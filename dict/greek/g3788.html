ὀφθαλμός

Transliterationophthalmós
Phoneticof-thal-mos'
Originfrom G3700;

Definition

the eye (literally or figuratively); by implication, vision; figuratively, envy (from the jealous side-glance)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

eye, sight.
