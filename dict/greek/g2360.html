θροέω

Transliterationthroéō
Phoneticthro-eh'-o
Originfrom θρέομαι to wail;

Definition

to clamor, i.e. (by implication) to frighten

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

trouble.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: θροέω

Transliteration: throeō

Phonetic: thro-eh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to cry aloud, make a noise by outcry
    1. in the NT, to trouble, frighten
    2. to be troubled in mind, to be frightened, alarmed

Origin: from threomai to wail

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
