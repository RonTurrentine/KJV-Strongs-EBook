ἄξιος

Transliterationáxios
Phoneticax'-ee-os
Originprobably from G0071;

Definition

deserving, comparable or suitable (as if drawing praise)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

due reward, meet, (un-)worthy.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἄξιος

Transliteration: axios

Phonetic: ax'-ee-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. weighing, having weight, having the weight of another thing of like value, worth as much
  2. befitting, congruous, corresponding to a thing
  3. of one who has merited anything worthy
    1. both in a good and a bad sense

Origin: probably from G0071

TDNT entry: 07:19,6

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
