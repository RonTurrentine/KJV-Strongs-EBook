ἀποτάσσομαι

Transliterationapotássomai
Phoneticap-ot-as'-som-ahee
Originmiddle voice from G0575 and G5021;

Definition

literally, to say adieu (by departing or dismissing); figuratively, to renounce

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bid farewell, forsake, take leave, send away.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀποτάσσομαι

Transliteration: apotassomai

Phonetic: ap-ot-as'-som-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to set apart, separate
    1. to separate one's self, withdraw one's self from anyone
      1. to take leave of, bid farewell to
    2. to renounce, forsake

Origin: middle voice from G0575 and G5021

TDNT entry: 8:33,*

Part(s) of speech: Verb
