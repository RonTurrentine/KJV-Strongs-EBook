Transliterationidiṓtēs
Phoneticid-ee-o'-tace
Originfrom G2398;

Definition

a private person, i.e. (by implication) an ignoramus (compare "idiot")

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

ignorant, rude, unlearned.
