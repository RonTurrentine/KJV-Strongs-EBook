BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: βαρύς

Transliteration: barus

Phonetic: bar-ooce'

Thayer Definition:

  1. heavy in weight
  2. metaphorically
    1. burdensome
    2. severe, stern
    3. weighty
      1. of great moment
    4. violent, cruel, unsparing

Origin: from the same as G0922

TDNT entry: 10:16,9

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
