θνήσκω

Transliterationthnḗskō
Phoneticthnay'-sko
Origina strengthened form of a simpler primary θάνωthan'-o (which is used for it only in certain tenses);

Definition

to die (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be dead, die.
