ἀρχιτρίκλινος

Transliterationarchitríklinos
Phonetic ar-khee-tree'-klee-nos
Originfrom G746 and a compound of G5140 and G2827 (a dinner-bed, because composed of three couches);

Definition

director of the entertainment

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

governor (ruler) of the feast.
