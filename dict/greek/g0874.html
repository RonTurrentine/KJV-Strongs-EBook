ἀφορμή

Transliterationaphormḗ
Phoneticaf-or-may'
Originfrom a compound of G0575 and G3729;

Definition

a starting-point, i.e. (figuratively) an opportunity

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

occasion.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀφορμή

Transliteration: aphormē

Phonetic: af-or-may'

Thayer Definition:

  1. a place from which a movement or attack is made, a base of operations
  2. metaphorically that by which endeavour is excited and from which it goes forth
    1. that which gives occasion and supplies matter for an undertaking, the incentive
    2. the resources we avail ourselves of in attempting or performing anything

Origin: from a compound of G0575 and G3729

TDNT entry: 12:52,7

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
