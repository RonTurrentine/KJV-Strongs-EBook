ἀναπληρόω

Transliterationanaplēróō
Phonetican-ap-lay-ro'-o
Originfrom G0303 and G4137;

Definition

to complete; by implication, to occupy, supply; figuratively, to accomplish (by coincidence ot obedience)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fill up, fulfill, occupy, supply.

BDB / Thayer Definition
