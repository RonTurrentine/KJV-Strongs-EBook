ἐπιγινώσκω

Transliterationepiginṓskō
Phoneticep-ig-in-oce'-ko
Originfrom G1909 and G1097;

Definition

to know upon some mark, i.e. recognize; by implication, to become fully acquainted with, to acknowledge

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(ac-, have, take)know(-ledge, well), perceive.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐπιγινώσκω

Transliteration: epiginōskō

Phonetic: ep-ig-in-oce'-ko

Thayer Definition:

  1. to become thoroughly acquainted with, to know thoroughly
    1. to know accurately, know well
  2. to know
    1. to recognise
      1. by sight, hearing, of certain signs, to perceive who a person is
    2. to know, i.e. to perceive
    3. to know, i.e. to find out, ascertain
    4. to know, i.e. to understand

Origin: from G1909 and G1097

TDNT entry: 12:29,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
