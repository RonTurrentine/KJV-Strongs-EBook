ἀκροβυστία

Transliterationakrobystía
Phoneticak-rob-oos-tee'-ah
Originfrom G0206 and probably a modified form of πόσθη (the penis or male sexual organ);

Definition

the prepuce; by implication, an uncircumcised (i.e. gentile, figuratively, unregenerate) state or person

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

not circumcised, uncircumcised (with G2192), uncircumcision.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀκροβυστία

Transliteration: akrobustia

Phonetic: ak-rob-oos-tee'-ah

Thayer Definition:

  1. having the foreskin, uncircumcised
  2. a Gentile
  3. a condition in which the corrupt desires rooted in the flesh were not yet extinct

Origin: from G0206 and probably a modified form of posthe (the penis or male sexual organ)

TDNT entry: 04:45,4

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
