ἀνήρ

Transliterationanḗr
Phonetican'-ayr
Origina primary word (compare G0444);

Definition

a man (properly as an individual male)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fellow, husband, man, sir.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀνήρ

Transliteration: anēr

Phonetic: an'-ayr

Thayer Definition:

  1. with reference to sex
    1. of a male
    2. of a husband
    3. of a betrothed or future husband
  2. with reference to age, and to distinguish an adult man from a boy
  3. any male
  4. used generically of a group of both men and women

Origin: a primary word cf G0444

TDNT entry: 07:00,6

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
