BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: Ἀλέξανδρος

Transliteration: Alexandros

Phonetic: al-ex'-an-dros

Thayer Definition: Alexander = "man defender"

  1. son of Simon of Cyrene who carried Jesus's cross, Mar 15:21
  2. a certain man of the kindred of the high priest, Act 4:6
  3. a certain Jew, Act 19:33
  4. a certain coppersmith who opposed the Apostle Paul, 1Ti 1:20

Origin: from the same as (the first part of) G0220 and G0435

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
