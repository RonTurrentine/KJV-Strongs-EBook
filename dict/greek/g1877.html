ἐπανάγω

Transliterationepanágō
Phoneticep-an-ag'-o
Originfrom G1909 and G0321;

Definition

to lead up on, i.e. (technical) to put out (to sea); (intransitively) to return

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

launch (thrust) out, return.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: επανάγω

Transliteration: epanagō

Phonetic: ep-an-ag'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to lead up upon
    1. a ship upon the deep, to put out
  2. to lead back
  3. to return

Origin: from G1909 and G0321

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
