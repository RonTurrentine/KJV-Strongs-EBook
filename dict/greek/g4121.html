πλεονάζω

Transliterationpleonázō
Phoneticpleh-on-ad'-zo
Originfrom G4119;

Definition

to do, make or be more, i.e. increase (transitively or intransitively); by extension, to superabound

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

abound, abundant, make to increase, have over.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πλεονάζω

Transliteration: pleonazō

Phonetic: pleh-on-ad'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to superabound
    1. of things
      1. to exist in abundance
      2. to increase
      3. be augmented
  2. to make to increase: one in a thing

Origin: from G4119

TDNT entry: 10:23,9

Part(s) of speech: Verb
