Originfrom ἕλη (a ray; perhaps akin to the alternate of G0138);

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἥλιος

Transliteration: hēlios

Phonetic: hay'-lee-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. the sun
  2. the rays of the sun
  3. the light of day

Origin: from hele (a ray, perhaps akin to the alternate of G0138)

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
