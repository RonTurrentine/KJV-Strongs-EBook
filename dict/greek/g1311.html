διαφθείρω

Transliterationdiaphtheírō
Phoneticdee-af-thi'-ro
Originfrom G1225 and G5351;

Definition

to rot thoroughly, i.e. (by implication) to ruin (passively, decay utterly, figuratively, pervert)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

corrupt, destroy, perish.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: διαφθείρω

Transliteration: diaphtheirō

Phonetic: dee-af-thi'-ro

Thayer Definition:

  1. to change for the worse, to corrupt
    1. of minds, morals
  2. to destroy, ruin
    1. to consume
      1. of bodily vigour and strength
      2. of the worm or moth that eats provisions, clothing, etc.
    2. to destroy, to kill

Origin: from G1223 and G5351

TDNT entry: 10:33,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
