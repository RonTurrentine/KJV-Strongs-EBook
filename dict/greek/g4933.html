συντηρέω

Transliterationsyntēréō
Phoneticsoon-tay-reh'-o
Originfrom G4862 and G5083;

Definition

to keep closely together, i.e. (by implication) to conserve (from ruin); mentally, to remember (and obey)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

keep, observe, preserve.
