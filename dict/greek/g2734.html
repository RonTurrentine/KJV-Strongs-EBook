κατοπτρίζομαι

Transliterationkatoptrízomai
Phonetickat-op-trid'-zom-ahee
Originmiddle voice from a compound of G2596 and a derivative of G3700 (compare G2072);

Definition

to mirror oneself, i.e. to see reflected (figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

behold as in a glass.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κατοπτρίζομαι

Transliteration: katoptrizomai

Phonetic: kat-op-trid'-zom-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to show in a mirror, to make to reflect, to mirror
  2. to look at one's self in a mirror
  3. to behold one's self in a mirror

Origin: middle voice from a compound of G2596 and a derivative of G3700 [cf G2072]

TDNT entry: 13:36,3

Part(s) of speech: Verb
