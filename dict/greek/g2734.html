κατοπτρίζομαι

Transliterationkatoptrízomai
Phonetickat-op-trid'-zom-ahee
Originmiddle voice from a compound of G2596 and a derivative of G3700 (compare G2072);

Definition

to mirror oneself, i.e. to see reflected (figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

behold as in a glass.
