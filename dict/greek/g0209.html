ἀκωλύτως

Transliterationakōlýtōs
Phonetic ak-o-loo'-toce
Originadverb from a compound of G1 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of G2967;

Definition

in an unhindered manner, i.e. freely

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

no man forbidding him.
