ξένος

Transliterationxénos
Phoneticxen'-os
Originapparently a primary word;

Definition

foreign (literally, alien, or figuratively, novel); by implication, a guest or (vice-versa) entertainer

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

host, strange(-r).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ξένος

Transliteration: xenos

Phonetic: xen'-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. a foreigner, a stranger
    1. alien (from a person or a thing)
    2. without the knowledge of, without a share in
    3. new, unheard of
  2. one who receives and entertains another hospitably
    1. with whom he stays or lodges, a host

Origin: apparently a primary word

TDNT entry: 05:01,7

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
