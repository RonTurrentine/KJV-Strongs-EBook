ἐκπορεύομαι

Transliterationekporeúomai
Phoneticek-por-yoo'-om-ahee
Originfrom G1537 and G4198;

Definition

to depart, be discharged, proceed, project

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

come (forth, out of), depart, go (forth, out), issue, proceed (out of).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐκπορεύομαι

Transliteration: ekporeuomai

Phonetic: ek-por-yoo'-om-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to go forth, go out, depart
  2. metaphorically
    1. to come forth, to issue, to proceed
      1. of feelings, affections, deeds, sayings
    2. to flow forth
      1. of a river
    3. to project, from the mouth of one
    4. to spread abroad, of a rumour

Origin: from G1537 and G4198

TDNT entry: 15:38,9

Part(s) of speech: Verb
