χρίω

Transliterationchríō
Phonetickhree'-o
Originprobably akin to G5530 through the idea of contact;

Definition

to smear or rub with oil, i.e. (by implication) to consecrate to an office or religious service

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

anoint.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: χρίω

Transliteration: chriō

Phonetic: khree'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to anoint
    1. consecrating Jesus to the Messianic office, and furnishing him with the necessary powers for its administration
    2. enduing Christians with the gifts of the Holy Spirit

Origin: probably akin to G5530 through the idea of contact
