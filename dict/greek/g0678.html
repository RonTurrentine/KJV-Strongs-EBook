ἀπροσωπολήπτως

Transliterationaprosōpolḗptōs
Phoneticap-ros-o-pol-ape'-tos
Originadverb from a compound of G1 (as a negative particle) and a presumed derivative of a presumed compound of G4383 and G2983 (compare G4381);

Definition

in a way not accepting the person, i.e. impartially

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

without respect of persons.
