προοράω

Transliterationprooráō
Phoneticpro-or-ah'-o
Originfrom G4253 and G3708;

Definition

to behold in advance, i.e. (actively) to notice (another) previously, or (middle voice) to keep in (one's own) view

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

foresee, see before.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: προοράω

Transliteration: prooraō

Phonetic: pro-or-ah'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to see before (whether as respects place or time)
  2. to keep before one's eye's
  3. metaphorically to be mindful of one always

Origin: from G4253 and G3708

TDNT entry: 11:21,7

Part(s) of speech: Verb
