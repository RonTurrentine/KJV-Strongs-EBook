Phoneticprod'-rom-os
Originfrom the alternate of G4390;

Definition

a runner ahead, i.e. scout (figuratively, precursor)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

forerunner.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πρόδρομος

Transliteration: prodromos

Phonetic: prod'-rom-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. a forerunner
    1. especially one who is sent before to take observations or act as a spy, a scout, a light armed soldier
    2. one who comes in advance to a place where the rest are to follow

Origin: from the alternate of G4390

TDNT entry: 11:55,1

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
