ὑπάγω

Transliterationhypágō
Phonetichoop-ag'-o
Originfrom G5259 and G0071;

Definition

to lead (oneself) under, i.e. withdraw or retire (as if sinking out of sight), literally or figuratively

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

depart, get hence, go (a-)way.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὑπάγω

Transliteration: hupagō

Phonetic: hoop-ag'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to lead under, bring under
  2. to withdraw one's self, to go away, depart

Origin: from G5259 and G0071

TDNT entry: 16:24,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
