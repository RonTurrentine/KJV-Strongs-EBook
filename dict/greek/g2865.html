κομίζω

Transliterationkomízō
Phonetickom-id'-zo
Originfrom a primary κομέω (to tend, i.e. take care of);

Definition

properly, to provide for, i.e. (by implication) to carry off (as if from harm; genitive case obtain)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bring, receive.
