Definition

a place of sacrifice, i.e. an altar (special or genitive case, literal or figurative)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

altar.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: θυσιαστήριον

Transliteration: thusiastērion

Phonetic: thoo-see-as-tay'-ree-on

Thayer Definition:

  1. the altar for slaying and burning of victims used of
    1. the altar of whole burnt offerings which stood in the court of the priests in the temple at Jerusalem
    2. the altar of incense which stood in the sanctuary or the Holy Place
    3. any other altar
      1. metaphorically, the cross on which Christ suffered an expiatory death: to eat of this altar, i.e. to appropriate to one's self the fruits of Christ's expiatory death

Origin: from a derivative of G2378

TDNT entry: 06:00,3

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
