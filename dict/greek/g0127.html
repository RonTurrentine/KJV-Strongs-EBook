αἰδώς

Transliterationaidṓs
Phoneticahee-doce'
Originperhaps from G1 (as a negative particle) and G1492 (through the idea of downcast eyes);

Definition

bashfulness, i.e. (towards men), modesty or (towards God) awe

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

reverence, shamefacedness.
