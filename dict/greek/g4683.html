σπαργανόω

Transliterationsparganóō
Phoneticspar-gan-o'-o
Originfrom σπάργανον (a strip; from a derivative of the base of G4682 meaning to strap or wrap with strips);

Definition

to swathe (an infant after the Oriental custom)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

wrap in swaddling clothes.
