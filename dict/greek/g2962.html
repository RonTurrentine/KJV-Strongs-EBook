κύριος

Transliterationkýrios
Phonetickoo'-ree-os
Originfrom κῦρος (supremacy);

Definition

supreme in authority, i.e. (as noun) controller; by implication, Master (as a respectful title)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

God, Lord, master, Sir.
