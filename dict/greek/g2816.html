Definition

to be an heir to (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be heir, (obtain by) inherit(-ance).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κληρονομέω

Transliteration: klēronomeō

Phonetic: klay-ron-om-eh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to receive a lot, receive by lot
    1. especially to receive a part of an inheritance, receive as an inheritance, obtain by right of inheritance
    2. to be an heir, to inherit
  2. to receive the portion assigned to one, receive an allotted portion, receive as one's own or as a possession
  3. to become partaker of, to obtain

Origin: from G2818

TDNT entry: 15:47,4

Part(s) of speech: Verb
