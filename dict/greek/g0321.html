ἀνάγω

Transliterationanágō
Phonetican-ag'-o
Originfrom G0303 and G0071;

Definition

to lead up; by extension to bring out; specially, to sail away

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bring (again, forth, up again), depart, launch (forth), lead (up), loose, offer, sail, set forth, take up.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀνάγω

Transliteration: anagō

Phonetic: an-ag'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to lead up, to lead or bring into a higher place
  2. of navigators: launch out, set sail, put to sea

Origin: from G0303 and G0071

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
