ἀφελότης

Transliterationaphelótēs
Phoneticaf-el-ot'-ace
Originfrom a compound of G1 (as a negative particle) and φέλλος (in the sense of a stone as stubbing the foot);

Definition

smoothness, i.e. (figuratively) simplicity

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

singleness.
