εὐθυμέω

Transliterationeuthyméō
Phoneticyoo-thoo-meh'-o
Originfrom G2115;

Definition

to cheer up, i.e. (intransitively) be cheerful; neuter comparative (adverbially) more cheerfully

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be of good cheer (merry).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: εὐθυμέω

Transliteration: euthumeō

Phonetic: yoo-thoo-meh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to put in good spirits, gladden, make cheerful
    1. to be of good spirits, to be cheerful
  2. to be joyful, be of good cheer, of good courage

Origin: from G2115
