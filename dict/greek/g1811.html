ἐξακολουθέω

Transliterationexakolouthéō
Phoneticex-ak-ol-oo-theh'-o
Originfrom G1537 and G0190;

Definition

to follow out, i.e. (figuratively) to imitate, obey, yield to

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

follow.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐξακολουθέω

Transliteration: exakoloutheō

Phonetic: ex-ak-ol-oo-theh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to follow out or up, tread in one's steps
    1. metaphorically, to imitate one's ways of acting
    2. to follow one's authority
    3. to comply with, yield to

Origin: from G1537 and G0190

TDNT entry: 04:35,3

Part(s) of speech: Verb
