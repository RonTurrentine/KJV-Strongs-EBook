ἐκζητέω

Transliterationekzētéō
Phoneticek-zay-teh'-o
Originfrom G1537 and G2212;

Definition

to search out, i.e. (figuratively)investigate, crave, demand, (by Hebraism) worship

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

en- (re-)quire, seek after (carefully, diligently).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐκζητέω

Transliteration: ekzēteō

Phonetic: ek-zay-teh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to seek out, search for
  2. to seek out, i.e. investigate, scrutinise
  3. to seek out for one's self, beg, crave
  4. to demand back, require

Origin: from G1537 and G2212

TDNT entry: 16:54,3

Part(s) of speech: Verb
