κεράννυμι

Transliterationkeránnymi
Phoneticker-an'-noo-mee
Originprolonged form of a more primary κεράωker-ah'-o (which is used in certain tenses);

Definition

to mingle, i.e. (by implication) to pour out (for drinking)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fill, pour out.
