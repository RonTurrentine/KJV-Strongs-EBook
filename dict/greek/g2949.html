Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

wave.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κῦμα

Transliteration: kuma

Phonetic: koo'-mah

Thayer Definition:

  1. a wave (swell) especially of the sea or of a lake
    1. of impulse and restless men, tossed to and fro by their raging passions

Origin: from kuo (to swell [with young], i.e bend, curve)

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
