ἀρχή

Transliterationarchḗ
Phoneticar-khay'
Originfrom G0756;

Definition

(properly abstract) a commencement, or (concretely) chief (in various applications of order, time, place, or rank)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beginning, corner, (at the, the) first (estate), magistrate, power, principality, principle, rule.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀρχή

Transliteration: archē

Phonetic: ar-khay'

Thayer Definition:

  1. beginning, origin
  2. the person or thing that commences, the first person or thing in a series, the leader
  3. that by which anything begins to be, the origin, the active cause
  4. the extremity of a thing
    1. of the corners of a sail
  5. the first place, principality, rule, magistracy
    1. of angels and demons

Origin: from G0756

TDNT entry: 08:59,8

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
