σάββατον

Transliterationsábbaton
Phoneticsab'-bat-on
Originof Hebrew origin (H7676);

Definition

the Sabbath (i.e. Shabbath), or day of weekly repose from secular avocations (also the observance or institution itself); by extension, a se'nnight, i.e. the interval between two Sabbaths; likewise the plural in all the above applications

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

sabbath (day), week.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: σάββατον

Transliteration: sabbaton

Phonetic: sab'-bat-on

Thayer Definition:

  1. the seventh day of each week which was a sacred festival on which the Israelites were required to abstain from all work
    1. the institution of the sabbath, the law for keeping holy every seventh day of the week
    2. a single sabbath, sabbath day
  2. seven days, a week

Origin: of Hebrew origin H7676

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
