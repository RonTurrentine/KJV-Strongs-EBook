ἀγαθός

Transliterationagathós
Phoneticag-ath-os'
Origina primary word;

Definition

"good" (in any sense, often as noun)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

benefit, good(-s, things), well.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀγαθός

Transliteration: agathos

Phonetic: ag-ath-os'

Thayer Definition:

  1. of good constitution or nature
  2. useful, salutary
  3. good, pleasant, agreeable, joyful, happy
  4. excellent, distinguished
  5. upright, honourable

Origin: a primary word

TDNT entry: 01:10,3

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
