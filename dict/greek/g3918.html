πάρειμι

Transliterationpáreimi
Phoneticpar'-i-mee
Originfrom G3844 and G1510 (including its various forms);

Definition

to be near, i.e. at hand; neuter present participle (singular) time being, or (plural) property

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

come, X have, be here, + lack, (be here) present.
