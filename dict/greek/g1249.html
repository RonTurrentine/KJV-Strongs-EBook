διάκονος

Transliterationdiákonos
Phoneticdee-ak'-on-os
Originprobably from an obsolete διάκω (to run on errands; compare G1377);

Definition

an attendant, i.e. (genitive case) a waiter (at table or in other menial duties); specially, a Christian teacher and pastor (technically, a deacon or deaconess)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

deacon, minister, servant.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: διάκονος

Transliteration: diakonos

Phonetic: dee-ak'-on-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. one who executes the commands of another, especially of a master, a servant, attendant, minister
    1. the servant of a king
    2. a deacon, one who, by virtue of the office assigned to him by the church, cares for the poor and has charge of and distributes the money collected for their use
    3. a waiter, one who serves food and drink

Origin: probably from an obsolete diako (to run on errands, cf G1377)

TDNT entry: 03:28,2

Part(s) of speech: Noun
