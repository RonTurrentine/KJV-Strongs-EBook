πηλίκος

Transliterationpēlíkos
Phoneticpay-lee'-kos
Origina quantitative form (the feminine) of the base of G4225;

Definition

how much (as an indefinite), i.e. in size or (figuratively) dignity

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

how great (large).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πηλίκος

Transliteration: pēlikos

Phonetic: pay-lee'-kos

Thayer Definition:

  1. how great, how large
    1. in a material reference (denoting geometrical magnitude as distinguished from arithmetical)
    2. in an ethical sense: how distinguished

Origin: a quantitative form (the feminine) of the base of G4225
