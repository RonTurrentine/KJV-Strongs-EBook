ἐπιβάλλω

Transliterationepibállō
Phoneticep-ee-bal'-lo
Originfrom G1909 and G0906;

Definition

to throw upon (literal or figurative, transitive or reflexive; usually with more or less force); specially (with G1438 implied) to reflect; impersonally, to belong to

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beat into, cast (up-)on, fall, lay (on), put (unto), stretch forth, think on.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐπιβάλλω

Transliteration: epiballō

Phonetic: ep-ee-bal'-lo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to cast upon, to lay upon
    1. used of seizing one to lead him off as a prisoner
    2. to put (i.e. sew) on
  2. to throw one's self upon, rush in
    1. used of waves rushing into a ship
    2. to put one's mind upon a thing
    3. attend to
  3. it belongs to me, falls to my share

Origin: from G1909 and G0906

TDNT entry: 09:48,9

Part(s) of speech: Verb
