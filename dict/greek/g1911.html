ἐπιβάλλω

Transliterationepibállō
Phoneticep-ee-bal'-lo
Originfrom G1909 and G906;

Definition

to throw upon (literal or figurative, transitive or reflexive; usually with more or less force); specially (with G1438 implied) to reflect; impersonally, to belong to

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beat into, cast (up-)on, fall, lay (on), put (unto), stretch forth, think on.
