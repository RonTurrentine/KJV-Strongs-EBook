ῥάβδος

Transliterationrhábdos
Phonetichrab'-dos
Originfrom the base of G4474;

Definition

a stick or wand (as a cudgel, a cane or a baton of royalty)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

rod, sceptre, staff.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ῥάβδος

Transliteration: rhabdos

Phonetic: hrab'-dos

Thayer Definition:

  1. a staff, a walking stick, a twig, rod, branch
  2. a rod with which one is beaten
  3. a staff
    1. as used on a journey, or to lean upon, or by shepherds
    2. when applied to kings
      1. with a rod of iron, indicates the severest, most rigorous rule
      2. a royal sceptre

Origin: from the base of G4474

TDNT entry: 22:07,0

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
