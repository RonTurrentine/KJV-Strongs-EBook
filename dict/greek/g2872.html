κοπιάω

Transliterationkopiáō
Phonetickop-ee-ah'-o
Originfrom a derivative of G2873;

Definition

to feel fatigue; by implication, to work hard

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(bestow) labour, toil, be wearied.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κοπιάω

Transliteration: kopiaō

Phonetic: kop-ee-ah'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to grow weary, tired, exhausted (with toil or burdens or grief)
  2. to labour with wearisome effort, to toil
    1. of bodily labour

Origin: from a derivative of G2873

TDNT entry: 16:47,5

Part(s) of speech: Verb
