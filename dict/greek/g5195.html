ὑβρίζω

Transliterationhybrízō
Phonetichoo-brid'-zo
Originfrom G5196;

Definition

to exercise violence, i.e. abuse

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

use despitefully, reproach, entreat shamefully (spitefully).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὑβρίζω

Transliteration: hubrizō

Phonetic: hoo-brid'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to be insolent, to behave insolently, wantonly, outrageously
  2. to act insolently and shamefully towards one, to treat shamefully
  3. of one who injures another by speaking evil of him

Origin: from G5196

TDNT entry: 12:55,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
