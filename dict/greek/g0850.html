αὐχμηρός

Transliterationauchmērós
Phonetic owkh-may-ros'
Originfrom αὐχμός (probably from a base akin to that of G109) (dust, as dried by wind);

Definition

properly, dirty, i.e. (by implication) obscure

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dark.
