ὑποτάσσω

Transliterationhypotássō
Phonetichoop-ot-as'-so
Originfrom G5259 and G5021;

Definition

to subordinate; reflexively, to obey

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be under obedience (obedient), put under, subdue unto, (be, make) subject (to, unto), be (put) in subjection (to, under), submit self unto.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὑποτάσσω

Transliteration: hupotassō

Phonetic: hoop-ot-as'-so

Thayer Definition:

  1. to arrange under, to subordinate
  2. to subject, put in subjection
  3. to subject one's self, obey
  4. to submit to one's control
  5. to yield to one's admonition or advice
  6. to obey, be subject

Origin: from G5259 and G5021

TDNT entry: 08:39,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
