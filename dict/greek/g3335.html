BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: μεταλαμβάνω

Transliteration: metalambanō

Phonetic: met-al-am-ban'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to be or to be made a partner
  2. to partake of, take [some] food
  3. of a thing, to get, find (a whole)
