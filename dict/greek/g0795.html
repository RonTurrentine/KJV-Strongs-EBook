ἀστοχέω

Transliterationastochéō
Phoneticas-tokh-eh'-o
Originfrom a compound of G1 (as a negative particle) and στοίχος (an aim);

Definition

to miss the mark, i.e. (figuratively) deviate from truth

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

err, swerve.
