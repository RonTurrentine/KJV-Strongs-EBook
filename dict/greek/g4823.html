συμβουλεύω

Transliterationsymbouleúō
Phonetic soom-bool-yoo'-o
Originfrom G4862 and G1011;

Definition

to give (or take) advice jointly, i.e. recommend, deliberate or determine

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

consult, (give, take) counsel (together).
