ἐκλεκτός

Transliterationeklektós
Phoneticek-lek-tos'
Originfrom G1586;

Definition

select; by implication, favorite

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

chosen, elect.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐκλεκτός

Transliteration: eklektos

Phonetic: ek-lek-tos'

Thayer Definition:

  1. picked out, chosen
    1. chosen by God
      1. to obtain salvation through Christ
        1. Christians are called "chosen or elect" of God
      2. the Messiah in called "elect" , as appointed by God to the most exalted office conceivable
      3. choice, select, i.e. the best of its kind or class, excellence preeminent: applied to certain individual Christians

Origin: from G1586

TDNT entry: 07:01,5

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
