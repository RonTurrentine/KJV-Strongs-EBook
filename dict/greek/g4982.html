σώζω

Transliterationsṓzō
Phoneticsode'-zo
Originfrom a primary σῶς (contraction for obsolete σάος, "safe");

Definition

to save, i.e. deliver or protect (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

heal, preserve, save (self), do well, be (make) whole.
