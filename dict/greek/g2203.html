Ζεύς

TransliterationZeús
Phoneticdzyooce
Originof uncertain affinity;

Definition

in the oblique cases there is used instead of it a (probably cognate) name Δίςdeece, which is otherwise obsolete; Zeus or Dis (among the Latins, Jupiter or Jove), the supreme deity of the Greeks

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Jupiter.
