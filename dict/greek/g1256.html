διαλέγομαι

Transliterationdialégomai
Phoneticdee-al-eg'-om-ahee
Originmiddle voice from G1223 and G3004;

Definition

to say thoroughly, i.e. discuss (in argument or exhortation)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dispute, preach (unto), reason (with), speak.
