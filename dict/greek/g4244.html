Transliterationpresbytérion
Phonetic pres-boo-ter'-ee-on
Originneuter of a presumed derivative of G4245;

Definition

the order of elders, i.e. (specially), Israelite Sanhedrin or Christian "presbytery"

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage
