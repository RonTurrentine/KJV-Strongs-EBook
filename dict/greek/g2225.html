ζωογονέω

Transliterationzōogonéō
Phonetic dzo-og-on-eh'-o
Originfrom the same as G2226 and a derivative of G1096;

Definition

to engender alive, i.e. (by analogy) to rescue (passively, be saved) from death

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

live, preserve.
