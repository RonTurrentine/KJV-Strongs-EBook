εὐτόνως

Transliterationeutónōs
Phoneticyoo-ton'-oce
Originadverb from a compound of G2095 and a derivative of τείνω (to stretch);

Definition

in a well-strung manner, i.e. (figuratively) intensely (in a good sense, cogently; in a bad one, fiercely)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

mightily, vehemently.
