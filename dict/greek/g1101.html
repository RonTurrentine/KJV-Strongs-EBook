Transliterationglōssókomon
Phoneticgloce-sok'-om-on
Originfrom G1100 and the base of G2889;

Definition

properly, a case (to keep mouthpieces of wind-instruments in) i.e. (by extension) a casket or (specially) purse

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: γλωσσόκομον

Transliteration: glōssokomon

Phonetic: gloce-sok'-om-on

Thayer Definition:

  1. a case in which to keep mouth-pieces of wind instruments
  2. a small box for other uses
    1. especially a casket, a purse to keep money in

Origin: from G1100 and the base of G2889

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
