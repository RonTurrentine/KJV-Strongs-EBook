ἐνεργέω

Transliterationenergéō
Phoneticen-erg-eh'-o
Originfrom G1756;

Definition

to be active, efficient

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

do, (be) effectual (fervent), be mighty in, shew forth self, work (effectually in).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐνεργέω

Transliteration: energeō

Phonetic: en-erg-eh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to be operative, be at work, put forth power
    1. to work for one, aid one
  2. to effect
  3. to display one's activity, show one's self operative

Origin: from G1756

TDNT entry: 12:52,3

Part(s) of speech: Verb
