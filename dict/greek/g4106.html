πλάνη

Transliterationplánē
Phoneticplan'-ay
Originfeminine of G4108 (as abstractly);

Definition

objectively, fraudulence; subjectively, a straying from orthodoxy or piety

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

deceit, to deceive, delusion, error.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πλάνη

Transliteration: planē

Phonetic: plan'-ay

Thayer Definition:

  1. a wandering, a straying about
    1. one led astray from the right way, roams hither and thither
  2. metaphorically
    1. mental straying
      1. error, wrong opinion relative to morals or religion
    2. error which shows itself in action, a wrong mode of acting
    3. error, that which leads into error, deceit or fraud

Origin: from G4108 (as abstractly)

TDNT entry: 09:48,9

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
