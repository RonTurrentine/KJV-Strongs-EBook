Transliterationkatakrínō
Phonetickat-ak-ree'-no
Originfrom G2596 and G2919;

Definition

to judge against, i.e. sentence

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

condemn, damn.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κατακρίνω

Transliteration: katakrinō

Phonetic: kat-ak-ree'-no

Thayer Definition:

  1. to give judgment against, to judge worthy of punishment
    1. to condemn
    2. by one's good example to render another's wickedness themore evident and censurable

Origin: from G2596 and G2919

TDNT entry: 18:51,5

Part(s) of speech: Verb
