φωνή

Transliterationphōnḗ
Phoneticfo-nay'
Originprobably akin to G5316 through the idea of disclosure;

Definition

a tone (articulate, bestial or artificial); by implication, an address (for any purpose), saying or language

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

noise, sound, voice.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: φωνή

Transliteration: phōnē

Phonetic: fo-nay'

Thayer Definition:

  1. a sound, a tone
    1. of inanimate things, as musical instruments
  2. a voice
    1. of the sound of uttered words
  3. speech
    1. of a language, tongue

Origin: probably akin to G5316 through the idea of disclosure

TDNT entry: 13:38,1

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
