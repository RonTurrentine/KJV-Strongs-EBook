χειρόγραφον

Transliterationcheirógraphon
Phonetic khi-rog'-raf-on
Originneuter of a compound of G5495 and G1125;

Definition

something hand-written ("chirograph"), i.e. a manuscript (specially, a legal document or bond (figuratively))

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

handwriting.
