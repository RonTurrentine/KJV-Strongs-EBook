ὑπωπιάζω

Transliterationhypōpiázō
Phonetichoop-o-pee-ad'-zo
Originfrom a compound of G5259 and a derivative of G3700;

Definition

to hit under the eye (buffet or disable an antagonist as a pugilist), i.e. (figuratively) to tease or annoy (into compliance), subdue (one's passions)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

keep under, weary.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὑπωπιάζω

Transliteration: hupōpiazō

Phonetic: hoop-o-pee-ad'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to beat black and blue, to smite so as to cause bruises and livid spots
    1. like a boxer one buffets his body, handle it roughly, discipline by hardships
  2. metaphorically
    1. to give one intolerable annoyance
      1. beat one out, wear one out
    2. by entreaties
  3. that part of the face that is under the eyes

Origin: from a compound of G5259 and a derivative of G3700

TDNT entry: 17:50,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
