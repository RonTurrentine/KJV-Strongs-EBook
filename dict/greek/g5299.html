ὑπωπιάζω

Transliterationhypōpiázō
Phonetichoop-o-pee-ad'-zo
Originfrom a compound of G5259 and a derivative of G3700;

Definition

to hit under the eye (buffet or disable an antagonist as a pugilist), i.e. (figuratively) to tease or annoy (into compliance), subdue (one's passions)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

keep under, weary.
