λαλέω

Transliterationlaléō
Phoneticlal-eh'-o
Origina prolonged form of an otherwise obsolete verb;

Definition

to talk, i.e. utter words

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

preach, say, speak (after), talk, tell, utter.
