ἔξεστι

Transliterationéxesti
Phoneticex'-es-tee
Originthird person singular present indicative of a compound of G1537 and G1510;

Definition

so also ἐξόνex-on' neuter present participle of the same (with or without some form of G1510 expressed); impersonally, it is right (through the figurative idea of being out in public)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be lawful, let, X may(-est).
