κάθημαι

Transliterationkáthēmai
Phonetickath'-ay-mahee
Originfrom G2596;

Definition

and ἧμαι (to sit; akin to the base of G1476); to sit down; figuratively, to remain, reside

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dwell, sit (by, down).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κάθημαι

Transliteration: kathēmai

Phonetic: kath'-ay-mahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to sit down, seat one's self
  2. to sit, be seated, of a place occupied
    1. to have a fixed abode, to dwell

Origin: from G2596, and hemai (to sit, akin to the base of G1476)

TDNT entry: 10:20,4

Part(s) of speech: Verb
