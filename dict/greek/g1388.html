δόλος

Transliterationdólos
Phoneticdol'-os
Originfrom an obsolete primary verb, δέλλω (probably meaning to decoy; compare G1185);

Definition

a trick (bait), i.e. (figuratively) wile

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

craft, deceit, guile, subtilty.
