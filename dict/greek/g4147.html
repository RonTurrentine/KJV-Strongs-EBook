Transliterationploutéō
Phoneticploo-teh'-o
Originfrom G4148;

Definition

to be (or become) wealthy (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be increased with goods, (be made, wax) rich.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πλουτέω

Transliteration: plouteō

Phonetic: ploo-teh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to be rich, to have abundance
    1. of outward possessions
  2. metaphorically to be richly supplied
    1. is affluent in resources so that he can give blessings of salvation to all

Origin: from G4148

TDNT entry: 11:18,9

Part(s) of speech: Verb
