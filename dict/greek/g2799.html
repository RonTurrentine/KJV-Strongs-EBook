κλαίω

Transliterationklaíō
Phoneticklah'-yo
Originof uncertain affinity;

Definition

to sob, i.e. wail aloud (whereas 1145 is rather to cry silently)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bewail, weep.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κλαίω

Transliteration: klaiō

Phonetic: klah'-yo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to mourn, weep, lament
    1. weeping as the sign of pain and grief for the thing signified (i.e. for the pain and grief)
    2. of those who mourn for the dead
  2. to weep for, mourn for, bewail, one

Origin: of uncertain affinity

TDNT entry: 15:02,4

Part(s) of speech: Verb
