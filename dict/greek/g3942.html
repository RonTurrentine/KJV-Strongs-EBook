παροιμία

Transliterationparoimía
Phoneticpar-oy-mee'-ah
Originfrom a compound of G3844 and perhaps a derivative of G3633;

Definition

apparently a state alongside of supposition, i.e. (concretely) an adage; specially, an enigmatical or fictitious illustration

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage
