ὑπερβάλλω

Transliterationhyperbállō
Phonetichoop-er-bal'-lo
Originfrom G5228 and G0906;

Definition

to throw beyod the usual mark, i.e. (figuratively) to surpass (only active participle supereminent)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

exceeding, excel, pass.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὑπερβάλλω

Transliteration: huperballō

Phonetic: hoop-er-bal'-lo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to surpass in throwing, to throw over or beyond any thing
  2. to transcend, surpass, exceed, excel
  3. excelling, exceeding

Origin: from G5228 and G0906

TDNT entry: 16:40,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
