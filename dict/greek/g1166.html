δεικνύω

Transliterationdeiknýō
Phoneticdike-noo'-o
Origina prolonged form of an obsolete primary of the same meaning;

Definition

to show (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

shew.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: δεικνύω

Transliteration: deiknuō

Phonetic: dike-noo'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to show, expose to the eyes
  2. metaphorically
    1. to give evidence or proof of a thing
    2. to show by words or teach

Origin: a prolonged form of an obsolete primary of the same meaning

TDNT entry: 2:25,*

Part(s) of speech: Verb
