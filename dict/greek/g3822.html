παλαιόω

Transliterationpalaióō
Phoneticpal-ah-yo'-o
Originfrom G3820;

Definition

to make (passively, become) worn out, or declare obsolete

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

decay, make (wax) old.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: παλαιόω

Transliteration: palaioō

Phonetic: pal-ah-yo'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to make ancient or old
    1. to become old, to be worn out
    2. of things worn out by time and use
  2. to declare a thing to be old and so about to be abrogated

Origin: from G3820

TDNT entry: 17:00,8

Part(s) of speech: Verb
