ἀνώγεον

Transliterationanṓgeon
Phonetican-ogue'-eh-on
Originfrom G507 and G1093;

Definition

above the ground, i.e. (properly) the second floor of a building; used for a dome or a balcony on the upper story

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

upper room.
