ἀντέχομαι

Transliterationantéchomai
Phonetican-tekh'-om-ahee
Originfrom G473 and the middle voice of G2192;

Definition

to hold oneself opposite to, i.e. (by implication) adhere to; by extension to care for

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

hold fast, hold to, support.
