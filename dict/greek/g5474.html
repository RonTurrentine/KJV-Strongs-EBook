χαλκολίβανον

Transliterationchalkolíbanon
Phonetic khal-kol-ib'-an-on
Originneuter of a compound of G5475 and G3030 (in the implied mean of whiteness or brilliancy);

Definition

burnished copper, an alloy of copper (or gold) and silver having a brilliant lustre

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fine brass.
