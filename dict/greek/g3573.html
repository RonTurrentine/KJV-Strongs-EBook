Definition

to nod, i.e. (by implication) to fall asleep; figuratively, to delay

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

slumber.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: νυστάζω

Transliteration: nustazō

Phonetic: noos-tad'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to nod in sleep, to sleep
    1. to be overcome or oppressed with sleep
    2. to fall asleep, drop off to sleep
  2. to be negligent, careless
    1. of a thing, i.e. to linger, delay

Origin: from a presumed derivative of G3506

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
