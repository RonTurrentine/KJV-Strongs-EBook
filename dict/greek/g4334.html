προσέρχομαι

Transliterationprosérchomai
Phoneticpros-er'-khom-ahee
Originfrom G4314 and G2064 (including its alternate);

Definition

to approach, i.e. (literally) come near, visit, or (figuratively) worship, assent to

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(as soon as he) come (unto), come thereunto, consent, draw near, go (near, to, unto).
