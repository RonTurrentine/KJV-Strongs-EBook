Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Egypt.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: Αἴγυπτος

Transliteration: Aiguptos

Phonetic: ah'-ee-goop-tos

Thayer Definition: Egypt = "double straits"

  1. a country occupying the northeast angle of Africa
  2. metaphorically Jerusalem, for the Jews persecuting the Christ and his followers, and so to be likened to the Egyptians treating the Jews

Origin: of uncertain derivation

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Location
