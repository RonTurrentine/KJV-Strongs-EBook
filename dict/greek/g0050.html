ἀγνοέω

Transliterationagnoéō
Phoneticag-no-eh'-o
Originfrom G0001 (as a negative particle) and G3539;

Definition

not to know (through lack of information or intelligence); by implication, to ignore (through disinclination)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(be) ignorant(-ly), not know, not understand, unknown.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀγνοέω

Transliteration: agnoeō

Phonetic: ag-no-eh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to be ignorant, not to know
  2. not to understand, unknown
  3. to err or sin through mistake, to be wrong

Origin: from G0001 (as a negative particle) and G3539

TDNT entry: 02:55,2

Part(s) of speech: Verb
