ἐμβριμάομαι

Transliterationembrimáomai
Phonetic em-brim-ah'-om-ahee
Originfrom G1722 and βριμάομαι (to snort with anger);

Definition

to have indignation on, i.e. (transitively) to blame, (intransitively) to sigh with chagrin, (specially) to sternly enjoin

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

straitly charge, groan, murmur against.
