προτίθεμαι

Transliterationprotíthemai
Phoneticprot-ith'-em-ahee
Originmiddle voice from G4253 and G5087;

Definition

to place before, i.e. (for oneself) to exhibit; (to oneself) to propose (determine)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

purpose, set forth.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: προτίθεμαι

Transliteration: protithemai

Phonetic: prot-ith'-em-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to place before, to set forth
    1. to set forth to be looked at, expose to view
    2. to expose to public view
      1. of the bodies of the dead
      2. to let lie in state
  2. to set before one's self, propose to one's self
    1. to purpose, determine

Origin: middle voice from G4253 and G5087

TDNT entry: 10:44,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
