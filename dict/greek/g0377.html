ἀναπίπτω

Transliterationanapíptō
Phonetican-ap-ip'-to
Originfrom G0303 and G4098;

Definition

to fall back, i.e. lie down, lean back

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

lean, sit down (to meat).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀναπίπτω

Transliteration: anapipto

Phonetic: an-ap-ip'-to

Thayer Definition:

  1. to lie back, lie down
  2. to recline at a table, to sit back

Origin: from G0303 and G4098

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
