number with.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: συγκαταψηφίζω

Transliteration: sugkatapsēphizō

Phonetic: soong-kat-aps-ay-fid'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. be depositing a ballot in the urn (i.e. by voting for) to assign one a place among, to vote one a place among
  2. to vote against with others, i.e. to condemn with others

Origin: from G4862 and a compound of G2596 and G5585

TDNT entry: 9:604,*

Part(s) of speech: Verb
