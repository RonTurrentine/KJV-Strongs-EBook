εἰδωλόθυτον

Transliterationeidōlóthyton
Phonetici-do-loth'-oo-ton
Originneuter of a compound of G1497 and a presumed derivative of G2380;

Definition

an image-sacrifice, i.e. part of an idolatrous offering

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(meat, thing that is) offered (in sacrifice, sacrificed) to (unto) idols.
