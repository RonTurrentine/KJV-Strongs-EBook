ἔτι

Transliterationéti
Phoneticet'-ee
Originperhaps akin to G2094;

Definition

"yet," still (of time or degree)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

after that, also, ever, (any) further, (t-)henceforth (more), hereafter, (any) longer, (any) more(-one), now, still, yet.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἔτι

Transliteration: eti

Phonetic: et'-ee

Thayer Definition:

  1. yet, still
    1. of time
      1. of a thing which went on formerly, whereas now a different state of things exists or has begun to exist
      2. of a thing which continues at present
        1. even, now
      3. with negatives
        1. no longer, no more
    2. of degree and increase
      1. even, yet
      2. besides, more, further

Origin: perhaps akin to G2094

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Adverb
