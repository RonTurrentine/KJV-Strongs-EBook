ἐμβλέπω

Transliterationemblépō
Phoneticem-blep'-o
Originfrom G1722 and G0991;

Definition

to look on, i.e. (relatively) to observe fixedly, or (absolutely) to discern clearly

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

behold, gaze up, look upon, (could) see.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐμβλέπω

Transliteration: emblepō

Phonetic: em-blep'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to turn one's eyes on
  2. look at
  3. metaphorically to look at with the mind, to consider

Origin: from G1722 and G0991

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
