πέραν

Transliterationpéran
Phonetic per'-an
Originapparently accusative case of an obsolete derivative of πείρω (to "pierce");

Definition

through (as adverb or preposition), i.e. across

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beyond, farther (other) side, over.
