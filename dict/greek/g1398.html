δουλεύω

Transliterationdouleúō
Phoneticdool-yoo'-o
Originfrom G1401;

Definition

to be a slave to (literal or figurative, involuntary or voluntary)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be in bondage, (do) serve(-ice).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: δουλεύω

Transliteration: douleuō

Phonetic: dool-yoo'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to be a slave, serve, do service
    1. of a nation in subjection to other nations
  2. metaphorically to obey, submit to
    1. in a good sense, to yield obedience
    2. in a bad sense, of those who become slaves to some base power, to yield to, give one's self up to

Origin: from G1401

TDNT entry: 06:21,2

Part(s) of speech: Verb
