ἐπιθυμέω

Transliterationepithyméō
Phoneticep-ee-thoo-meh'-o
Originfrom G1909 and G2372;

Definition

to set the heart upon, i.e. long for (rightfully or otherwise)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

covet, desire, would fain, lust (after).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐπιθυμέω

Transliteration: epithumeō

Phonetic: ep-ee-thoo-meh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to turn upon a thing
  2. to have a desire for, long for, to desire
  3. to lust after, covet
    1. of those who seek things forbidden

Origin: from G1909 and G2372

TDNT entry: 05:48,3

Part(s) of speech: Verb
