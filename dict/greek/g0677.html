ἀπρόσκοπος

Transliterationapróskopos
Phoneticap-ros'-kop-os
Originfrom G0001 (as a negative particle) and a presumed derivative of G4350;

Definition

actively, inoffensive, i.e. not leading into sin; passively, faultless, i.e. not led into sin

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

none (void of, without) offence.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀπρόσκοπος

Transliteration: aproskopos

Phonetic: ap-ros'-kop-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. having nothing to strike against, not causing to stumble
    1. of a smooth road
    2. metaphorically of not leading others to sin by one's mode of life
  2. not striking against or stumbling
    1. metaphorically not led into sin, blameless
  3. without offense, not troubled by a consciousness of sin

Origin: from G0001 (as a negative particle) and a presumed derivative of G4350

TDNT entry: 18:25,9

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
