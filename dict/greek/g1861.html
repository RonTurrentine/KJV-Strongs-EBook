ἐπαγγέλλω

Transliterationepangéllō
Phoneticep-ang-el'-lo
Originfrom G1909 and the base of G0032;

Definition

to announce upon (reflexively), i.e. (by implication) to engage to do something, to assert something respecting oneself

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

profess, (make) promise.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐπαγγέλλω

Transliteration: epaggellō

Phonetic: ep-ang-el'-lo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to announce that one is about to do or furnish something
    1. to promise (of one's own accord) to engage voluntarily
  2. to profess
    1. an art, to profess one's skill in something

Origin: from G1909 and the base of G0032

TDNT entry: 11:36,2

Part(s) of speech: Verb
