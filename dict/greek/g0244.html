Transliterationallotriepískopos
Phonetical-lot-ree-ep-is'-kop-os
Originfrom G245 and G1985;

Definition

overseeing others' affairs, i.e. a meddler (specially, in Gentile customs)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage
