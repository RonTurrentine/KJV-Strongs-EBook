Phoneticop-so'-nee-on
Originneuter of a presumed derivative of the same as G3795;

Definition

rations for a soldier, i.e. (by extension) his stipend or pay

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

wages.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὀψώνιον

Transliteration: opsōnion

Phonetic: op-so'-nee-on

Thayer Definition:

  1. a soldier's pay, allowance
    1. that part of the soldier's support given in place of pay [i.e. rations] and the money in which he is paid
  2. metaphorically wages: hire or pay of sin

Origin: from a presumed derivative of the same as G3795

TDNT entry: 14:51,8

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
