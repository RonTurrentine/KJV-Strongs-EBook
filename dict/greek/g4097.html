πιπράσκω

Transliterationpipráskō
Phoneticpip-ras'-ko
Origincontracted from περάω (to traverse;

Definition

from the base of G4008); to traffic (by travelling), i.e. dispose of as merchandise or into slavery (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

sell.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πιπράσκω πράω

Transliteration: pipraskō praō

Phonetic: pip-ras'-ko

Thayer Definition:

  1. to sell
    1. of price, one into slavery
    2. of the master to whom one is sold as a slave
  2. metaphorically
    1. sold under sin, entirely under the control of the love of sinning
    2. of one bribed to give himself up wholly to another's will

Origin: a reduplicated and prolonged form of prao prah'-o, (which occurs only as an alternate in certain tenses), contracted from perao (to traverse, from the base of G4008)

TDNT entry: 08:40,8

Part(s) of speech: Verb
