σπεῖρα

Transliterationspeîra
Phoneticspi'-rah
Originof immediate Latin origin, but ultimately a derivative of G138 in the sense of its cognate G1507;

Definition

a coil (spira, "spire"), i.e. (figuratively) a mass of men (a Roman military cohort; also (by analogy) a squad of Levitical janitors)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

band.
