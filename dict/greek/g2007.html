ἐπιτίθημι

Transliterationepitíthēmi
Phoneticep-ee-tith'-ay-mee
Originfrom G1909 and G5087;

Definition

to impose (in a friendly or hostile sense)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

add unto, lade, lay upon, put (up) on, set on (up), + surname, X wound.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐπιτίθημι

Transliteration: epitithēmi

Phonetic: ep-ee-tith'-ay-mee

Thayer Definition:

  1. in the active voice
    1. to put or lay upon
    2. to add to
  2. in the middle voice
    1. to have put on, bid to be laid on
    2. to lay or throw one's self upon
    3. to attack one, to make an assault on one

Origin: from G1909 and G5087

TDNT entry: 10:39,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
