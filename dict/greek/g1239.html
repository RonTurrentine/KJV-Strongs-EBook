διαδίδωμι

Transliterationdiadídōmi
Phonetic dee-ad-id'-o-mee
Originfrom G1223 and G1325;

Definition

to give throughout a crowd, i.e. deal out; also to deliver over (as to a successor)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(make) distribute(-ion), divide, give.
