Transliterationen
Phoneticen
Origina primary preposition denoting (fixed) position (in place, time or state), and (by implication) instrumentality (medially or constructively), i.e. a relation of rest (intermediate between G1519 and G1537);

Definition

"in," at, (up-)on, by, etc.
