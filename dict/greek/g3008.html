λειτουργέω

Transliterationleitourgéō
Phoneticli-toorg-eh'-o
Originfrom G3011;

Definition

to be a public servant, i.e. (by analogy) to perform religious or charitable functions (worship, obey, relieve)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage
