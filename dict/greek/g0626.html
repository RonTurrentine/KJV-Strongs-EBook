ἀπολογέομαι

Transliterationapologéomai
Phoneticap-ol-og-eh'-om-ahee
Originmiddle voice from a compound of G0575 and G3056;

Definition

to give an account (legal plea) of oneself, i.e. exculpate (self)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

answer (for self), make defence, excuse (self), speak for self.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀπολογέομαι

Transliteration: apologeomai

Phonetic: ap-ol-og-eh'-om-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to defend one's self, make one's defence
  2. to defend a person or a thing
  3. to give a full account of
    1. to calculate or consider well

Origin: middle voice from a compound of G0575 and G3056

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
