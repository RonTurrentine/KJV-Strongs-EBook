ἀκοή

Transliterationakoḗ
Phoneticak-o-ay'
Originfrom G0191;

Definition

hearing (the act, the sense or the thing heard)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

audience, ear, fame, which ye heard, hearing, preached, report, rumor.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀκοή

Transliteration: akoē

Phonetic: ak-o-ay'

Thayer Definition:

  1. the sense of hearing
  2. the organ of hearing, the ear
  3. the thing heard
    1. instruction, namely oral
      1. of preaching the gospel
    2. hearsay, report or rumour

Origin: from G0191

TDNT entry: 04:41,3

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
