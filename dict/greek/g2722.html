κατέχω

Transliterationkatéchō
Phonetickat-ekh'-o
Originfrom G2596 and G2192;

Definition

to hold down (fast), in various applications (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

have, hold (fast), keep (in memory), let, X make toward, possess, retain, seize on, stay, take, withhold.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κατέχω

Transliteration: katechō

Phonetic: kat-ekh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to hold back, detain, retain
    1. from going away
    2. to restrain, hinder (the course or progress of)
      1. that which hinders, Antichrist from making his appearance
      2. to check a ship's headway, i.e. to hold or head the ship
    3. to hold fast, keep secure, keep firm possession of
  2. to get possession of, take
    1. to possess

Origin: from G2596 and G2192

TDNT entry: 15:49,3

Part(s) of speech: Verb
