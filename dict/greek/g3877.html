παρακολουθέω

Transliterationparakolouthéō
Phoneticpar-ak-ol-oo-theh'-o
Originfrom G3844 and G190;

Definition

to follow near, i.e. (figuratively) attend (as a result), trace out, conform to

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

attain, follow, fully know, have understanding.
