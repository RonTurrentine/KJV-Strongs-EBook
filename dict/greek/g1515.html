εἰρήνη

Transliterationeirḗnē
Phonetici-ray'-nay
Originprobably from a primary verb εἴρω (to join);

Definition

peace (literally or figuratively); by implication, prosperity

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

one, peace, quietness, rest, + set at one again.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: εἰρήνη

Transliteration: eirēnē

Phonetic: i-ray'-nay

Thayer Definition:

  1. a state of national tranquillity
    1. exemption from the rage and havoc of war
  2. peace between individuals, i.e. harmony, concord
  3. security, safety, prosperity, felicity, (because peace and harmony make and keep things safe and prosperous)
  4. of the Messiah's peace
    1. the way that leads to peace (salvation)
  5. of Christianity, the tranquil state of a soul assured of its salvation through Christ, and so fearing nothing from God and content with its earthly lot, of whatsoever sort that is
  6. the blessed state of devout and upright men after death

Origin: probably from a primary verb eiro (to join)

TDNT entry: 08:40,2

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
