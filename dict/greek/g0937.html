βασιλικός

Transliterationbasilikós
Phoneticbas-il-ee-kos'
Originfrom G0935;

Definition

regal (in relation), i.e. (literally) belonging to (or befitting) the sovereign (as land, dress, or a courtier), or (figuratively) preeminent

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

king's, nobleman, royal.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: βασιλικός

Transliteration: basilikos

Phonetic: bas-il-ee-kos'

Thayer Definition:

  1. of or belong to a king, kingly, royal, regal
    1. of a man, the officer or minister of a prince, a courtier
  2. subject to a king
    1. of a country
  3. befitting or worthy of a king, royal
  4. metaphorically principal, chief

Origin: from G0935

TDNT entry: 10:52,0

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
