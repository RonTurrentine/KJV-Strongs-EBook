ἀρχιερεύς

Transliterationarchiereús
Phoneticar-khee-er-yuce'
Originfrom G746 and G2409;

Definition

the high-priest (literally, of the Jews, typically, Christ); by extension a chief priest

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

chief (high) priest, chief of the priests.
