προσφωνέω

Transliterationprosphōnéō
Phoneticpros-fo-neh'-o
Originfrom G4314 and G5455;

Definition

to sound towards, i.e. address, exclaim, summon

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

call unto, speak (un-)to.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: προσφωνέω

Transliteration: prosphōneō

Phonetic: pros-fo-neh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to call to, to address by calling
  2. to call to one's self, summon

Origin: from G4314 and G5455

TDNT entry: None
