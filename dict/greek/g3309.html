BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: μεριμνάω

Transliteration: merimnaō

Phonetic: mer-im-nah'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to be anxious
    1. to be troubled with cares
  2. to care for, look out for (a thing)
    1. to seek to promote one's interests
    2. caring or providing for

Origin: from G3308

TDNT entry: 13:49,6
