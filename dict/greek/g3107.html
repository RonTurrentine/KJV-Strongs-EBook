μακάριος

Transliterationmakários
Phoneticmak-ar'-ee-os
Origina prolonged form of the poetical μάκαρ (meaning the same);

Definition

supremely blest; by extension, fortunate, well off

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

blessed, happy(X -ier).
