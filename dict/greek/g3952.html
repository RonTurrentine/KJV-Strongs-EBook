παρουσία

Transliterationparousía
Phoneticpar-oo-see'-ah
Originfrom the present participle of G3918;

Definition

a being near, i.e. advent (often, return; specially, of Christ to punish Jerusalem, or finally the wicked); (by implication) physically, aspect

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

coming, presence.
