μέχρι

Transliterationméchri
Phonetic mekh'-ree
Originfrom G3372;

Definition

as far as, i.e. up to a certain point (as a preposition, of extent (denoting the terminus, whereas G891 refers especially to the space of time or place intervening) or conjunction)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

till, (un-)to, until.
