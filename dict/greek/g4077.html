πηγή

Transliterationpēgḗ
Phoneticpay-gay'
Originprobably from G4078 (through the idea of gushing plumply);

Definition

a fount (literally or figuratively), i.e. source or supply (of water, blood, enjoyment) (not necessarily the original spring)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fountain, well.
