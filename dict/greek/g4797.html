συγχέω

Transliterationsynchéō
Phoneticsoong-kheh'-o
Originfrom G4862 and χέω (to pour) or its alternate;

Definition

to commingle promiscuously, i.e. (figuratively) to throw (an assembly) into disorder, to perplex (the mind)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

confound, confuse, stir up, be in an uproar.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: συγχέω

Transliteration: sugcheō

Phonetic: soong-kheh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to pour together, commingle
  2. to disturb the mind of one, to stir up to tumult or outbreak
  3. to confound or bewilder

Origin: from G4862 and cheo (to pour) or its alternate

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
