νήπιος

Transliterationnḗpios
Phoneticnay'-pee-os
Originfrom an obsolete particle νή‐ (implying negation) and G2031;

Definition

not speaking, i.e. an infant (minor); figuratively, a simple-minded person, an immature Christian

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

babe, child (+ -ish).
