πλείων

Transliterationpleíōn
Phoneticpli-own
Origincomparative of G4183;

Definition

more in quantity, number, or quality; also (in plural) the major portion

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

X above, + exceed, more excellent, further, (very) great(-er), long(-er), (very) many, greater (more) part, + yet but.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πλείων πλεῖον πλέον

Transliteration: pleiōn pleion pleon

Phonetic: pli-own

Thayer Definition:

  1. greater in quantity
    1. the more part, very many
  2. greater in quality, superior, more excellent

Origin: comparative of G4183

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
