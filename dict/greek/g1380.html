δοκέω

Transliterationdokéō
Phoneticdok-eh'-o
Origina prolonged form of a primary verb, δόκωdok'-o (used only in an alternate in certain tenses;

Definition

compare the base of G1166) of the same meaning; to think; by implication, to seem (truthfully or uncertainly)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be accounted, (of own) please(-ure), be of reputation, seem (good), suppose, think, trow.
