δικαίωμα

Transliterationdikaíōma
Phoneticdik-ah'-yo-mah
Originfrom G1344;

Definition

an equitable deed; by implication, a statute or decision

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

judgment, justification, ordinance, righteousness.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: δικαίωμα

Transliteration: dikaiōma

Phonetic: dik-ah'-yo-mah

Thayer Definition:

  1. that which has been deemed right so as to have force of law
    1. what has been established, and ordained by law, an ordinance
    2. a judicial decision, sentence
      1. of God
        1. either the favourable judgment by which he acquits man and declares them acceptable to Him
        2. unfavourable: sentence of condemnation
  2. a righteous act or deed

Origin: from G1344

TDNT entry: 05:39,2

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
