χωρίζω

Transliterationchōrízō
Phonetickho-rid'-zo
Originfrom G5561;

Definition

to place room between, i.e. part; reflexively, to go away

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

depart, put asunder, separate.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: χωρίζω

Transliteration: chōrizō

Phonetic: kho-rid'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to separate, divide, part, put asunder, to separate one's self from, to depart
    1. to leave a husband or wife
    2. of divorce
    3. to depart, go away

Origin: from G5561

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
