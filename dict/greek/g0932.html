βασιλεία

Transliterationbasileía
Phoneticbas-il-i'-ah
Originfrom G0935;

Definition

properly, royalty, i.e. (abstractly) rule, or (concretely) a realm (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

kingdom, + reign.
