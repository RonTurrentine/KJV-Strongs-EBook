μαρτύρομαι

Transliterationmartýromai
Phonetic mar-too'-rom-ahee
Originmiddle voice from G3144;

Definition

to be adduced as a witness, i.e. (figuratively) to obtest (in affirmation or exhortation)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

take to record, testify.
