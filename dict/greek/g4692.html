σπεύδω

Transliterationspeúdō
Phoneticspyoo'-do
Originprobably strengthened from G4228;

Definition

to "speed" ("study"), i.e. urge on (diligently or earnestly); by implication, to await eagerly

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(make, with) haste unto.
