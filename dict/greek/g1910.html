ἐπιβαίνω

Transliterationepibaínō
Phoneticep-ee-bah'-ee-no
Originfrom G1909 and the base of G939;

Definition

to walk upon, i.e. mount, ascend, embark, arrive

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

come (into), enter into, go abroad, sit upon, take ship.
