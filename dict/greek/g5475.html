χαλκός

Transliterationchalkós
Phonetic khal-kos'
Originperhaps from G5465 through the idea of hollowing out as a vessel (this metal being chiefly used for that purpose);

Definition

copper (the substance, or some implement or coin made of it)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

brass, money.
