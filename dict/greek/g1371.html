διχοτομέω

Transliterationdichotoméō
Phonetic dee-khot-om-eh'-o
Originfrom a compound of a derivative of G1364 and a derivative of τέμνω (to cut);

Definition

to bisect, i.e. (by extension) to flog severely

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cut asunder (in sunder).
