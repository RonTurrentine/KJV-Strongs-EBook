σκεῦος

Transliterationskeûos
Phoneticskyoo'-os
Originof uncertain affinity;

Definition

a vessel, implement, equipment or apparatus (literally or figuratively [specially, a wife as contributing to the usefulness of the husband])

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

goods, sail, stuff, vessel.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: σκεῦος

Transliteration: skeuos

Phonetic: skyoo'-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. a vessel
  2. an implement
    1. in the plural
      1. household utensils, domestic gear
      2. the tackle and armament of vessels, used specifically of sails and ropes
  3. metaphorically
    1. a man of quality, a chosen instrument
    2. in a bad sense, an assistant in accomplishing an evil deed

Origin: of uncertain affinity

TDNT entry: 12:58,1

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
