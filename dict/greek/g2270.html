ἡσυχάζω

Transliterationhēsycházō
Phonetichay-soo-khad'-zo
Originfrom the same as G2272;

Definition

to keep still (intransitively), i.e. refrain from labor, meddlesomeness or speech

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cease, hold peace, be quiet, rest.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἡσυχάζω

Transliteration: hēsuchazō

Phonetic: hay-soo-khad'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to keep quiet
    1. to rest, cease from labour
    2. to lead a quiet life, said of those who are not running hither and thither, but stay at home and mind their business
    3. to be silent, i.e. to say nothing, hold one's peace

Origin: from the same as G2272

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
