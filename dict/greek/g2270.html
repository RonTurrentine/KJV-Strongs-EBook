ἡσυχάζω

Transliterationhēsycházō
Phonetic hay-soo-khad'-zo
Originfrom the same as G2272;

Definition

to keep still (intransitively), i.e. refrain from labor, meddlesomeness or speech

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cease, hold peace, be quiet, rest.
