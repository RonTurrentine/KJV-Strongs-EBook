σπένδω

Transliterationspéndō
Phoneticspen'-do
Originapparently a primary verb;

Definition

to pour out as a libation, i.e. (figuratively) to devote (one's life or blood, as a sacrifice) ("spend")

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(be ready to) be offered.
