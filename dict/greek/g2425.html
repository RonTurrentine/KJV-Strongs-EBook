ἱκανός

Transliterationhikanós
Phonetichik-an-os'
Originfrom ἵκω (ἱκάνω or ἱκνέομαι, akin to G2240) (to arrive);

Definition

competent (as if coming in season), i.e. ample (in amount) or fit (in character)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

able, + content, enough, good, great, large, long (while), many, meet, much, security, sore, sufficient, worthy.
