σιωπάω

Transliterationsiōpáō
Phoneticsee-o-pah'-o
Originfrom σιωπή (silence, i.e. a hush; properly, muteness, i.e. involuntary stillness, or inability to speak; and thus differing from G4602, which is rather a voluntary refusal or indisposition to speak, although the terms are often used synonymously);

Definition

to be dumb (but not deaf also, like 2974 properly); figuratively, to be calm (as quiet water)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dumb, (hold) peace.
