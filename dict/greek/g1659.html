ἐλευθερόω

Transliterationeleutheróō
Phoneticel-yoo-ther-o'-o
Originfrom G1658;

Definition

to liberate, i.e. (figuratively) to exempt (from moral, ceremonial or mortal liability)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

deliver, make free.
