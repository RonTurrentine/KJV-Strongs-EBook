ἀγοραῖος

Transliterationagoraîos
Phoneticag-or-ah'-yos
Originfrom G0058;

Definition

relating to the market-place, i.e. forensic (times); by implication, vulgar

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

baser sort, low.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀγοραῖος

Transliteration: agoraios

Phonetic: ag-or-ah'-yos

Thayer Definition:

  1. in, of or belonging to the market place
  2. frequenting the market place
    1. hucksters, petty traffickers, retail dealers
    2. idlers, loungers, the common sort, low, mean vulgar
  3. generally, proper to the assembly, suited to forensic speaking, business-like transactions

Origin: from G0058

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
