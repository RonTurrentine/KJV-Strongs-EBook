καταρτίζω

Transliterationkatartízō
Phonetickat-ar-tid'-zo
Originfrom G2596 and a derivative of G0739;

Definition

to complete thoroughly, i.e. repair (literally or figuratively) or adjust

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fit, frame, mend, (make) perfect(-ly join together), prepare, restore.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: καταρτίζω

Transliteration: katartizō

Phonetic: kat-ar-tid'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to render, i.e. to fit, sound, complete
    1. to mend (what has been broken or rent), to repair
      1. to complete
    2. to fit out, equip, put in order, arrange, adjust
      1. to fit or frame for one's self, prepare
    3. ethically: to strengthen, perfect, complete, make one what he ought to be

Origin: from G2596 and a derivative of G0739

TDNT entry: 08:55,8

Part(s) of speech: Verb
