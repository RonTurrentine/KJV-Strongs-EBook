καταρτίζω

Transliterationkatartízō
Phonetickat-ar-tid'-zo
Originfrom G2596 and a derivative of G739;

Definition

to complete thoroughly, i.e. repair (literally or figuratively) or adjust

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fit, frame, mend, (make) perfect(-ly join together), prepare, restore.
