πνίγω

Transliterationpnígō
Phoneticpnee'-go
Originstrengthened from G4154;

Definition

to wheeze, i.e. (causative, by implication) to throttle or strangle (drown)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

choke, take by the throat.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πνίγω

Transliteration: pnigō

Phonetic: pnee'-go

Thayer Definition:

  1. to choke, strangle
    1. of thorns crowding out the seed in the field and hindering its growth
  2. to wring one's neck, throttle

Origin: strengthened from G4154
