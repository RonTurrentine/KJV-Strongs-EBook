ἐγγύς

Transliterationengýs
Phoneticeng-goos'
Originfrom a primary verb ἄγχω (to squeeze or throttle; akin to the base of G0043);

Definition

near (literally or figuratively, of place or time)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

from , at hand, near, nigh (at hand, unto), ready.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐγγύς

Transliteration: eggus

Phonetic: eng-goos'

Thayer Definition:

  1. near, of place and position
    1. near
    2. those who are near access to God
      1. Jews, as opposed to those who are alien from God and his blessings
      2. The Rabbis used the term "to make nigh" as equivalent to "to make a proselyte"
  2. of time
    1. of times imminent and soon to come pass

Origin: "from a primary verb agcho (to squeeze or throttle; akin to the base of G0043)"

TDNT entry: 07:30,2

Part(s) of speech: Adverb
