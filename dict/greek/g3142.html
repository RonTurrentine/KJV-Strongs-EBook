μαρτύριον

Transliterationmartýrion
Phoneticmar-too'-ree-on
Originneuter of a presumed derivative of G3144;

Definition

something evidential, i.e. (genitive case) evidence given or (specially), the Decalogue (in the sacred Tabernacle)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

to be testified, testimony, witness.
