διατίθεμαι

Transliterationdiatíthemai
Phonetic dee-at-ith'-em-ahee
Originmiddle voice from G1223 and G5087;

Definition

to put apart, i.e. (figuratively) dispose (by assignment, compact, or bequest)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

appoint, make, testator.
