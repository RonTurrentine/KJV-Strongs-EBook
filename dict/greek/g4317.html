προσάγω

Transliterationproságō
Phonetic pros-ag'-o
Originfrom G4314 and G71;

Definition

to lead towards, i.e. (transitively) to conduct near (summon, present), or (intransitively) to approach

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bring, draw near.
