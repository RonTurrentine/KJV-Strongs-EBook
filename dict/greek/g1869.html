ἐπαίρω

Transliterationepaírō
Phoneticep-ahee'-ro
Originfrom G1909 and G0142;

Definition

to raise up (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

exalt self, poise (lift, take) up.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐπαίρω

Transliteration: epairō

Phonetic: ep-ahee'-ro

Thayer Definition:

  1. to lift up, raise up, raise on high
  2. metaphorically to be lifted up with pride, to exalt one's self

Origin: from G1909 and G0142

TDNT entry: 04:06,3

Part(s) of speech: Verb
