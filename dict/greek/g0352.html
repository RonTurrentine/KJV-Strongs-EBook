ἀνακύπτω

Transliterationanakýptō
Phonetican-ak-oop'-to
Originfrom G0303 (in the sense of reversal) and G2955;

Definition

to unbend, i.e. rise; figuratively, be elated

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

lift up, look up.
