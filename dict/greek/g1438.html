ἑαυτοῦ

Transliterationheautoû
Phoneticheh-ow-too'
Originfrom a reflexive pronoun otherwise obsolete and the genitive case (dative case or accusative case) of G846;

Definition

him- (her-, it-, them-, also (in conjunction with the personal pronoun of the other persons) my-, thy-, our-, your-) self (selves), etc.

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

alone, her (own, -self), (he) himself, his (own), itself, one (to) another, our (thine) own(-selves), + that she had, their (own, own selves), (of) them(-selves), they, thyself, you, your (own, own conceits, own selves, -selves).
