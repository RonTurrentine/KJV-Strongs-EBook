ἰῶτα

Transliterationiōta
Phonetic ee-o'-tah
Originof Hebrew origin (the tenth letter of the Hebrew alphabet);

Definition

"iota", the name of the eighth letter of the Greek alphabet, put (figuratively) for a very small part of anything

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

jot.
