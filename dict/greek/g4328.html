προσδοκάω

Transliterationprosdokáō
Phoneticpros-dok-ah'-o
Originfrom G4314 and dokeuo (to watch);

Definition

to anticipate (in thought, hope or fear); by implication, to await

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(be in) expect(-ation), look (for), when looked, tarry, wait for.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: προσδοκάω

Transliteration: prosdokaō

Phonetic: pros-dok-ah'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to expect (whether in thought, in hope, or in fear)
  2. to look for, wait for

Origin: from G4314 and dokeuo (to watch)

TDNT entry: 18:05,9

Part(s) of speech: Verb
