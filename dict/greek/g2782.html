κήρυγμα

Transliterationkḗrygma
Phonetickay'-roog-mah
Originfrom G2784;

Definition

a proclamation (especially of the gospel; by implication, the gospel itself)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

preaching.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κήρυγμα

Transliteration: kērugma

Phonetic: kay'-roog-mah

Thayer Definition:

  1. that which is proclaimed by a herald or public crier, a proclamation by herald
  2. in the NT the message or proclamation of the heralds of God or Christ

Origin: from G2784

TDNT entry: 14:54,4

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
