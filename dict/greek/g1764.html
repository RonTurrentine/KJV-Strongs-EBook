ἐνίστημι

Transliterationenístēmi
Phoneticen-is'-tay-mee
Originfrom G1722 and G2476;

Definition

to place on hand, i.e. (reflexively) impend, (participle) be instant

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

come, be at hand, present.

BDB / Thayer Definition
