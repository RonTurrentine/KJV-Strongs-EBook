περισσός

Transliterationperissós
Phoneticper-is-sos'
Originfrom G4012 (in the sense of beyond);

Definition

superabundant (in quantity) or superior (in quality); by implication, excessive; adverbially (with G1537) violently; neuter (as noun) preeminence

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

exceeding abundantly above, more abundantly, advantage, exceedingly, very highly, beyond measure, more, superfluous, vehement(-ly).
