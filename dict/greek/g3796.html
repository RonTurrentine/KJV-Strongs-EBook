Originfrom the same as G3694 (through the idea of backwardness);

Definition

(at) even, in the end.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὀψέ

Transliteration: opse

Phonetic: op-seh'

Thayer Definition:

  1. after a long time, long after, late
    1. late in the day, i.e. at evening
    2. the sabbath having just passed, after the sabbath
      1. at the early dawn of the first day of the week

Origin: from the same as G3694 (through the idea of backwardness)
