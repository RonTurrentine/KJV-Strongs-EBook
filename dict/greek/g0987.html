βλασφημέω

Transliterationblasphēméō
Phoneticblas-fay-meh'-o
Originfrom G0989;

Definition

to vilify; specially, to speak impiously

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(speak) blaspheme(-er, -mously, -my), defame, rail on, revile, speak evil.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: βλασφημέω

Transliteration: blasphēmeō

Phonetic: blas-fay-meh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to speak reproachfully, rail at, revile, calumniate, blaspheme
  2. to be evil spoken of, reviled, railed at

Origin: from G0989

TDNT entry: 11:21,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
