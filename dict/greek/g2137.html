εὐοδόω

Transliterationeuodóō
Phoneticyoo-od-o'-o
Originfrom a compound of G2095 and G3598;

Definition

to help on the road, i.e. (passively) succeed in reaching; figuratively, to succeed in business affairs

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(have a) prosper(-ous journey).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: εὐοδόω

Transliteration: euodoō

Phonetic: yoo-od-o'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to grant a prosperous and expeditious journey, to lead by a direct and easy way
  2. to grant a successful issue, to cause to prosper
  3. to prosper, be successful

Origin: from a compound of G2095 and G3598

TDNT entry: 06:49,7

Part(s) of speech: Verb
