μηνύω

Transliterationmēnýō
Phoneticmay-noo'-o
Originprobably from the same base as G3145 and G3415 (i.e. μάω, to strive);

Definition

to disclose (through the idea of mental effort and thus calling to mind), i.e. report, declare, intimate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

shew, tell.
