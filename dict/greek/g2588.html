καρδία

Transliterationkardía
Phonetic kar-dee'-ah
Originprolonged from a primary κάρ (Latin cor, "heart");

Definition

the heart, i.e. (figuratively) the thoughts or feelings (mind); also (by analogy) the middle

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(+ broken-)heart(-ed).
