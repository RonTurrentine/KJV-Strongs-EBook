φυλάσσω

Transliterationphylássō
Phoneticfoo-las'-so
Originprobably from G5443 through the idea of isolation;

Definition

to watch, i.e. be on guard (literally of figuratively); by implication, to preserve, obey, avoid

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beward, keep (self), observe, save.
