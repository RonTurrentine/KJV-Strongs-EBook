ἀναιρέω

Transliterationanairéō
Phonetican-ahee-reh'-o
Originfrom G0303 and (the active of) G0138;

Definition

to take up, i.e. adopt; by implication, to take away (violently), i.e. abolish, murder

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

put to death, kill, slay, take away, take up.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀναιρέω

Transliteration: anaireō

Phonetic: an-ahee-reh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to take up, to lift up (from the ground)
    1. to take up for myself as mine
    2. to own (an exposed infant)
  2. to take away, abolish
    1. to do away with or abrogate customs or ordinances
    2. to put out of the way, kill slay a man

Origin: from G0303 and (the active of) G0138

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
