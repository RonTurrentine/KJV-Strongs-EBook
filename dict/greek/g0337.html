ἀναιρέω

Transliterationanairéō
Phonetican-ahee-reh'-o
Originfrom G303 and (the active of) G138;

Definition

to take up, i.e. adopt; by implication, to take away (violently), i.e. abolish, murder

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

put to death, kill, slay, take away, take up.
