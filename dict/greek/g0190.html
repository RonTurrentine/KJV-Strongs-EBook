ἀκολουθέω

Transliterationakolouthéō
Phoneticak-ol-oo-theh'-o
Originfrom G0001 (as a particle of union) and κέλευθος (a road);

Definition

properly, to be in the same way with, i.e. to accompany (specially, as a disciple)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

follow, reach.
