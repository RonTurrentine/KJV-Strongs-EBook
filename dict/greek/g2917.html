κρίμα

Transliterationkríma
Phonetickree'-mah
Originfrom G2919;

Definition

a decision (the function or the effect, for or against ("crime"))

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

avenge, condemned, condemnation, damnation, + go to law, judgment.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κρίμα

Transliteration: krima

Phonetic: kree'-mah

Thayer Definition:

  1. a decree, judgments
  2. judgment
    1. condemnation of wrong, the decision (whether severe or mild) which one passes on the faults of others
    2. in a forensic sense
      1. the sentence of a judge
      2. the punishment with which one is sentenced
      3. condemnatory sentence, penal judgment, sentence
  3. a matter to be judicially decided, a lawsuit, a case in court

Origin: from G2919

TDNT entry: 18:42,5

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
