ὁποῖος

Transliterationhopoîos
Phonetichop-oy'-os
Originfrom G3739 and G4169;

Definition

of what kind that, i.e. how (as) great (excellent) (specially, as an indefinite correlative to the definite antecedent G5108 of quality)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

what manner (sort) of, such as whatsoever.
