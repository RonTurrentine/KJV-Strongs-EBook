σαρκικός

Transliterationsarkikós
Phoneticsar-kee-kos'
Originfrom G4561;

Definition

pertaining to flesh, i.e. (by extension) bodily, temporal, or (by implication) animal, unregenerate

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

carnal, fleshly.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: σαρκικός

Transliteration: sarkikos

Phonetic: sar-kee-kos'

Thayer Definition:

  1. fleshly, carnal
    1. having the nature of flesh, i.e. under the control of the animal appetites
      1. governed by mere human nature not by the Spirit of God
      2. having its seat in the animal nature or aroused by the animal nature
      3. human: with the included idea of depravity
    2. pertaining to the flesh
      1. to the body: related to birth, linage, etc

Origin: from G4561

TDNT entry: 08:38,1

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
