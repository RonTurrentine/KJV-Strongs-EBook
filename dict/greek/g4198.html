πορεύομαι

Transliterationporeúomai
Phoneticpor-yoo'-om-ahee
Originmiddle voice from a derivative of the same as G3984;

Definition

to traverse, i.e. travel (literally or figuratively; especially to remove (figuratively, die), live, etc.);

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

--depart, go (away, forth, one's way, up), (make a, take a) journey, walk.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πορεύομαι

Transliteration: poreuomai

Phonetic: por-yoo'-om-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to lead over, carry over, transfer
    1. to pursue the journey on which one has entered, to continue on one's journey
    2. to depart from life
    3. to follow one, that is: become his adherent
      1. to lead or order one's life

Origin: middle voice from a derivative of the same as G3984

TDNT entry: 15:26,9

Part(s) of speech: Verb
