κόπτω

Transliterationkóptō
Phonetickop'-to
Origina primary verb;

Definition

to "chop"; specially, to beat the breast in grief

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cut down, lament, mourn, (be-)wail.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κόπτω

Transliteration: koptō

Phonetic: kop'-to

Thayer Definition:

  1. to cut, strike, smite
  2. to cut from, cut off
  3. to beat one's breast for grief

Origin: a root word

TDNT entry: 16:50,5

Part(s) of speech: Verb
