δέομαι

Transliterationdéomai
Phoneticdeh'-om-ahee
Originmiddle voice of G1210;

Definition

to beg (as binding oneself), i.e. petition

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beseech, pray (to), make request.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: δέομαι

Transliteration: deomai

Phonetic: deh'-om-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to want, lack
  2. to desire, long for
  3. to ask, beg
    1. the thing asked for
    2. to pray, make supplications

Origin: middle voice of G1210
