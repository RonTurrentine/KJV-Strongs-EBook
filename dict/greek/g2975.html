λαγχάνω

Transliterationlanchánō
Phoneticlang-khan'-o
Origina prolonged form of a primary verb, which is only used as an alternate in certain tenses;

Definition

to lot, i.e. determine (by implication, receive) especially by lot

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

his lot be, cast lots, obtain.
