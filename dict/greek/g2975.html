λαγχάνω

Transliterationlanchánō
Phoneticlang-khan'-o
Origina prolonged form of a primary verb, which is only used as an alternate in certain tenses;

Definition

to lot, i.e. determine (by implication, receive) especially by lot

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

his lot be, cast lots, obtain.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: λαγχάνω

Transliteration: lagchanō

Phonetic: lang-khan'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to obtain by lot
    1. to receive by divine allotment, obtain
  2. to cast lots, determine by lot

Origin: a prolonged form of a primary verb, which is only used as an alternate in certain tenses
