πήγνυμι

Transliterationpḗgnymi
Phoneticpayg'-noo-mee
Origina prolonged form of a primary verb (which in its simpler form occurs only as an alternate in certain tenses);

Definition

to fix ("peg"), i.e. (specially) to set up (a tent)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

pitch.
