σοφίζω

Transliterationsophízō
Phoneticsof-id'-zo
Originfrom G4680;

Definition

to render wise; in a sinister acceptation, to form "sophisms", i.e. continue plausible error

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cunningly devised, make wise.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: σοφίζω

Transliteration: sophizō

Phonetic: sof-id'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to make wise, teach
  2. to become wise, to have understanding
    1. to invent, play the sophist
    2. to devise cleverly or cunningly
