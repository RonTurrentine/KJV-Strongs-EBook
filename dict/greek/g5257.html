ὑπηρέτης

Transliterationhypērétēs
Phonetic hoop-ay-ret'-ace
Originfrom G5259 and a derivative of ἐρέσσω (to row);

Definition

an under-oarsman, i.e. (generally) subordinate (assistant, sexton, constable)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

minister, officer, servant.
