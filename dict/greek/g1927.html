ἐπιδημέω

Transliterationepidēméō
Phoneticep-ee-day-meh'-o
Originfrom a compound of G1909 and G1218;

Definition

to make oneself at home, i.e. (by extension) to reside (in a foreign country)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(be) dwelling (which were) there, stranger.
