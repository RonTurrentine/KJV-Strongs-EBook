μάστιξ

Transliterationmástix
Phonetic mas'-tix
Originprobably from the base of G3145 (through the idea of contact);

Definition

a whip (literally, the Roman flagellum for criminals; figuratively, a disease)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

plague, scourging.
