θεωρέω

Transliterationtheōréō
Phonetictheh-o-reh'-o
Originfrom a derivative of G2300 (perhaps by addition of G3708);

Definition

to be a spectator of, i.e. discern, (literally, figuratively (experience) or intensively (acknowledge))

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

behold, consider, look on, perceive, see.
