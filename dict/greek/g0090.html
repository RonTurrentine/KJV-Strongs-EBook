ἀδιαφθορία

Transliterationadiaphthoría
Phoneticad-ee-af-thor-ee'-ah
Originfrom a derivative of a compound of G1 (as a negative particle) and a derivative of G1311;

Definition

incorruptibleness, i.e. (figuratively) purity (of doctrine)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage
