Definition

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

deep(-ness, things), depth.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: βάθος

Transliteration: bathos

Phonetic: bath'-os

Thayer Definition:

  1. depth, height
    1. of "the deep" sea
    2. metaphorically
      1. deep, extreme, poverty
      2. of the deep things of God
