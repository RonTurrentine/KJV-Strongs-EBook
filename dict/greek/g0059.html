ἀγοράζω

Transliterationagorázō
Phoneticag-or-ad'-zo
Originfrom G0058;

Definition

properly, to go to market, i.e. (by implication) to purchase; specially, to redeem

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

buy, redeem.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀγοράζω

Transliteration: agorazō

Phonetic: ag-or-ad'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to be in the market place, to attend it
  2. to do business there, buy or sell
  3. of idle people: to haunt the market place, lounge there

Origin: from G0058

TDNT entry: 03:04,2

Part(s) of speech: Verb
