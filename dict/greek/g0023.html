ἀγανακτέω

Transliterationaganaktéō
Phoneticag-an-ak-teh'-o
Originfrom ἄγαν (much) and ἄχθος (grief; akin to the base of G43);

Definition

to be greatly afflicted, i.e. (figuratively) indignant

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be much (sore) displeased, have (be moved with, with) indignation.
