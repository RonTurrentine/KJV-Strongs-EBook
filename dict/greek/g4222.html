BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ποτίζω

Transliteration: potizō

Phonetic: pot-id'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to give to drink, to furnish drink
  2. to water, irrigate (plants, fields etc.)
  3. metaphorically to imbue, saturate one's mind

Origin: from a derivative of the alternate of G4095

TDNT entry: 08:39,8

Part(s) of speech: Verb
