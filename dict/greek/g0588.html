ἀποδέχομαι

Transliterationapodéchomai
Phoneticap-od-ekh'-om-ahee
Originfrom G0575 and G1209;

Definition

to take fully, i.e. welcome (persons), approve (things)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

accept, receive (gladly).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Thayer Definition:
