προστίθημι

Transliterationprostíthēmi
Phoneticpros-tith'-ay-mee
Originfrom G4314 and G5087;

Definition

to place additionally, i.e. lay beside, annex, repeat

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

add, again, give more, increase, lay unto, proceed further, speak to any more.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: προστίθημι

Transliteration: prostithēmi

Phonetic: pros-tith'-ay-mee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to put to
  2. to add
    1. i.e. to join to, gather with any company, the number of one's followers or companions
      1. he was gathered to his fathers, i.e. died

Origin: from G4314 and G5087

TDNT entry: 10:47,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
