προστίθημι

Transliterationprostíthēmi
Phoneticpros-tith'-ay-mee
Originfrom G4314 and G5087;

Definition

to place additionally, i.e. lay beside, annex, repeat

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

add, again, give more, increase, lay unto, proceed further, speak to any more.
