ἔρχομαι

Transliterationérchomai
Phoneticer'-khom-ahee
Originmiddle voice of a primary verb (used only in the present and imperfect tenses, the others being supplied by a kindred (middle voice) ἐλεύθομαιel-yoo'-thom-ahee, or (active) ἔλθωel'-tho, which do not otherwise occur);

Definition

to come or go (in a great variety of applications, literally and figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

accompany, appear, bring, come, enter, fall out, go, grow, X light, X next, pass, resort, be set.
