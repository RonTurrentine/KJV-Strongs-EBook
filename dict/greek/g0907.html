βαπτίζω

Transliterationbaptízō
Phoneticbap-tid'-zo
Originfrom a derivative of G0911;

Definition

to immerse, submerge; to make whelmed (i.e. fully wet); used only (in the New Testament) of ceremonial ablution, especially (technically) of the ordinance of Christian baptism

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Baptist, baptize, wash.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: βαπτίζω

Transliteration: baptizō

Phonetic: bap-tid'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to dip repeatedly, to immerse, to submerge (of vessels sunk)
  2. to cleanse by dipping or submerging, to wash, to make clean with water, to wash one's self, bathe
  3. to overwhelm

Origin: from a derivative of G0911

TDNT entry: 09:49,9
