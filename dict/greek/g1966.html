ἐπιοῦσα

Transliterationepioûsa
Phonetic ep-ee-oo'-sah
Originfeminine singular participle of a comparative of G1909 and εἶμι (to go);

Definition

supervening, i.e. (G2250 or G3571 being expressed or implied) the ensuing day or night

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage
