χρυσίον

Transliterationchrysíon
Phonetickhroo-see'-on
Origindiminutive of G5557;

Definition

a golden article, i.e. gold plating, ornament, or coin

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

gold.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: χρυσίον

Transliteration: chrusion

Phonetic: khroo-see'-on

Thayer Definition:

  1. gold, both that which lies imbedded in the earth and is dug out of it
  2. that which has been smelted and wrought
    1. of a gold coin
    2. of golden ornaments
    3. of precious things made of gold

Origin: diminutive of G5557

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
