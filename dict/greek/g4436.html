Πύθων

TransliterationPýthōn
Phonetic poo'-thone
Originfrom Πυθώ (the name of the region where Delphi, the seat of the famous oracle, was located);

Definition

a Python, i.e. (by analogy, with the supposed diviner there) inspiration (soothsaying)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

divination.
