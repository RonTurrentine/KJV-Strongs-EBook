ἐπίκειμαι

Transliterationepíkeimai
Phoneticep-ik'-i-mahee
Originfrom G1909 and G2749;

Definition

to rest upon (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

impose, be instant, (be) laid (there-, up-)on, (when) lay (on), lie (on), press upon.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐπίκειμαι

Transliteration: epikeimai

Phonetic: ep-ik'-i-mahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to lie upon or over, rest upon, be laid or placed upon
    1. on the burning coals
  2. metaphorically
    1. of things, of the pressure of a violent tempest
    2. of men, to press upon, to be urgent

Origin: from G1909 and G2749

TDNT entry: 13:55,4

Part(s) of speech: Verb
