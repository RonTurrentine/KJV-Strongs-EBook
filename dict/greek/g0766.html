ἀσέλγεια

Transliterationasélgeia
Phoneticas-elg'-i-a
Originfrom a compound of G0001 (as a negative particle) and a presumed σελγής (of uncertain derivation, but apparently meaning continent);

Definition

licentiousness (sometimes including other vices)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

filthy, lasciviousness, wantonness.
