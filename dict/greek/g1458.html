ἐγκαλέω

Transliterationenkaléō
Phoneticeng-kal-eh'-o
Originfrom G1722 and G2564;

Definition

to call in (as a debt or demand), i.e. bring to account (charge, criminate, etc.)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

accuse, call in question, implead, lay to the charge.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐγκαλέω

Transliteration: egkaleō

Phonetic: eng-kal-eh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to come forward as accuser against, bring charge against
  2. to be accused

Origin: from G1722 and G2564

TDNT entry: 11:16,4

Part(s) of speech: Verb
