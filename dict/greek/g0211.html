ἀλάβαστρον

Transliterationalábastron
Phonetic al-ab'-as-tron
Originneuter of ἀλάβαστρος (of uncertain derivation), the name of a stone;

Definition

properly, an "alabaster" box, i.e. (by extension) a perfume vase (of any material)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(alabaster) box.
