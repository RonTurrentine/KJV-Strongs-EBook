προσκόπτω

Transliterationproskóptō
Phoneticpros-kop'-to
Originfrom G4314 and G2875;

Definition

to strike at, i.e. surge against (as water); specially, to stub on, i.e. trip up (literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

beat upon, dash, stumble (at).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: προσκόπτω

Transliteration: proskoptō

Phonetic: pros-kop'-to

Thayer Definition:

  1. to strike against
    1. of those who strike against a stone or other obstacle in the path, to stumble
    2. to strike one's foot against a stone
      1. i.e. to meet with some harm
    3. to rush upon, beat against
    4. to be made to stumble by a thing
      1. i.e. metaphorically to be induced to sin

Origin: from G4314 and G2875

TDNT entry: 18:25,9

Part(s) of speech: Verb
