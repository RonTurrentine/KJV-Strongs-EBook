ἔπω

Transliterationépō
Phoneticep'-o
Origina primary verb (used only in the definite past tense, the others being borrowed from G2046, G4483, and G5346);

Definition

to speak or say (by word or writing)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

answer, bid, bring word, call, command, grant, say (on), speak, tell.
