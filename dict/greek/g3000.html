λατρεύω

Transliterationlatreúō
Phoneticlat-ryoo'-o
Originfrom λάτρις (a hired menial);

Definition

to minister (to God), i.e. render religious homage

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

serve, do the service, worship(-per).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: λατρεύω

Transliteration: latreuō

Phonetic: lat-ryoo'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to serve for hire
  2. to serve, minister to, either to the gods or men and used alike of slaves and freemen
    1. in the NT, to render religious service or homage, to worship
    2. to perform sacred services, to offer gifts, to worship God in the observance of the rites instituted for his worship
      1. of priests, to officiate, to discharge the sacred office

Origin: from latris (a hired menial)

TDNT entry: 04:58,5

Part(s) of speech: Verb
