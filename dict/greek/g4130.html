πλήθω

Transliterationplḗthō
Phoneticplay'-tho
Origina prolonged form of a primary πλέωpleh'-o (which appears only as an alternate in certain tenses and in the reduplicated form πίμπλημι);

Definition

to "fill" (literally or figuratively (imbue, influence, supply)); specially, to fulfil (time)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

accomplish, full (…come), furnish.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πλήθω

Transliteration: plēthō

Phonetic: play'-tho

Thayer Definition:

  1. to fill
  2. to be fulfilled, to be filled

Origin: a prolonged form of a primary pleo pleh'-o (which appears only as an alternate in certain tenses and in the reduplicated form pimplemi)

TDNT entry: 6:128,*

Part(s) of speech: Verb
