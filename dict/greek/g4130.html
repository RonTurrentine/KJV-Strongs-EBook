πλήθω

Transliterationplḗthō
Phoneticplay'-tho
Origina prolonged form of a primary πλέωpleh'-o (which appears only as an alternate in certain tenses and in the reduplicated form πίμπλημι);

Definition

to "fill" (literally or figuratively (imbue, influence, supply)); specially, to fulfil (time)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

accomplish, full (…come), furnish.
