κρίνω

Transliterationkrínō
Phonetickree'-no
Originproperly, to distinguish, i.e. decide (mentally or judicially);

Definition

by implication, to try, condemn, punish

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

avenge, conclude, condemn, damn, decree, determine, esteem, judge, go to (sue at the) law, ordain, call in question, sentence to, think.
