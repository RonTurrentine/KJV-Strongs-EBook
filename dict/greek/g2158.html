BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: εὐσχήμων

Transliteration: euschēmōn

Phonetic: yoo-skhay'-mone

Thayer Definition:

  1. of elegant figure
    1. shapely, graceful, comely, bearing one's self becomingly in speech or behaviour
  2. of good standing
    1. honourable, influential, wealthy, respectable

Origin: from G2095 and G4976

TDNT entry: 14:50,3
