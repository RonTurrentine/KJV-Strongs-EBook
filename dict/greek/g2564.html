καλέω

Transliterationkaléō
Phonetickal-eh'-o
Originakin to the base of G2753;

Definition

to "call" (properly, aloud, but used in a variety of applications, directly or otherwise)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bid, call (forth), (whose, whose sur-)name (was (called)).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: καλέω

Transliteration: kaleō

Phonetic: kal-eh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to call
    1. to call aloud, utter in a loud voice
    2. to invite
  2. to call, i.e. to name, by name
    1. to give a name to
      1. to receive the name of, receive as a name
      2. to give some name to one, call his name
    2. to be called, i.e. to bear a name or title (among men)
    3. to salute one by name

Origin: akin to the base of G2753

TDNT entry: 11:07,4

Part(s) of speech: Verb
