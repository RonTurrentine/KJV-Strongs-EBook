Transliterationóphis
Phoneticof'-is
Originprobably from G3700 (through the idea of sharpness of vision);

Definition

a snake, figuratively, (as a type of sly cunning) an artful malicious person, especially Satan

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

serpent.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὄφις

Transliteration: ophis

Phonetic: of'-is

Thayer Definition:

  1. snake, serpent
  2. with the ancients, the serpent was an emblem of cunning and wisdom. The serpent who deceived Eve was regarded by the Jews as the devil.

Origin: probably from G3700 (through the idea of sharpness of vision)

TDNT entry: 14:26,7

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
