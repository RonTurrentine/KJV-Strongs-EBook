μετατίθημι

Transliterationmetatíthēmi
Phoneticmet-at-ith'-ay-mee
Originfrom G3326 and G5087;

Definition

to transfer, i.e. (literally) transport, (by implication) exchange, (reflexively) change sides, or (figuratively) pervert

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

carry over, change, remove, translate, turn.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: μετατίθημι

Transliteration: metatithēmi

Phonetic: met-at-ith'-ay-mee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to transpose (two things, one of which is put in place of the other)
    1. to transfer
    2. to change
    3. to transfer one's self or suffer one's self to be transferred
      1. to go or pass over
      2. to fall away or desert from one person or thing to another

Origin: from G3326 and G5087

TDNT entry: 10:41,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
