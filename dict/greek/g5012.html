BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ταπεινοφροσύνη

Transliteration: tapeinophrosunē

Phonetic: tap-i-nof-ros-oo'-nay

Thayer Definition:

  1. the having a humble opinion of one's self
  2. a deep sense of one's (moral) littleness
  3. modesty, humility, lowliness of mind

Origin: from a compound of G5011 and the base of G5424

TDNT entry: 08:01,1

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
