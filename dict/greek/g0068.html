ἀγρός

Transliterationagrós
Phoneticag-ros'
Originfrom G0071;

Definition

a field (as a drive for cattle); genitive case, the country; specially, a farm, i.e. hamlet

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

country, farm, piece of ground, land.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀγρός

Transliteration: agros

Phonetic: ag-ros'

Thayer Definition:

  1. land
    1. the field, the country
    2. a piece of land, bit of tillage
    3. the farms, country seats, neighbouring hamlets

Origin: from G0071

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
