Originfrom the same as G0040;

Definition

properly, clean, i.e. (figuratively) innocent, modest, perfect

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

chaste, clean, pure.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἁγνός

Transliteration: hagnos

Phonetic: hag-nos'

Thayer Definition:

  1. exciting reverence, venerable, sacred
  2. pure
    1. pure from carnality, chaste, modest
    2. pure from every fault, immaculate
    3. clean

Origin: from the same as G0040

TDNT entry: 03:02,2

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
