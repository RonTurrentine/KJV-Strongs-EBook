ἐπισκέπτομαι

Transliterationepisképtomai
Phoneticep-ee-skep'-tom-ahee
Originmiddle voice from G1909 and the base of G4649;

Definition

to inspect, i.e. (by implication) to select; by extension, to go to see, relieve

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

look out, visit.
