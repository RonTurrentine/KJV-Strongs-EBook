ἀτενίζω

Transliterationatenízō
Phoneticat-en-id'-zo
Originfrom a compound of G0001 (as a particle of union) and τείνω (to stretch);

Definition

to gaze intently

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

behold earnestly (stedfastly), fasten (eyes), look (earnestly, stedfastly, up stedfastly), set eyes.
