ὅριον

Transliterationhórion
Phonetichor'-ee-on
Originneuter of a derivative of an apparently primary ὅρος (a bound or limit);

Definition

a boundary-line, i.e. (by implication) a frontier (region)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

border, coast.
