ἐπιλαμβάνομαι

Transliterationepilambánomai
Phonetic ep-ee-lam-ban'-om-ahee
Originmiddle voice from G1909 and G2983;

Definition

to seize (for help, injury, attainment, or any other purpose; literally or figuratively)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

catch, lay hold (up-)on, take (by, hold of, on).
