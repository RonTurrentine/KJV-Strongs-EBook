προσφέρω

Transliterationprosphérō
Phoneticpros-fer'-o
Originfrom G4314 and G5342 (including its alternate);

Definition

to bear towards, i.e. lead to, tender (especially to God), treat

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bring (to, unto), deal with, do, offer (unto, up), present unto, put to.
