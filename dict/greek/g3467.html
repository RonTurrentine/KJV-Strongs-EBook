μυωπάζω

Transliterationmyōpázō
Phoneticmoo-ope-ad'-zo
Originfrom a compound of the base of G3466 and ὤψ (the face; from G3700);

Definition

to shut the eyes, i.e. blink (see indistinctly)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

cannot see far off.
