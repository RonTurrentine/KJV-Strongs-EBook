τίκτω

Transliterationtíktō
Phonetic tik'-to
Origina strengthened form of a primary τέκωtek'-o (which is used only as alternate in certain tenses);

Definition

to produce (from seed, as a mother, a plant, the earth, etc.), literally or figuratively

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bear, be born, bring forth, be delivered, be in travail.
