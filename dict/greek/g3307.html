μερίζω

Transliterationmerízō
Phoneticmer-id'-zo
Originfrom G3313;

Definition

to part, i.e. (literally) to apportion, bestow, share, or (figuratively) to disunite, differ

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

deal, be difference between, distribute, divide, give part.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: μερίζω

Transliteration: merizō

Phonetic: mer-id'-zo

Thayer Definition:

  1. to divide
    1. to separate into parts, cut into pieces
      1. to divide into parties, i.e. be split into factions
    2. to distribute
      1. a thing among people
      2. bestow, impart

Origin: from G3313

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
