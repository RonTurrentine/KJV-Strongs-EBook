BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀρχηγός

Transliteration: archēgos

Phonetic: ar-khay-gos'

Thayer Definition:

  1. the chief leader, prince
    1. of Christ
  2. one that takes the lead in any thing and thus affords an example, a predecessor in a matter, pioneer
  3. the author

Origin: from G0746 and G0071

TDNT entry: 09:07,8

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
