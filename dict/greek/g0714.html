ἀρκέω

Transliterationarkéō
Phoneticar-keh'-o
Originapparently a primary verb (but probably akin to G142 through the idea of raising a barrier);

Definition

properly, to ward off, i.e. (by implication) to avail (figuratively, be satisfactory)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be content, be enough, suffice, be sufficient.
