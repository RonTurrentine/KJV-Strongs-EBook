ἀρκέω

Transliterationarkéō
Phoneticar-keh'-o
Originapparently a primary verb (but probably akin to G0142 through the idea of raising a barrier);

Definition

properly, to ward off, i.e. (by implication) to avail (figuratively, be satisfactory)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be content, be enough, suffice, be sufficient.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀρκέω

Transliteration: arkeō

Phonetic: ar-keh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to be possessed of unfailing strength
    1. to be strong, to suffice, to be enough
      1. to defend, ward off
    2. to be satisfied, to be contented

Origin: apparently a primary word [but probably akin to G0142 through the idea of raising a barrier]

TDNT entry: 08:44,8

Part(s) of speech: Verb
