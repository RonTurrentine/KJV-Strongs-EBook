Transliterationkagṓ
Phonetic kag-o'
Originfrom G2532 and G1473;

Definition

so also the dative case κἀμοίkam-oy', and accusative case κἀμέkam-eh' and (or also, even, etc.) I, (to) me

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

(and, even, even so, so) I (also, in like wise), both me, me also.
