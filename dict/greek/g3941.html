πάροικος

Transliterationpároikos
Phoneticpar'-oy-kos
Originfrom G3844 and G3624;

Definition

having a home near, i.e. (as noun) a by-dweller (alien resident)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

foreigner, sojourn, stranger.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: πάροικος

Transliteration: paroikos

Phonetic: par'-oy-kos

Thayer Definition:

  1. dwelling near, neighbouring
  2. in the NT, a stranger, a foreigner, one who lives in a place without the right of citizenship
  3. metaphorically
    1. without citizenship in God's kingdom
    2. one who lives on earth as a stranger, a sojourner on the earth
    3. of Christians whose home is in heaven

Origin: from G3844 and G3624

TDNT entry: 19:01,8

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
