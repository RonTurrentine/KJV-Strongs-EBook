βούλομαι

Transliterationboúlomai
Phoneticboo'-lom-ahee
Originmiddle voice of a primary verb;

Definition

to "will," i.e. (reflexively) be willing

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be disposed, minded, intend, list, (be, of own) will (-ing).
