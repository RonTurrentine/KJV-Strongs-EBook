διασώζω

Transliterationdiasṓzō
Phoneticdee-as-odze'-o
Originfrom G1223 and G4982;

Definition

to save thoroughly, i.e. (by implication or analogy) to cure, preserve, rescue, etc.

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bring safe, escape (safe), heal, make perfectly whole, save.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: διασώζω

Transliteration: diasōzō

Phonetic: dee-as-odze'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to preserve through danger, to bring safely through
    1. to save, i.e. cure one who is sick, bring him through
  2. to save, keep from perishing
  3. to save out of danger, rescue

Origin: from G1223 and G4982

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
