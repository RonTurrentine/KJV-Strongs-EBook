Ἰλλυρικόν

TransliterationIllyrikón
Phoneticil-loo-ree-kon'
Originneuter of an adjective from a name of uncertain derivation;

Definition

(the) Illyrican (shore), i.e. (as a name itself) Illyricum, a region of Europe

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

Illyricum.
