ἱλάσκομαι

Transliterationhiláskomai
Phonetichil-as'-kom-ahee
Originmiddle voice from the same as G2436;

Definition

to conciliate, i.e. (transitively) to atone for (sin), or (intransitively) be propitious

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be merciful, make reconciliation for.
