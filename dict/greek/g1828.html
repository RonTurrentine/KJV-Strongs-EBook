ἐξέλκω

Transliterationexélkō
Phoneticex-el'-ko
Originfrom G1537 and G1670;

Definition

to drag forth, i.e. (figuratively) to entice (to sin)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

draw away.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐξέλκω

Transliteration: exelkō

Phonetic: ex-el'-ko

Thayer Definition:

  1. to draw out
  2. metaphorically lure forth: in hunting and fishing as game is lured from its hiding place, so man by lure is allured from the safety of self-restraint to sin. In Jas 1:14, the language of the hunting is transferred to the seduction of a harlot.

Origin: from G1537 and G1670

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Verb
