βδελύσσω

Transliterationbdelýssō
Phonetic bdel-oos'-so
Originfrom a (presumed) derivative of βδέω (to stink);

Definition

to be disgusted, i.e. (by implication) detest (especially of idolatry)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

abhor, abominable.
