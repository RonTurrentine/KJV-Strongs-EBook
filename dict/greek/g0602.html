BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀποκάλυψις

Transliteration: apokalupsis

Phonetic: ap-ok-al'-oop-sis

Thayer Definition:

  1. laying bear, making naked
  2. a disclosure of truth, instruction
    1. concerning things before unknown
    2. used of events by which things or states or persons hitherto withdrawn from view are made visible to all
  3. manifestation, appearance

Origin: from G0601

TDNT entry: 12:23,4

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
