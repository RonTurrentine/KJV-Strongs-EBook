καταπέτασμα

Transliterationkatapétasma
Phonetickat-ap-et'-as-mah
Originfrom a compound of G2596 and a congener of G4072;

Definition

something spread thoroughly, i.e. (specially) the door screen (to the Most Holy Place) in the Jewish Temple

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

vail.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: καταπέτασμα

Transliteration: katapetasma

Phonetic: kat-ap-et'-as-mah

Thayer Definition:

  1. a veil spread out, a curtain
    1. the name given to the two curtains in the temple at Jerusalem, one of them at the entrance to the temple separated the Holy Place from the outer court, the other veiled the Holy of Holies from the Holy Place

Origin: from a compound of G2596 and a congener of G4072

TDNT entry: 13:28,4

Part(s) of speech: Noun Neuter
