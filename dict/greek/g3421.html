μνημονεύω

Transliterationmnēmoneúō
Phonetic mnay-mon-yoo'-o
Originfrom a derivative of G3420;

Definition

to exercise memory, i.e. recollect; by implication, to punish; also to rehearse

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

make mention; be mindful, remember.
