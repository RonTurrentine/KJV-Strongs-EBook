ἐγκομβόομαι

Transliterationenkombóomai
Phoneticeng-kom-bo'-om-ahee
Originmiddle voice from G1722 and κομβόω (to gird);

Definition

to engirdle oneself (for labor), i.e. figuratively (the apron as being a badge of servitude) to wear (in token of mutual deference)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be clothed with.
