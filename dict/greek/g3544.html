νομικός

Transliterationnomikós
Phoneticnom-ik-os'
Originfrom G3551;

Definition

according (or pertaining) to law, i.e. legal (ceremonially); as noun, an expert in the (Mosaic) law

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

about the law, lawyer.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: νομικός

Transliteration: nomikos

Phonetic: nom-ik-os'

Thayer Definition:

  1. pertaining to the law, one learned in the law
  2. in the NT an interpreter and teacher of the Mosaic law

Origin: from G3551

TDNT entry: 22:08,6

Part(s) of speech: Adjective
