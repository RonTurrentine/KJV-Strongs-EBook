φέρω

Transliterationphérō
Phoneticfer'-o
Origina primary verb (for which other, and apparently not cognate ones are used in certain tenses only; namely, οἴωoy'-o; and ἐνέγκωen-eng'-ko;

Definition

to "bear" or carry (in a very wide application, literally and figuratively, as follows)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be, bear, bring (forth), carry, come, + let her drive, be driven, endure, go on, lay, lead, move, reach, rushing, uphold.
