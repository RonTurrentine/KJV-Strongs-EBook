ἐκχέω

Transliterationekchéō
Phoneticek-kheh'-o
Originfrom G1537 and χέω (to pour);

Definition

to pour forth; figuratively, to bestow

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

gush (pour) out, run greedily (out), shed (abroad, forth), spill.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἐκχέω ἐκχύνω

Transliteration: ekcheō ekchunō

Phonetic: ek-kheh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to pour out, shed forth
  2. metaphorically to bestow or distribute largely

Origin: from G1537

TDNT entry: 09:47,2

Part(s) of speech: Verb
