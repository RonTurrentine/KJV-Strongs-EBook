μετεωρίζω

Transliterationmeteōrízō
Phonetic met-eh-o-rid'-zo
Originfrom a compound of G3326 and a collateral form of G142 or perhaps rather G109 (compare "meteor");

Definition

to raise in mid-air, i.e. (figuratively) suspend (passively, fluctuate or be anxious)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be of doubtful mind.
