γεύομαι

Transliterationgeúomai
Phoneticghyoo'-om-ahee
Origina primary verb;

Definition

to taste; by implication, to eat; figuratively, to experience (good or ill)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

eat, taste.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: γεύομαι

Transliteration: geuomai

Phonetic: ghyoo'-om-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to taste, to try the flavour of
  2. to taste
    1. i.e. perceive the flavour of, partake of, enjoy
    2. to feel, make trial of, experience
  3. to take food, eat, to take nourishment, eat

Origin: a root word

TDNT entry: 12:15,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
