οὐρανός

Transliterationouranós
Phoneticoo-ran-os'
Originperhaps from the same as G3735 (through the idea of elevation);

Definition

the sky; by extension, heaven (as the abode of God); by implication, happiness, power, eternity; specially, the Gospel (Christianity)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

air, heaven(-ly), sky.
