ὀργή

Transliterationorgḗ
Phoneticor-gay'
Originfrom G3713;

Definition

properly, desire (as a reaching forth or excitement of the mind), i.e. (by analogy), violent passion (ire, or (justifiable) abhorrence); by implication punishment

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

anger, indignation, vengeance, wrath.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὀργή

Transliteration: orgē

Phonetic: or-gay'

Thayer Definition:

  1. anger, the natural disposition, temper, character
  2. movement or agitation of the soul, impulse, desire, any violent emotion, but especially anger
  3. anger, wrath, indignation
  4. anger exhibited in punishment, hence used for punishment itself
    1. of punishments inflicted by magistrates

Origin: from G3713

TDNT entry: 11:22,7

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
