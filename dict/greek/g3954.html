παῤῥησία

Transliterationparrhēsía
Phoneticpar-rhay-see'-ah
Originfrom G3956 and a derivative of G4483;

Definition

all out-spokenness, i.e. frankness, bluntness, publicity; by implication, assurance

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

bold (X -ly, -ness, -ness of speech), confidence, X freely, X openly, X plainly(-ness).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: παῤῥησία

Transliteration: parrhēsia

Phonetic: par-rhay-see'-ah

Thayer Definition:

  1. freedom in speaking, unreservedness in speech
    1. openly, frankly, i.e without concealment
    2. without ambiguity or circumlocution
    3. without the use of figures and comparisons
  2. free and fearless confidence, cheerful courage, boldness, assurance
  3. the deportment by which one becomes conspicuous or secures publicity

Origin: from G3956 and a derivative of G4483

TDNT entry: 19:31,8

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
