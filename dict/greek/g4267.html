Transliterationproginṓskō
Phoneticprog-in-oce'-ko
Originfrom G4253 and G1097;

Definition

to know beforehand, i.e. foresee

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

foreknow (ordain), know (before).

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: προγινώσκω

Transliteration: proginōskō

Phonetic: prog-in-oce'-ko

Thayer Definition:

  1. to have knowledge before hand
  2. to foreknow
    1. of those whom God elected to salvation
  3. to predestinate

Origin: from G4253 and G1097

TDNT entry: 12:55,1

Part(s) of speech: Verb
