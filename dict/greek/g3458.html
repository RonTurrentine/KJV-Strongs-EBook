Originprobably ultimately from the base of G3433 (through the idea of hardship);

Definition

a "mill", i.e. (by implication), a grinder (millstone)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

millstone.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: μύλος

Transliteration: mulos

Phonetic: moo'-los

Thayer Definition:

  1. a mill stone
    1. a large mill consisted of two stones, an upper and an under one
    2. the "nether" stone was stationary, but the upper one was turned by an ass
  2. a mill, the noise made by a mill

Origin: probably ultimately from the base of G3433 (through the idea of hardship)

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
