ἀνατολή

Transliterationanatolḗ
Phonetican-at-ol-ay'
Originfrom G0393;

Definition

a rising of light, i.e. dawn (figuratively); by implication, the east (also in plural)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

dayspring, east, rising.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ἀνατολή

Transliteration: anatolē

Phonetic: an-at-ol-ay'

Thayer Definition:

  1. a rising (of the sun and stars)
  2. the east (the direction of the sun's rising)

Origin: from G0393

TDNT entry: 06:52,6

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
