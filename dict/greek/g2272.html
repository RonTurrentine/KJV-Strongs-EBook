ἡσύχιος

Transliterationhēsýchios
Phonetic hay-soo'-khee-os
Origina prolonged form of a compound probably of a derivative of the base of G1476 and perhaps G2192;

Definition

properly, keeping one's seat (sedentary), i.e. (by implication) still (undisturbed, undisturbing)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

peaceable, quiet.
