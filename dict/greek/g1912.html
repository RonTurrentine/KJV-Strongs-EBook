ἐπιβαρέω

Transliterationepibaréō
Phonetic ep-ee-bar-eh'-o
Originfrom G1909 and G916;

Definition

to be heavy upon, i.e. (pecuniarily) to be expensive to; figuratively, to be severe towards

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be chargeable to, overcharge.
