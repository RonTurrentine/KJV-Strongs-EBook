ἀποστοματίζω

Transliterationapostomatízō
Phoneticap-os-tom-at-id'-zo
Originfrom G575 and a (presumed) derivative of G4750;

Definition

to speak off-hand (properly, dictate), i.e. to catechize (in an invidious manner)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

provoke to speak.
