μνάομαι

Transliterationmnáomai
Phonetic mnah'-om-ahee
Originmiddle voice of a derivative of G3306 or perhaps of the base of G3145 (through the idea of fixture in the mind or of mental grasp);

Definition

to bear in mind, i.e. recollect; by implication, to reward or punish

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

be mindful, remember, come (have) in remembrance.
