κατασκευάζω

Transliterationkataskeuázō
Phonetickat-ask-yoo-ad'-zo
Originfrom G2596 and a derivative of G4632;

Definition

to prepare thoroughly (properly, by external equipment; whereas G2090 refers rather to internal fitness); by implication, to construct, create

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

build, make, ordain, prepare.
