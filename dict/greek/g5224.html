ὑπάρχοντα

Transliterationhypárchonta
Phonetichoop-ar'-khon-tah
Originneuter plural of present participle active of G5225 as noun;

Definition

things extant or in hand, i.e. property or possessions

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

goods, that which one has, things which (one) possesseth, substance, that hast.
