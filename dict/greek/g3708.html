ὁράω

Transliterationhoráō
Phonetichor-ah'-o
Originproperly, to stare at (compare G3700), i.e. (by implication) to discern clearly (physically or mentally);

Definition

by extension, to attend to; by Hebraism, to experience; passively, to appear

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

behold, perceive, see, take heed.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὁράω

Transliteration: horaō

Phonetic: hor-ah'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to see with the eyes
  2. to see with the mind, to perceive, know
  3. to see, i.e. become acquainted with by experience, to experience
  4. to see, to look to
    1. to take heed, beware
    2. to care for, pay heed to
  5. I was seen, showed myself, appeared

Origin: properly, to stare at [cf G3700]

TDNT entry: 10:15,7

Part(s) of speech: Verb
