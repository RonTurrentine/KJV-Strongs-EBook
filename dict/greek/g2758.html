κενόω

Transliterationkenóō
Phoneticken-o'-o
Originfrom G2756;

Definition

to make empty, i.e. (figuratively) to abase, neutralize, falsify

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

make (of none effect, of no reputation, void), be in vain.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: κενόω

Transliteration: kenoō

Phonetic: ken-o'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to empty, make empty
    1. of Christ, he laid aside equality with or the form of God
  2. to make void
    1. deprive of force, render vain, useless, of no effect
  3. to make void
    1. cause a thing to be seen to be empty, hollow, false

Origin: from G2756

TDNT entry: 14:01,4

Part(s) of speech: Verb
