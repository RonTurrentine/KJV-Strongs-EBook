πρασιά

Transliterationprasiá
Phoneticpras-ee-ah'
Originperhaps from πράσον (a leek, and so an onion-patch);

Definition

a garden plot, i.e. (by implication, of regular beds) a row (repeated in plural by Hebraism, to indicate an arrangement)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

in ranks.
