BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ὀφθαλμοδουλεία

Transliteration: ophthalmodouleia

Phonetic: of-thal-mod-oo-li'-ah

Thayer Definition:

  1. service performed [only] under the master's eyes
    1. for the master's eye usually stimulates to greater diligence
    2. his absence, on the other hand, renders sluggish

Origin: from G3788 and G1397

Part(s) of speech: Noun Feminine
