Definition

to traverse (literally)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

come, depart, go (about, abroad, everywhere, over, through, throughout), pass (by, over, through, throughout), pierce through, travel, walk through.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: διέρχομαι

Transliteration: dierchomai

Phonetic: dee-er'-khom-ahee

Thayer Definition:

  1. to go through, pass through
    1. to go, walk, journey, pass through a place
    2. to travel the road which leads through a place, go, pass, travel through a region
  2. to go different places
    1. of people, to go abroad
    2. of a report, to spread, go abroad

Origin: from G1223 and G2064

TDNT entry: 13:16,3

Part(s) of speech: Verb
