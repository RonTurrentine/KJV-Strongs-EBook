κύκλῳ

Transliterationkýklōi
Phonetickoo'-klo
Originas if dative case of κύκλος (a ring, "cycle"; akin to G2947);

Definition

i.e. in a circle (by implication, of G1722), i.e. (adverbially) all around

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage
