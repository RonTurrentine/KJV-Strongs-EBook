φλυαρέω

Transliterationphlyaréō
Phoneticfloo-ar-eh'-o
Originfrom G5397;

Definition

to be a babbler or trifler, i.e. (by implication) to berate idly or mischievously

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

prate against.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: φλυαρέω

Transliteration: phluareō

Phonetic: floo-ar-eh'-o

Thayer Definition:

  1. to utter nonsense, talk idly, prate
  2. to bring forward idle accusations, make empty charges
  3. to accuse one falsely with malicious words
