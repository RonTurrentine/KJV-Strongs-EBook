ὑπεραίρομαι

Transliterationhyperaíromai
Phonetic hoop-er-ah'-ee-rom-ahee
Originmiddle voice from G5228 and G142;

Definition

to raise oneself over, i.e. (figuratively) to become haughty

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

exalt self, be exalted above measure.
