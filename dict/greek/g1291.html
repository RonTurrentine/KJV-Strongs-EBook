διαστέλλομαι

Transliterationdiastéllomai
Phoneticdee-as-tel'-lom-ahee
Originmiddle voice from G1223 and G4724;

Definition

to set (oneself) apart (figuratively, distinguish), i.e. (by implication) to enjoin

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

charge, that which was (give) commanded(-ment).
