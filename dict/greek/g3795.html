ὀψάριον

Transliterationopsárion
Phoneticop-sar'-ee-on
Originneuter of a presumed derivative of the base of G3702;

Definition

a relish to other food (as if cooked sauce), i.e. (specially), fish (presumably salted and dried as a condiment)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

fish.
