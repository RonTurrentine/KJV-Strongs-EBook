ξέστης

Transliterationxéstēs
Phoneticxes'-tace
Originas if from ξέω (properly, to smooth;

Definition

by implication, (of friction) to boil or heat); a vessel (as fashioned or for cooking) (or perhaps by corruption from the Latin sextarius, the sixth of a modius, i.e. about a pint), i.e. (specially), a measure for liquids or solids, (by analogy, a pitcher)

Strong's Definition / KJV Usage

pot.

BDB / Thayer Definition

Original: ξέστης

Transliteration: xestēs

Phonetic: xes'-tace

Thayer Definition:

  1. a sextarius
    1. a vessel for measuring liquids, holding about a pint (.5 litre)
  2. a wooden pitcher or ewer from which water or wine is poured, whether holding a sextarius or not

Origin: as if from xeo (properly, to smooth, by implication [of friction] to boil or heat)

TDNT entry: None

Part(s) of speech: Noun Masculine
